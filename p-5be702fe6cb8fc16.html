  • Day 7: Shoulders, Biceps, Triceps, Abdominals/Core, Forearms, Neck - YouTube

    • About

      • The shoulders and arms day of the Chapter Two resistance training program. The video covers shoulders, biceps, and triceps; the resources page links forearm, face pull, and neck videos for the rest of the day.
        • Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols/YouTube/18/12/The Perfect Forearm Workout

    • Video

        • 00:11 Barbell overhead press

          • Compound movement for the deltoids. Move the head out of the way as the bar comes down. Huberman uses a full range of motion rather than stopping with the elbows at 90 degrees.

        • 00:52 Dumbbell overhead press

          • Lift the dumbbells off the floor with bent knees, as in a deadlift, then kick them up to the shoulders. Seated on a flat bench or with back support.

        • 01:45 Machine presses

          • Also compound movements. Huberman favors barbells and dumbbells because balancing the weight takes extra coordination.

        • 02:22 Lateral and front raises

          • Light dumbbells, sometimes as little as 5 pounds, raised under control with thumbs level with or higher than the pinkies, slightly in front of the body. Front raises with dumbbells or cables if the bench and shoulder presses don't develop the front deltoids enough.

        • 03:20 Rear deltoid fly

          • Bent-over dumbbell fly with a stance a little wider than the shoulders, or a cable or machine version. Elbows stay bent and fixed; keep the sternum high and the chin in. Back training works the rear deltoids only indirectly.

        • 04:44 Biceps curls

          • The biceps flex the elbow, turn the palm up (supination), and lift the shoulder slightly. Dumbbells allow all three; curl both arms at once or alternate. Barbell and EZ-bar curls are other options.

        • 06:16 Preacher curl machine

          • Isolation movement; only the elbow bends. At failure, stand up and set the weight down instead of letting the arm drop straight, which can tear a biceps.

        • 07:06 Triceps overview

          • Dips on torso day already work the triceps, so shoulders and arms day adds only a little direct work.

        • 07:40 Overhead cable triceps extension

          • A compound movement with the cable set above and behind; it stretches the long head of the triceps. Lock the lats and core. Usually three sets to or near failure.

        • 08:43 Triceps isolation

          • Same cable setup with only the elbows moving, pressing the handles out behind and to the sides. Two or three sets to failure.
