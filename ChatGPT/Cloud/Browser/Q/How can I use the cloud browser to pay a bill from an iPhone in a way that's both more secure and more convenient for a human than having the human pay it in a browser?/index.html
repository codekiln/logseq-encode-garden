  • How can I use the ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser to pay a bill from an iPhone in a way that's both more secure and more convenient for a human than having the human pay it in a browser?

    • Note: for a human, copying and pasting from another app into a VM is less convenient than using browser integration with 1Password or other password managers.

    • AI Answer

      • Pay an existing lab bill directly on your iPhone. Use the lab’s patient billing portal. As of November 21, 2026, there’s no verified ChatGPT workflow for this kind of lab bill. OpenAI says cloud browser checkout depends on the website and the transaction. Cloud browser help
      • What you can delegate today. On ChatGPT/Plus or ChatGPT/Pro, ChatGPT/Work can open supported biller sites and prepare payment. OpenAI documents compatible password managers in the iPhone sign-in form. Work asks before submitting a payment it can complete, and some sites require you to take over. If you have to sign in and enter payment details yourself, paying in Safari with your usual AutoFill has fewer handoffs. ChatGPT browser guide · Cloud browser help
      • Where ChatGPT can pay. On eligible US Pro accounts, a dot can use ChatGPT/Wallet to submit a supported purchase after Face ID or Touch ID approval. Support depends on the checkout. OpenAI Wallet guide
      • PayPal. If your biller appears in PayPal/Bill Pay, you can submit payment in the PayPal app. A supported ChatGPT payment through Bill Pay remains unverified.
      • For recurring bills. Biller autopay can remove the monthly checkout step where it’s offered. Check whether the biller offers recurring autopay or asks you to authorize each visit separately.
      • Claude. Anthropic prohibits purchases and financial transactions in Claude in Chrome. Claude Cowork’s built-in browser uses the same safeguards. Claude in Chrome permissions · Cowork browser guide
