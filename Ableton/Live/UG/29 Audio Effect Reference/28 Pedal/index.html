  • 29.28 Pedal

    • Pedal is a guitar distortion. With Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/39 Tuner, Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/01 Amp and Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/06 Cabinet it covers guitar processing, and it also works on vocals, synths and drums.
    • Gain — distortion amount; 0% still distorts. Start at 0% and raise it. Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/40 Utility before Pedal can lower the input further.
    • Output — output gain
    • Pedal Type:
      • Overdrive — warm and smooth
      • Distortion — tight and aggressive
      • Fuzz — unstable, like a broken amp
    • A three-band adaptive EQ after the distortion; Q rises with boost.
      • Bass — peak at 100 Hz, for punch in bass and drums or to thin out guitars
      • Mid — boost with a three-position Mid Frequency switch: 500 Hz (narrow), 1 kHz, 2 kHz (wide), as on guitar pedals
      • Treble — shelf at 3.3 kHz, to remove or add harshness
      • For finer EQ, leave these neutral and add Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/15 EQ Eight after.
    • Sub — low shelf boosting below 250 Hz. Pair it with Bass: Sub on with Bass −100%, or Sub off with Bass 100%.
    • Dry/Wet — balance of processed and dry signal
    • Hi-Quality in the title bar's context menu reduces aliasing, for slightly more CPU.
    • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/28 Pedal/01 Pedal Tips

      • 29.28.1 Pedal Tips

        • 29.28.1.1 Positioning Pedal in the Device Chain

          • What comes before Pedal changes how it distorts. A Compressor first gives a more even result; a resonant, high-gain EQ or filter first gives a screaming distortion.

        • 29.28.1.2 Techno Kick

          • Pick a kick with a long decay (such as Kit-Core 909 with decay raised), choose the Distortion type, turn on Sub and set Gain to taste.
          • More whack: Mid Frequency fully right and Mid up. More thump: Bass up. Less air: Treble down.

        • 29.28.1.3 Drum Group Fizzle

          • Fuzz, Gain 50%, Sub off; Bass and Mid at −100%, Mid Frequency to taste, Treble 100%; Output −20 dB. Then raise Dry/Wet from 0% until the drums fizz enough.

        • 29.28.1.4 Broken Speaker

          • Fuzz, Sub off, Bass all the way down, Treble 25%, Mid 100% with Mid Frequency fully right, Gain 100%.

        • 29.28.1.5 Sub Warmer

          • To warm a plain sub bass: Overdrive type, Sub on, Bass up, then raise Gain slowly; shape the mids with Mid.
