  • 26.11.14-Fallveter-04-perf-post

      • The Session View mixer of the copy saved after the performance, with the transport stopped at bar 71. MF AUDIO 1, POOL PIANO DRUMS, MF AUDIO 2 and Cueball Drums run left to right, followed by the folded END, rec archive and utility groups; the two return tracks and the Main track sit off the right edge.

    • The performance and this copy

      • Live saved the Set during the performance as 26.11.14-Fallveter-04-perf-pre.als. It kept recording for about an hour; the performance is the first 4:40, between the locators "1" and "end". At 60 BPM a beat lasts one second, so beat numbers in the Arrangement read as seconds of the episode. Ableton/Live/UG/07 Session View/05 Recording Sessions into the Arrangement
      • This page describes the copy saved afterwards, 26.11.14-Fallveter-04-perf-post.als, which rendered the episode. In it, MF AUDIO 1 and MF AUDIO 2 are switched off and the rec archive tracks play to Master, so the MicroFreak comes from the archive recordings. The drums were raised by 4 dB, reverb sends were added to the archive tracks, and the Arrangement ends at 4:40.

    • Signal path

      • The Microfreak arrives on external inputs 1/2 at both MF AUDIO 1 (orange) and MF AUDIO 2 (green). Both are armed with monitoring on Auto, so each records takes from the MicroFreak and loops them in Session View. Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/01 Monitoring
      • Rec MF AUDIO 1 and rec MF AUDIO 2 record the two MicroFreak tracks after their faders. Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/05 Internal Routings
      • POOL PIANO DRUMS (purple) listens to channel 2 of the Launchpad Pro's MIDI port, where Launchpad sequencer track 2 plays the Piano Pool drum rack. Cueball Drums (coral) listens to channel 4. Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/13 Settings/05 MIDI Channel
      • LPP to MF passes the Launchpad's channel 1 to the MicroFreak but carried no notes during the performance. MF to LPP is not armed, so the MicroFreak's keys left no MIDI.
      • No track is assigned to either side of the crossfader. Ableton/Live/UG/18 Mixing/05 Using Live's Crossfader
      • A-Reverb holds a Reverb and B-Delay a Delay.

    • Session View

      • Sixteen unnamed scenes. "silence" clips sit in scenes 1, 8 and 9 on the four main tracks, in scene 16 on MF AUDIO 1 and MF AUDIO 2, and in scenes 1 to 9 on the END tracks.
      • The performance takes sit in scenes 2 to 4: "MF AUDIO 1 22", "MF AUDIO 1 24-1" and "MF AUDIO 1 27-1" on MF AUDIO 1, and "MF AUDIO 2 8" and "MF AUDIO 2 11-1" on MF AUDIO 2.
    • GitP/A/Session/26/11/14-Sat/Set/Track/MF AUDIO 1

      • MF AUDIO 1

        • The orange MicroFreak track. It recorded three takes during the performance and looped each one: "MF AUDIO 1 22" from 00:20, "MF AUDIO 1 24-1" from 02:08 and "MF AUDIO 1 27-1" from 03:44.
        • Its fader moved live: down to −12 dB at 01:24, back near −5 dB at 01:34, out from 03:20 to 03:25, back at −6 dB at 03:31, and out again by 04:39. Its pan moved to 34R at 00:42.
        • In this copy of the Set the track is switched off, and GitP/A/Session/26/11/14-Sat/Set/Track/rec archive/Rec MF AUDIO 1 plays what it sent out.
    • GitP/A/Session/26/11/14-Sat/Set/Track/POOL PIANO DRUMS- LPP sequencer track 2

      • POOL PIANO DRUMS- LPP sequencer track 2

        • The purple track. Launchpad/UI/Mode/5-Sequencer track 2 plays the Piano Pool Ableton/Instrument/Drum Rack on MIDI channel 2. Its part is through-composed in the sequencer: patterns chained into one five-minute line and saved as a Launchpad Project. Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/03 Patterns View/01 Chain
        • Its pan moved to 34L at 00:42. After the performance its fader was raised from −6 dB to −2 dB until 04:24; it comes down to −17.6 dB by 04:30 and is out by 04:38.

        • Piano Pool pads

          • C1 to A1: ten piano recordings from the Ableton/Note set Veteran, made on 2026-11-11 Wed (11_11_26-R2, R5, R7, R6, R8, R9, R10, R11, R12, R13, in pad order).
          • A♯1 and B1: Pool Cue 39 curated, a pool-cue strike.
          • C2: 30_06_24-R10, a recording from Ableton/Live/Instrument/Drum/Forevertron.
          • C♯2: Pool Cue 45 curated. D2: Pool Cue 28 curated.
          • D♯2: a recording of the chirp from Microfreak/Preset/399 FireAlarmLoBat.
    • GitP/A/Session/26/11/14-Sat/Set/Track/MF AUDIO 2

      • MF AUDIO 2

        • The green MicroFreak track. It recorded two takes and looped each one: "MF AUDIO 2 8" from 01:00 to 02:44, and the four-second "MF AUDIO 2 11-1" from 03:04.
        • Its pan moved to 34R at 00:42, and its fader came down from 04:34 to 04:35.
        • In this copy of the Set the track is switched off, and GitP/A/Session/26/11/14-Sat/Set/Track/rec archive/rec MF AUDIO 2 plays what it sent out.
    • GitP/A/Session/26/11/14-Sat/Set/Track/Cueball Drums - LPP Track 4

      • Cueball Drums - LPP Track 4

        • The coral track. It plays a drum rack of 38 pool-cue strikes, Pool Cue 12 on F1 up to Pool Cue 14 on G4, from Launchpad track 4 on MIDI channel 4.
        • Its only notes in the performance are sixteen strikes played by hand between 04:09 and 04:21: Pool Cue 28 on F♯4 and Pool Cue 45 on F4. Its pan moved to 34L at 00:42.
