- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Deactivate or reactivate the selected clips, notes or track** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `0`
		- Description: Deactivates or reactivates the selected Arrangement clips or time, Session clips, MIDI notes, or a selected track. The Session View list calls it Deactivate Selected Clip and the track commands call it Deactivate Selected Track. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/09 Selecting Clips and Time]], [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]], [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/15 Session View]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/19 Commands for Tracks]].
