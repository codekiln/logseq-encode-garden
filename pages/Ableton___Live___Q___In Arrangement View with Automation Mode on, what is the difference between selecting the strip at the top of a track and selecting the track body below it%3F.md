- # In [Arrangement View]([[Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement-View]]) with [Automation Mode]([[Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement-View/Toggle/Automation-Mode]]) on, what is the difference between selecting the strip at the top of a track and selecting the track body below it?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- **Short answer:** with Automation Mode on, the body below the strip is the track's main automation lane, and a time selection there edits only the automation: Cut, Copy, Duplicate and Delete change the part of the envelope inside the selection and leave the clips and the rest of the envelope alone. The strip at the top is the track's clip row, where clips show their title bars. Dragging there selects time on the track itself, the way dragging anywhere in the track does with Automation Mode off. [[Answer/Official]] from [Live 12 Manual, 26.9.3 Using Edit Menu Commands](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/automation/#using-edit-menu-commands)

		- **Names for the parts.** The manual calls the body the **main automation lane**: turning on Automation Mode "opens the main automation lane for each track below the track header". Its Device and Automation Control choosers, Mixer and Track Volume in your screenshots, sit in the lane's header on the right, and the red line across the lane is the envelope. The manual has no name for the strip; its own picture of automation lanes shows a clip's title bar running along it, with the envelope drawn over the clip below. [[Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/05 Viewing Automation/02 Viewing Automation in Arrangement View]] · [26.5.2 Viewing Automation in Arrangement View](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/automation/#viewing-automation-in-arrangement-view)
			- ![A clip's title bar above its main automation lane and an additional lane, from the Live 12 manual](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MultipleAutomationLanesL12.png?auto=&w=320)
		- **"Track header" means something else in Live.** The track header is the box with the track's name at the right end of its lane, MF2-AUDIO in your screenshots. Selecting it selects the track itself: `0` then deactivates the whole track, and `Delete` deletes it. [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/09 Selecting Clips and Time]] · [6.9 Selecting Clips and Time](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/arrangement-view/#selecting-clips-and-time)
		- **With Automation Mode off**, the strip and the lane are gone, and a selection covers the track's full height, selecting time and clips on the track. The `Lane` row under MF2-AUDIO in the third screenshot is a take lane from comping, not an automation lane. [[Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping/01 Take Lanes]]
			- ![Time selection on the track with Automation Mode off](../assets/.remote/logseq-encode-garden/Ableton___Live___Q___In-Arrangement-View-with-Automation-Mode-on,-what-is-the-difference-between-selecting-the-strip-at-the-top-of-a-track-and-selecting-the-track-body-below-it___Asset___Image___Automation-Mode-Off.png)
		- **What the manual doesn't say.** It says moving Arrangement clips moves their automation with them unless [Lock Envelopes]([[Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement-View/Toggle/Lock-Envelopes]]) is on, but not whether deleting clips from a selection in the strip also deletes the envelope under them. Try `Delete` on a selection in the strip in a copy of the Set and look at the lane. [26.9.2 Locking Envelopes in Automation Lanes](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/automation/#locking-envelopes-in-automation-lanes)
