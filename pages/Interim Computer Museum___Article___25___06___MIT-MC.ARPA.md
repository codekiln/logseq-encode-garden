- # [MIT-MC.ARPA](https://icm.museum/blog/?p=38)
	- ## Summary
		- [[Interim Computer Museum]] describes MIT-MC, a DECSYSTEM-10 KL10 Model 1080 operated by the MIT MACSYMA Consortium, as a major ARPANET node and an influential environment for symbolic computing and artificial intelligence.
	- ## Notes
		- MIT-MC ran the Incompatible Timesharing System (ITS), hosted MACSYMA, and connected programmers, researchers, and students through remote access.
		- The preservation story connects with the book discussion through MIT Project MAC, the MIT AI Lab, and the article's account of the MIT hacker ethic.
