- # TS — Tip-Sleeve
	- Two contacts, one insulating ring. The tip carries a single unbalanced signal; the sleeve is its ground return.
	- The oldest of the phone layouts, descended from the 19th-century telephone switchboard plug.
	- ## Sizes
		- [[Audio/Connector/Phone/TS/3.5mm]] — mono earpieces, and patch points on compact gear such as modular synthesizers.
		- 6.35 mm (1/4 in) — the full-sized original: electric guitars, amplifiers and effects pedals.
