- # [Pi](https://pi.dev)
	- A minimal, self-modifying AI coding agent. Stripped of most built-in features (no MCP support, no plan mode); users extend it themselves by writing hook-based extensions the agent loads into its own process.
	- That mechanism is [[PiAI/Extension]].
	- [[OpenClaw]] started as a fork of Pi (originally called Tau) and later adopted Pi directly as its underlying agent core.
	- Written in [[TypeScript]].
	- Developed under [[Earendil]] (Earendil Works), the company founded by [[Person/Armin Ronacher]].
