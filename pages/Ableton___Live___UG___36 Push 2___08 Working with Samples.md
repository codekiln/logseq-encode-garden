- # [36.8 Working with Samples](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#working-with-samples)
	- Push 2 plays samples through [[Ableton/Instrument/Simpler]], with its controls on the encoders and display; see [[Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference]].
	- Load a sample with Browse on a new or existing MIDI track. An empty Simpler can't play, and the display prompts you to browse.
	- In Device mode the display shows the waveform and Simpler's main parameter bank.
		- ![Simpler's main parameter bank in the display](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2SimplerMain.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Simpler sets itself up from the sample's length: short samples play once, long ones loop and warp. Warped samples play at the Set tempo on any note, and warp settings from clips or files carry over; see [[Ableton/Live/UG/09 Audio Clips Tempo and Warping]].
	- **Mode** picks one of three playback modes:
		- **Classic** (default) — pitched, polyphonic instruments with a full ADSR envelope and looping, playing on the melodic grid.
		- **One-Shot** — monophonic, for drum hits and short phrases; simple envelope, no looping, the whole sample plays whatever the note length.
		- **Slicing** — slices the sample without changing it, one slice per pad, made by hand or automatically; suits drum breaks.
