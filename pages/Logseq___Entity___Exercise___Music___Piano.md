- # Piano Exercise
	- In this garden, **Piano Exercise** pages model music exercises practiced at the piano or keyboard.
	- Extends [[Logseq/Entity/Exercise/Music]] and inherits its page shape.
	- Setup identifies the keyboard range, hand or hands, and fingering or coordination pattern where relevant. Practice states the notes or musical pattern and the relation between note values and the beat.
	- Scales and arpeggios can supply musical material. A scale or arpeggio concept describes that material; a piano exercise describes a particular way to practice it.
	- Instances use `Exercise/Music/Piano/<Name>`.
	- Example: [[Exercise/Music/Piano/Scale with Increasing Octaves and Subdivisions]].
