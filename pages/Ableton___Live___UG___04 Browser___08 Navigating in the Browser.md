- # [4.8 Navigating in the Browser](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/working-with-the-browser/#navigating-in-the-browser)
	- Scroll with the arrow keys, the mouse wheel, or `Cmd+Option`-drag (Mac) / `Ctrl+Alt`-drag (Win).
	- Left and right arrows open and close folders and move between the sidebar and the content pane.
	- Opening a folder closes the others unless you hold `Cmd` (Mac) / `Ctrl` (Win).
