- # [36.12 Recording Automation](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#recording-automation)
	- Press **Automation** (lit red) to arm Session automation recording; encoder moves are then recorded into the clip. Press again to stop.
		- ![The Automation button](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2Automation.png?auto=&w=182)
	- Hold Delete and touch an encoder to delete its automation, or reset the parameter to default if it has none.
	- A white dot marks automated parameters; a grey dot marks overridden ones. `Shift`+Automation re-enables all overridden automation.
