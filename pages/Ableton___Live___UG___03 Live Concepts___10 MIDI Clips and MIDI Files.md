- # [3.10 MIDI Clips and MIDI Files](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-concepts/#midi-clips-and-midi-files)
	- A MIDI clip holds notes and controller envelopes. Importing a `.mid` file copies its data into the Set; the file isn't referenced afterward.
	- Edit notes in the Clip View. Besides recording from devices, notes can come from Draw Mode, MIDI Tools, or audio-to-MIDI conversion.
