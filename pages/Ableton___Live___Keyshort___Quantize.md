- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Quantize the selected notes or audio transients with the current settings** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+U` (Win `Ctrl+U`)
		- Description: In the MIDI Note Editor, moves the selected notes to the grid with the Quantize tool's settings; in the Sample Editor, moves each transient to the nearest grid line.
