- # `record-label::` — who released a recording
	- The [[Logseq/Entity/Music/Label]] that released a [[Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording]].
	- ## Values
		- One or more comma-separated links to label pages, as `record-label:: [[Label/Invada Records]], [[Label/Temporary Residence]]`. A co-release lists each label.
		- When the original release and a later reissue have different labels, list the label of the original release.
