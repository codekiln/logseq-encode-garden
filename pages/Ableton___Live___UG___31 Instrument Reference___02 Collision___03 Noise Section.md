- # [31.2.3 Noise Section](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#noise-section)
	- A second exciter: white noise through a multimode filter with its own envelope, usable instead of or with the Mallet. The **Noise** button turns it on.
		- ![Collision's Noise Section](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/CollisionNoiseL12.png)
	- Filter types LP, HP, BP and LP+HP. **Freq** sets cutoff or center; **Res** sets resonance, or bandwidth in LP+HP. Cutoff can follow note pitch, velocity or the envelope.
	- **Volume** — output level, modulated by pitch and velocity in the MIDI tab.
	- **Env Amt** with **A**, **D**, **S**, **R** — an [[ADSR]] envelope on the filter. S at 0 removes sustain; at 100 removes decay.
