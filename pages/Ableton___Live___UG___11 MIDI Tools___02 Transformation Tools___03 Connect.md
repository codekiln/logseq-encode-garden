- # [11.2.3 Connect](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/midi-tools/#connect)
	- Fills the gaps between notes with new notes at random positions.
	- Spread limits how far their pitch can stray from the originals; Density sets what share of each gap is filled, all of it at 100%. Rate sets their length; Tie is the chance a note extends to the next original note.
