- # Prelude in C-sharp Minor, Op. 3 No. 2
	- A 62-bar prelude in ternary form, also called *The Bells of Moscow*. Rachmaninoff gave the first performance on 26 September 1892 at the Moscow Electrical Exhibition, and it became his most famous piece.
	- He later wrote 23 more preludes, Op. 23 and Op. 32, so that his preludes cover all 24 major and minor keys.
	- Source: [Prelude in C-sharp minor (Rachmaninoff)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prelude_in_C-sharp_minor_(Rachmaninoff)) on Wikipedia.
