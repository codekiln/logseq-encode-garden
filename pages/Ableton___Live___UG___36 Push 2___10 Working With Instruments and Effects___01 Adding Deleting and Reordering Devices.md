- # [36.10.1 Adding, Deleting, and Reordering Devices](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#adding-deleting-and-reordering-devices)
	- **Add Device** opens the browser with every device type the track can take. Loading an instrument this way replaces the current one, as in Browse.
		- ![The Add Device button](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2AddDevice.png?auto=&w=294)
	- New devices go after the selected one; MIDI effects always go before the instrument and audio effects after.
	- Hold **Delete** and press a device's upper button to delete it. Hold **Mute** and press it to switch the device off or back on (shown grey). Holding Mute for a moment locks it on until pressed again.
	- Hold an effect's upper button and turn any encoder to move it in the chain.
