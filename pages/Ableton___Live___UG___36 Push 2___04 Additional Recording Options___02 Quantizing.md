- # [36.4.2 Quantizing](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#quantizing)
	- **Quantize** snaps the selected clip's notes to the grid; hold it for options:
		- ![Quantization options](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2QuantizeMenu.png?auto=&w=320)
		- **Swing Amount** — swing applied while quantizing (also the Swing knob);
		- **Quantize To** — the note value;
		- **Quantize Amount** — how far notes move;
		- **Record Quantize** — quantize while recording, at the value set by encoder 5. Recorded notes don't get swing this way.
	- Hold Quantize and tap a drum pad to quantize only that drum's notes.
