- # How a preset stores its patch settings
	- A preset keeps each setting as a named field, such as `VCO.BendRng`, holding a descriptor and a 16-bit value. [[Microfreak/CLI/Inspect a preset]] prints them. The descriptor of a choice field is the number of choices minus one, and the field stores choice × 32767 ÷ descriptor, rounded. Continuous controls store 0–32767 for 0–1, and matrix amounts store a signed value for −1 to 1.
	- The conversions below were measured on my MicroFreak by writing values into test slots with `mise run microfreak:probe` and recording the result with the [[Zoom H5/My Unit]]. [[Microfreak/Patch/Research]] lists the runs. [lib/patch_fields.py](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-encode-garden/blob/main/mise-tasks/microfreak/lib/patch_fields.py) holds them in code, and `mise run microfreak:inspect FILE --interpret-fw5` adds a `display` value for each field it covers.
	- ## Voice
		- These are the Voice settings of Utility > Preset ([[Microfreak/UG/14 Config/01 Utility & MCC/01 Preset]]).
		- ### Bend Range: `VCO.BendRng`
			- Descriptor 24. The stored choice is the bend in semitones, up and down: 0, 1, 2, 5, 7, 12 and 24 bent C4 by that many semitones at full pitch bend either way.
		- ### Glide Mode: `Kbd.GlMode`
			- Descriptor 2. 0 is Time, 1 is Sync, 2 is Rate.
		- ### Glide: `Kbd.Glide`
			- A continuous 0–1 value for the Glide knob ([[Microfreak/UG/10 Keyboard/03 Glide]]). 0 is no glide. The pitch slides in a straight line in semitones, and every new note glides from wherever the previous glide had reached, whether or not the notes overlap.
			- Time mode: a glide of any size takes 10 seconds × Glide³. 0.3 took 0.27 s, 0.5 took 1.25 s and 0.7 took 3.44 s, for an octave and for two octaves alike.
			- Rate mode: an octave takes 10 seconds × Glide³, so two octaves take twice as long.
			- Sync mode: the glide lasts a note division at the Arp/Seq tempo. floor(10 × Glide) picks the division: 3 is 1/32, 4 is 1/16T, 5 is 1/16, 6 is 1/8T, 7 is 1/8, 8 is 1/4T, 9 is 1/4 and 10 (Glide 1.0) is 1/2. Below 0.3 the glide was too short to measure at 30 BPM. The guide lists divisions up to 1/1; the longest measured was 1/2.
		- ### Unison Count: `Gen.UniCnt`
			- Descriptor 2. 0, 1 and 2 give 2, 3 and 4 voices.
		- ### Unison Spread: `Gen.UniSprd`
			- A continuous 0–1 value. The spread S, in semitones, is 2 × the stored value up to 0.5, then 1 + 22 × (stored − 0.5), reaching 12 at 1.0: the guide's 0.001–12.000, where 12 is an octave ([[Microfreak/UG/16 Paraphonic Chord Mode/01 Unison]]).
			- Two voices sound at −S and +S around the played note, three at −S, 0 and +S, and four at −2S, −S, +S and +2S.
	- ## Paraphonic modes
		- ### Paraphonic: `Gen.Parafon`
			- Descriptor 1. 0 is monophonic: a held four-note chord sounded only its last note. 1 sounds up to four notes at once.
		- ### Mode: `Gen.Chord`
			- Descriptor 3, read with paraphonic on. 0 is ordinary paraphonic play. 2 is Paraphonic Chord Mode ([[Microfreak/UG/16 Paraphonic Chord Mode]]): one key plays the stored chord. 3 is Unison. 1 played like 0, both with one key and with a held chord, and its meaning is unknown.
		- ### Chord offsets: `Gen.ChOffs1`–`Gen.ChOffs3`
			- The stored chord, as three voices added above or below the played note. Each stores 0x4000 + 128 × semitones: +4 is 0x4200 and −5 is 0x3D80. 0x7F80 leaves that voice out. Offsets of −5, +3, +4, +7, +10 and +12 sounded where expected.
		- ### `Gen.PolyCnt`
			- Descriptor 1. Saved presets hold 32765 or 0. Neither value changed a single note or a held chord, and its meaning is unknown.
	- ## Modulations
		- These are the Modulations settings of Utility > Preset. Each is a choice field, and the stored choices follow the guide's order.
		- ### Envelope Legato: `EG2.Legato`
			- 0 is Off: a note that starts while another is held restarts the main envelope. 1 is On: an overlapping note keeps the envelope running, and a note after a gap still restarts it.
		- ### Envelope Snap: `EG2.Snap`
			- 0 is Off and 1 (stored 32767) is On. On drops the level about 2 dB faster in the first 50–100 ms after the peak, then the decay runs parallel to Off, 2–2.5 dB lower. Every saved preset read holds the value 1, which is choice 0, Off.
		- ### LFO Retrig: `LFO.Retrig`
			- 0 is Off: the LFO runs freely and each note starts wherever it is. 1 is On: every note restarts the LFO, overlapping notes included. 2 is Legato: a note after a gap restarts the LFO and an overlapping note does not.
		- ### Press Mode: `Kbd.Veloc`
			- What drives the matrix's PRESSURE source. 0 is Aftertouch: channel pressure changes it and velocity does not. 1 is Velocity: each note's velocity sets it and channel pressure does not.
		- ### Key/Arp Mode: `Mat.KyMdSrc`
			- What the matrix's KEY/ARP source sends. 0 is Linear: the played key, with C4 at zero, higher keys positive and lower keys negative. 1 is Random: a new random value for each note, whatever the key.
	- ## Scale
		- ### Scale: `Kbd.Scale`
			- Descriptor 8, in the guide's order ([[Microfreak/UG/15 Using Scales]]): 0 Global, 1 Off, 2 Major, 3 Minor, 4 Harmonic Minor, 5 Dorian, 6 Mixolydian, 7 Blues, 8 Pentatonic, which is the minor pentatonic C, E♭, F, G, B♭. Global follows the global scale setting, which played unquantized on my MicroFreak.
			- The scale changes the notes the MicroFreak sounds: notes arriving over MIDI and notes the sequencer plays. MIDI out still sends the stored notes, so `microfreak:verify-sequence` matches on MIDI while the audio hears the scale's notes.
			- A note in the scale plays as written. In the seven-note scales a note outside the scale plays as the scale's note with the same letter name, reading a black key as the white key below it: on D major, C plays C♯ and F plays F♯; on C minor, E plays E♭, A plays A♭ and B plays B♭; a C♯, D♯, F♯, G♯ or A♯ outside the scale plays the scale's C, D, F, G or A.
			- Blues and Pentatonic do not follow that rule. What each note from C to B played as, on root C:
				- Major: C C D D E F F G G A A B.
				- Minor: C C D E♭ E♭ F F G A♭ A♭ B♭ B♭.
				- Harmonic Minor: C C D E♭ E♭ F F G A♭ A♭ A♭ B.
				- Dorian: C C D E♭ E♭ F F G G A B♭ B♭.
				- Mixolydian: C C D D E F F G G A B♭ B♭.
				- Blues: C C C E♭ E♭ F F♯ F♯ F♯ G B♭ B♭.
				- Pentatonic: C C C E♭ E♭ F F G G G B♭ B♭.
				- Blues on D: C C D D D F F G G♯ G♯ G♯ A.
		- ### Root Note: `Kbd.Root`
			- Descriptor 11. 0 to 11 are C, C♯, D, D♯, E, F, F♯, G, G♯, A, A♯ and B. Major on D played C♯ C♯ D D E F♯ F♯ G G A A B for C to B, and Minor on A played the same as Major on C.
	- ## Modulation matrix
		- The matrix ([[Microfreak/UG/05 Connections/02 Matrix and Encoder/01 Sources and Destinations]]) stores one amount per crossing as `CoN.ROW`. The column number is the destination: `Co1` Pitch, `Co2` Wave, `Co3` Timbre, `Co4` Cutoff, and `Co5`–`Co7` Assign 1–3. The row is the source: `EG1` the Cycling Envelope, `EG2` the Envelope, `LFO`, `Xpr` Pressure and `Key` Key/Arp.
		- An amount is −1 to 1, stored as a signed value over 32767. `Co2` did what assigning `VCO.Param1` did, and `Co3` what assigning `VCO.Param2` did.
		- On Pitch, 1.0 is 48 semitones, four octaves, at the source's full value: 0.25 is an octave, 0.02 about one semitone, and a negative amount bends down. Full channel pressure counts as 127/128. Pressure into Pitch at 1.0 stopped rising near B6.
		- ### Assign destinations: `Mat.Assign1`–`Mat.Assign3`
			- Each stores the destination id of the parameter its Assign column modulates. The sequencer's modulation tracks use the same ids ([[Microfreak/Sequence/Storage]]).
			- A destination id has four hex digits: the first two name a section and the last two a field in it. Sections: 0x00 oscillator (`VCO`), 0x01 filter (`VCF`), 0x02 Cycling Envelope (`EG1`), 0x03 keyboard (`Kbd`), 0x04 arpeggiator (`Arp`), 0x05 `LFO`, 0x06 Envelope (`EG2`), 0x07 general (`Gen`), 0x09 matrix settings (`Mat`), 0x0A–0x10 the matrix columns `Co1`–`Co7`, 0x12 `Seq` and 0x13 `Voc`. [Freakout's live map](https://github.com/kmorrill/freakout/blob/main/src/minifreak_patch/microfreak_live_map.py) lists two or three addresses per field; the destination id is the one in the field's own section, such as 0x0101 for `VCF.Cutoff`. `lib/patch_fields.py` builds the full list.
			- Heard on the instrument: 0x0001 Wave, 0x0003 Timbre, 0x0005 Shape, 0x0101 Cutoff, 0x0102 Resonance, 0x0300 Glide, 0x0502 LFO Rate, 0x0602 Envelope Decay, 0x0603 Envelope Sustain, 0x0705 Unison Spread and 0x0A02, the LFO → Pitch amount. 0x0F0E, Freakout's other address for cutoff, did nothing.
			- An Assign column aimed at a matrix amount adds to it: with LFO → Pitch stored at 0 and Pressure → Assign 1 at 0.05 aimed at 0x0A02, full pressure gave the vibrato that an LFO → Pitch amount of 0.05 gives.
	- ## Preset Volume: `Gen.PrstVol`
		- Descriptor 24; the panel's −12 to +12 is the stored choice minus 12. Each step changes the level by 0.5 dB, except that 9 and 10 (−3 and −2) play at the same level. From 0 to 24 the level spans 11.5 dB: −5.5 dB to +6.0 dB around choice 12.
	- ## Arp/Seq rate: `Arp.Rate`
		- The free-running tempo of the arpeggiator and sequencer, and the tempo Sync glide follows. BPM = 30 + stored value ÷ 150, up to 240: 13500 is 120 BPM. The stored value is not a 0–1 fraction, so a write gives `raw:` (BPM − 30) × 150.
		- The arpeggiator only runs on its own tempo when the global Clock Source is Internal. On USB it waits for clock from the Mac.
