- # [42.20 Transport](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-keyboard-shortcuts/#transport)
	- Play from Start Marker/Stop — `Space`
	- Continue Play from Stop Point — `Shift+Space`
	- Stop Playback at End of Selection — `Option+Space` (Win `Ctrl+Space`)
	- Play Arrangement View Selection — `Space`
	- Move Insert Marker to Beginning — `Home` or `Function` left arrow key (Win `Home`)
	- Record — `F9`
	- Arm Recording in Arrangement View — `Shift+F9`
	- Record to Session View — `Cmd+Shift+F9` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+F9`)
	- Back to Arrangement — `F10`
	- Activate/Deactivate Track 1…8 — `F1`…`F8`
	- Toggle Metronome — `O`
