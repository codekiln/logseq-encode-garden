- # [The Gettysburg Powerpoint Presentation](https://www.norvig.com/Gettysburg/)
	- ## Summary
		- Peter Norvig imagines what Abraham Lincoln's Gettysburg Address might have looked like if it had been delivered as a corporate PowerPoint presentation.
		- The presentation turns Lincoln's speech into agenda items, bullet points, objectives, an organizational overview, a summary, and a gratuitous graph.
		- The satire shows how presentation software can preserve the speech's keywords while stripping away its flow, eloquence, and force.
	- ## Notes
		- Norvig made the presentation after sitting through too many meetings where visual aids obscured the point.
		- He used PowerPoint's Company Meeting (Online) template and its Autocontent Wizard, then amplified the template's poor design choices.
		- The related essay explains that the joke is about PowerPoint and presentation habits, rather than about the historical significance of Gettysburg.
