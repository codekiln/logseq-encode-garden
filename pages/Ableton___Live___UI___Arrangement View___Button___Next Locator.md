- # Next Locator button
	- Jumps playback to the next locator, or the Arrangement end after the last locator.
	- **Behavior** — trigger. The jump is quantized to the global launch quantization.
	- **Remote control** — MIDI or key mapping. [[Ableton/Manual/06/03 Launching the Arrangement with Locators]] keeps a suggested key for it.
	- ## Source
		- [6.4 Launching the Arrangement with Locators](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/arrangement-view/#launching-the-arrangement-with-locators)
