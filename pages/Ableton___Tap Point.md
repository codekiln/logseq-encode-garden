- # Tap Point
	- In [[Ableton]] Live, a **tap point** is the place in one track's signal path where another track picks up its audio. To *tap* a track is to take a copy of its signal from that point, leaving the source track's own sound unchanged.
	- Set it on the receiving track: **Audio From** names the source track, and **Input Channel** picks the tap point ([[Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/05 Internal Routings/01 Internal Routing Points]]):
		- **Pre FX** — the signal as it enters the track, before its devices.
		- **Post FX** — after the devices, before volume and pan.
		- **Post Mixer** — the track's final output, after volume and pan.
	- Instrument, Effect and Drum Rack chains have the same three tap points.
