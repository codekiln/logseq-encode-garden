- # [36.3.5 Step Sequencing Beats](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#step-sequencing-beats)
	- Tapping a drum pad selects it for step sequencing. Tap steps to place notes; the clip starts playing at the first tap. Steps are 16th notes by default; the Scene/Grid buttons change the step size.
	- The **Tempo** encoder changes tempo by 1 BPM per click, or 0.1 BPM with `Shift`.
	- A moving green step shows playback (red while recording). Tap a filled step to delete it; hold **Mute** and tap to deactivate it; hold **Solo** and tap a pad to solo that sound.
	- **Delete** clears the pattern; hold Delete and tap a pad to clear its notes, or its devices if it has no notes in the pattern.
	- Step colours:
		- grey — empty;
		- clip colour — note, brighter for higher velocity;
		- lighter clip colour — muted note;
		- unlit — unavailable; with triplet steps only the first six pads of each row are used.
	- Velocity and micro-timing are covered under step sequencing automation; loop length under Adjusting the Loop Length.
