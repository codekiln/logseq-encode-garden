- # Hans P. Kjorstad
	- ## About
		- Norwegian multi-instrumentalist based in Lillehammer who grew up playing folk music on the fiddle, then moved into jazz and experimental music.
		- His 2026 solo album [[Person/Hans P. Kjorstad/Music/Recording/Inn]] pairs fiddle with microtonal synth and drum sampler.
		- Has worked with Sam Gendel and Ernst Reijseger.
		- Source: [Phantom Limb, Inn](https://www.phantom-limb.co.uk/portfolio-1/hans-p.-kjorstad---inn)
