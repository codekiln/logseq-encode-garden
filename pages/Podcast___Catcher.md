- # Podcatcher
	- An app that subscribes to podcast RSS feeds, downloads new episodes and plays them, such as Apple Podcasts, Pocket Casts or Overcast.
	- A podcatcher shows an episode's title and show notes from the feed. For a [[Ghost in the Patch]] episode, the show notes are the episode page's opening description.
