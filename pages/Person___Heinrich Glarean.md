- # [Heinrich Glarean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_Glarean)
	- ## About
		- Swiss music theorist, poet and humanist ([[14/8/8]]–[[15/6/3]]).
		- His *Dodecachordon* ([[15/4/7]]) proposed twelve modes in place of eight, adding the Aeolian and Ionian modes, the equivalents of minor and major.
