- # Measuring MicroFreak patch settings
	- Each run wrote a variant of one base preset into a test slot, selected it, played notes, pitch bend or CCs over USB MIDI and recorded the MicroFreak through the [[Zoom H5/My Unit]] at 48 kHz. [[Microfreak/CLI/Probe a patch]] describes the task that does this, and [[Microfreak/Patch/Storage]] the conversions these runs support.
	- ## Base sound
		- Slot 359, [[Microfreak/Preset/359 Init]], saved on firmware 5 and holding every field, written to slot 501 with: Basic Waves, Morph 0, Symmetry 0.5, no sub; low-pass filter fully open without resonance; Amp Mod on, attack 0, sustain 1.0; paraphony, arpeggiator, sequencer and glide off; keyboard octave 0; all 35 matrix amounts 0.
		- For pitch counts the tone was darkened to Morph 1.0 with the cutoff at 0.3 or 0.45, which left the fundamental 15–30 dB above its harmonics, so each unison voice showed as one spectral peak.
		- Pitch is the median YIN estimate over frames of 4096 samples; levels are RMS dBFS over 1024 samples; peaks come from one Hann-windowed spectrum of 1.5–3.4 seconds.
	- ## [[2026-11-17 Tue]]
		- Preset Volume: C4 with `Gen.PrstVol` at every choice from 0 to 24. Choices 0, 12 and 24 measured −28.0, −22.6 and −16.5 dBFS; each choice above or below moved the level by 0.46–0.56 dB. Choices 9 and 10 both measured −23.55 dBFS, and so did stored values 12900, 13654 and 13800 between them.
		- Bend Range: C4 bent to full up for half a second, then full down, then back. With `VCO.BendRng` at 0, 1, 2, 5, 7, 12 and 24 the pitch read 60, then 60 + n, then 60 − n, to within 0.01 semitone.
		- Glide Mode: C3, then C4 overlapping it; C3, then C5; C3, then a separate C4. At Glide 0.3, mode 0 took 0.29 s for both intervals, mode 2 took 0.29 s for the octave and 0.56 s for two octaves, and mode 1 took 0.08–0.10 s. The separate note glided too.
		- Glide time: C3 held 4 seconds so any earlier glide had ended, then C4. A straight line fitted to the pitch between 15 % and 85 % of the octave gave octave times of 0.272, 0.641, 1.252, 2.162 and 3.438 s at Glide 0.3–0.7 in Time mode, and the same in Rate mode. 10 × Glide³ gives 0.270, 0.640, 1.250, 2.160 and 3.430.
		- Sync glide: with Glide 1.0, the octave took 4.00 s at `Arp.Rate` 0, 1.42 s at 0.25, 0.86 s at 0.5 and 0.50 s at 1.0, so the tempo comes from the Arp/Seq rate. At `Arp.Rate` 0, Glide from 0.30 to 1.00 in steps of 0.05 gave 0.25, 0.25, 0.34, 0.34, 0.50, 0.50, 0.67, 0.67, 1.00, 1.00, 1.34, 1.34, 2.00, 2.00 and 4.00 s. Below 0.3 the fit found too few frames.
		- Arp/Seq tempo: with the global Clock Source set to Internal for the run and then back to USB, the arpeggiator repeated C4 at 30.0, 84.5, 120.0, 139.5, 193.5 and 240.0 notes a minute at `Arp.Rate` 0, 0.25, 0.412, 0.5, 0.75 and 1.0, with `Arp.Div` at 1/4. 30 + raw ÷ 150 gives 30.0, 84.6, 120.0, 139.2, 193.8 and 248 (240 at the top of the range). `Arp.Sync` 0 and 1 played alike.
		- Unison: A2 with `Gen.Parafon` 1 and `Gen.Chord` 3. With two voices the spectrum showed peaks at ±0.19, ±1.01, ±2.09, ±3.23, ±4.28, ±5.42, ±6.5, ±7.62, ±8.67, ±9.8, ±10.9 and ±12.0 semitones at `Gen.UniSprd` 0.1, 0.5, 0.55, 0.6, 0.65, 0.7, 0.75, 0.8, 0.85, 0.9, 0.95 and 1.0. Three voices added a peak at the played note. Four voices on A3 at 0.05, 0.1, 0.25 and 0.5 sat at ±0.1/±0.2, ±0.2/±0.4, ±0.5/±1.0 and ±1.0/±2.0 semitones.
		- Chord mode: A2 alone with `Gen.Chord` 2 played A2, C♯3 and E3 with offsets +4, +7 and 0x7F80; A2, C3, E3 and G3 with +3, +7, +10; and E2, A2 and A3 with −5, +12. `Gen.Chord` 0 and 1 played A2 alone with the same offsets stored.
		- Paraphony: A2, C♯3, E3 and G3 held together. `Gen.Parafon` 0 sounded G3 alone; 1 sounded all four, with `Gen.Chord` 0 or 1 and with `Gen.PolyCnt` 0 or 1.
		- Envelope Legato: main envelope with attack 0, decay 0.45 and sustain 0, so each restart shows as a new peak. C4, then D4 starting 50 ms before C4 ended; later C4, a 0.1 s gap, then D4. With `EG2.Legato` 0 the overlapping D4 rose from −42.7 to −22.9 dBFS, the same peak as the separate D4. With 1 the overlapping D4 stayed at −43.5 dBFS while the separate D4 rose to −22.9.
		- Envelope Snap: the same envelope, C4 held 2.5 s. With `EG2.Snap` at choice 1 the level fell 6, 12, 20, 30 and 40 dB below its peak after 0.26, 0.55, 0.86, 1.22 and 1.59 s; at choice 0, at raw 0 and at raw 1 after 0.32, 0.61, 0.95, 1.29 and 1.65 s. Over the first 100 ms, choice 1 fell 3.9 dB and choice 0 1.6 dB.
		- LFO Retrig: sine LFO at rate 0.55 (about 4 Hz) to pitch at amount 0.02, about ±0.9 semitones. Five separate C4s started at uneven times, then C4 with D4 overlapping it. At 50–450 ms after each start, the pitch of the five C4s differed by up to 0.8 semitones (standard deviation) with `LFO.Retrig` 0 and by at most 0.03 with 1 and 2. The overlapping D4 followed the same curve as the separate notes with 1, and continued the C4's curve with 2.
		- Press Mode: PRESSURE to cutoff at 0.5 with the cutoff at 0.25. C3 at velocity 20, then 127, then a held C3 with channel pressure 0, 127, 0. With `Kbd.Veloc` 0 the spectral centroid read 421 and 424 Hz for the two velocities and rose from 423 to 1287 Hz under pressure 127. With 1 it read 326 and 1289 Hz for the velocities and stayed at 448 Hz under pressure.
		- Key/Arp Mode: KEY/ARP to pitch at 0.05. C4 five times, then C5 and C3. With `Mat.KyMdSrc` 0 every C4 sounded at C4, C5 0.23 semitones sharp and C3 0.22 flat. With 1 the five C4s landed −0.82, +2.31, −0.98, +0.91 and −0.69 semitones off.
