- # Measuring MicroFreak patch settings
	- Each run wrote a variant of one base preset into a test slot, selected it, played notes, pitch bend or CCs over USB MIDI and recorded the MicroFreak through the [[Zoom H5/My Unit]] at 48 kHz. [[Microfreak/CLI/Probe a patch]] describes the task that does this, and [[Microfreak/Patch/Storage]] the conversions these runs support.
	- ## Base sound
		- Slot 359, [[Microfreak/Preset/359 Init]], saved on firmware 5 and holding every field, written to slot 501 with: Basic Waves, Morph 0, Symmetry 0.5, no sub; low-pass filter fully open without resonance; Amp Mod on, attack 0, sustain 1.0; paraphony, arpeggiator, sequencer and glide off; keyboard octave 0; all 35 matrix amounts 0.
		- For pitch counts the tone was darkened to Morph 1.0 with the cutoff at 0.3 or 0.45, which left the fundamental 15–30 dB above its harmonics, so each unison voice showed as one spectral peak.
		- Pitch is the median YIN estimate over frames of 4096 samples; levels are RMS dBFS over 1024 samples; peaks come from one Hann-windowed spectrum of 1.5–3.4 seconds.
