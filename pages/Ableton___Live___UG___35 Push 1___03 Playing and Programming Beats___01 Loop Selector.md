- # [35.3.1 Loop Selector](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-1/#loop-selector)
	- The grid splits into three parts so you can play, step-sequence and set the clip length at once.
		- ![The Pad Grid When Working With Drums](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/PushDrumLayout.png)
	- The 16 drum pads use Drum Rack's 4x4 layout. Pad colors: bright yellow has a sound, dull yellow is empty, green is playing, light blue is selected, dark blue is soloed, orange is muted.
	- The touch strip or Octave Up and Down move through larger racks 16 pads at a time; add `Shift` to move by single rows.
	- Holding Note briefly shows the 16 Velocities layout; `Shift` + Note locks it, and Note again unlocks it.
