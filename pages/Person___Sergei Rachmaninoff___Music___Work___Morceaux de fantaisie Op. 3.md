- # Morceaux de fantaisie, Op. 3
	- Five piano pieces, dedicated to Anton Arensky, Rachmaninoff's harmony teacher at the Moscow Conservatory, and published in 1893.
	- ## Pieces
		- 01 - Elegie in E-flat Minor
		- 02 - [[Person/Sergei Rachmaninoff/Music/Work/Morceaux de fantaisie Op. 3/02 Prelude in C-sharp Minor]]
		- 03 - Melodie in E Major
		- 04 - Polichinelle in F-sharp Minor
		- 05 - Serenade in B-flat Minor
	- Source: [Morceaux de fantaisie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morceaux_de_fantaisie) on Wikipedia.
