- # [Zoox](https://zoox.com/)
	- Zoox builds a purpose-built, fully driverless robotaxi and runs it as a ride-hailing service. [[Amazon]] acquired Zoox in 2020, and it operates as an Amazon subsidiary based in Foster City, California.
	- The vehicle is a bidirectional, battery-electric pod with no steering wheel or pedals. Four passengers sit facing each other in carriage-style seating.
	- Zoox opened its robotaxi service to the public in Las Vegas in 2025 and is testing in San Francisco and other cities.
	- Comparable driverless ride-hailing efforts include Waymo and [[Uber]]'s autonomous partnerships.
