- # [Zoox](https://zoox.com/)
	- Zoox builds a purpose-built robotaxi driven entirely by AI, with no human driver, and runs it as a ride-hailing service. [[Amazon]] acquired Zoox in 2020, and it operates as an Amazon subsidiary based in Foster City, California.
	- The vehicle is a bidirectional, battery-electric pod with no steering wheel or pedals. Four passengers sit facing each other in carriage-style seating.
	- Zoox opened its robotaxi service to the public in Las Vegas in 2025, followed by a smaller public service in San Francisco. It is testing in other cities.
	- It operates at [[SAE/J3016/Level 4]]: fully driverless, but only inside mapped service areas.
	- Its main US competitor is [[Waymo]].
