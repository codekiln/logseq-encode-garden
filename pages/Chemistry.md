- # Chemistry
	- ## Overview
		- Chemistry studies matter: its composition, structure, properties, and transformations. It connects the behavior of atoms and molecules with the materials and reactions observed in everyday life and in nature.
	- ## Organizing ideas
		- The [[Periodic Table of Elements]] arranges elements to make recurring patterns in atomic structure and chemical behavior visible.
		- Chemical bonds and molecular structure help explain why substances have different properties and how reactions rearrange matter.
	- ## Fields
		- Major areas include organic, inorganic, physical, analytical, and biochemistry. Each emphasizes different materials, questions, or methods while sharing chemical principles.
