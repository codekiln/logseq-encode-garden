- # Octave Up button
	- Shifts the keyboard one octave up, as far as three octaves.
	- ![Octave Up button](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/Microfreak___UI___Front___Row___Bottom___01%20Octave___Button___Up___Asset___Panel.png)
	- ## Behavior
		- The buttons blink faster the farther the shift is from zero.
	- ## Values
		- Each press raises the shift by one, as far as +3, and the OLED shows the new value. [[Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/03 Bottom/01 Octave/Ref/Key Range]] lists the notes the keys send at each value.
