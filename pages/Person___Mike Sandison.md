- # [Mike Sandison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boards_of_Canada)
	- ## About
		- Electronic musician, born Michael Peter Sandison on 14 July 1971, who releases music with his younger brother [[Person/Marcus Eoin]] as [[Boards of Canada]], through [[Label/Warp Records]]. The brothers grew up in Cullen, Moray, on the northeast coast of Scotland, and spent their teenage years in Balerno, beside the Pentland Hills.
		- Studied English literature at the University of Edinburgh.
		- Sole producer of [[Boards of Canada/Music/Recording/Inferno]] (2026), and credited as writer on all of its tracks.
