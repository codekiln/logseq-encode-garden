- # [15.1 Loading a Tuning System](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-tuning-systems/#loading-a-tuning-system)
	- Double-click a tuning file in the browser, or select it and press `Enter`, to load it. This opens the browser's Tuning section, which is hidden by default and can also be shown from the browser's view control menu.
		- ![The Tuning section in the browser](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/TuningSectionBrowser.png?auto=&w=320)
	- .scl and .ascl files can also be dragged into the Tuning section from the computer. A loaded tuning is saved with the Live Set.
	- With a tuning loaded, the piano roll shows the tuning's notes instead of standard MIDI notes. Hovering over a note shows its pitch and frequency in the Status Bar.
		- ![Updated notes in the MIDI Note Editor](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/RetunedNotesMIDINoteEditor.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Changing or removing a tuning leaves notes where they are, so they may now play different pitches. With **Retune Set On Loading Tuning Systems** on in the Options menu, Live moves notes to the closest pitches in the new tuning after a confirmation dialog.
		- Retuning can delete or shorten a note when two overlapping notes map to the same new pitch.
	- While a tuning is loaded, the Scale Mode choosers disappear and scale-aware devices have Use Current Scale turned off.
