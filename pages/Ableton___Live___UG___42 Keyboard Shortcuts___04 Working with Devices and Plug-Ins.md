- # [42.4 Working with Devices and Plug-Ins](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-keyboard-shortcuts/#working-with-devices-and-plug-ins)
	- Group Devices — `Cmd+G` (Win `Ctrl+G`)
	- Ungroup Devices — `Cmd+Shift+G` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+G`)
	- Activate/Deactivate All Devices in Group — Hold `Option` and click on device activator (Win Hold `Alt` and click on device activator)
	- Add Device(s) to Selection — `Shift` click
	- See List of Devices on a Track — right-click on Device View selector
	- Reset Parameter Value — `Delete` or double-click
	- Load Selected Device from Browser — `Enter`
	- Hot-Swap Selected Device — `Q`
	- Hide/Show Open Plug-In Windows — `Cmd+Option+P` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+P`)
	- Open Multiple Windows with Show/Hide Plug-In Window Button — `Cmd` (Win `Ctrl`)
	- Compare A/B: Switch Device State — `P`
