- # Four Lanterns
	- A kick, a shiny hat, a reshaped techno stab and a road of bass. Each one gets its own colored track on the Launchpad's sequencer, is caught as a loop, and keeps glowing while the next sound is summoned. Then the hat comes back on the first track and the lights dim together.
	- ![Episode recording](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/GitP___A___Session___26___11___22-Sun___Asset___Audio___Episode.mp3)
	- ## Ceremonial Annotations
		- The four tracks of the [[Launchpad/Sequencer]] glow orange, violet, green and salmon, and each one gets its own MicroFreak preset. One at a time, a pattern goes into the sequencer, the sound is recorded as audio, and its loop keeps going while the instrument turns to the next voice.
		- Listen for the third voice changing shape while it records, for the room opening as reverb rises and the loops pan apart, and for the hat returning on the first track in the second scene.
	- ## Chapters
		- ### 00:00 enter [[Microfreak/Preset/296 Kick It]]
			- The kick is already looping on the first track when the recording begins.
		- ### 00:28 enter [[Microfreak/Preset/297 Shiny Hat]]
			- 00:43 - the hat pattern goes into the second sequencer track from the MicroFreak's keys.
				- Notes from [[GitP/Acolyte]]
					- The MicroFreak's own MIDI output shows the keys played into the sequencer, and no knob moves while this take records. The [[Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/05 Keys]] page describes the keyboard.
			- 02:00 - the take becomes a loop.
		- ### 02:20 enter [[Microfreak/Preset/295 Tactile Techno]]
			- 02:39 - two notes go into the third sequencer track.
			- #### 02:51 the stab is reshaped as it records
				- Notes from [[GitP/Acolyte]]
					- The session MIDI records the main envelope's [[Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/03 Bottom/05 Envelope/Knob/Attack]] and [[Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/03 Bottom/05 Envelope/Knob/Decay Rel]] first. By 03:21 the [[Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/02 Main/04 Cycling Envelope]] has had its Amount, Fall, Rise and Hold changed.
					- From 03:33 to the end of the take, the oscillator's Wave, Timbre and Shape knobs move. On SawX they set [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type/SAWX/Saw Mod]], [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type/SAWX/Shape]] and [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type/SAWX/Noise]]. The [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type]] stays on SawX.
			- 04:08 - the take becomes a loop.
		- ### 05:28 enter [[Microfreak/Preset/293 Digital Roads]]
			- #### 05:39 the road is shaped
				- Notes from [[GitP/Acolyte]]
					- The session MIDI records the Cycling Envelope's Rise and Fall, Attack, Decay/Release and [[Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/03 Bottom/05 Envelope/Knob/Filter Amt]]. Then Wave, Timbre and Shape move by 06:00; on Bass they set [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type/BASS/Saturate]], [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type/BASS/Fold]] and [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type/BASS/Noise]].
			- #### 06:45 the room opens
				- Reverb rises on the first two tracks. At 06:53 the four tracks pan apart, the first and third to the right and the second and fourth to the left.
			- 07:04 - the fourth track's pattern goes in from the MicroFreak's keys, a few notes at a time, until 08:16.
			- #### 07:47 the stab fades away
				- The third track fades out.
			- 09:02 - the take becomes a loop. At 09:33 delay and reverb open on it.
			- #### 11:36 the tracks trade places
				- The fourth track drops back as the third returns. The third sweeps from left to right at 12:00, picks up reverb at 12:11 and fades out at 12:25. The second track swells at 11:43 and settles at 12:32 as the first comes up.
		- ### 12:55 enter [[Microfreak/Preset/297 Shiny Hat]]
			- The first sequencer track starts a new pattern for the second scene. At 13:34 the hat's new take replaces the kick on the first track. The kick returns at 14:02, and at 14:40 the hat's loop takes over.
			- #### 14:12 the lights dim
				- The mix fades out and is silent by 15:47.
				- Notes from [[GitP/Acolyte]]
					- On the way down, the session MIDI records [[Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/03 Bottom/05 Envelope/Knob/Attack]] at 14:48, then a sweep of [[Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/02 Main/03 Analog Filter/Knob/Cutoff]] and [[Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/02 Main/03 Analog Filter/Knob/Resonance]] at 15:22.
