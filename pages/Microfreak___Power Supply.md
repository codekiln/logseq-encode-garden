- # MicroFreak power supply
	- The Arturia power supply that ships with the [[Microfreak]]. It turns mains power into 12 V DC for [[Microfreak/UI/Rear/08 DC In Jack]] and grounds the touch keyboard.
	- ## Input
		- Mains power through a grounded three-pin plug.
	- ## Output
		- 12 V DC at 1 A, on a center-positive barrel plug: 5.5 mm outside diameter, 2.1 mm inside diameter, 10 mm long.
	- ## Works with
		- [[Microfreak/UI/Rear/08 DC In Jack]]. With this supply plugged in, the MicroFreak runs on it instead of USB power.
		- The touch keyboard needs a ground to work fully. USB power or a power bank will run the MicroFreak, and Arturia recommends this supply for the ground.
		- Arturia supports only its own supply and takes no responsibility for damage from another.
