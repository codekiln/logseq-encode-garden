- # [Fired OpenAI Safety Researchers Speak Out + We Look Inside Pandora’s Box](https://share.snipd.com/episode/611797e1-1484-462f-aa8b-c7d3d87b46e8)
	- Podcast: [[Machine Gods/Pod]]
	- Hosts: [[Person/Kevin Roose]], [[Person/Casey Newton]]
	- Summary: Two fired OpenAI safety researchers on a leak investigation and why AI audits need independent investigators, plus a look at how OpenAI runs.
	- ## Readwise highlights
		- ### [When AI Safety Preparation Became Reality](https://share.snipd.com/snip/ed224e28-1759-4a0a-b697-8906f235b9d4)
			- Tomek describes the Hugging Face incident as the most intense period of his career, when AI safety preparation suddenly became real.
			- He realized earlier work had been a drill and decisions now carried immediate consequences.
		- ### [Why AI Audits Need Embedded Investigators](https://share.snipd.com/snip/d4a9674a-9e2a-4ea1-a748-cdbd34a66c05)
			- Short, contractor-style audits struggle because investigators cannot know every relevant question beforehand.
			- Tomek Korbak recommends continuously embedded auditors with unrestricted access, like employees rather than temporary consultants.
		- ### [Mikita Balesni Pushed Monitorability Standards](https://share.snipd.com/snip/6d43b268-2e67-46e9-8eb3-ca2fc05686e9)
			- Mikita Balesni worked with Jasmine Wang on cross-industry standards for monitoring whether AI systems remain understandable.
			- He shared internal memos with OpenAI leaders and external experts while saying he disclosed no confidential intellectual property.
		- ### [OpenAI Investigated Researchers Over an Alleged Leak](https://share.snipd.com/snip/e887ddf3-8134-4e14-a13d-6b39887b7e7f)
			- OpenAI investigated Mikita Balesni and Tomek Korbak over a suspected leak about less-monitorable AI architectures.
			- They denied leaking information and surrendered Signal messages from their personal phones to investigators.
		- ### [OpenAI Researchers Faced A Leak Investigation](https://share.snipd.com/snip/142e3ed0-f6d3-4ac2-a1dd-c74aaa74994b)
			- OpenAI investigated whether the researchers leaked information about less-monitorable architectures to journalists.
			- Investigators asked them to show personal Signal messages, yet they were fired without a chance to explain the context.
		- ### [OpenAI Presumed Guilt Without Proof](https://share.snipd.com/snip/67a2a62b-546b-43bb-a22a-36e6797c0809)
			- The researchers considered Signal and disappearing messages standard security practices for sensitive work.
			- Tomek Korbak said OpenAI effectively presumed guilt when employees could not prove their innocence.
		- ### [Give AI Auditors Real Independence](https://share.snipd.com/snip/f64e26c4-d4df-4088-8115-57692fd6b094)
			- Require independent AI auditors to have broad access, confidential employee channels, and authority beyond voluntary contractor arrangements.
			- Tomek Korbak points to regulated industries where auditors can inspect everything and speak privately with staff.
		- ### [OpenAI Insiders Fear Speaking To Third Parties](https://share.snipd.com/snip/28abf0ec-5e98-46d8-8292-a7d26e7c36ff)
			- OpenAI employees reportedly fear speaking to journalists and third parties after researchers were asked to show personal phones.
			- Rumors suggest OpenAI may cut off Meter and discredit the independent safety organization.
		- ### [Profit Incentives Conflict With AI Safety](https://share.snipd.com/snip/26564cf1-d0cc-41d5-98f4-63f4ca1871bd)
			- Frontier labs may lack the incentives to prioritize safety because private-company leadership must maximize revenue for stakeholders.
			- That financial pressure can conflict directly with minimizing risks from increasingly capable systems.
		- ### [OpenAI Runs Like A SaaS Company](https://share.snipd.com/snip/eb840b3c-d2d8-4bd4-983d-ca9caceddc82)
			- Mikita Balesni sees OpenAI operating more like a SaaS company than an organization building a potentially superhuman system.
			- Fast product innovation and the rigor required for safe AGI development create a deep cultural tension.
		- ### [Regulation Can Reduce AI Risk](https://share.snipd.com/snip/b5abd8d6-a0cb-4e09-a242-95a1b1a5bbc9)
			- The researchers reject hopelessness and argue that enforceable checks and balances can substantially reduce AI risks.
			- They compare AI governance with aviation and nuclear regulation, where oversight coexists with continued innovation.
		- ### [Protect Monitorability And Internal Dissent](https://share.snipd.com/snip/12637fb6-38ff-4ed9-8a0a-c6456ef052bc)
			- OpenAI's former researchers urged the company to maintain embedded audits, preserve monitorability, and restore its open internal culture.
			- They argued employees must be able to raise safety concerns and collaborate with outside experts.
