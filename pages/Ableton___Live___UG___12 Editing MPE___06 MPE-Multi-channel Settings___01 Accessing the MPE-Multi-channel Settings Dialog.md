- # [12.6.1 Accessing the MPE/Multi-channel Settings Dialog](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/editing-mpe/#accessing-the-mpemulti-channel-settings-dialog)
	- **Ext. Instrument:** pick a target in MIDI To, choose MPE in the second menu, then open that menu again and choose MPE Settings….
		- ![MPE Settings in Ext. Instrument](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/ExtInstMPESettings.png?auto=&w=299)
	- **Mixer I/O section:** with the target device chosen in the track's MIDI To, choose MPE in the second menu, then reopen it and choose MPE Settings…, in either the Session or Arrangement mixer.
		- ![MPE Settings in the I/O section](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MPESettingsSessionMixer.png?auto=&w=320)
	- **Plug-ins:** right-click the device's title bar.
		- ![MPE Settings in a plug-in's context menu](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/PluginMPESettings.png?auto=&w=320)
