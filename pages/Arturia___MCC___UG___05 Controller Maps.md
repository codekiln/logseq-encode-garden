- # 5. Controller Maps
	- A Controller Map assigns MIDI messages to configurable knobs, pads, buttons, and pedals. Depending on the product, assignments may include CC, RPN/NRPN, MMC, bank/program changes, and channel aftertouch.
	- Controller Map support and the allowed message types vary by device.
