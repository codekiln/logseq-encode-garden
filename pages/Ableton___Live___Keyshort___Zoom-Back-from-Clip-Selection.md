- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Step back out after zooming in on a clip selection** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `X`
		- Description: In the Sample Editor or the MIDI Note Editor, undoes one Zoom to Clip Selection per press. With the Computer MIDI Keyboard on, the key raises its octave instead.
