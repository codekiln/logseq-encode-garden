- # [Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/)
	- An independent, community-contributed archive documenting real-world typography — the fonts used in branding, posters, books, film titles, and other design work — indexed by typeface, format, industry, and designer.
	- ## Why it is notable
		- A reference for seeing how specific typefaces perform in the wild: each entry credits the fonts, foundries, and designers behind a piece of design, which makes it useful for type selection and study.
	- ## Example use
		- [A Piedi Nudi Nel Parco](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/78761/a-piedi-nudi-nel-parco) — visual identity for the Cremona urban-theatre festival (April 2026) by Paolo Mazzini, pairing Ouverture (Zetafonts) with Polymath (OHno Type Co.).
