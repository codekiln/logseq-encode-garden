- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Send the insert marker to the end of the clip** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `End` or `Fn+Right` (Win `End`)
		- Description: In the MIDI Note Editor, moves the insert marker to the clip's end; `Home` goes back to its start. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/10 Editing MIDI/05 Editing MIDI Notes/02 Selecting Notes and Timespan]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/12 Clip View MIDI Note Editor]].
