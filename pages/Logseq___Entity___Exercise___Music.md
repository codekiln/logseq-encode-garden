- # Music Exercise
	- In this garden, **Music Exercise** pages model exercises that practice a specific musical technique or listening skill.
	- Extends [[Logseq/Entity/Exercise]] and inherits its page shape.
	- Setup identifies the instrument or listening context, musical material, and relevant key, meter, or pulse. Practice specifies the passage or pattern and how it repeats.
	- Cues describe audible or physical goals such as even timing, articulation, tone, or coordination. Tempo and repetition count belong under dose and progression.
	- Instances use the most specific music exercise namespace, such as `Exercise/Music/Piano/<Name>`.
