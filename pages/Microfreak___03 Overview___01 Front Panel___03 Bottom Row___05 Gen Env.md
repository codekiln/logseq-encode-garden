- # 03.01.03.05 The General Envelope Generator
	- The Envelope Generator shapes a tone’s loudness or timbre and can be patched to destinations in the Matrix. **Attack**, **Decay/Release**, and **Sustain** affect the filter by default; **Filter Amt** sets the envelope’s effect on the filter. [[Microfreak/09 Envelope Gen]] explains the controls in detail.
	- The Envelope Generator
		- ![01 The Envelope Generator](../assets/Microfreak___03-Overview___01-Front-Panel___03-Bottom-Row___05-Gen-Env___01-The-Envelope-Generator.png)
	- With **Amp Mod** active, the envelope also shapes VCA loudness.
