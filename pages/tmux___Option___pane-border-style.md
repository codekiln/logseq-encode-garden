- ### [`pane-border-style`](https://man.openbsd.org/tmux#pane-border-style)
	- Sets the foreground and background style of borders around panes other than the [[tmux/Pane/Active]]. Text attributes are ignored.
	- Example: `tmux set-option -g pane-border-style fg=colour238`
