- # Clear button
	- In Session mode, hold Clear and press a pad to delete its clip.
	- In Note mode, press Clear to delete the selected clip.
	- In Sequencer mode, hold Clear and press a step, Pattern, Scene or Micro Step to erase it. A cleared Scene goes back to Pattern 1 on every track.
	- ## Remote control
		- Programmer mode: CC 60
