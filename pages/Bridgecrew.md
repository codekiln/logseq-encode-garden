- # [Bridgecrew](https://github.com/bridgecrewio)
	- Cloud-security company that created [[Checkov]], the infrastructure-as-code static-analysis scanner.
	- Acquired by Palo Alto Networks in 2021 and folded into [Prisma Cloud](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/prisma/cloud); the `bridgecrewio` GitHub org still hosts [[Checkov]] and related open-source tooling.
