- # 2.5 Using Standalone
	- Power Launchpad Pro from the included wall adapter with the USB-A to USB-C cable.
	- Connect external hardware with the included TRS minijack-to-DIN adapters and MIDI cables.
	- Sequencer, Custom, Note, Chord, and Programmer Modes can send MIDI through the MIDI Out ports.
