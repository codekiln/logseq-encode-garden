- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Reach an audio clip's fade handles while Automation Mode shows envelopes** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: hold `F`
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, holding the key over an automation lane shows the clip's fade handles until you let go. The breakpoint envelope commands list it as Momentarily Toggle Fade Controls. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/08 Audio Clip Fades and Crossfades]], [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/07 Commands for Breakpoint Envelopes]].
