- # Record Quantise
	- Hold Shift and press Quantise to turn Record Quantise on or off. When it is on, newly recorded MIDI snaps to the record quantisation grid set in Live under Edit → Record Quantisation.
	- While Shift is held, Quantise glows green when Record Quantise is on and red when it is off.
