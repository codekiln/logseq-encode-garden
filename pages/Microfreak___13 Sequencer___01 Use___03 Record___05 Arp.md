- # 13.1.3.5. Using the Arpeggiator to Create Sequences
	- Create an arpeggio and transfer it to a sequence to retain its Spice & Dice variations. Standard sequence recording disables Spice & Dice, so transferring an arpeggio is the way to include those effects in a sequence. See [[Microfreak/12 Arpeggiator]] for the transfer procedure.
