- # [42.17 Comping](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-keyboard-shortcuts/#comping)
	- Show Take Lanes — `Cmd+Option+U` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+U`)
	- Add Selected Take Lane Area to Main Track Lane — `Enter`
	- Audition Selected Take Lane — `T`
	- Add Take Lane — `Shift+Option+T` (Win `Shift+Alt+T`)
	- Duplicate Selected Take Lane — `Cmd+D` (Win `Ctrl+D`)
	- Replace Main Take Clip with Next/Previous Take — `Cmd` up and down arrow keys (Win `Ctrl` up and down arrow keys)
