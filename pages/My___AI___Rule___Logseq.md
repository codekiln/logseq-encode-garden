- # Aliases
	- Add an `alias::` to a page only when I ask for that alias.
	- Every alias becomes another entry in Logseq's link autocomplete. Aliases an agent adds on its own pile up quickly and crowd out the pages I'm actually trying to link.
	- When an alias seems useful, suggest it in chat and I'll decide.
