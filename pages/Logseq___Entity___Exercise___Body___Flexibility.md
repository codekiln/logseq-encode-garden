- # Flexibility Exercise
	- In this garden, **Flexibility Exercise** pages model body exercises that practice comfortable range of motion through a stretch, position, or controlled transition.
	- Extends [[Logseq/Entity/Exercise/Body]] and inherits its page shape.
	- Practice identifies the intended area of stretch or mobility, entry and exit from the position, and a comfortable range. Cues describe relaxed breathing and gradual movement rather than forcing an endpoint.
	- Dose and progression describes holds or cycles and ways to add range or reduce support over time.
	- Instances use `Exercise/Body/Flexibility/<Name>`.
