- # [OpenUsage](https://openusage.sh)
	- Repository: [janekbaraniewski/openusage](https://github.com/janekbaraniewski/openusage) — 226 [[GitHub/Star]]s, checked [[2026/10/16 Fri]].
	- A local terminal dashboard that combines live quotas, resets, usage, and spend for coding agents and model providers.
	- Supports [[Claude/Code]] and [[Codex/CLI]], among many providers; includes daily, weekly, monthly, and session reports plus optional background collection.
	- Written in [[Go]].
