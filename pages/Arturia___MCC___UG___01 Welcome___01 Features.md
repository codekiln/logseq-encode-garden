- # 1.1. Features of the MIDI Control Center
	- Depending on the device, MCC can back up memories; configure response curves, sync, CV/Gate, drum maps, and user scales; and import or export settings.
	- Assignable performance controls may transmit MIDI CC, RPN/NRPN, aftertouch, bank/program changes, or transport commands.
	- Supported sequencer and pattern parameters can include length, notes, gate, swing, scale, and step size. Some products also provide keyboard zones and split points.
	- These are examples across Arturia products; an individual device may expose only some of them.
