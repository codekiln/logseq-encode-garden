- # Aletheia
	- Aletheia is the Greek word Heidegger uses for truth understood as unconcealment: something comes out of hiddenness and becomes manifest.
	- [[Person/Martin Heidegger]] develops this sense of truth in [[Person/Martin Heidegger/Essay/35/The Origin of the Work of Art]], where a work of art can open a world and let beings appear.
	- In [[GitP/Light]], aletheia names the character of a listening moment in which a sound reveals something about a [[Microfreak/Preset]].
	- Related terms: [[unconcealment]] describes the appearing; [[insight]] names the recognition that can come with it.
