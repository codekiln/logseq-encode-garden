- # Content (Harmo)
	- Set by the [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Wave]] while the oscillator model is [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type/Harmo]].
	- The Wave knob morphs through different tables of harmonic amplitudes and switches between them. Higher values provide tones with richer harmonic content.
