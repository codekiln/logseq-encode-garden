- ### 1. What [[Keyshort]] scrolls forward a full page, and 2. what [[Keyshort]] scrolls backward a full page? [[card]]
	- 1. `<C-f>` — **f**orward one screen; accepts a count (e.g. `5<C-f>` for five pages)
	- 2. `<C-b>` — **b**ackward one screen; also count-prefixable unlike half-page scroll in the book's emphasis
