- # 5 Snoozenet — I Post, Therefore I Am
	- Summary:: Follows Usenet's growth from informal netnews to sprawling newsgroups, flame wars, difficult tooling, and a culture of posting by default.
	- Why it is included:: Extends the critique from Unix software to the social and network systems built on it, where scale magnifies disorder instead of solving it.
