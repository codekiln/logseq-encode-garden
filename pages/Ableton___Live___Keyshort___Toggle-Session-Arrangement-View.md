	- **Switch between the Session View and the Arrangement View** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Tab`
		- Description: Does what the Control Bar's [[Ableton/Live/UI/Control-Bar/Toggle/View-Selector]] does; holding the key for about half a second switches back when you let go.
