- # [ChatGPT Pro](https://chatgpt.com/pricing)
	- The top individual tier of [[ChatGPT]], sold at three prices as of [[2026-11-21 Sat]], per [ChatGPT pricing docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing) and the [release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes):
		- **Pro $100** — added 2026-04-09.
		- **Pro $200** — reopened to new subscribers 2026-09-29.
		- **Pro $500** — added 2026-09-29; the only tier with Ultrafast mode.
	- Includes Pro reasoning models, longer [[ChatGPT/Work]] and [[Codex]] sessions with no five-hour limit, the most deep research, and monthly API credits matching the price.
	- Pro is the only consumer tier with dots, OpenAI's always-on agent ("Dots are currently available to Pro users", release notes, 2026-11-19).
	- [[ChatGPT/Wallet]], which lets a dot pay merchants, is rolling out to US Pro subscribers from 2026-11-13.
