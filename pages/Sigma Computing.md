- # [Sigma Computing](https://www.sigmacomputing.com/)
	- Hosted [[BI]] product, usually just called Sigma, that queries a cloud data warehouse such as [[Snowflake]] directly and presents the results in a spreadsheet-like interface.
	- Made by the company of the same name, founded in 2014 in San Francisco.
	- Often compared with [[Tableau]] as a newer alternative for dashboards.
