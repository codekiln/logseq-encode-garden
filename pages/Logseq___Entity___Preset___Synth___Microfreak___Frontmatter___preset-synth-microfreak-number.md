- # Preset Slot
	- Owning type: [[Logseq/Entity/Preset/Synth/Microfreak]].
	- Integer from 1 to 512: the last observed device slot. A moved sound can retain its page title while this value changes.
	- The slot locates a saved sound; a slot alone cannot establish the identity of its contents.
