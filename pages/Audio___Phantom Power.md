- # Phantom Power
	- DC power a mixer or recorder sends up a mic cable to power a condenser mic. The cable carries the audio and the power together, on the same pins of an [[Audio/Connector/XLR]].
	- The standard is +48 V, labelled P48. The voltage sits equally on pins 2 and 3, so the [[Audio/Balanced]] input that reads the difference between them doesn't hear it. Some gear also offers +12 V and +24 V, as the [[Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/03 Phantom Power]] does.
	- Dynamic mics and balanced line sources ignore it. Turn it off before plugging in a ribbon mic or an unbalanced source, or when patching with the channel up, to avoid damage and loud pops.
	- Mixers often switch it for a group of inputs at once, as on the [[Behringer/Mixer/XENYX 802S]].
