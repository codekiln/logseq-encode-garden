- # 07.01.02 Band Pass Filter
	- A Band Pass Filter lets only a narrow frequency range pass. At minimum resonance, all frequencies pass; turning Resonance clockwise narrows the range. At its maximum, the filter self-oscillates and can act as a sine-wave oscillator, while blocking the Digital Oscillator's sound.
