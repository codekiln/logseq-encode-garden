- # Atomic Design
	- A methodology from [[Person/Brad Frost]] for building interface design systems as a hierarchy of five stages[^1].
	- > Atomic design is a methodology composed of five distinct stages working together to create interface design systems in a more deliberate and hierarchical manner.[^1]
	- ## The five stages
		- [[UX/Design/Atomic/L1 - Atom]]
		- [[UX/Design/Atomic/L2 - Molecule]]
		- [[UX/Design/Atomic/L3 - Organism]]
		- [[UX/Design/Atomic/L4 - Template]]
		- [[UX/Design/Atomic/L5 - Page]]
	- ## A mental model, not a sequence
		- > Atomic design is not a linear process, but rather a mental model to help us think of our user interfaces as both a cohesive whole and a collection of parts at the same time.[^1]
	- ## Used in this garden
		- [[Logseq/Entity/UI/Area]] and [[Logseq/Entity/UI/Control]] map the parts of hardware and software interfaces into pages.
