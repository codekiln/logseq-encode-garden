- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Step back out after zooming in on the Arrangement time selection** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `X`
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, returns to the zoom level before the last Zoom to Arrangement Time Selection; after zooming in several times, each press goes back one step. With the Computer MIDI Keyboard on, the same key raises its octave instead. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/02 Navigation and Zooming]], [[Ableton/Menu/View/Zoom/Arrangement Time Selection/To]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
