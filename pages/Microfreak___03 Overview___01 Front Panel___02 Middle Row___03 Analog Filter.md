- # 03.01.02.03 Analog filter
	- The Analog Filter emphasizes or suppresses harmonics in the Digital Oscillator sound, changing its timbre. **Cutoff** sets where filtering begins; **Resonance** emphasizes frequencies near that point. [[Microfreak/07 Filter]] explains the filter in detail.
	- The Analog Filter
		- ![01 The Analog Filter](../assets/Microfreak___03-Overview___01-Front-Panel___02-Middle-Row___03-Analog-Filter___01-The-Analog-Filter.png)
	- The three filter types
		- Low-pass (LPF) attenuates frequencies above cutoff.
		- Band-pass (BPF) attenuates frequencies above and below cutoff.
		- High-pass (HPF) attenuates frequencies below cutoff.
