- # 19.2.3. The Vocoder Oscillator
	- A carrier rich in overtones gives the vocoder more material to shape. Use the **Wave** knob to move through sawtooth, pulse width, and noise waves; see also [[Microfreak/06 Dig Osc/03 Types/22 Vocoder]].
	- The Digital Oscillator
		- ![01 The Digital Oscillator](../assets/Microfreak___19-Appendix-B-Vocoder___02-How-It-Works___03-Vocoder-Osc___01-Digital-Oscillator.png)
	- Wave positions
		- 0%: sawtooth.
		- Around 11%: pulse wave at 50% duty cycle.
		- From 11% to 90%: increasing pulse width, reaching 97% duty cycle.
		- 91–100%: noise.
	- > [[Note/Info]] The manual gives different duty cycles at Wave 90%: 97% here and 99% in [[Microfreak/06 Dig Osc/03 Types/22 Vocoder]].
	- Sawtooth, pulse-width, and noise waveforms
		- ![02 Vocoder waveforms](../assets/Microfreak___19-Appendix-B-Vocoder___02-How-It-Works___03-Vocoder-Osc___02-Waveforms.png)
	- **Timbre** and **Shape** also control the Vocoder Oscillator.
	- > [[Note/Info]] Oscillator Type cannot be modulated while the Vocoder Oscillator is active.
