- # [Wicker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wicker_(film))
	- ## About
		- Eleanor Wilson and Alex Huston Fischer's 2026 comedic fantasy. Olivia Colman plays a fisherwoman who commissions a basket-weaver to make her a husband out of wicker, and Alexander Skarsgård plays the husband. Peter Dinklage and Elizabeth Debicki are in the supporting cast.
		- Wilson and Fischer co-wrote it from Ursula Wills-Jones's short story "The Wicker Husband".
	- ## Release
		- Premiered at the 2026 Sundance Film Festival. Black Bear opened it in limited US release on October 23, 2026 and went wide on October 30; the UK and Ireland release was also October 30.
