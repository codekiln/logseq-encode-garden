- # 140W USB-C Power Adapter
	- Turns mains power into up to 140 W of USB-C power for a MacBook Pro.
	- ## Input
		- Mains power through a removable AC plug. [[Apple/Cable/Power Adapter Extension]] takes the plug's place to add a 1.8 m cord.
	- ## Output
		- One USB-C port, up to 140 W.
	- ## Works with
		- [[Apple/Cable/USB-C to MagSafe 3]]. The adapter and that cable ship together with the MacBook Pro (16-inch, 2023).
	- ## Source
		- [MacBook Pro (16-inch, 2023) - Tech Specs](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111838)
