- # Mutation view
	- The [sixth button down the right-hand column]([[Launchpad/UI/Area/3-Right/6-Mutation-Button]]) opens Mutation view. Select a step in the top half of the grid, then press a pad on the eight-pad slider at the top of the Play Area to vary the pitch of its notes during playback. The leftmost pad means no Mutation.
