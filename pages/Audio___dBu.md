- # dBu
	- A voltage level in decibels relative to 0.775 V RMS, the voltage that delivers 1 mW into 600 Ω.
	- +4 dBu (about 1.23 V) is pro line level. Consumer line level is −10 dBV, about −7.8 dBu, where dBV is relative to 1 V.
	- Mixer specs give maximum input and output levels in dBu, and noise as a negative dBu figure. See [[Audio/Signal Level]].
