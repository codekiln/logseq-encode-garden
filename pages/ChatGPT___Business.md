- # [ChatGPT Business](https://openai.com/business/pricing/)
	- The per-seat workspace tier of [[ChatGPT]], formerly called Team. The workspace owner manages billing.
	- Seat prices as of [[2026-11-21 Sat]], per [business pricing](https://openai.com/business/pricing/):
		- **Standard** — $25 per user a month, or $20 billed annually.
		- **Premium** — $125 per user a month, or $100 billed annually; no five-hour limit, and includes dots.
	- Includes [[ChatGPT/Work]], [[Codex]], workspace agents and plugins, and up to $500 a month in API credits.
	- OpenAI's help articles disagree on the minimum number of seats (one or two); the pricing page says "available starting at 2 users".
	- The next tier up is [[ChatGPT/Enterprise]].
