- # How do I launch [[Launchpad]] sequencer patterns across tracks as a scene?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- In **Sequencer → Patterns**, the 16 pads at the bottom of the grid are sequencer Scenes. Press a Scene to launch its saved Patterns or Pattern chains across the four sequencer tracks. These Scenes control the internal sequencer, while Session View scenes launch Ableton Live clips. See [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes]].
		- To assign Patterns, hold a Scene pad and select a Pattern or Pattern chain for each track. Release the Scene pad, then select it to launch the assignments. Editing a Scene does not change playback until the Scene is selected again or its Scene chain cycles back. See [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/01 Assign]].
		- During playback, selecting a Scene queues it until track 1's current Pattern ends. Hold **Shift** while selecting a Scene to switch immediately. Press two Scene pads together to chain the Scenes between them. See [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/03 Queue]] and [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/02 Chain]].
