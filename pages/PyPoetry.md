- # [Poetry](https://python-poetry.org)
	- Source repository: [python-poetry/poetry](https://github.com/python-poetry/poetry). [[GitHub/Star]] count: 34315 (checked [[2026-11-25 Wed]])
	- Dependency manager and packaging tool for [[Python]]. A project declares its dependencies in `pyproject.toml`, and Poetry resolves them into a `poetry.lock` file, installs them into a virtual environment, and builds and publishes packages.
	- Written in [[Python]].
	- The page is named PyPoetry because [[Poetry]] is the page for the art form.
