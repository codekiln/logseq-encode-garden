- # 03.01.03.02 Shift
	- Hold **Shift** to access functions printed in blue on the panel and other secondary functions. [[Microfreak/UG/20 Appendix C - Cheat Sheet]] lists them.
	- The shift button
		- ![01 The shift button](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/Microfreak___UG___03%20Overview___01%20Front%20Panel___03%20Bottom%20Row___02%20Shift___Asset___01-The-shift-button.png)
	- Shift functions
		- Toggle between Arpeggiator and Sequencer.
		- Set the swing rate.
		- Control the Attack and Fall shapes of the Cycling Envelope.
		- Transpose or reload sequences, or copy an arpeggio to a sequence.
