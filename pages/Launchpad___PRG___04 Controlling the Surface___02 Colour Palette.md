- # Colour palette
	- Notes and CCs choose colours by index into a fixed 128-entry palette. Index 0 is off.
	- Colours named in the guide's examples: 5 red, 13 yellow, 19 green, 21 green, 37 turquoise, 45 blue.
	- Palette, decimal indices
		- ![Colour palette, decimal](../assets/.remote/logseq-encode-garden/Launchpad___PRG___04-Controlling-the-Surface___02-Colour-Palette___Asset___Palette-decimal.png)
	- Palette, hex indices
		- ![Colour palette, hex](../assets/.remote/logseq-encode-garden/Launchpad___PRG___04-Controlling-the-Surface___02-Colour-Palette___Asset___Palette-hex.png)
	- For arbitrary colours use RGB lighting in [[Launchpad/PRG/04 Controlling the Surface/06 LED Lighting SysEx]].
