- # Jules Verne
	- ## About
		- French novelist, 1828–1905.
		- Wrote the adventure novels *Journey to the Center of the Earth*, *Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas* and *Around the World in Eighty Days*, which are often counted among the roots of science fiction.
