- # [If Your Hip Doesn’t Rotate You Can’t Be at Full Power - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aG-uRxQ-RLA)
	- Channel: [The Ready State](https://www.youtube.com/@TheReadyState) · 11:45
	- Linked from the Mobility & Flexibility additional resources of [[Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols]] Chapter Two, as "Kelly Starrett Hip Mobility."
	- ## About
		- A "Mobilization of the Week" episode on a hip joint capsule mobilization.
		- Long hours of sitting, even with standing desks and daily walking, seldom take the hip through its full rotation. Like the shoulder, the hip has a rotator cuff.
		- First assess internal and external rotation against a 75 to 90 degree range, then hold the mobilization for about two minutes. If it pinches, add a band for distraction.
