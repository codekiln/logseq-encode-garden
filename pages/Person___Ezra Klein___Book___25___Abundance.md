- # [[Person/Ezra Klein/Book/25/Abundance]]
	- ## About
		- [[Abundance]] is a 2025 nonfiction book by [[Person/Ezra Klein]] and [[Person/Derek Thompson]] about the political and institutional barriers that limit the building of housing, clean energy, infrastructure, and other public goods.
		- The authors argue for a politics that increases society's capacity to build and provide. Their account connects [[Abundan/ce]] to housing affordability, public administration, energy, and technological progress.
