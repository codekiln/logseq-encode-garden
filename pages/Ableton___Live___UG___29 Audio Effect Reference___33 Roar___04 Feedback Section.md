- # [29.33.4 Feedback Section](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-audio-effect-reference/#feedback-section)
	- ![Roar’s Feedback Section](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/RoarFeedbackSectionL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Feeding Roar back into itself adds ringing, strange tones.
	- **Feedback Mode** — **Time**, **Synced**, **Triplet** and **Dotted** use the feedback as a delay; **Note** makes it ring at a chosen pitch.
	- **Feedback Amount** — how much is fed back. The compressor in the loop pulls feedback down on loud signals.
	- **Feedback Invert** — inverts the feedback phase for cancellation effects
	- **Feedback Gate** — feedback fades out once input stops; off, it continues forever
	- **Feedback Filter Frequency** and **Width** — band-pass filter on the feedback
