- # [29.2.6 Global Controls](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-audio-effect-reference/#global-controls)
	- ![Global Controls in Auto Filter](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/AutoFilterGlobalL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- **Clip** — soft-clips the output to tame resonant peaks; its LED flashes when clipping
	- **Output** — output level, to make up for loudness changes from Drive
	- **Dry/Wet** — balance of processed and dry signal
