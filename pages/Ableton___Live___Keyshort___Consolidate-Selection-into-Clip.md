- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Join the selected Arrangement clips into one clip per track** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+J` (Win `Ctrl+J`)
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, merges the selected adjacent clips, gaps included, into a single clip on each track; for audio it writes a new sample that includes clip gain, warping and clip envelopes but no effects. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/13 Consolidating Clips]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
