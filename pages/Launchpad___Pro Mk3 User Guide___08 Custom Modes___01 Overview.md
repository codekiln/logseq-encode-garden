- # 8.1 Overview
	- Create, edit, download, and back up Custom Modes in [Novation Components](https://components.novationmusic.com/) using a WebMIDI browser or its standalone application. Modes can be shared among Launchpad Mini Mk3, Launchpad X, and Launchpad Pro.
