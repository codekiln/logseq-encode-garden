- # AI Model Lab
	- ## Overview
		- An AI model lab is an organization whose central work includes researching, training, evaluating, and deploying artificial intelligence models. Labs may also build products and infrastructure around those models.
		- The phrase describes an organizational role, not one model, one technical method, or a formal legal entity type. A lab can be part of a larger company or operate as a company itself.
	- ## Why the distinction matters
		- Model releases, products, research groups, and companies change at different rates. Treating them as separate entities helps keep a model's technical history distinct from the organization that develops it.
		- Public-market activity can affect employees, investors, and company financing, while the availability and cost of models depend on product and technical decisions. Those effects should not be collapsed into one claim about AI.
	- ## Examples
		- [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]] develop and offer AI models as part of broader organizations.
