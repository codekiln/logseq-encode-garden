- # Deep Life Stack
	- [[Person/Cal Newport]]'s tool for changing a life that feels aimless or shallow into a deep one. He presented it on [[Person/Cal Newport/Pod]] as a stack of layers in 2023, then replaced the stack with a map in 2026.
	- ## Versions
		- ### v1.0, June 2023
			- Introduced in [Ep. 252: The Deep Life Stack](https://www.thedeeplife.com/podcasts/episodes/ep-252-the-deep-life-stack/).
			- Four layers, bottom to top: Discipline, Values, Calm, Plan.
		- ### v2.0, November 2023
			- Introduced in [Ep. 274: The Deep Life Stack v2.0](https://www.thedeeplife.com/podcasts/episodes/ep-274-the-deep-life-stack-v2-0/).
			- Two stacks worked in order. Capability, bottom to top: Discipline, Control, Craft, Simplification. Then transformation, bottom to top: Values, Service, Transformation, Legacy.
		- ### Deep Life Map, August 2026
			- Introduced in [[Person/Cal Newport/Pod/26/08 Rethinking the Deep Life Stack (Again!)]].
			- Three territories: Capability, Values and Vision. Capability and Values each feed Vision. You start in the territory you lack and move back and forth among them.
	- ## Related
		- [[Person/John Mavrick]] wrote up v2.0 in [The Deep Life Stack V2](https://notes.johnmavrick.com/The+Deep+Life+Stack+V2), a page in his public knowledge garden.
