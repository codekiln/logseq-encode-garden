- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Split a note at an exact spot with the mouse** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: hold `E`, then click to split or drag to place the cut
		- Description: In the MIDI Note Editor, splits the note under the pointer; dragging across several notes splits them all, and adding `Cmd` snaps the cut to the grid. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/10 Editing MIDI/05 Editing MIDI Notes/08 Note Operations]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/12 Clip View MIDI Note Editor]].
