- # [The UNIX-HATERS Handbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_UNIX-HATERS_Handbook)
	- ## About
		- A semi-humorous compilation of complaints from the UNIX-HATERS mailing list, edited by Simson Garfinkel, Daniel Weise, and Steven Strassmann.
		- The book examines Unix's command-line interface, design philosophy, system administration, networking, and X Window System through the frustrations of its users.
		- Published by IDG Books in 1994, with a foreword by [[Person/Don Norman]] and an anti-foreword by [[Person/Dennis Ritchie]].
