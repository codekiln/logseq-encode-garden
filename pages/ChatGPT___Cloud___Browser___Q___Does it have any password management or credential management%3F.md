- # Does [[ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser]] have any [[Password Management]] or [[Credential/Manage/ment]]?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- **Short answer:** it has no password store. When a site asks you to log in, ChatGPT shows you a form in its app; what you type goes to the website without the model seeing it. The site then stays logged in until the session expires or you clear it.
		- [[Answer/Official]] from [Using cloud browser in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001280-using-cloud-browser-in-chatgpt): "Credentials entered through the secure form go directly to the remote browser. The username and password entered there are not visible to the model, and ChatGPT does not store those sign-in credentials." A review model checks each sign-in request for phishing before the form appears.
		- **Signed-in sessions are shared across Work threads.** A site you log in to in one [[ChatGPT/Work]] thread stays logged in for tasks in other threads.
			- [[Answer/Official]] from the same article: "The authentication will persist for future tasks until it expires, so you do not need to sign in each time," and "A cloud browser session may remain active for later tasks until it expires or you clear its data."
			- Settings > Cloud browser > Browser data > Cookies shows one list of logged-in sites for the whole account. There is no list per thread, and clearing a site from that list logs every later task out of it.
			- The article never says "other threads" outright. It says "future tasks" and "later tasks", and since Settings keeps only one list for the account, those tasks most likely include ones in other threads. Whether two members of a [[ChatGPT/Business]] workspace share one list is undocumented.
			- A saved session can open the account without the password or second factor, so clearing it after a sensitive task is worth the step.
		- The 2026-08-25 [release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes) say the browser "supports the use of password managers", without naming any. Whether iOS AutoFill or [[1Password]] fills the secure form is unverified.
		- [[1Password]] announced "1Password for Agents" on 2026-10-22 and named ChatGPT Work and dots as partners ([press release](https://1password.com/press/2026/october/1password-for-agents-trust-layer-personal-ai-agents)), but its [blog](https://1password.com/blog/1password-for-ai-agents) lists that integration as coming soon, and its [support page](https://support.1password.com/1password-for-agents/) of 2026-11-04 does not list ChatGPT.
