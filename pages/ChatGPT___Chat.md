- # ChatGPT Chat
	- The conversation-centered experience within the general-purpose ChatGPT side of the [[ChatGPT/App/Desktop]].
	- Chat organizes work around a sequence of messages: a person sends a message, ChatGPT responds, and the conversation continues.
	- OpenAI presents Chat as the place to ask questions, explore ideas, compare options, and make plans. See the [official ChatGPT overview](https://learn.chatgpt.com/).
	- [[ChatGPT/App/Desktop/Concept/Chat, Work, and Codex]] compares Chat with [[ChatGPT/Work]] and [[Codex]].
