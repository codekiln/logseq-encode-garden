- # LPProMK3 MIDI
	- Carries the Launchpad Pro MK3's notes from Note, Chord, Custom and Sequencer modes, MIDI from external input, and the messages that light its pads in Custom and Programmer modes ([[Launchpad/PRG/03 MIDI]], [[Launchpad/UI/Area/5-Rear/1-USB-C-Port]]).
	- ## In [[Ableton/Live]]
		- Input and output label: `Launchpad Pro MK3 (LPProMK3 MIDI)`.
		- A track records the Launchpad's sequencer with this port as its MIDI From ([[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/09 Ableton Live]]).
		- [[Launchpad/Custom/Mode/1 Arrangement]] needs Remote on for this input.
		- codekiln's [[Ableton/Live/MIDI/Port]] settings on [[2026-11-14 Sat]]: the input has Track and Remote on; the output has Track, Sync and Remote on.
		- On [[GitP/A/Log/26/11/13-Fri]], with the Launchpad in Note mode, a track listening to this port stayed silent, while [[Launchpad/MIDI/Port/LPProMK3 DAW]] played the Drum Rack.
