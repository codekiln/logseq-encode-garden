- # [22. Stem Separation](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/stem-separation/)
	- Splitting recorded audio into separate stems such as vocals, drums and bass.
	- Stem separation splits recorded audio — a song, loop or sample — into parts you can warp, chop, rearrange and process like any audio clip.
	- Uses include isolating vocals for remixes, pulling apart melodies and rhythms, sampling single instruments, and blending stems in DJ mixes.
