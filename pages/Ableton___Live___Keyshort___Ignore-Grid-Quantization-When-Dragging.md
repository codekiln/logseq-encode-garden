	- **Drag clips, notes or breakpoints without snapping to the grid** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: hold `Cmd` while dragging (Win `Alt`)
		- Description: Bypasses the grid for one action, or turns snapping on for it while the grid is off. The grid list calls it Bypass Snapping While Dragging.
