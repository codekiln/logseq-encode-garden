- # [Invada Records](http://www.invada.co.uk/)
	- Record label started in Australia in 2001 by [[Person/Geoff Barrow]] and Ashley Anderson of Katalyst. Barrow set up its UK arm in 2003 with partner Fat Paul. [[Group/Beak]], Barrow's band, releases its albums through Invada.
