- # [Maimonides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maimonides)
	- ## About
		- Moses ben Maimon (1138 to 1204), rabbi, physician and philosopher who lived in Córdoba, Fez and Cairo. His Mishneh Torah restated the whole of Jewish law as a code, leaving out the [[Judaism/Talmud]]'s arguments, and his Guide for the Perplexed read the tradition through Aristotelian philosophy. Both works became objects of commentary in their turn.
