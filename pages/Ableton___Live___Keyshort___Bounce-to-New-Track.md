- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Render the selected clips or time to a new audio track** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+B` (Win `Ctrl+B`)
		- Description: Bounces the selection with its effects printed onto a new audio track, carries the volume, pan and sends over as settings, and mutes the originals. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio/01 Bouncing Individual Tracks]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/18 Bounce to Audio]].
