- # [Claude Artifacts](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9487310-what-are-artifacts-and-how-do-i-use-them)
	- A feature of [[Claude]] that shows substantial standalone output, such as a document, code, a diagram or a small web app, in a panel beside the chat, where it can be edited, published and shared by link.
	- Launched in June 2024 alongside [[Anthropic/Model/Claude/3.5/Sonnet]].

		- [[Claude/Desktop/Capability/AI-powered artifacts]] — artifacts that call Claude from inside the page.
		- [[Claude/Artifact/Data Dashboard]] — the artifact type for dashboards that show data.
