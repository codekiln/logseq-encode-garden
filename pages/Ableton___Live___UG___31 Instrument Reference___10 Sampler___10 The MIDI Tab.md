- # [31.10.10 The MIDI Tab](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#the-midi-tab)
	- Key, Velocity, Release Velocity, Aftertouch, Mod Wheel, Foot Controller and Pitch Bend each map to two destinations with Amount A and Amount B. Example: Velocity to Loop Length at 100 gives longer loops for harder notes.
		- ![The MIDI Tab](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/SamplerMIDITabL12.png)
	- **Pitch Bend Range** — up to 24 semitones.
	- Clicking the Sampler picture scrolls its credits.
