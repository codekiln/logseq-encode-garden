- # Ada Lovelace
	- ## About
		- English mathematician, 1815–1852. Born Augusta Ada Byron, the daughter of the poet Lord Byron; later Countess of Lovelace.
		- In 1843 she translated Luigi Menabrea's article on [[Person/Charles Babbage]]'s [[Analytical/Engine]] and added her own notes, about three times the length of the article.
		- Note G gives a method for computing Bernoulli numbers on the engine, often called the first published computer program.
		- Her notes also argue that the engine could act on symbols other than numbers, such as musical notes, if their relations were expressed in its terms.
