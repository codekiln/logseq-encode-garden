- # [8.2.3 Clip Groove](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/clip-view/#clip-groove)
	- The Clip Groove chooser picks a groove from the Groove Pool. Hot-Swap Groove picks a new one from the browser, replacing it in the clip and the Groove Pool.
	- Commit writes the groove into the clip and turns the groove off. On audio clips, positive groove velocity becomes a volume clip envelope, overwriting any existing one. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/14 Grooves]].
