- # EQ
	- Short for equalizer: raising or lowering the level of a range of frequencies to change a sound's tone.
	- **Shelving** bands lift or cut everything above or below a frequency. A mixer's LOW and HIGH knobs are usually shelves.
	- **Peaking** bands lift or cut a range around a centre frequency. A MID knob is one.
	- A small mixer's channel EQ has fixed frequencies, so each knob sets only the amount, often ±15 dB. A software EQ such as [[Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/16 EQ Three]] or [[Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/15 EQ Eight]] lets you move the frequencies as well.
