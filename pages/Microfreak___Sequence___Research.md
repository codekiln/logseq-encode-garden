- # Sequence research
	- I want MicroFreak presets that hold a sequence I describe in words. This page logs what the sources say and what the uploads and playback runs on my MicroFreak showed.
	- The results live on other pages. [[Microfreak/Sequence/Storage]] describes where a preset keeps patterns A and B and what each step holds. [[Microfreak/CLI/Upload a preset]] writes a preset to a slot, and [[Microfreak/Sequence/Write a sequence]] writes a described sequence into a slot and checks it on the instrument. Nothing found so far switches to pattern B without the panel; B data written from this Mac plays as described from pattern A's place.
	- ## What the sources say
		- [[Microfreak/UG/13 Sequencer]] describes two patterns per preset, A and B, that share one length and hold notes, velocity, duration and four modulation tracks. [[Microfreak/UG/13 Sequencer/01 Use/03 Record/01 Step Time]] describes rests and ties.
		- `microfreak:inspect` ([[Microfreak/CLI/Inspect a preset]]) reads the named parameters, including the sequence length, gate length and smoothing settings in the `Seq` section. Its `--sequence` flag decodes the patterns stored after them.
		- [Elektroid's MicroFreak connector](https://github.com/dagargo/elektroid/blob/7806ecafda5fbed214e0e0c4f48a92ead52bdc73/src/connectors/microfreak.c) uploads a complete preset and passes its contents through unread. [[Microfreak/CLI/Preset metadata over USB]] records successful reads from the instrument, and [[Microfreak/CLI/Upload a preset]] records uploads that read back unchanged.
		- [Freakout's firmware notes](https://github.com/kmorrill/freakout/blob/c04166d7b880860879827befa088af6c0e9e789e/docs/microfreak-firmware-notes.md) shape how the garden reads saved parameters. Their [section on the active-preset serializer](https://github.com/kmorrill/freakout/blob/c04166d7b880860879827befa088af6c0e9e789e/docs/microfreak-firmware-notes.md#active-preset-serializer-and-the-operation-19-boundary) has leads on the sequence:
			- A saved preset holds a fixed-size Sequence A/B area alongside the named parameters.
			- In [[Microfreak/Preset/005 MotivSeq]], the data right after Pattern A names the modulation-track destinations, then holds the gate length and a copy of the sequence length. Playback follows the `Seq.Length` parameter and ignores the copy.
			- With Clock Source set to USB, a host can play the sequence with MIDI Start, Clock and a transposing note, then record the notes the MicroFreak sends back over MIDI. No audio is needed.
	- ## Findings, 2026-11-16
		- Uploads of edited presets to slots 330–339, played with MIDI Start and Clock while Arp/Seq MIDI Out was on, matched what was written: notes, chords of up to four notes, velocities, rests, ties, modulation tracks and lengths from 8 to 64 steps. Each slot read back unchanged.
		- Freakout's step and trailer layout held. Playback follows `Seq.GateLen` and ignores the gate copy after Pattern A, where Freakout's notes credit the copy. A pattern whose trailer marks it erased (`0xFF` in place of `0xA5`) plays nothing even with notes in its steps.
		- With Clock Source on USB, MIDI Start and Clock alone start pattern A; no transposing note is needed.
		- Descriptions written with `microfreak:write-sequence` to slots 431–446 played as described on MIDI out and in the H5 recording, and each slot downloaded afterwards still held its description: lengths 1, 7, 16, 32 and 64, rests, ties to the same note, to a different note and across the loop, chords of two to four notes, per-step velocity, two modulation tracks, and pattern A only, B only and both. Each slot is listed under Written descriptions played on the instrument, and the attempts to start pattern B under Pattern B.
		- Slot 358 stores Velo Amp Mod (`Kbd.PresAmp`) at 0, and its steps at velocity 127 and 80 played at the same level. With it at 32767, the steps at 127 were 3.5 dB louder. `microfreak:write-sequence` sets it from the description's `velo_amp_mod`; the runs are under The arpeggio request.
		- The open questions are listed on [[Microfreak/Sequence/Storage]].
	- ## Storage experiments, 2026-11-16
		- Each experiment preset started from [[Microfreak/Preset/382 Anit]], a preset with the sequencer on and paraphony on. `lib/sequence.py` wrote the steps, sometimes followed by a direct edit of one value, and [[Microfreak/CLI/Upload a preset]] wrote the result to a slot from 330 to 339 and read it back unchanged.
		- A script then selected the slot (CC 0 bank select, program change), turned the global Arp/Seq MIDI Out setting on (SysEx operation `0x42`, code `0x2B`), sent MIDI Start and 24-per-quarter-note clock at 240 BPM, recorded the notes and CCs the MicroFreak sent back, sent Stop and set Arp/Seq MIDI Out back to off. Clock Source (code `0x2E`) was already USB. [[Microfreak/CLI/Audition a sequence]] builds the same procedure into a task.
		- Before any upload, playing [[Microfreak/Preset/382 Anit]], [[Microfreak/Preset/113 D3 Chords]], [[Microfreak/Preset/005 MotivSeq]] and [[Microfreak/Preset/035 FM Seq]] sent the notes, chords and velocities the decoder reads from their downloads.
		- Experiments on [[2026-11-16 Mon]], one slot each:
			- 330: a 16-step pattern A with single notes, a rest, a four-note chord with four velocities, and three kinds of tie. Everything played as listed under Step record on [[Microfreak/Sequence/Storage]], and step 17 started the loop again.
			- 331 and 333: gate and length copies against `Seq.GateLen` and `Seq.Length`.
			- 332: pattern A marked erased with its steps in place.
			- 334: two modulation tracks with smoothing off.
			- 335: status 3, empty voice 1, a note only in voice 4, velocities 0 and 200, non-zero reserved values.
			- 336: 64 steps with notes 36–99 and velocities 1–64. All 64 played in order, then looped.
			- 337: trailer values 10 and 11 swapped between the patterns.
			- 338: a voice gap, a tie into a different note, and a tie across the loop.
			- 339: [[Microfreak/Preset/003 Trance]] with `Arp.SeqOn` on.
	- ## Written descriptions played on the instrument, 2026-11-16
		- Each case was one `verify-sequence` run with `--base 358` ([[Microfreak/Preset/358 20241116.1]], Basic Waves, paraphonic, no pitch modulation), two loops at 120 BPM, plain timbre, with nobody at the panel. In every case MIDI out matched the description on every played step, and the slot downloaded afterwards decoded to the description and equalled the upload.
		- 431 SeqV L1: one step, E4, stored as 4 steps. Audio matched 8 of 8.
		- 432 SeqV L7: C4 to B4, 7 steps. Audio matched 14 of 14.
		- 433 SeqV L16: 16 steps with 5 rests. Audio matched 22 notes and heard 10 silent rests.
		- 434 SeqV L32: a 32-step scale up and down. Audio matched 64 of 64.
		- 435 SeqV L64: 64 steps, a rest every eighth step, velocities 60–119. Audio matched 112 notes and heard 16 silent rests.
		- 436 SeqV Rests: rests on the first step, on two steps in a row and on the last step. Audio matched 8 notes and 8 rests.
		- 437 SeqV TieSame: G4 held over two steps and F4 over three. MIDI out sent one Note On for each; on the held steps it listed the note as held, and the audio heard the held pitch.
		- 438 SeqV TieDiff: C5 tied into D5, A4 tied into a rest, E4 tied into G4 tied into C4. Each different note started with its own Note On. Audio matched 12 notes and 4 rests.
		- 439 SeqV TieLoop: D4 on step 8 tied into D4 on step 1. The second loop's step 1 sent no Note On and held D4.
		- 440 SeqV Chords: chords of two, three and four notes, a three-note chord held over two steps, and a four-note chord with velocities 40, 70, 100 and 127. MIDI out carried every note with its velocity. The audio chord estimate found every note on 10 of the 12 chord steps; on the other 2, the F3 A3 C4 E4 chord, it found some.
		- 441 SeqV Velocity: A4 at velocities 1, 20, 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 and 127. MIDI out sent each velocity. Audio matched 16 of 16.
		- 442 SeqV ModTrack: track 1 on filter cutoff with 0, 64, 128, 192 (on a rest), 255 and 10, sent as CC 23 0, 32, 64, 96, 127 and 5; track 2 on envelope decay with 40 and 200, sent as CC 106 20 and 100. In the audio the cutoff of 0 and 5 muted the note, as a closed filter does.
		- 443 SeqV A only: pattern A plays, B erased. Audio matched 8 of 8.
		- 444 SeqV B only: A erased, B holds A3 C4 E4 A4. Playback sent no notes and was silent; the download holds B.
		- 445 SeqV A and B: A is C4 E4 G4 C5 and B is A3 C4 E4 A4. Playback played A; the download holds both.
		- 446 SeqV B in A: slot 445's stored pattern B moved into pattern A's place in a downloaded copy, uploaded and played. It played A3 C4 E4 A4, matching on MIDI out and in the audio, so the data written for B plays as described.
		- The same cases with [[Microfreak/Preset/382 Anit]] as the base also matched on MIDI out and in the download, but held notes read a semitone sharp in the audio. A pitch track of the recording showed each Anit note rising about a semitone within 120 ms of its start, which a short note barely shows and a tied one does. Anit stores a modulation-matrix amount, `Co1.EG1`, and a glide that slot 358 lacks.
	- ## The arpeggio request, 2026-11-16
		- The request "a 16-step minor arpeggio on A with a rest every fourth step, pattern B the same an octave up, accent the downbeats" was written following [[Microfreak/Sequence/Write a sequence]], with `--base 358`, and checked with `verify-sequence` at its defaults: two loops at 120 BPM, plain timbre, with nobody at the panel.
		- 481 A min Arp: velocities 127 on steps 1, 5, 9 and 13 and 80 elsewhere. MIDI out matched all 32 played steps, the audio matched 24 notes and heard 8 rests, and the download held patterns A and B as described. In the recording, the steps at 127 and at 80 measured the same level (mean level over the first 50 ms of each step).
		- 482 Arp VeloAmp: the same description written from a copy of slot 358 with `Kbd.PresAmp` set to 32767. The same matches; the steps at 127 measured 3.5 dB louder than the steps at 80.
		- 483 A min Arp: the same description with `velo_amp_mod: 10`, which wrote `Kbd.PresAmp` 32767. The same matches and the same 3.5 dB difference.
		- 484 E Bass Sweep: the modulation-track example from [[Microfreak/Sequence/Write a sequence]], 8 steps with filter cutoff values 60 to 255. MIDI out matched all 16 played steps, cutoff CCs included, and the audio matched 16 of 16.
		- `Kbd.PresAmp` is the panel's Velo Amp Mod, 0–10, as far as these runs show: the 23 presets read on [[2026-11-16 Mon]] to decode the storage layout hold 0, 19660 or 22937, which fit 0, 6 and 7 on a 0–10 scale.
	- ## Step length, accents and rests, 2026-11-16
		- An agent that knew only the garden put a bass line, E Octave Bass, into slot 490 from a plain-language request. MIDI out matched all 24 played steps and the download matched its description. The runs below answer the gaps it reported in [#444 Close the gaps the fresh-agent test of the MicroFreak sequence workflow found](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-encode-garden/issues/444). Each used `--base 358` with nobody at the panel.
		- 485–489 and 491–496: each slot held a copy of [[Microfreak/Preset/358 20241116.1]] with the 4-step pattern C4 D4 E4 F4 and one `Arp.Div` value, covering all eleven divisions. Each was uploaded, read back unchanged, and played at 180 BPM while the clocks the MicroFreak echoed were counted between its note-ons. Every interval in each run equalled the clocks per step listed under Settings shared by both patterns on [[Microfreak/Sequence/Storage]], and the notes came in order: 496 `1`, 485 `1/2`, 486 `1/2T`, 487 `1/4`, 488 `1/4T`, 489 `1/8`, 491 `1/8T`, 492 `1/16`, 493 `1/16T`, 494 `1/32`, 495 `1/32T`.
		- 497 Eighth Bass: the eighth-note example from [[Microfreak/Sequence/Write a sequence]], `division: 1/8`. `verify-sequence` played it at 12 clocks per step; MIDI out matched all 16 played steps and the audio matched 16 of 16. At the 50% gate each note lasted 6 clocks, a sixteenth.
		- 498 Tie Into D4: C4 tied into D4, then E4 and G4. MIDI out matched all 8 played steps. C4 lasted 6 clocks, its whole step, and D4 started on step 2 with its own Note On and lasted 3.
		- 499 Accent Levels: E2, E3, A3 and A4, each at velocity 127 and then 80, with a rest after every note and `velo_amp_mod: 10`; four loops. With `--preset-sound`, 127 measured 3.5–3.7 dB above 80 on every pitch, and each rest step sounded the previous note's release 7.9–8.3 dB below the note, where the pitch analysis still heard that note. With the default plain timbre, 127 measured 3.4–3.7 dB above 80 as well, and the rest steps measured 58–60 dB below the peak.
		- 500 A min Arp: the arpeggio example with `--preset-sound`. MIDI out matched all 32 played steps. Each note's release carried into the next step, so on 12 steps the strongest pitch the audio found was the previous note and the step read `match_in_chord`.
		- In slot 490, where every note follows the last without a rest, the agent measured velocity 127 1.0–1.2 dB above 80 on E2 and 2.7–4.0 dB above on E3.
		- `microfreak:empty-slots` read all 512 headers in about five seconds and listed 325–329, 340–349, 383, 403–430, 447–480 and 497–512 as empty before slots 497–500 were written.
	- ## Pattern B
		- No MIDI or SysEx message found so far starts pattern B. Selecting a slot loads pattern A, and Start plays it.
		- Tried with slot 445 playing and MIDI out recorded:
			- Song Select 0, 1 and 2 before Start, and Song Select 1 during playback: pattern A each time.
			- Reading every global setting code from 0x00 to 0x7F with operation 0x43, which changes nothing. Every code answers. 0x20–0x4D are the settings named in MIDI Control Center's dictionary, which [Freakout's MIDI Control Center notes](https://github.com/kmorrill/freakout/blob/main/docs/microfreak-mcc-static-analysis.md#microfreak-subcommand-dictionary) recover; none of them concerns patterns. 0x4E–0x7F read 0. 0x00–0x1F hold unnamed values that stayed the same with slot 444 selected, slot 445 selected, during playback and after Stop.
			- Writing the three unnamed codes inside the dictionary's range, 0x2C, 0x48 and 0x4B: each read back unchanged, so the instrument ignored the write.
			- The unnamed codes 0x00–0x1F were left unwritten. How the firmware handles a write to them is unknown.
			- After these runs, all 128 global codes read back the values they had before.
		- Tried earlier: program change, CCs 1–119 one at a time during playback, and swapping pattern trailer values 10 and 11.
		- [Freakout's firmware notes](https://github.com/kmorrill/freakout/blob/main/docs/microfreak-firmware-notes.md#active-preset-serializer-and-the-operation-19-boundary) found no SysEx handler that reaches the sequencer's working copy of patterns A and B.
		- Pattern B is therefore checked by the download (slots 444 and 445) and by playing B's data from A's place (slot 446). Hearing B itself takes one press on the panel.
		- To hear it, select preset 445, SeqV A and B, on the panel, press the B icon in the sequencer row, then run `mise run microfreak:audition --slot 445 --no-select`. Pattern B should play a rising A minor arpeggio, A3 C4 E4 A4, where pattern A plays C4 E4 G4 C5. Nobody has pressed B for these checks yet. Run without pressing B, `microfreak:verify-sequence --slot 445 --pattern B --keep-selection` reported pattern A's notes on all 8 steps as mismatches.
