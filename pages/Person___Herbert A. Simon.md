- # Herbert A. Simon
	- American economist, political scientist and cognitive scientist, born 15 June 1916 in Milwaukee and died 9 February 2001.[^1] Longtime faculty member at Carnegie Mellon University (earlier the Carnegie Institute of Technology).[^2]
	- Won the 1978 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences "for his pioneering research into the decision-making process within economic organizations."[^2]
	- Won the 1975 A.M. Turing Award with [[Person/Allen Newell]].[^3]
	- ## Ideas
		- Coined "satisficing" in 1956: [[Satis/fice]]
