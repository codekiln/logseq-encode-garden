- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Paste copied clips or time as freshly bounced audio** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+Option+V` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+V`)
		- Description: Bounces the copied material as it sounds at the moment of pasting, into an audio track, an empty MIDI track or a take lane. The view list gives the same keys to Hide/Show Video Window. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio/03 Pasting Bounced Audio]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/18 Bounce to Audio]].
