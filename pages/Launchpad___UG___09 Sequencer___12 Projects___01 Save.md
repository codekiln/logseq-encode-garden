- # 9.12.1 Saving your Sequencer Project
	- From Steps, Projects, or Patterns View, hold [Shift]([[Launchpad/UI/Area/0-Top/0-Shift-Button]]) and press [Projects]([[Launchpad/UI/Area/0-Top/8-Projects-Button]]) to start saving. Save pulses green. Press Projects again to save to the active slot; Save flashes briefly while the device writes, and controls are unavailable during that moment.
	- To cancel saving, press a button other than Shift, Projects, or Setup.
	- To save to a different slot in Projects View, hold Shift and press Projects. Save pulses white. You may choose one of eight Project colors with the track-select buttons, then press the desired Project slot. Save and the slot flash once the Project is saved.
	- A saved Project also includes Note and Chord mode settings and the MIDI channel of every Custom Mode.
