- # [34.2 The Mapping Browser](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/midi-and-key-remote-control/#the-mapping-browser)
	- ![The Mapping Browser](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MappingBrowserMIDI.png?auto=&w=320)
	- The Mapping Browser manages every manual MIDI, computer-key and Macro Control mapping. It appears only while one of the three mapping modes is on, and shows the mappings for that mode.
	- Each row shows the control, the path to the mapped parameter, the parameter name, and the mapping's Min and Max. Min and Max can be edited at any time and swapped with a context menu command. Delete a mapping with Backspace (Windows) or Delete (Mac).
	- Instant mappings depend on context and are not listed here.
