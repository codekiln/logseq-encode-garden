- ### Inflation
	- In this trade-off, inflation means a broad rise in prices over time. Mild, stable inflation gives households and businesses a predictable planning environment and lets relative prices adjust without requiring nominal prices and wages to fall.
	- When inflation is high or unpredictable, purchasing power shifts between borrowers and savers, contracts become harder to plan around, and people spend effort adjusting prices rather than producing.
