- # [Marcus Eoin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boards_of_Canada)
	- ## About
		- Electronic musician, born Marcus Eoin Sandison on 27 May 1973, who releases music with his older brother [[Person/Mike Sandison]] as [[Boards of Canada]], through [[Label/Warp Records]]. He joined Mike's band as bassist in 1986, the year the band took the name Boards of Canada.
		- Studied artificial intelligence at the University of Edinburgh, and left before finishing his degree.
		- Credited as instrumentalist and sound designer on [[Boards of Canada/Music/Recording/Inferno]] (2026). ASCAP lists him as co-writer on four of its tracks.
