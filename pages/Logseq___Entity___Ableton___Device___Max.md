- # Ableton Max for Live Device
	- In this garden, **Ableton Max for Live Device** pages document one [[Ableton/Max for Live]] device codekiln built or changed: what it does, how to set it up in [[Ableton/Live]], and where its files are.
	- ## When a page is a Max for Live Device
		- The subject is one `.amxd` device with its own name, such as MF Number Pad.
		- Not this entity: Ableton's own Max for Live devices, which the manual pages under [[Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices]] cover, or ideas for devices not yet built, which go under `Ableton/Max for Live/Idea/`.
	- ## Naming
		- `Ableton/Live/Device/Max/<Name>`, with the device's name in Live's browser and its words joined by hyphens: `MF Number Pad.amxd` is [[Ableton/Live/Device/Max/MF-Number-Pad]].
	- ## Page shape
		- H1 with the device's name, then one sentence on what it does and its kind in Live's browser (Max MIDI Effect, Max Audio Effect or Max Instrument).
		- `## Used by` — the [[Logseq/Entity/Launchpad/Custom/Mode]] or other controller pages that need the device. Those pages link back under `## Depends on`.
		- `## Set up in Live` — the track to put it on and that track's routing.
		- `## Display` — what each readout on the device means.
		- `## How it works` — collapsed: the messages in and out, and the logic between them.
		- `## Files` — the `.amxd` and its patcher as [[Logseq/Entity/Asset]] pages, and the User Library path of the installed copy.
		- `## History` — dated tests and changes, linking the logs or idea pages where they happened.
