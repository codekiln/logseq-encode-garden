- # [Tracking (hunting) - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracking_(hunting))
		- > **Tracking** in [hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunting) and [ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology) is the science and art of observing [animal tracks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_track) and other signs, with the goal of gaining understanding of the landscape and the animal being tracked (the "quarry"). A further goal of tracking is the deeper understanding of the systems and patterns that make up the environment surrounding and incorporating the tracker.
		- In [[Person/J. R. R. Tolkien/Book/Series/The Lord of the Rings]], [[Person/J. R. R. Tolkien/Book/Series/The Lord of the Rings/Character/Aragorn]] is a Ranger whose woodcraft is this art applied to both quarry and people.
