- # [PayPal Agent Ready](https://developer.paypal.com/agent-ready/overview/)
	- PayPal’s tools for merchants to accept payments initiated by shopping agents. PayPal can also appear as the shopper’s payment method when the checkout offers it.
	- **What the shopper sees.** PayPal describes a limited rollout of Pay with PayPal for eligible purchases in ChatGPT. When the option appears, the shopper signs in or confirms with a passkey and approves the purchase in PayPal. Which ChatGPT plans and retailers currently offer this checkout is unverified. [PayPal’s AI shopping guide](https://www.paypal.com/us/money-hub/article/how-paypal-powers-ai-shopping)
	- **What the merchant receives.** In PayPal’s documented integration for ChatGPT apps, the shopper authorizes PayPal and the merchant’s app receives a delegated payment token. Braintree processes the payment using that token. This lets the checkout use the shopper’s approval without passing the shopper’s PayPal password to the agent. [PayPal developer guide](https://developer.paypal.com/agent-ready/overview/)
	- **How ChatGPT checkout changed.** PayPal announced the OpenAI partnership in October 2025 around Instant Checkout. OpenAI shifted toward merchant-run checkouts in March 2026. A specific seller’s checkout determines whether a shopper can choose PayPal. [PayPal partnership announcement](https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2025-10-28-OpenAI-and-PayPal-Team-Up-to-Power-Instant-Checkout-and-Agentic-Commerce-in-ChatGPT) · [OpenAI shopping update](https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/)
	- **Where ChatGPT Wallet fits.** [[ChatGPT/Wallet]] uses an eligible saved card for purchases approved in a conversation with a dot. A Pay with PayPal button opens PayPal’s own approval flow. Listed personal bills can be paid through [[PayPal/Bill Pay]]. [ChatGPT Wallet guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet) · [PayPal’s AI shopping guide](https://www.paypal.com/us/money-hub/article/how-paypal-powers-ai-shopping)
