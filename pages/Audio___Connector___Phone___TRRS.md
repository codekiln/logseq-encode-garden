- # TRRS — Tip-Ring-Ring-Sleeve
	- Four contacts, three insulating rings. Adds a microphone and inline-remote contact to the stereo pair of [[Audio/Connector/Phone/TRS]].
	- Two wiring orders are in circulation, CTIA/AHJ and OMTP. Both put left on the tip and right on the first ring, and they swap the microphone and ground between the second ring and the sleeve.
	- Five-contact successors exist: TRRRS at 3.5 mm under ITU-T P.382, adding a second microphone or a power line, and the 4.4 mm Pentaconn under JEITA RC-8141C for balanced headphone cable.
	- ## Sizes
		- [[Audio/Connector/Phone/TRRS/3.5mm]] — phone and tablet headsets. Defined by JEITA/EIAJ RC-5325A.
