- # [Johannes Brahms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Brahms)
	- ## About
		- German composer and pianist of the Romantic period ([[18/3/3]]–[[18/9/7]]).
		- The finale of his Piano Quartet No. 1 is a "Rondo alla zingarese", and his Hungarian Dances draw on the "gypsy-style" music he played with the violinist Ede Reményi.
