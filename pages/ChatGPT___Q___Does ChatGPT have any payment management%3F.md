- # Does [[ChatGPT]] have any [[Pay/ment/Manage/ment]]?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- **Short answer:** yes, as of [[2026-11-21 Sat]], through [[ChatGPT/Wallet]], for US [[ChatGPT/Pro]] subscribers only. Every other tier manages only the card that pays OpenAI for the subscription.
		- [[Answer/Official]] from [Making purchases with ChatGPT Wallet](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet): Pro subscribers save cards in Settings > Wallet or by asking their dot, and approve each purchase with Face ID or Touch ID. Merchants receive a network token in place of the card number.
		- **Subscription billing** is managed under Settings > Billing on every paid tier, through Apple or Google for app-store subscriptions, or by the workspace owner on [[ChatGPT/Business]].
		- **Before Wallet:** Instant Checkout launched on 2025-09-29 for US Free, Plus and Pro users and let them pay merchants with the subscription card on file ([Buy it in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/)). OpenAI scaled it back on 2026-03-24 and let merchants use their own checkout ([Powering product discovery in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/)).
		- **[[ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser]]** asks for confirmation in chat before a payment, but stores no payment methods; card details are typed into the merchant's site during takeover.
