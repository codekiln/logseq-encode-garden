- # Does [[ChatGPT]] have any [[Pay/ment/Manage/ment]]?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- **Yes.** With [[ChatGPT/Wallet]], an eligible US [[ChatGPT/Pro]] subscriber can ask their dot to make a supported purchase. You review the seller, items, total and card, then approve with Face ID or Touch ID. The dot checks out using a substitute card number that keeps your real card details from the dot and seller. [OpenAI Wallet guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet)
		- Wallet works in conversations with your dot. A [[ChatGPT/Work]] task uses its own [[ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser]] and asks for confirmation before a supported payment. [OpenAI cloud browser guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001280-using-cloud-browser-in-chatgpt)
		- The card that pays for your ChatGPT subscription is managed separately. OpenAI’s earlier Instant Checkout could use the card already on file for participating retailers. In March 2026, OpenAI shifted toward merchant-run checkout. [Instant Checkout launch](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/) · [Shopping update](https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/)
