- # Atul Gawande
	- **Bio:** American surgeon, writer, and public-health researcher. General and endocrine surgeon at Brigham and Women's Hospital, professor at Harvard, and longtime staff writer at *The New Yorker*. Known for books on medicine, performance, and systems, including [[Person/Atul Gawande/Book/09/The Checklist Manifesto]] (2009), *Being Mortal* (2014), *Better* (2007), and *Complications* (2002).
	- **Online presence:** https://atulgawande.com
