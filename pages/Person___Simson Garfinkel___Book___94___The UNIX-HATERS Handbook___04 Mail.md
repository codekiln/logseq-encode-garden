- # 4 Mail — Don't Talk to Me, I'm Not a Typewriter!
	- Summary:: Uses sendmail, bounced messages, mailer-daemon behavior, and Apple's 1991 mail failure to explore Unix mail's complexity and fragility.
	- Why it is included:: Demonstrates how a supposedly basic everyday service becomes a sprawling operational hazard when reliability and usability are secondary concerns.
