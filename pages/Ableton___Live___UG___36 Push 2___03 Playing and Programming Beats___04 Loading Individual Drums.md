- # [36.3.4 Loading Individual Drums](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#loading-individual-drums)
	- To load or replace a single pad, press **Device**, tap the pad, then press the second upper display button (the square icon marks a pad). Browse now loads into that pad, which flashes; tap other pads to browse for them in turn.
		- ![Selecting an individual pad in a Drum Rack](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2IndividualPad.png?auto=&w=320)
	- After loading, Load becomes **Load Next**, with **Load Previous** beside it, for auditioning neighbours in context.
	- To select a pad without playing it, hold **Select** while tapping, or press the Drum Rack track's lower display button to expand its pads onto the lower buttons and move with the arrow keys.
	- ## 36.3.4.1 Additional Pad Options for Push 2
		- Hold **Duplicate**, press a pad, then press another to copy it there. This replaces the destination's devices and sound but keeps its recorded notes.
		- With one pad selected, encoder 1 sets its choke group and encoder 2 transposes it.
		- `Shift`+tap a pad, then tap a pad in the outer ring, to colour it. Pad colours are saved with the Set but appear only on Push 2.
		- ![Choosing a colour for a drum pad](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2PadColor.png?auto=&w=320)
