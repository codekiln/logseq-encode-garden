- # Preset Category
	- Owning type: [[Logseq/Entity/Preset/Synth/Microfreak]].
	- The category reported in the saved preset header, represented by one of the value pages below. The category labels the sound for browsing; the oscillator engine has its own property.
	- [Elektroid’s category decoder](https://github.com/dagargo/elektroid/blob/6f3d50e2588f0236afb3510e1c55bbb292446aa2/src/connectors/microfreak.c#L174-L217) maps the raw category byte to these names. Unknown codes must be reported before importing; their meaning cannot be inferred.
