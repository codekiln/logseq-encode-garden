- # USB-C port
	- Powers Launchpad Pro and connects it to a computer. With the included wall adapter on this port, Launchpad Pro runs without a computer.
	- ## Remote control
		- A computer sees three USB MIDI interfaces.
			- `LPProMK3 MIDI In/Out`: Note, Chord, Custom and Sequencer modes, and pad lighting in Custom and Programmer modes.
			- `LPProMK3 DIN In/Out`: the MIDI jacks on the rear panel.
			- `LPProMK3 DAW In/Out`: Session mode, driven by a DAW.
