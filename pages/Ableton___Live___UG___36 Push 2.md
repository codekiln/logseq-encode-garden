- # [36. Using Push 2](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/)
	- Playing, sequencing, sampling, mixing and controlling Live with the Push 2 controller.
	- Push 2 is a hardware instrument for building songs in Live: playing melodies, harmonies and beats, working with samples and sounds, and shaping song structure. In the studio it fills Session View with clips without touching the computer; on stage it plays, step-sequences and launches clips.
		- ![Overview of Push 2's controls](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2Overview.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Much of its behaviour depends on the current mode and the selected track type. The chapter walks through the main workflows, then lists every control. Ableton's videos are at https://www.ableton.com/learn-push/
