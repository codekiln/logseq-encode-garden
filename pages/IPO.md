- # IPO
	- **Initial public offering** — a company's first sale of shares to the public, usually as it begins trading on a public market.
	- An IPO can raise capital for the company and give existing shareholders a route to sell shares, subject to the offering and lockup terms. It does not mean every employee can immediately sell all equity.
	- [Investor.gov: Initial Public Offering](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/initial-public-offering-ipo)
