- # [31.10.2 Multisampling](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#multisampling)
	- Multisampling records an instrument at many pitches and dynamics instead of transposing one sample, so the timbre changes the way the real instrument's does. A piano, for example, might have three or more samples per key for soft to very loud playing.
	- Sampler works with multisample presets, imported third-party multisamples, your own multisamples, or a single sample run through its modulation.
