- # [Whoz](https://www.whoz.com/en/)
	- Whoz, pronounced "whooze," sells a workforce optimization platform to IT services, consulting, and engineering firms. It maps what each person in a services firm can do, matches them to open projects, and schedules the work. The company headlines it as "AI-Driven Workforce Optimization. *Built to Scale.*"
	- ## One AI agent staffs the whole organization
		- Whoz gives a customer firm a single managing agent that assigns every worker in it.
		- The [Whoz home page](https://www.whoz.com/en/), read on [[2026-09-22 Tue]], sells the platform on "agent-led orchestration": "Leverage AI and agent-led orchestration to map skills, match talent to projects, and optimize scheduling for complex, large-scale operations." It addresses the firm as the owner of one agent — "Your AI agent delivers instant, accurate talent matching for every opportunity."
	- ## What Whoz sells
		- [Talent Cloud](https://www.whoz.com/en/product/talent-cloud/) — a map of the workforce's skills, meant to surface people a staffing manager would otherwise miss.
		- [Business & Staffing Studio](https://www.whoz.com/en/product/business-staffing-studio/) — where a staffer assigns people to projects and balances their load.
		- [Performance Analytics](https://www.whoz.com/en/product/performance-analytics/) — reporting on billable rates and utilization.
		- [Whoz AImplify](https://www.whoz.com/en/product/whoz-aimplify/) — the AI layer: skill matching, generated profiles, suggested candidates.
		- [Integrations & Workflows](https://www.whoz.com/en/product/integrations-workflows/) — connections to the systems a firm already runs.
		- [Privacy by Design](https://www.whoz.com/en/product/privacy-by-design/) — how Whoz handles the employee data the platform holds.
	- ## The company
		- Twenty-one people founded Whoz in 2016. Its [Why Whoz](https://www.whoz.com/en/why-whoz/) page tells the story as consultants who got tired of staffing spreadsheets: "What if we developed it ourselves? Pretty soon, there was no looking back. Whoz becomes the largest start-up in history, with twenty-one founders!"
		- The same page says the team "work[s] from cities all across France," and claims "500 k+ users worldwide" and "30 millions euros raised in Series A."
