- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Zoom in or out in time around the selection** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `+` / `-`
		- Description: In the MIDI Note Editor and the Arrangement View, zooms the time ruler around the selection, which the zooming list calls Zoom In Time Ruler and Zoom Out Time Ruler; `+` is the `=` key, with no `Shift` needed. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/10 Editing MIDI/02 Zooming and Navigating in the MIDI Note Editor]], [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/12 Clip View MIDI Note Editor]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/09 Zooming Display and Selections]].
