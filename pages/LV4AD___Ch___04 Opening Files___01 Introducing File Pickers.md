	- ### What is the fastest [[Keyshort]] to close [[LazyVim]]? [[card]]
		- `<leader> qq`
		- it's fastest because we don't have to hit shift to get `:`, also the space leader is easier than `:`
	- ### 1. What are two [[Keyshort]]s for activating the [[Fuzzy/Matching]] [[LazyVim/Picker/File]] explorer, 2. what is the command called? [[card]]
		- 1. Keyshort 1: `<leader> Space` aka `Space` twice aka `Space Space`, 
		  Keyshort 2: `<leader>ff`, which opens the same thing. `<leader>f` is the "file/find" menu.
		- 2. `Find Files (Root Directory)`
	- ### 1. What's the default case sensitivity for the [[Fuzzy/Matching]] [[LazyVim/Picker/File]], 2. what [[Keyshort]] lets you toggle it, and 3. what is its case sensitivity "called?" [[card]]
		- the default is case insensitive
		  logseq.order-list-type:: number
