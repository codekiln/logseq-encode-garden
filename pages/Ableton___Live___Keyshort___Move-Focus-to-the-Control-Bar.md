- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Move keyboard focus to the Control Bar** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Option+0` (Win `Alt+0`)
		- Description: Puts keyboard focus on the Control Bar, from anywhere in Live; `Tab` then steps through its groups, and focus returns to the control used last.
