- # Signal Level
	- The rough voltage range an audio output delivers, which sets the kind of input it needs. An input built for one level clips or sounds too quiet with another.
	- **Mic level** — tens of dB below line level, needing a [[Audio/Mic Preamp]].
	- **Instrument level** — a passive guitar or bass pickup, below line level and from a high-impedance source, needing a [[Audio/Hi-Z]] input or a [[Audio/DI Box]].
	- **Line level** — +4 dBu for pro gear, −10 dBV for consumer gear ([[Audio/dBu]]). Synths, interfaces and mixer outputs work at line level.
	- **Speaker level** — the output of a power amplifier, many volts, for passive speakers only.
