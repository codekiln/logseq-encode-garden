- # [Franz Liszt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Liszt)
	- ## About
		- Hungarian composer and virtuoso pianist of the Romantic period ([[18/1/1]]–[[18/8/6]]).
		- His Hungarian Rhapsodies for piano were influenced by the Romani bands he heard while visiting Hungary.
		- Wrote *Des Bohémiens et de leur musique en Hongrie*, about the Romani and their influence on Hungarian folk music.
