- # Band
	- In this garden, **Band** pages model music identities shared by musicians working as a named band.
	- ## Inherited rules
		- Attribution and release placement follow [[Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music]], which inherits [[Logseq/Entity/Identity]]. A Band instance carries only the Band entity from this lineage.
	- ## Membership
		- A band's identity and release namespace persist through changes of membership. Albums belong under the band, rather than under an individual member.
		- Known people can be linked through [[Logseq/Entity/Identity/Frontmatter/identity-of]]. Membership periods, instruments and roles belong in dated body blocks when known; an undated person link makes no claim about current membership.
		- A recording lists individual participants only when their participation on that recording is known. Neither a current nor a historical membership list establishes a recording credit.
		- A one-off joint credit is not automatically a band. A named project receives a Band page when it has a band identity of its own.
	- ## Examples
		- [[Jane's Addiction]]
		- [[Smashing Pumpkins]]
