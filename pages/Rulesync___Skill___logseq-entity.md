# [codekiln/logseq-encode-garden/.rulesync/skills/logseq-entity](https://github.com/codekiln/logseq-encode-garden/tree/main/.rulesync/skills/logseq-entity)
	- This [[Agent/Skills]] skill is the "linux kernel" of how I use AI with Logseq. The basic idea is that it points agents to look at the [[Logseq/Entity]] namespace for how to create and maintain the types of things I want to put in the garden.
	- This skill is inherited by all my Logseq knowledge gardens, and it's even got a descendent in Obsidian that I use.
	- My goal is to keep this skill tight and free from domain-specific info, so that pages in the garden are the sole source of information about how gardening is done in the garden. This is still a work in progress, though. If I do my job right, this skill will get smaller and smaller over time, and the variety of things I track in [[Logseq/Entity]] namespace will increase.
	- The reason this is important is that different gardens track different things, and also I use different [[AI Agent Harnesses]]. This one skill's job is to ensure all my Logseq gardens organize their information *according to certain documented principles* without having to redo AI skills again and again, particularly since Skills are not displayed in the garden, and information that's garden-specific should be stored in the garden.
