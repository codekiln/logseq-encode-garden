- # Touch strip
	- A touch strip that sets Spice, Dice or Bend, whichever icon is selected.
	- ![Touch strip](../assets/.remote/logseq-encode-garden/Microfreak___UI___Front___Row___04-Icon-Strip___Strip___Touch___Asset___Panel.png)
	- ## Values
		- Left end is the least and right end the most for Spice and Dice; for Bend the center is no bend.
