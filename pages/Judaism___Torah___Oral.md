- # [Oral Torah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oral_Torah)
	- The interpretive tradition that [[Judaism/Rabbinic]] holds was revealed alongside the [[Judaism/Torah/Written]] and is needed to understand and practice it.
	- The [[Judaism/Mishnah]] wrote down part of it around 200 CE, and the [[Judaism/Talmud]] records the arguments over that writing. Because interpretation is built into the transmission of revelation, the chain of interpretation itself carries religious authority.
