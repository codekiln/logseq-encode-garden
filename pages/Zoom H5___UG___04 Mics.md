- # Mic overview
	- The XY mic that comes with the H5 can be swapped for another input capsule to suit the recording.
	- ## XY mic
		- Two directional mics cross at 90°, one aimed left and one aimed right.
			- ![01 XY mic pickup](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/Zoom%20H5___UG___04%20Mics___Asset___01-XY-mic-pickup.png)
		- A shock mount reduces vibration noise from handling.
		- The mic handles sound pressure up to 140 dB SPL.
		- It records stereo with natural depth and width.
		- > [[Note/Info]] The XY mic has a **MIC/LINE** input jack for an external mic or line-level device. The jack can supply [[Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/04 Plug-in Power]] to mics that use it.
	- ## Connecting mics
		- Press the buttons on both sides of the mic, then push it onto the main unit until it is fully seated.
			- ![02 Connecting a mic](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/Zoom%20H5___UG___04%20Mics___Asset___02-Connecting-a-mic.png)
	- ## Disconnecting mics
		- Press the buttons on both sides of the mic and pull it off the main unit.
			- ![03 Disconnecting a mic](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/Zoom%20H5___UG___04%20Mics___Asset___03-Disconnecting-a-mic.png)
		- > [[Note/Warning]] Pulling too hard can damage the mic or the main unit.
		- > [[Note/Info]] Removing a mic during recording stops the recording.
