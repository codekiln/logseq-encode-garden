- # [3.3 The Browser](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-concepts/#the-browser)
	- The [[Ableton/Browser]] is where you reach the Core Library, installed Packs, your saved presets and samples, built-in and third-party devices, Sets in Ableton Cloud, files on Push, and folders you add.
	- Show or hide it with the Control Bar toggle or `Cmd+Option+5` (Mac) / `Ctrl+Alt+5` (Win).
