- # Input 2 jack
	- An XLR/TRS combo jack for a mic, a keyboard, a mixer or another line-level device, recorded on track 2.
	- The XLR socket takes dynamic and condenser mics: pin 1 ground, pin 2 hot, pin 3 cold.
	- The TRS socket takes a [[Audio/Connector/Phone/TRS/6.35mm]] plug from a line source: tip hot, ring cold, sleeve ground.
	- [[Zoom H5/UI/Area/2-Front/2-Input-Volume-2-Knob]] sets its level, and [[Zoom H5/UI/Area/2-Front/3-Track/4-2-Button]] selects its track.
	- ## Values
		- Input gain of −∞ to 55 dB with PAD off, and −∞ to 35 dB with PAD on.
		- Maximum input level of +22 dBu with PAD on.
	- ## Settings
		- Phantom power at +12, +24 or +48 V, set separately for each input: [[Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/03 Phantom Power]]
		- PAD, for a source with a +4 dB output level: [[Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/05 PAD]]
