- # [33.1.1 DS Clang](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/max-for-live-devices/#ds-clang)
	- ![The DS Clang Instrument](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MaxDSClangL12.png?auto=&w=314)
	- Cowbell, clave and noise percussion from two tones, white noise and a filter.
	- **Tone A** and **Tone B** — level of each cowbell tone
	- **Filter** — high-pass and band-pass cutoff; higher is brighter
	- **Noise** — amount of white noise
	- **Clave** — switches to clave; **Repeat** then adds repeats
	- **Pitch**, **Decay** (length) and **Volume**
