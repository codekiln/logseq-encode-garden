- # Hi-Z
	- A high-impedance input, around 1 MΩ, built for passive guitar and bass pickups. Labels also read INST or GUITAR.
	- A passive pickup sounds dull and quiet on a normal line input, whose 10–20 kΩ loads it down. The high impedance leaves the pickup's treble intact.
	- Without a Hi-Z input, a [[Audio/DI Box]] does the same job in front of a mic input. See [[Audio/Signal Level]].
