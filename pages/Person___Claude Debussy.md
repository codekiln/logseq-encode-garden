- # [Claude Debussy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Debussy)
	- ## About
		- French composer ([[18/6/2]]–[[1918]]).
		- Heard Javanese gamelan at the [[18/8/9]] Exposition Universelle in Paris. "Pagodes", in *Estampes* ([[1903]]), uses pentatonic scales.
		- "Voiles", in Book I of his *Préludes* ([[1909]]–[[1910]]), stays almost entirely within one whole-tone scale.
