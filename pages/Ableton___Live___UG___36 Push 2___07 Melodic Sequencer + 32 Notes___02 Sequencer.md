- # [36.7.2 Sequencer](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#sequencer)
	- Tap a step in the top half to add all selected notes there. Hold a step to see its notes highlighted below; tap one to remove it. Holding several steps adds notes to all of them; hold **Duplicate** to copy one step to another.
	- Step colours: clip colour has notes; green playing; white selected; light grey muted note; grey empty; unlit unavailable with triplet steps.
	- Hold Layout for the loop length pads in the fifth row; `Shift`+Layout locks them (per track); Page Left/Right move between pages. Duplicate and Delete with a loop length pad copy or clear a page.
