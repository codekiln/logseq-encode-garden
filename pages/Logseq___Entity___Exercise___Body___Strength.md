- # Strength Exercise
	- In this garden, **Strength Exercise** pages model body exercises that develop the ability to produce or sustain muscular force against resistance.
	- Extends [[Logseq/Entity/Exercise/Body]] and inherits its page shape.
	- Setup names the resistance and target movement or muscle group. Practice defines the lifting and lowering phases, or the position maintained in an isometric hold.
	- Dose and progression describes adjustable load, sets, repetitions or hold duration, rest, and the criterion for ending a set. Exercise technique remains separate from a particular training program's prescription.
	- Instances use `Exercise/Body/Strength/<Name>`.
