- # Anker USB-C Data Hub (4-in-1, 5Gbps)
	- Turns one USB-C port into four USB-A ports. Anker's model number is A8309.
	- ## Input
		- A [[USB/Connector/Ref/USB-C]] plug, into a USB-C laptop or tablet running macOS 12 or later, Windows 10 or later, or ChromeOS. The hub has no power input of its own and runs on the power of the port it plugs into.
	- ## Output
		- Four [[USB/Connector/Ref/USB-A]] USB 3.0 ports, up to 5 Gbps.
		- Anker rates the four ports for a combined 900 mA.
