- # [ChatGPT Pages](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001549-getting-started-with-space-in-chatgpt)
	- An editable document inside ChatGPT that people and ChatGPT write together. OpenAI announced Pages at DevDay 2026 as "a new type of document, built for human and agent collaboration." [^1]
	- Pages live in ChatGPT Space, a home for pages, uploaded files, and folders. Space replaces the ChatGPT Library for accounts that have it; [[ChatGPT/Project]]s stay separate and keep their chats, files, and instructions. [^2]
	- ## What a page holds
		- Text written by hand or by ChatGPT, started from a conversation, from a blank page, or from a template on Space home. [^2]
		- Charts, trackers, interactive tools, images, and research that ChatGPT adds on request. [^1] [^2]
		- Subpages. A page nests under a parent page, the way a [[Confluence]] page tree does, and subpages inherit the parent's access. [^2]
		- Uploaded files, which follow the page's permissions. Linked files keep the permissions of the service they live in. [^2]
		- The starter page in Space, "Your guide to pages," teaches the editor: `/` inserts headings, images, checklists, and tables; `@ChatGPT` asks ChatGPT for help in place; `/page` creates a subpage; `/visualize` builds an interactive visualization.
	- ## Collaboration
		- A page or a whole space can be shared with view or edit access. Editors change the page together in real time. [^2]
		- A comment on a selected passage asks for a change to that passage. The page's own chat handles questions and changes across the whole document. [^2]
		- Each collaborator works with their own ChatGPT. Sharing a page keeps private chats and ChatGPT Memory private, while everything written onto the page is visible to everyone who can view it. [^2]
		- Each collaborator's training and Memory settings govern what their ChatGPT does with shared content, so one person turning off training leaves a collaborator's ChatGPT free to train on the same page. [^2]
		- Deleting a page moves it and all its subpages to Trash, including subpages other people own. [^2]
	- ## Availability at launch
		- Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans. Editing works on the web and in the [[ChatGPT/App/Desktop]]; mobile finds, reads, and shares pages. [^1] [^2]
		- Collaborative Slides and Sheets are announced for the coming weeks. [^1] [^2]
	- ## How it compares
		- TechCrunch reads Pages and Space together as ChatGPT's own office suite, with Pages competing against Google Docs and Microsoft Word. [^3]
		- The Decoder sets Pages against Google Docs and [[Notion]]. [^4]
		- A page with `@ChatGPT` in it works like a [[Slack]] canvas: a shared document living next to the team's AI conversations.
