- # Ableton Group Track
	- In this garden, **Ableton Group Track** pages model one group track in an [[Logseq/Entity/Ableton/Set]].
	- A group page sits at `<performance>/Set/Track/<group name>`. It records the group's own settings and embeds each of its tracks, [[Logseq/Entity/Ableton/Track/Track]] pages, in Live's order.
	- The Set page embeds the group. The shared names and page shape live on [[Logseq/Entity/Ableton/Set]].
