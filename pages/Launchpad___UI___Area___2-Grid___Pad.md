- # Pad
	- The grid's 64 pads sense velocity and pressure and light in color. What a pad does depends on the mode.
	- [[Launchpad/UI/Mode/1-Session]]: launches the clip in its slot of Ableton Live's Session View. In the Volume, Pan, Sends and Device views, a row or column of pads is a fader.
	- [[Launchpad/UI/Mode/2-Note]]: plays a note in the Chromatic or Scale layout, or a Drum Rack slot when the armed Live track holds a Drum Rack.
	- [[Launchpad/UI/Mode/3-Chord]]: plays a scale note in the Note Area, a triad in the orange column, or a stored chord in the white Chord Bank.
	- [[Launchpad/UI/Mode/4-Custom]]: sends the MIDI note, control change or Program Change that the Custom Mode assigns to it. A whole row or column can be a fader.
	- [[Launchpad/UI/Mode/5-Sequencer]]: in Steps View, a pad in the top half is one of the Pattern's 32 steps, and a pad in the bottom half plays a note in the Play Area.
	- ## Remote control
		- Programmer mode: notes 11–88. A pad sends note 10 × row + column, with rows and columns counted 1 to 8 from the bottom left, so the bottom row sends 11–18 and the top row 81–88.
