- # Clip
	- In [[Ableton]]'s software and instruments, a **clip** is one piece of musical material on a track, such as a melody, a drum pattern, a bassline or a whole song. [[Ableton/Live]], [[Ableton/Note]], Move and Push all play clips.
	- ## In [[Ableton/Live]]
		- A clip comes in two kinds, and each plays only on a track of its own kind:
			- [[Ableton/Clip/Audio]] — plays part of a sample on an [audio track]([[Ableton/Track/Audio]]).
			- [[Ableton/Clip/MIDI]] — holds notes and controller envelopes and plays on a [MIDI track]([[Ableton/Track/MIDI]]).
		- A clip sits in a slot of the [Session View]([[Ableton/Live/UG/07 Session View]]), where its own launch button starts it, or on the timeline of the [Arrangement View]([[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View]]). A track plays one clip at a time ([[Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/06 Arrangement and Session]], [[Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/07 Tracks]]).
		- The [Clip View]([[Ableton/Live/UG/08 Clip View]]) edits one clip's properties, such as its start and end, looping and launch settings. In the Session View, a clip's [Follow Actions]([[Ableton/Live/Action/Follow]]) decide which clip plays after it.
		- A Live Clip, an [[Ableton/File/.alc]] file, saves one clip for reuse ([[Ableton/Live/UG/05 Managing Files and Sets/03 Live Clips]]).
