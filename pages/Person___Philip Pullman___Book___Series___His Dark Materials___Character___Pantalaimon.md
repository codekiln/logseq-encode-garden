- # [Pantalaimon](https://truth-secrets-and-prophecies.fandom.com/wiki/Pantalaimon)
	- ## About
		- Dæmon of [[Person/Philip Pullman/Book/Series/His Dark Materials/Character/Lyra Belacqua]] in [[Person/Philip Pullman/Book/Series/His Dark Materials]]; also called Pan. An example of a [[Person/Philip Pullman/Book/Series/His Dark Materials/Concept/Daemon]]. Named from Greek *Panteleimon*, "all-compassionate." He first appears as a dark brown moth; favored forms include a snow-white ermine, a wildcat, and a mouse; he settles as a red-gold pine marten with a cream-white throat.
