- # [Yatsuhashi Kengyō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yatsuhashi_Kengyo)
	- ## About
		- Japanese koto player and composer ([[16/1/4]]–[[16/8/5]]) from Kyoto, known as the "Father of Modern Koto".
		- Adapted the hirajōshi and insen scales from shamisen music as koto tunings, and is credited with the solo koto piece *Rokudan no shirabe*.
