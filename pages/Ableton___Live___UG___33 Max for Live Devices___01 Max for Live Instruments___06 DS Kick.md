- # [33.1.6 DS Kick](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/max-for-live-devices/#ds-kick)
	- ![The DS Kick Instrument](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MaxDSKickL12.png?auto=&w=314)
	- Kick drum from a modulated sine wave.
	- **Pitch** — tuning in Hz
	- **Drive** — distortion; **OT** — added harmonics
	- **Attack** — smooths the start of the sine
	- **Click** — adds a click for a sharper attack
	- **Env** — pitch modulation amount
	- **Decay** (length) and **Volume**
