- # [Answers](https://warp.net/answers)
	- Debut collaborative album by Bibio and Dorian Concept. Released on 16 October 2026; [Bandcamp release and credits](https://bibio.bandcamp.com/album/answers).
	- ## Collaboration
		- Built through experimentation and improvisation, combining traditional instruments with layered electronic textures. Warp describes the album as looking to the past to develop new music.
		- The collaboration follows Bibio’s appearance on An Unopened Letter, the closing track of [[Person/Oliver Thomas Johnson/Music/Recording/Miniatures]].
		- The release page includes official videos for Womb Glow, Hammock Portal, Lichen Trip and Shame.
