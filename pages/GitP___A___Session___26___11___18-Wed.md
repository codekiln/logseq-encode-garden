- # A Tiny Descant
	- A line of Qbit Brass goes into the Launchpad and comes back as a second voice. A tiny descant slips over it. Then the commentary mic goes quiet and the MicroFreak carries on.
	- ![Episode recording](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/GitP___A___Session___26___11___18-Wed___Asset___Audio___Full.mp3)
	- ## Ceremonial Annotations
		- Listen for the second voice at 00:33. It began as a pattern on track 2 of the [[Launchpad/Sequencer]], then went into Live as audio. The commentary calls out live recording into the sequencer, panning and reverb as the layers take shape.
