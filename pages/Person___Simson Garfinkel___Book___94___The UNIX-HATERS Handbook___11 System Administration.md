- # 11 System Administration — Unix's Hidden Cost
	- Summary:: Covers the labor of keeping Unix running: tuning systems, partitioning disks, making backups, maintaining configuration files, and operating mail services.
	- Why it is included:: Makes visible the human and organizational cost that Unix's low purchase price and apparent flexibility conceal.
