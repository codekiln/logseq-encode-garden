	- **Replace the sample with the next most similar one** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+Right` (Win `Ctrl+Right`)
		- Description: In a Drum Rack pad, Simpler or Drum Sampler, loads the sample that sounds next most like the reference. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/04 Sound Similarity]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/23 Similar Sample Swapping]].
