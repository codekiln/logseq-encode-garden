- # Session MIDI
	- [Download the session MIDI](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/GitP___A___Session___26___11___23-Mon___Asset___MIDI___Session.mid)
	- Standard MIDI file with a tempo track at 58.39 BPM and two Arrangement tracks. The first carries the notes recorded on the third [[Launchpad/Sequencer]] track, on MIDI channel 3. The second carries the MicroFreak's own output on channel 1: notes played on its keyboard and its knob movements.
	- Clip positions and trims follow the edited Ableton Live arrangement, so the file lines up with the episode recording from its first beat. The other sequencer tracks recorded no notes in the Arrangement.
