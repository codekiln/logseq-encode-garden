- # Left button
	- In Session mode, scrolls the 8 × 8 clip window left through the Live Set; in Session Overview, it steps between 8 × 8 blocks of clips.
	- In Note mode, transposes the grid by a semitone; in Drum mode, it scrolls the Drum Rack by four slots.
	- In Sequencer mode, on a Drum track, shifts the Play Area's drum layout by one row of four notes.
	- ## Remote control
		- Programmer mode: CC 91
