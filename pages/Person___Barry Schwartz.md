- # Barry Schwartz
	- American psychologist, emeritus professor of psychology at Swarthmore College.[^1]
	- First author of the 2002 paper "Maximizing versus satisficing: happiness is a matter of choice," which measured maximizing as a trait and linked it to lower happiness and more regret: [[Satis/fice]].[^2]
	- Wrote [[Barry Schwartz/Book/2004/The Paradox of Choice: Why More Is Less]], which argues that an excess of choice harms well-being.[^1]
