- # [32.5 Max Dependencies](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/max-for-live/#max-dependencies)
	- A Max device can depend on other files: samples, images, other Max patches. **Freezing** a Max device bundles all of those files into the device.
	- Freezing a Max device has nothing to do with Live's Freeze Track command.
	- The Max documentation covers freezing and dependency management in more detail.
