- # Oscillator Type
	- Owning type: [[Logseq/Entity/Preset/Synth/Microfreak]].
	- A verified oscillator model page under [[Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc/03 Types]]. The linked guide section defines the engine and the meanings of its Wave, Timbre and Shape controls.
	- This field is omitted until a saved value has been verified. The saved-header inventory supplies no oscillator type; a category such as Keys cannot determine the engine.
	- A manual observation must identify the saved preset being observed. The sync preserves this field but a change to other sound parameters requires a fresh verification.
