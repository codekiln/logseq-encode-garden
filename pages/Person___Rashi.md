- # [Rashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashi)
	- ## About
		- Shlomo Yitzchaki (1040 to 1105), rabbi of Troyes in northern France, whose commentaries on the Hebrew Bible and the [[Judaism/Talmud]] became the standard companions to both. His Talmud commentary runs down the inner margin of the traditional printed page, and later commentators wrote on his commentary in turn.
