- # What is the keyshort to see the error log after a notification message disappears?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- [[Answer/Official]] from [LazyVim Keymaps](https://www.lazyvim.org/keymaps)
		- Short answer: `<leader>n` — [[nvim/Plugin/snacks.nvim/Notifier]]'s "Notification History" — reopens every notification that has flashed by, including error messages, so nothing is lost once the popup times out.
		- `<leader>L` is a different keymap: "LazyVim Changelog", which is why it surfaces plugin breaking-change notes instead of the notification you just saw.
