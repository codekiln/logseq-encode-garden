- # Record button
	- In Session mode, Record overdubs the playing clip on an armed track. While a clip records, press Record to end the recording; the clip flashes red until it stops.
	- In Sequencer mode, press Record to turn on live recording; the button lights red. Notes played in the Play Area, and MIDI arriving over USB or MIDI In, are recorded while the sequencer plays.
