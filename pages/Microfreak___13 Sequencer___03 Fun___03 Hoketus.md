- # 13.3.3. Second Experiment: Hoketus
	- Hoketus keeps one pitch while changing timbre, articulation, and rhythm. On the MicroFreak, use modulation tracks to change parameters while the sequence repeats that pitch.
	- Create a 16-step sequence with the same pitch at every step. Add rests and ties for rhythm, then press Record while playback is stopped. Turn a control to capture its position, use Rate/Swing to move to the next step, and repeat. Up to four controls can change across the pattern.
	- **Variation:** To imitate a monochord, use one or two modulation tracks to introduce nearby pitches around the repeated central pitch.
