- # LINE OUT jack
	- A [[Audio/Connector/Phone/TRS/3.5mm]] stereo jack that sends the H5's output at line level to the input of another device, such as a camera, an audio interface or a PA.
	- Earlier pages link this jack as [[Zoom H5/Port/Line Out]].
	- ## Values
		- Rated output level of −10 dBm into a load of 10 kΩ or more.
		- `MENU > IN/OUT > LINE OUT LEVEL` lowers it by up to 30 dB, without changing the headphone level.
