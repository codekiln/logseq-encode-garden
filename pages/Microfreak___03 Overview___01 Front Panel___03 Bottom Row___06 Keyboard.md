- # 03.01.03.06 Keyboard section
	- The 25-key capacitive keyboard generates gate, pitch, and pressure. The octave buttons extend its two-octave range. The section also includes the Icon strip and the Bend, Spice, and Dice touch controls.
	- > [[Note/Info]] Utility can make the keyboard generate velocity in place of pressure.
	- The Keyboard
		- ![01 The Keyboard](../assets/Microfreak___03-Overview___01-Front-Panel___03-Bottom-Row___06-Keyboard___01-The-Keyboard.png)
	- Utility or MIDI Control Center settings determine whether the keyboard provides aftertouch or velocity. USB and MIDI out can send fully polyphonic MIDI control to other devices.
