- # USB-C to MagSafe 3 Cable (2 m)
	- Carries charging power from a USB-C power adapter to the MagSafe 3 port of a MacBook Pro.
	- ## Ends
		- [[USB/Connector/Ref/USB-C]] plug, into the power adapter
		- MagSafe 3 magnetic plug, into the Mac
	- ## Length
		- 2 m
	- ## Carries
		- Charging power from the adapter to the Mac
	- ## Works with
		- [[Apple/Adapter/140W USB-C Power]]. The cable and that adapter ship together with the MacBook Pro (16-inch, 2023).
	- ## Source
		- [MacBook Pro (16-inch, 2023) - Tech Specs](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111838)
