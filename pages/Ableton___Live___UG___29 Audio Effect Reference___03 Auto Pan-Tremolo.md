- # [29.3 Auto Pan-Tremolo](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-audio-effect-reference/#auto-pan-tremolo)

	- ![The Effect Can Be Used in Panning Mode or Tremolo Mode](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/AutoPanTremoloPanningTremoloDisplayL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- ![The Harmonic and Vintage Tremolo Mode Toggles](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/AutoPanTremoloHarmonicVintageL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- ![Use the Controls Below the Display to Adjust the LFO Modulation](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/AutoPanTremoloLFOControlsL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- ![Use the Shape Control to Create Ramps for Gating and Ducking Effects](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/AutoPanTremoloRampsL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Auto Pan-Tremolo uses LFOs to move a signal across the stereo field or vary its level: auto-panning, tremolo and gating.
	- Modes:
		- **Panning** — two LFOs shift level between left and right
		- **Tremolo** — one LFO varies level
		- The display shows the wave and, below it, the live pan position or level.
	- **Waveform** — Sine, Triangle, Shark Tooth, Saw Up, Saw Down, Square, Random, Wander, S&H
	- Time modes — **Rate** (Hz), **Time** (100 ms to 200 s, for long cycles), **Synced**, **Dotted**, **Triplet**, **16th**
	- Stereo offset:
		- **Phase** — offsets the left and right waves in degrees; 180° is fully opposite
		- **Spin** — runs the two channels at rates that differ by a percentage
		- Random, Wander and S&H allow Phase only.
	- **Phase Offset**, in the beat-based time modes, moves each LFO's start against the beat grid. With Random, Wander or S&H it widens the stereo spread instead.
	- **Invert** flips the wave. With ramp waves, inverted gives ducking and normal gives gating.
	- Tremolo-only options:
		- **Harmonic** — splits the signal at a fixed 600 Hz and modulates highs and lows in alternation
		- **Vintage** — adds a non-linear curve for a warmer, grittier tremolo
	- Controls below the display:
		- **Amount** — modulation depth
		- the rate control, labeled Freq, Time or Rate depending on time mode. Amount and rate are shared by both modes.
		- **Shape** — in Panning, pushes the waves toward their limits for harder switches between channels; in Tremolo, rounds or sharpens the peaks. At the extremes it makes ramps for gating (Invert off) or pumping (Invert on).
		- **Attack Time** — how long after a transient the modulation reaches full depth, so attacks stay centered or keep their punch
		- **Dynamic Frequency Modulation** — louder input speeds up the LFO (positive) or slows it (negative)
