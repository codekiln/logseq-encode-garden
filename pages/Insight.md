- # Insight
	- Insight is the recognition that comes when a relation, character, or possibility becomes clear.
	- In a [[GitP/Light]], a sound first appears through [[unconcealment]]; listening may then bring insight into what the [[Microfreak/Preset]] can express.
	- [[aletheia]] names the event of truth as unconcealment in Heidegger's thought. Insight is related to that event, while naming the listener's recognition.
