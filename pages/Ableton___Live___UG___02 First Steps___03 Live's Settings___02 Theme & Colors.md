- # [2.3.2 Theme & Colors](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/first-steps/#theme--colors)
	- Theme: color scheme; light, dark, or follow the operating system; warm, cool or neutral tone; high contrast.
	- Customization: grid line opacity, brightness, and the color intensity and hue of the interface.
	- Track and Clip Colors: automatic track colors on or off, a default track color, Reduced Automatic Colors, and whether new clips get random colors or the track's color.
