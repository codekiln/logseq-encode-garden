- ### [`pane-active-border-style`](https://man.openbsd.org/tmux#pane-active-border-style)
	- Sets the foreground and background style of the [[tmux/Pane/Active]] border. Text attributes such as `bold` are ignored.
	- Example: `tmux set-option -g pane-active-border-style fg=colour46`
