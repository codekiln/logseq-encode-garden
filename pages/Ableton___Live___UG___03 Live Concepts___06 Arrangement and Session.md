- # [3.6 Arrangement and Session](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-concepts/#arrangement-and-session)
	- Clips are Live's building blocks: a melody, a drum pattern, a bassline, or a whole song.
	- The Arrangement lays clips out on a linear timeline; the Session is a launching base where each clip has its own play button and can start at any time in any order. See [[Ableton/Live/Mode/Arrangement]] and [[Ableton/Live/Mode/Session]].
	- ![Clips in the Arrangement View (Left) and in the Session View (Right)](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/ClipsInSessionAndArrangementL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Switch views with `Tab` or the view controls at the top right. With "Use Tab Key to Navigate" on, use `Option+1` / `Option+2` (Mac) or `Alt+1` / `Alt+2` (Win), or the Navigate menu. With two windows, `Tab` swaps the views between windows.
	- Each view holds its own clips. Switching views changes only the display, not what is playing.
	- The views connect through tracks, so you can improvise with Session clips and record the result into the Arrangement.
