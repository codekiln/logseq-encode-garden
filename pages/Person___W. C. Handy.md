- # [W. C. Handy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._C._Handy)
	- ## About
		- American songwriter, cornetist and bandleader who called himself the Father of the Blues ([[18/7/3]]–[[1958]]).
		- One of the first to publish music in the blues form, with "The Memphis Blues" ([[1912]]) and "St. Louis Blues" ([[1914]]).
