- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Slide the audio or notes inside an Arrangement clip without moving the clip** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Shift+Option`-drag (Win `Shift+Alt`-drag)
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, dragging a warped audio clip's or MIDI clip's display with these modifiers moves its contents while its edges stay put; add `Cmd` to ignore the grid. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/07 Moving and Resizing Clips]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
