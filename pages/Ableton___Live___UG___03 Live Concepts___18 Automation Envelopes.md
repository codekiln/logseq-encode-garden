- # [3.18 Automation Envelopes](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-concepts/#automation-envelopes)
	- Automation is a control's change over time, stored as a breakpoint envelope you can draw, edit or record. Almost every mixer and device control can be automated, tempo included.
	- With Automation Arm and Arrangement Record on, control changes are recorded into the Arrangement. With Automation Arm on while Session recording, they go into the clip.
	- Moving an automated control while not recording overrides its automation until you press Re-Enable Automation or launch a Session clip that carries automation.
