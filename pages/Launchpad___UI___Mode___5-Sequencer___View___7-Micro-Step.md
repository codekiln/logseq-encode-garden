- # Micro Step editing view
	- The [seventh button down the right-hand column]([[Launchpad/UI/Area/3-Right/7-Micro-Step-Button]]) opens Micro Step editing view. Select a step in the top half of the grid; the six leftmost pads of the Play Area's top row are that step's Micro Steps, for finer note placement within the step.
