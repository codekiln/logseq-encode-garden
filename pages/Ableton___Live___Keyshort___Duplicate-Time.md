- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Repeat the selected span of time right after itself across every Arrangement track** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+Shift+D` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+D`)
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, inserts a copy of the selected time after the selection on every track and pushes the rest of the song later. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/11 Using the Time Commands]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
