		- DONE [[Ghost Gardener]] What should we call these ceremonies that happen in the open garden, with ceremonial participants, attendants (e.g. live performances)
		  id:: 6af99352-972f-4517-8ac3-81b530674ef5
			- Call them *gatherings*. A ceremony given before people in a room is a gathering, and the episode recorded there is how [[GitP/Participant]]s take part later.
				- [[GitP/Vibe]] already reaches for the word: its ritual offerings descend from "myth making and ritual gatherings," and the ghost travels from planet to planet "gathering signals." The poem of [[GitP/Ceremon/y/Fallveter]] has its own spelling of it: "athering."
				- Someone at a synth meetup understands it without a glossary.
			- For the people in the room, *attendant* fits a ceremony, but [[GitP/Acolyte]] already attends the rite. *witness* keeps the two apart: witnesses were in the room, and participants take part through the recording.
			- Other names that fit: *circle*, while the gatherings stay small enough to sit around the instruments, and *vigil*, for the ones held at night.
