- # [which-key.nvim](https://github.com/folke/which-key.nvim)
	- Shows a popup of available keybindings as you type a key sequence (e.g. after `<leader>` or `<C-w>`), grouped by prefix. [[LazyVim]] uses it to surface its keymaps.
	- [[nvim/Plugin/which-key.nvim/Hydra Mode]] — keeps a group's popup open across repeated presses instead of closing after one key.
