- # Musical Instrument Digital Interface (MIDI)
	- A standard, first published in 1983, for how electronic instruments, controllers and computers send performance and control data to each other. It sends instructions such as "play this note" or "turn this knob", not sound.
	- [[MIDI/Message]] — the kinds of messages MIDI sends: [[MIDI/Note]], [[MIDI/Aftertouch]], [[MIDI/CC]], [[MIDI/Program-Change]], [[MIDI/Pitch Bend]], [[MIDI/SysEx]] and [[MIDI/Clock]].
	- [[Logseq/Entity/MIDI/Port]] — the named connections a device presents to a computer.
