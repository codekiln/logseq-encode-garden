- # Mic Preamp
	- The amplifier at the front of a mic input that raises the mic's signal to line level, so the rest of the mixer can work with it. Its GAIN knob sets how much.
	- A mic delivers a signal tens of dB below line level ([[Audio/Signal Level]]), so the preamp needs up to 60 dB of gain or more. Its own noise at that gain is measured as [[Audio/EIN]].
	- Too much gain clips the preamp, which a CLIP LED shows. Too little leaves the signal close to the noise floor.
	- Mixer channels with a mic preamp usually also offer [[Audio/Phantom Power]].
