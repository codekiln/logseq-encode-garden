- # [41.5.1 Navigating Between Breakpoints](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/accessibility-and-keyboard-navigation/#navigating-between-breakpoints)
	- Left and right arrows move the insert marker along the envelope, showing the value at each point.
		- ![The Automation Value is Shown at the Insert Marker Location](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/AccessibilityAutomationValueL12.png)
	- `Enter` creates a breakpoint at the insert marker; press `Enter` again to place it, or move it with the arrows first.
	- `Option` plus left or right (Mac) / `Alt` plus left or right (Win) jumps to the previous or next breakpoint. The focused breakpoint takes the same color as a mouse hover.
