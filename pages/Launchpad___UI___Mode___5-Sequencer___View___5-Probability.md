- # Probability view
	- The [fifth button down the right-hand column]([[Launchpad/UI/Area/3-Right/5-Probability-Button]]) opens Probability view. Select a step in the top half of the grid, then press a pad on the eight-pad slider at the top of the Play Area to set the chance that its notes play, from 13% to 100%.
