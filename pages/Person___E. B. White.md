- # E. B. White
	- ## About
		- Essayist and contributing editor at The New Yorker; wrote *Stuart Little*, *Charlotte's Web* and *The Trumpet of the Swan*.
		- Took [[Person/William Strunk Jr]]'s English course at Cornell and revised [[Book/The Elements of Style]] in 1959.
