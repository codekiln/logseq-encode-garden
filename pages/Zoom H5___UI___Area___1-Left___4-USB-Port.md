- # USB port
	- A [[USB/Connector/Ref/USB-B Mini]] port that powers the H5 and connects it to a computer or iPad, as a card reader or as an audio interface.
	- Earlier pages link this port as [[Zoom H5/Port/USB-B Micro]].
	- ## Values
		- Power from the ZOOM AD-17 AC adapter (DC 5 V 1 A) or from USB bus power.
