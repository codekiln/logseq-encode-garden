- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Fit the whole song into the Arrangement View's width** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `W`
		- Description: Optimize Arrangement Width zooms the Arrangement View horizontally until the whole song fits, which makes it easy to select from a track's first clip to its last. `H`, Optimize Arrangement Height, fits every track vertically. Both also have small buttons at the top right of the Arrangement View, next to its overview. See [[Ableton/Arrangement View/Optimize/Width]], [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/02 Navigation and Zooming]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
