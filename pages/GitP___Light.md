- # GitP Light
	- A GitP Light is a moment in a [[Ghost in the Patch]] performance when a sound or musical gesture lets a quality of a [[Microfreak/Preset]] come into view or hearing. It is recorded with the preset and the expression that revealed it.
	- The name joins three ideas: [[aletheia]], [[unconcealment]], and [[insight]]. In Heidegger's writing, aletheia names truth as unconcealment. A GitP Light gives that thought a practical place in listening: something latent in the patch becomes perceptible in a particular moment.
	- ## Register
		- [[GitP/Light/High]] — a quality emerges in the high register.
		- [[GitP/Light/Mid]] — a quality emerges in the middle register.
		- [[GitP/Light/Low]] — a quality emerges in the low register.
	- ## Recording a light
		- Name the active preset, the register and sound that drew attention, and the gesture or setting that brought the sound forward.
		- Link the observation to the relevant [[GitP/A/Session]] so the moment can be heard in context.
