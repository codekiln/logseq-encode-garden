- # 9.13.4 Selecting the Scale
	- Press one of the 16 scale pads. The selected scale lights bright white; the others are dim blue. The choice applies in Note, Chord, and Sequencer modes.
	- Available scales:
		- [Natural Minor]([[Music/Scale/Minor/Natural]]); [Natural Major]([[Music/Scale/Major]]); [Dorian]([[Music/Scale/Dorian]]); [Phrygian]([[Music/Scale/Phrygian]]); [Mixolydian]([[Music/Scale/Mixolydian]]); [Melodic Minor]([[Music/Scale/Minor/Melodic]]); [Harmonic Minor]([[Music/Scale/Minor/Harmonic]]); [Bebop Dorian]([[Music/Scale/Dorian/Bebop]]).
		- [Blues]([[Music/Scale/Blues]]); [Minor Pentatonic]([[Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor]]); [Hungarian Minor]([[Music/Scale/Minor/Hungarian]]); [Ukrainian Dorian]([[Music/Scale/Dorian/Ukrainian]]); [Marva]([[Music/Scale/Marwa]]); [Todi]([[Music/Scale/Todi]]); [Whole Tone]([[Music/Scale/Whole Tone]]); [Hirajoshi]([[Music/Scale/Hirajoshi]]).
