- # [31.1.8 Global Parameters](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#global-parameters)
	- Volume, vibrato, unison, glide, routing presets and keyboard settings.
		- ![Display and Shell Parameters for the Global Options](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/AnalogGlobalL12.png)
	- **Volume** — overall output.
	- **Vib** — a dedicated pitch LFO on both oscillators with amount and **Rate**. Display adds **Delay**, **Attack**, **Error** (random variation per voice) and **Amt < MW** (mod wheel control of depth).
	- **Uni** — stacks voices per note with **Detune**; the display picks two or four **Voices** and a **Delay** between them.
	- **Gli** — glide with **Time**; with **Legato**, glide only on overlapping notes. **Glide Mode** Const keeps time fixed; Prop scales it with the interval.
	- **Quick Routing** — four buttons set common routings without touching levels, tuning or waveforms: each oscillator to its own filter and amp; both oscillators split across both filters; everything through Filter 1 and Amp 1; or serial Filter 1 into Filter 2 and Amp 2.
	- **Keyboard** section:
		- **Octave**, **Semi**, **Detune** (±50 cents) for the whole instrument.
		- **PB Range** — pitch bend range in semitones.
		- **Stretch** — stretch tuning as on pianos: positive raises high notes and lowers low ones; negative does the reverse.
		- **Error** — random tuning per note.
		- **Voices** and **Priority** — polyphony, and which notes get cut when it runs out: High keeps higher notes, Low keeps lower, Last keeps the newest.
