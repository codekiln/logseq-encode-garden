- # [jiti](https://github.com/unjs/jiti)
	- Runtime [[TypeScript]] and ESM support for [[NodeJS]]. Lets a program `import`/`require` TypeScript and ESM modules directly at runtime, transforming them on the fly with caching, so no separate build step is needed.
	- [[GitHub/Star]]: 2914 (checked 2026-07-25).
	- Part of the [unjs](https://github.com/unjs) collective, created and maintained by [[Person/Pooya Parsa]].
	- Written in [[TypeScript]].
