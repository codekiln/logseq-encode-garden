- Accepting risk means choosing an option whose outcomes spread wide, including outcomes that lose.
- **Gains** — access to payoffs that safe options never reach. The spread is what makes the large upside available at all, and bearing it is what the reward pays for.
- **Costs** — the chance of loss, the attention it takes to hold the exposure in mind, and the compounding damage when a loss cannot be recovered from.
- **Too far** — ruin: a single bad draw that ends the game, so no later reward is ever collected. A portfolio, a project or a reputation staked on one outcome.
