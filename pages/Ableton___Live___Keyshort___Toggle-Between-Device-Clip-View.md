- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Swap the bottom of the window between the Device View and the Clip View** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Shift+Tab` or `F12`
		- Description: Shows whichever of the two views is hidden in place of the other. With Use Tab to Move Focus on, `Shift+Tab` moves focus backward instead. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/12 Clip and Device View]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/01 Showing and Hiding Views]].
