- # MIDI Clip
	- In [[Ableton/Live]], a **MIDI clip** is a [clip]([[Ableton/Clip]]) that holds notes and controller envelopes ([[Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/10 MIDI Clips and MIDI Files]]).
	- It plays on a [MIDI track]([[Ableton/Track/MIDI]]), where an instrument or an external synth turns its notes into sound.
	- Its notes come from recording, drawing, [MIDI Tools]([[Ableton/Live/UG/11 MIDI Tools]]) or [audio-to-MIDI conversion]([[Ableton/Live/UG/13 Audio to MIDI]]). Importing a `.mid` file copies the file's notes into the Set.
	- The MIDI Note Editor in the Clip View edits its notes ([[Ableton/Live/UG/10 Editing MIDI]]).
