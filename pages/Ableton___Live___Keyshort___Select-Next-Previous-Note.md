- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Step the selection to the next or previous note in time** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Option+Up` / `Option+Down` (Win `Alt+Up` / `Alt+Down`)
		- Description: In the MIDI Note Editor, selects the nearest note and then steps through the notes in time order; add `Shift` to keep adding notes to the selection. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/10 Editing MIDI/05 Editing MIDI Notes/02 Selecting Notes and Timespan]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/12 Clip View MIDI Note Editor]].
