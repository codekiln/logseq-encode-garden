- # [Novation Components](https://components.novationmusic.com/)
	- [[Novation]]'s app for its hardware. It runs in a browser with WebMIDI or as a standalone application ([[Launchpad/UG/08 Custom Modes/01 Overview]]).
	- For the [[Novation/Launchpad/Pro/Mk3]], it:
		- updates firmware;
		- builds [[Launchpad/Custom]] modes and sends them to the Launchpad;
		- backs up and organizes sequencer [[Launchpad/Project]]s, without editing what is inside them.
