- # DAW Drumrack
	- A DAW can make Note mode show a Drum Rack instead, and light its pads to match the loaded drum samples with the channel 9-11 messages in [[Launchpad/PRG/05 Software Integration/02 DAW Interface]].
	- The Drum Rack grid is two 4x8 halves. The left half runs notes 36-67 and the right half 68-99, each rising four notes per row from the bottom. Buttons keep their Session CCs.
