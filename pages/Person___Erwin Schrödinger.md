- # [Erwin Schrödinger](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1933/schrodinger/biographical/)
	- ## About
		- Austrian physicist (1887–1961), born and died in Vienna, and one of the founders of quantum mechanics.
		- In 1926 he published the wave equation that bears his name, which describes how a quantum system's wave function changes over time. He shared the 1933 Nobel Prize in Physics with Paul Dirac.
		- His 1944 book *What Is Life?* asked how physics could explain heredity and influenced the early molecular biologists.
