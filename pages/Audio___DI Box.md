- # DI Box
	- A direct injection box: it takes an instrument's unbalanced, high-impedance signal and turns it into a [[Audio/Balanced]], low-impedance, mic-level signal on an [[Audio/Connector/XLR]].
	- It lets a guitar, bass or keyboard plug into a mic input on a long cable. Its input gives the pickup the same light load as a [[Audio/Hi-Z]] input.
	- **Passive** DIs use a transformer and need no power. **Active** DIs use electronics powered by a battery or by [[Audio/Phantom Power]].
	- A ground-lift switch on most DIs breaks a hum loop between the instrument and the mixer.
