- # Arranger
	- In this garden, **Arranger** pages model people whose adaptations of existing musical works are a significant part of their public work.
	- An arranger retains a person hub under `Person/<Full Name>`. Add this role alongside [[Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition]] or [[Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance]] when those roles apply.
	- A score links the person credited for that edition through `arranged-by::`. A distinct arrangement can be modeled as a [[Logseq/Entity/Music/Work]] with `based-on::` linking the underlying work.
