- # Molecule
	- Stage 2 of [[UX/Design/Atomic]].
	- > Molecules are collections of atoms that form relatively simple UI components.[^1]
	- > For example, a form label, search input, and button can join together to create a search form molecule.[^1]
	- ## In this garden
		- A small group of controls that work as a unit, such as the MicroFreak's Type, Wave, Timbre and Shape knobs.
