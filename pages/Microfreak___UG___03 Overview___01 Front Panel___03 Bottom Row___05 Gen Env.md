- # 03.01.03.05 The General Envelope Generator
	- The Envelope Generator shapes a tone’s loudness or timbre and can be patched to destinations in the Matrix. **Attack**, **Decay/Release**, and **Sustain** affect the filter by default; **Filter Amt** sets the envelope’s effect on the filter. [[Microfreak/UG/09 Envelope Gen]] explains the controls in detail.
