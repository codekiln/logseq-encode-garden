- # Equivalent Input Noise (EIN)
	- The noise of a [[Audio/Mic Preamp]] at its input. It is measured at full gain and divided by the gain, so preamps with different gain ranges can be compared.
	- Given in [[Audio/dBu]], or as a positive dB figure that means the same thing. More negative is quieter.
	- Compare EIN only between figures measured the same way. A lower source impedance, a narrower bandwidth or A-weighting makes the number look better, so a 50 Ω figure looks a few dB quieter than a 150 Ω one.
