- # Comping
	- Comping is building one performance out of the best parts of several recorded takes. The result is a *comp*, short for composite.
	- ## In Ableton Live
		- [[Ableton/Live]] records each pass over the same span of the Arrangement onto its own take lane. Only the main lane plays; take lanes are heard when auditioned.
		- Selecting a stretch of a take copies it to the main lane, so the comp is assembled piece by piece while every take stays intact.
		- Take lanes can also hold alternative arrangements of clips, or samples dragged in from the browser for chopping.
		- [[Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping]] covers take lanes, recording takes, auditioning and creating a comp. Its shortcuts are in [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/17 Comping]].
