- # `podcast-published-at::` — episode publication time
	- The date and time an episode entered its podcast feed, with an explicit UTC offset.
	- ## Values
		- `podcast-published-at:: 2026-10-03T12:00:00-04:00`.
	- ## Ownership
		- The publishing garden stores this property on its [[Logseq/Entity/Podcast/Episode]] page. A [[Logseq/Entity/Proxy/Page]] preserves the property when its source body is synced.
