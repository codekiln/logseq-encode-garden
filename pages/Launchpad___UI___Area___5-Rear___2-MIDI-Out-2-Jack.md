- # MIDI Out 2/Thru jack
	- A MIDI output. In the Setup menu's MIDI Settings, Out 2 makes it repeat [[Launchpad/UI/Area/5-Rear/3-MIDI-Out-1-Jack]], and Thru makes it pass on the MIDI arriving at [[Launchpad/UI/Area/5-Rear/4-MIDI-In-Jack]].
	- ## Remote control
		- Over USB, a computer reaches the MIDI jacks on the `LPProMK3 DIN In/Out` interface.
