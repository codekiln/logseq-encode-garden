- # Leo Bernard
	- Musician and developer who makes [[Ableton/Live]] tools under the name leolabs. Co-founder of Festify.
	- ## Online presence
		- [leolabs.org](https://leolabs.org/)
		- [GitHub: leolabs](https://github.com/leolabs)
	- ## Projects
		- [[AbleSet]] — setlist manager for Live performances.
		- [[Person/Leo Bernard/GitHub/ableton-js]] — Node.js library for controlling Live, which AbleSet is built on.
		- LTC to MTC Converter and LTC Display — plugins for Live that read LTC timecode from an audio signal, sold through the leolabs store.
