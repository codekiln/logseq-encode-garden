- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Move keyboard focus to the Clip View's panels** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Option+Shift+P` (Win `Alt+Shift+P`)
		- Description: Puts keyboard focus on the Clip View's panels, from anywhere in Live; `Option+3` returns focus to the editor.
