- # Sound Artist
	- In this garden, **Sound Artist** pages model a real person who makes art whose material is sound, presented as art: sound installations, sound sculptures, soundwalks, and site-specific or gallery works with sound. A Sound Artist is a kind of [[Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art]], so a page marked Sound Artist leaves Artist out.
	- ## What counts as an instance
		- A person whose sound art is a significant part of their public identity: [[Person/George Rhoads]] and his audiokinetic sculptures, [[Person/Courtney Brown]].
	- ## Boundary cases
		- A piece for concert or release on fixed media is a composition. A person who makes both installations and such pieces carries Sound Artist and [[Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition]]: [[Person/Wei Yang]].
		- A sound artist who performs their work live carries Sound Artist and [[Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance]]: [[Person/Huichun Yang]].
		- A sound artist who also makes music in an unknown role carries Sound Artist and [[Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music]]: [[Person/Ranger Liu]].
	- ## Examples in this garden
		- [[Person/George Rhoads]], [[Person/Courtney Brown]]
