- # Raymond Carver
	- **Role:** American short-story writer and poet, associated with literary minimalism.
	- **Bio:** In his essay "On Writing," Carver quotes [[Person/Isaac Babel]]'s line about the period from "Guy de Maupassant"—"No iron can pierce the heart with such force as a period put just at the right place"—and says it "ought to go on a three-by-five card."
