- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Swap the selected part of the comp for the next or previous take** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+Up` / `Cmd+Down` (Win `Ctrl+Up` / `Ctrl+Down`)
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, with clips or a time range selected in the main lane, replaces them with the same span from the next or previous take that has material there. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping/06 Creating a Comp]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/17 Comping]].
