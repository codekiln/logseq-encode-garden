- # Launchpad Custom Modes
	- The Launchpad Pro holds eight Custom Modes, one per slot. Open [[Launchpad/UI/Mode/4-Custom]] with the [Custom button]([[Launchpad/UI/Area/0-Top/6-Custom-Button]]), then press [Track Select]([[Launchpad/UI/Area/4-Bottom/1-Track-Select]]) button 1 to 8 to choose slot 1 to 8 ([[Launchpad/UG/08 Custom Modes/02 Default Custom Modes]]).
	- Each slot starts with a factory layout ([[Launchpad/UG/11 Appendix/01 Default MIDI Mappings]]). Sending a mode from [[Novation/Components]] to a slot replaces whatever the slot held.
	- ## Pages in this namespace
		- [[Launchpad/Custom/Slot]] — the eight slots on the device. Each `Launchpad/Custom/Slot/<N>` page points to the mode the slot holds now and keeps its history ([[Logseq/Entity/Launchpad/Custom/Slot]]).
		- `Launchpad/Custom/Available/<Name>` — the named modes saved in Components, whether a slot holds them or not ([[Logseq/Entity/Launchpad/Custom/Mode]]).
		- Sending a mode to a slot adds a History entry to the slot's page and points the slot at the mode. No page is renamed.
		- [[Launchpad/Custom/Pad/Mode]] is the Components setting for how a pad behaves when pressed and released.
