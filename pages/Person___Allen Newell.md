- # Allen Newell
	- American researcher in computer science and cognitive psychology, born 19 March 1927 and died 19 July 1992, who worked at the RAND Corporation and Carnegie Mellon University.[^1]
	- With [[Person/Herbert A. Simon]] he built early artificial intelligence programs, the Logic Theory Machine (1956) and the General Problem Solver (1957), and shared the 1975 A.M. Turing Award with Simon.[^1]
	- Herbert Simon thanked Newell for "numerous enlightening conversations" in the 1956 paper that introduced satisficing: [[Satis/fice]].[^2]
