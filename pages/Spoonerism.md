- # Spoonerism
	- A slip of the tongue that swaps the opening sounds of two words, as in "a well-boiled icicle" for "a well-oiled bicycle."
	- Named after William Archibald Spooner, warden of New College, Oxford, who was known for the habit. Most of the spoonerisms attributed to him were likely invented by others.
	- A kind of [[Turn of Speech]] made by accident; comedians and poets also make them on purpose.
