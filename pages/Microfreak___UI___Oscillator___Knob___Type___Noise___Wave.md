- # Wave (Noise)
	- Set by the [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Wave]] while the oscillator model is [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type/Noise]].
	- Selects the noise source, from particle noise through white noise to metallic noise. Turning the Wave knob clockwise morphs the resulting wave from particle noise to white noise to metallic noise.
