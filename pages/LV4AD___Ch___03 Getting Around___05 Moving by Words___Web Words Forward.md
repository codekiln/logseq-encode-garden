- ### 1. What [[Keyshort]] moves to the beginning of the next word, and 2. what moves to the end of the current (or next) word? [[card]]
	- 1. `w` — start of the **next** word (count repeats for farther jumps)
	- 2. `e` — end of the **current** word, or end of the next word if already at the current word's end
