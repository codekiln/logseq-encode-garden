- # [Temporary Residence](https://temporaryresidence.com)
	- Temporary Residence Limited, an independent label started in Louisville, Kentucky in 1996 by Jeremy deVine and now based in Brooklyn. Its roster includes Explosions in the Sky, Mogwai, William Basinski, Mono and Grails.
