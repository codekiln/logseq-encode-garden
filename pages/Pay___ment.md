- # Payment
	- [[Definition/My]]
		- A transfer of money from a payer to a payee to settle a purchase, a bill or a debt, made through a payment method such as a card, a bank transfer or a wallet.
	- [[Pay/ment/Manage/ment]] explains how software systems let people store and choose payment methods.
