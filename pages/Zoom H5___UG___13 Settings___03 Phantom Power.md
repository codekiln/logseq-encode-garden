- # Changing the phantom power setting
	- Inputs 1 and 2 can supply phantom power at +12V, +24V or +48V.
	- 1. Press **MENU**, then use the scroll button to select `IN/OUT > In1/2 PHANTOM`.
	- 2. Use the scroll button to select `ON/OFF` or `VOLTAGE`, then press it.
	- ## Turning phantom power on or off
		- 1. After selecting `ON/OFF`, use the scroll button to select `INPUT 1`, `INPUT 2` or `ALL`, then press it.
			- > [[Note/Info]] Select `ALL` to supply phantom power to both inputs.
		- 2. Select `ON` and press the scroll button.
	- ## Setting the voltage
		- After selecting `VOLTAGE`, use the scroll button to select `+12V`, `+24V` or `+48V`, then press it.
			- Voltage screen
				- ![01 Voltage screen](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/Zoom%20H5___UG___13%20Settings___03%20Phantom%20Power___Asset___01-Voltage-screen.png)
		- > [[Note/Info]] If a condenser mic works with less than +48V, a lower voltage reduces the power the H5 uses.
