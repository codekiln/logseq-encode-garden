- # [36.14 Clip Mode](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#clip-mode)
	- **Clip** mode adjusts the selected clip. On an audio track with no clip, Push prompts you to load a sample.
		- ![The Clip button](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2ClipButton.png?auto=&w=268)
	- The display takes the clip's colour and name. Drum notes show in their pad colours; note opacity shows velocity. The view scrolls with a playing clip.
		- ![Clip mode with a MIDI clip selected](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2MIDIClipDisplay.png?auto=&w=320)
	- The second upper button toggles **Loop**. On: **Loop Position** (where the loop starts), **Loop Length**, and **Start Offset** (where playback starts within the loop; it moves with Loop Position when they coincide). Off: **Start** and **End** of the region played once. `Shift` adjusts in 16ths.
	- Encoder 1 zooms around the last position control touched.
	- Audio clips also have **Warp Mode**, **Gain** and **Transpose** (`Shift` for cents, which sets Detune in Live).
		- ![Clip mode with an audio clip selected](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2AudioClipDisplay.png?auto=&w=320)
