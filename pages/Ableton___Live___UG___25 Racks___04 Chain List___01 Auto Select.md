- # [25.4.1 Auto Select](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/instrument-drum-and-effect-racks/#auto-select)
	- ![Auto Select in an Audio Effect Rack](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/RackEffectAuto.png?auto=&w=320)
	- With **Auto Select** on, chains are selected as they process signal: in a Drum Rack, when their note arrives; in other Racks, according to their zones. Useful for troubleshooting.
