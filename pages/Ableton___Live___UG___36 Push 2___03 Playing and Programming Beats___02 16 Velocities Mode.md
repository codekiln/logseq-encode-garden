- # [36.3.2 16 Velocities Mode](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#16-velocities-mode)
	- The bottom-right 16 pads play the selected drum at 16 velocities; tap one to enter steps at that velocity.
		- ![The pad grid in the 16 Velocities layout](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push216VelocityLayout.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Hold Layout for the loop length controls; `Shift`+Layout locks them; Layout unlocks.
