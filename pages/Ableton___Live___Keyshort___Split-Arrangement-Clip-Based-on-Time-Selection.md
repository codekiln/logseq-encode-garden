- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Split an Arrangement clip at the time selection made in its MIDI Note Editor** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+Shift+E` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+E`)
		- Description: In the MIDI Note Editor of an Arrangement clip, cuts the clip at the edges of the time selection so that the range becomes its own clip. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/12 Clip View MIDI Note Editor]].
