- # Charles Babbage
	- ## About
		- English mathematician and mechanical engineer, 1791–1871.
		- Designed the Difference Engine, a machine for calculating and printing mathematical tables, and then the [[Analytical/Engine]], a general-purpose calculating machine he first described in [[18/3/7]].
		- Neither engine was finished in his lifetime. He worked with [[Person/Ada Lovelace]], whose notes on the Analytical Engine are its best-known description.
		- Wrote the memoir *Passages from the Life of a Philosopher* (1864).
