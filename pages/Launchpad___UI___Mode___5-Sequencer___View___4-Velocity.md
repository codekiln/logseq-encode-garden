- # Velocity view
	- The [fourth button down the right-hand column]([[Launchpad/UI/Area/3-Right/4-Velocity-Button]]) opens Velocity view. Select a step in the top half of the grid, then press a pad on the two-row slider at the top of the Play Area to set its velocity from 1 to 16.
