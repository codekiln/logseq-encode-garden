- # [Dylint](https://github.com/trailofbits/dylint)
	- [[GitHub/Star]]: 630 (checked 2026-07-25).
	- Runs [[Rust]] lints from user-supplied dynamic libraries via `cargo dylint`, letting developers write and maintain their own out-of-tree lints without forking [[Clippy]]. Dylint exists because Clippy deliberately accepts only built-in, upstreamed lints.
	- Created and maintained by [[Trail of Bits]] (primarily Samuel Moelius).
	- Written in [[Rust]].
