- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Select a range of time from an Arrangement clip's title bar** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Shift+Option`-drag in the clip title bar (Win `Shift+Alt`-drag)
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, dragging across a clip's title bar with these modifiers selects time inside the clip; a plain drag there moves the clip. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/09 Selecting Clips and Time]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
