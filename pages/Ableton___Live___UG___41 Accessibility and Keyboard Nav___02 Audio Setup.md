- # [41.2 Audio Setup](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/accessibility-and-keyboard-navigation/#audio-setup)
	- Open the Settings with `Cmd+,` (Mac) / `Ctrl+,` (Win), arrow to the Audio page, and `Tab` through its options.
	- On macOS Live's inputs and outputs can use any CoreAudio device, and the Driver Type menu offers other drivers. On Windows choose a driver such as ASIO4ALL and manage devices in that driver's own program.
