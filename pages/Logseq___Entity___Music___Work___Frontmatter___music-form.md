- # `music-form::` — the form a work is written in
	- Links a [[Logseq/Entity/Music/Work]] to the [[Logseq/Entity/Music/Form]] pages it belongs to.
	- ## Values
		- One or more comma-separated links to form pages, as `music-form:: [[Music/Form/Prelude]]`.
		- Set it when a work is plainly an instance of the form, by its title or by common description. Leave it out rather than guess.
