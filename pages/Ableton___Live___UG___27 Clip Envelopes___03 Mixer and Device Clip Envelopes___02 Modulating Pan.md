- # [27.3.2 Modulating Pan](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/clip-envelopes/#modulating-pan)
	- Pan modulation scales with the Pan knob: centred, it can sweep fully left to right; turned towards either side, its range shrinks; fully left or right, it does nothing.
