- # In the [[Microfreak]], how can I copy a sequence from sequence A to sequence B?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- [[Answer/Official]] from [[Microfreak/UG/13 Sequencer/01 Use/03 Record/04 Copy]], the garden's copy of the Arturia MicroFreak user guide
		- **Short answer:** with the sequencer on, hold the A icon and press the B icon. The display confirms the copy. Hold B and press A to copy the other way.
		- **Sequencer on first:** Shift + Seq turns the icon strip into Tie/Rest, A, B, Record/Stop and Play/Stop, per [[Microfreak/UG/13 Sequencer/01 Use]].
		- **Keeping the copy:** patterns are saved with their preset, so save the preset to keep B. Until you do, Shift + B reloads B as last saved, per [[Microfreak/UG/13 Sequencer/01 Use/01 Select & Play]].
		- **Why copy:** the guide suggests keeping the same melody in both patterns while recording different modulation in each, then switching between A and B as you play.
		- **Not documented:** copying a pattern into a different preset. The guide covers A and B within one preset only.
