- # Flashing colour
	- A flashing pad alternates at 50% duty cycle between colour A, its static or pulsing colour, and colour B, the colour in the flashing message.
	- One period is one beat, synced to incoming MIDI beat clock. Without clock it uses 120 BPM or the last clock received.
