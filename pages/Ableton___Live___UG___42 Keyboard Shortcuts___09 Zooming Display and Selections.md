- # [42.9 Zooming, Display and Selections](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-keyboard-shortcuts/#zooming-display-and-selections)
	- Zoom In Window — `Cmd` `+` (Win `Ctrl` `+`)
	- Zoom Out Window — `Cmd` `-` (Win `Ctrl` `-`)
	- Zoom In Time Ruler — `+`
	- Zoom Out Time Ruler — `-`
	- Scroll Display to Follow Playback — `Option+Shift+F` (Win `Alt+Shift+F`)
	- Scroll Left/Right — `Shift` scroll
	- Pan Left/Right of Selection — `Cmd+Option` (Win `Ctrl+Alt`)
	- Add Items to a Selection — `Shift` click or drag
	- Add Adjacent Clips/Tracks/Scenes to Multi-Selection — `Shift` click
	- Add Nonadjacent Clips/Tracks/Scenes to a Multi-Selection — `Cmd` click (Win `Ctrl` click)
