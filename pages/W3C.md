- # [W3C](https://www.w3.org/)
	- The World Wide Web Consortium is the international standards organization for the World Wide Web, developing open standards through a community process of Working Groups, public review, and consensus.
	- Its standards-track documents, once ratified, are published as Recommendations.
	- In 2017, the W3C and the International Digital Publishing Forum (IDPF) merged, bringing publishing standards such as [[ePub]] under the W3C.
