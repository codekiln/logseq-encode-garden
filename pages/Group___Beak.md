- # Beak>
	- English experimental electronic rock band from Bristol, formed in 2009 by [[Person/Geoff Barrow]] of Portishead with [[Person/Billy Fuller]] and Matt Williams. [[Person/Will Young]] replaced Williams in 2016. Barrow left in September 2024.
	- Their song "Sex Music" reached number 1 on the UK vinyl singles chart on Valentine's Day.
