- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Lengthen or shorten an Arrangement clip from the keyboard** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Enter`, then `Left` / `Right`, then `Enter` to confirm or `Esc` to cancel
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, with the [[Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement-View/Marker/Insert-Marker]] on a clip's start or end, moves that edge by the grid. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/41 Accessibility and Keyboard Nav/04 Navigating in Live/01 Navigate Menu]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
