- # [The PERFECT Forearm Workout (Sets and Reps Included) - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4EkKhkSNjWY)
	- Speaker: Jeff Cavaliere
	- Channel: [ATHLEAN-X](https://www.youtube.com/@athleanx) · 14:25
	- Linked from the Day 7 additional resources of [[Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols]] Chapter Two, as "Training forearms with Jeff Cavaliere."
	- ## About
		- A forearm workout that goes past wrist curls and extensions to cover the other things the forearm does.
		- Prone (palm-down) wrist curls replace standard wrist curls, which let the biceps take over and push the bar into the fingers, straining the finger tendons linked to medial elbow pain.
		- Farmer's carries with heavy dumbbells go between sets.
		- Reverse wrist rolls for the tops of the forearms pair with reverse barbell curls for the brachioradialis.

			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 0}} Forearm actions
				- The forearm flexes and extends the wrist, bends it side to side, rotates, and flexes the fingers, and each action lights up a different area. A complete workout trains all of them; sets and reps come at the end.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 97}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Prone Wrist Curl]]
				- Standard wrist curls let the biceps take over and let the bar sink into the fingers, which strains the inner elbow. A cable or band curl with the elbow bent and palm down fixes both.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 266}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Farmer's Carry]]
				- One lap around the gym with heavy dumbbells between every set of every exercise, for grip endurance.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 316}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Reverse Wrist Roll]] and [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Reverse Curl]]
				- Standing keeps the wrist extensors loaded at the top. Alternate seconds of rolling with reverse curls in a climbing ladder until failure.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 422}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Wrist Deviation]]
				- A cable rope held at the side trains ulnar deviation, then, turned around, radial deviation. A sledgehammer does the same job.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 558}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Forearm Rotation]]
				- Twirling a dumbbell lets the hand fall into each position. The rope resists pronation and supination on every rep.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 670}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Collar Squeeze]]
				- Squeeze a spring collar to failure, then hold light tension while flexing and extending the wrist, as a drop-set burnout.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 764}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Dead Hang]]
				- The finisher: hang from a bar and try for one more minute without letting the bar slide into the fingers. The full workout with sets and reps follows on screen.
