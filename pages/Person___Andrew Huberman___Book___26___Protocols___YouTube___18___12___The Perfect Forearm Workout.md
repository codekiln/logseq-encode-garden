- # [The PERFECT Forearm Workout (Sets and Reps Included) - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4EkKhkSNjWY)
	- Speaker: Jeff Cavaliere
	- Channel: [ATHLEAN-X](https://www.youtube.com/@athleanx) · 14:25
	- Linked from the Day 7 additional resources of [[Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols]] Chapter Two, as "Training forearms with Jeff Cavaliere."
	- ## About
		- A forearm workout that goes past wrist curls and extensions to cover the other things the forearm does.
		- Prone (palm-down) wrist curls replace standard wrist curls, which let the biceps take over and push the bar into the fingers, straining the finger tendons linked to medial elbow pain.
		- Farmer's carries with heavy dumbbells go between sets.
		- Reverse wrist rolls for the tops of the forearms pair with reverse barbell curls for the brachioradialis.
