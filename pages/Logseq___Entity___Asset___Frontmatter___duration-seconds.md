- # `duration-seconds::` — playback length
	- The measured playback length of an audio or video asset, in seconds.
	- ## Values
		- A positive number, with fractional seconds allowed. For example, `duration-seconds:: 757.761066`.
		- Optional for audio and video assets; omitted for files without a playback duration.
