- # `duration-seconds::` — playback length
	- The measured playback length of an audio or video [[Logseq/Entity/Asset]], in seconds.
	- ## Values
		- A positive number, with fractional seconds allowed. For example, `duration-seconds:: 757.761066` describes the September 24 episode MP3.
		- Optional for audio and video assets; omitted for files without a playback duration.
	- ## Owning entity
		- [[Logseq/Entity/Asset]]
