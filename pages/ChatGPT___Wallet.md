- # [ChatGPT Wallet](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet)
	- Save a card for your dot to use at supported checkouts after you approve the purchase. Wallet is available in dot conversations. Chat, Work and standalone Codex threads use separate flows. Announced on 2026-11-13 in [Introducing ChatGPT Wallet](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/).
	- **Who can use it** — US [[ChatGPT/Pro]] subscribers aged 18 or over with eligible US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard or Visa cards. The rollout is gradual, so some eligible accounts may not have it yet.
	- **Adding and managing cards** — ask the dot, or open Settings > Wallet > Add Card. Card details go into a secure form, outside the chat.
	- **Paying** — every purchase needs approval with Face ID or Touch ID. The dot and merchant use a substitute card number. The dot submits the approved order and the merchant decides whether to accept it. "Ready to check out" means approval is done; the merchant still places the order.
	- **Separate from billing** — Wallet cards are separate from the card that pays for the ChatGPT subscription. The merchant is the seller of record, per the [Wallet terms](https://openai.com/policies/chatgpt-wallet-terms/).
	- Virtual cards and Chase debit cards are not supported at launch.
	- An example of [[Pay/ment/Manage/ment]] for an [[AI/Agent]].
