- # Down button
	- In Session mode, scrolls the 8 × 8 clip window down through the Live Set; in Session Overview, it steps between 8 × 8 blocks of clips.
	- In Note mode, shifts the grid down an octave; in Drum mode, it scrolls the Drum Rack by sixteen slots.
	- In Sequencer mode, shifts the Play Area down an octave on Chromatic and Scale tracks, and by sixteen notes on Drum tracks.
	- In the sequencer's Tempo and Swing views, lowers the value; hold it to change the value quickly.
	- ## Remote control
		- Programmer mode: CC 70
