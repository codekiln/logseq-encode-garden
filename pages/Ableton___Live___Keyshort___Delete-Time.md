- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Delete the selected span of time from every Arrangement track and close the gap** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+Shift+Delete` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+Delete`)
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, removes the selected time from every track, time signature markers included, and shortens the Arrangement. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/11 Using the Time Commands]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
