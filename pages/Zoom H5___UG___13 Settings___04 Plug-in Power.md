- # Using plug-in power
	- Turn plug-in power on before connecting a mic that needs it to the **MIC/LINE** input jack on the XY mic.
	- 1. Press **MENU**, then use the scroll button to select `IN/OUT > PLUGIN POWER`.
	- 2. Select `ON` and press the scroll button.
