- # [Ableton Live 12](https://www.ableton.com/en/release-notes/live-12/)
	- A guide for someone coming from Live 11, through Live 12.4.16. [Ableton's full list of new features](https://www.ableton.com/en/live/all-new-features/) and [12.4 notes](https://help.ableton.com/hc/en-us/articles/26963870210076-What-s-new-in-Live-12-4) cover more changes.
	- ## Compose and control MIDI
		- [[Ableton/Live/v/12/Note Probability Group]] — Turn familiar per-note Chance into a shared decision for a chord, a drum fill, or a choice of pitches.
		- [[Ableton/Live/v/12/MIDI Tools]] — Generate notes and reshape them directly in a clip. Try Seed, then use Follow Actions if the next phrase should depend on the current one.
		- [[Ableton/Live/v/12/CC Control]] — Set up an automatable filter control for a MicroFreak using a stock MIDI effect.
		- **Keys and scales** — Choose a root and scale in the Control Bar or clip properties, then enable Scale Mode. Highlight or fold the piano roll to the scale while writing; compatible devices and MIDI tools can follow the clip's scale. [[Ableton/Live/UG/10 Editing MIDI/06 Folding and Scales]]
		- **Tuning systems** — Load a Scala tuning from the Browser to explore pitches outside twelve-tone equal temperament. Live's instruments follow it; check plug-in and hardware support before relying on external instruments to match. [[Ableton/Live/UG/15 Tuning Systems]]
	- ## Find sounds and work in Arrangement
		- **Browser tags** — Select a Browser category and use Filter View to narrow sounds by descriptive tags across content types. Add your own tags to make sounds easier to find later. [[Ableton/Live/UG/04 Browser/04 Filters and Tags]]
		- **Arrangement mixer** — Open Mixer using the view control at the bottom right or `Cmd+Option+M` on Mac. Mix levels and sends while the Arrangement stays visible; Session and Arrangement mixer visibility can be set independently. [Ableton's view options FAQ](https://help.ableton.com/hc/en-us/articles/12243771208092-Navigation-and-View-Options-in-Live-12-FAQ)
		- **Stacked Detail Views** — Show the Clip View and Device View together to edit a clip while adjusting its devices. [Ableton's Live 12 feature guide](https://www.ableton.com/en/live/all-new-features/)
		- **Bounce** — Select a clip or time range and choose **Bounce to New Track** to print its devices to audio; Live mutes the source selection. The bounce is pre-mixer, so volume, pan and sends remain mixer settings. **Bounce Track in Place** replaces the earlier Freeze and Flatten command. [[Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio/01 Bouncing Individual Tracks]]
	- ## Explore audio and devices
		- **Stem Separation** — In Live 12.3 Suite and later, right-click an audio clip and choose **Separate Stems to New Audio Tracks** to isolate vocals, drums, bass or other sounds. Live 12.4 can process a selected Arrangement time range. [[Ableton/Live/UG/22 Stem Separation/02 Separating Audio Files and Clips]]
		- **Link Audio** — Live 12.4 can stream audio between compatible Link Audio peers on the same network. Enable Link Audio in the Link menu, then route audio between peers. Original Link synchronizes timing; Link Audio also carries audio. [Ableton's Link Audio FAQ](https://help.ableton.com/hc/en-us/articles/25425913328924-Link-Audio-FAQ)
		- **New and updated devices** — Try [[Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/33 Roar]] for multistage saturation, or Auto Shift for pitch correction on monophonic audio. Live 12.4 also updates Erosion and other devices; older Sets retain Erosion Legacy. [Live 12 release notes](https://www.ableton.com/en/release-notes/live-12/)
	- [[Ableton/Live/UG]] has the reference by chapter. [Compare Live editions](https://www.ableton.com/en/live/compare-editions/) shows which tools and devices come with each edition.
