- # [Ableton Live 12](https://www.ableton.com/en/release-notes/live-12/)
	- ## Coming from Live 11
		- [[Ableton/Live/v/12/Note Probability Group]] — Turn familiar per-note Chance into a shared decision for a chord, a drum fill, or a choice of pitches.
		- [[Ableton/Live/v/12/MIDI Tools]] — Generate notes and reshape them directly in a clip. Try Seed, then use Follow Actions if the next phrase should depend on the current one.
		- [[Ableton/Live/v/12/CC Control]] — Set up an automatable filter control for a MicroFreak using a stock MIDI effect.

		- [[Ableton/Live/UG/04 Browser/04 Filters and Tags]] — Filter instruments, effects, and sounds by tags.
		- [[Ableton/Live/UG/18 Mixing]] — Use the mixer from Arrangement View while editing a song.
		- [[Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/33 Roar]] — Explore multistage saturation and modulation in Suite.
