- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Fold or unfold the selected Arrangement tracks** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `U`, or `Left` / `Right` on a track title bar
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, unfolding shows a track's clips tall enough to select time inside them, and folding shrinks it to a thin row; on a Group Track it collapses or expands the group, which the track commands list as Collapse/Expand Grouped Tracks. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/09 Selecting Clips and Time]], [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/19 Commands for Tracks]].
