- # 6. Note Mode
	- Play melodic instruments and drums from the velocity- and pressure-sensitive grid. The layout follows the currently armed Live instrument: a Drum Rack selects Drum Mode, while other instruments use Chromatic or Scale Mode.
	- Use Up/Down to shift octaves and Left/Right to transpose by semitones. In Live, arm the MIDI track and set monitoring to Auto to hear the instrument.
