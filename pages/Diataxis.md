- # [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/)
	- A framework by [[Person/Daniele Procida]] that sorts documentation by what the reader needs, rather than by subject. Its four types are distinct in purpose and should not be blended on one page.
	- ## The compass
		- Pick the type by the need, not by what is easiest to write.
		- | Content…              | serves the reader’s…   | Type                      |
		  |-----------------------|------------------------|---------------------------|
		  | informs **action**    | application of skill   | [[Diataxis/How To]]       |
		  | guides **learning**   | acquisition of skill   | [[Diataxis/Tutorial]]     |
		  | informs **cognition** | theoretical knowledge  | [[Diataxis/Reference]]    |
		  | explains **why**      | understanding          | [[Diataxis/Explanation]]  |
	- ## The four types in this garden
		- [[Logseq/Entity/Diataxis]] and its four child entities say how a page of each type is named, tagged and shaped here.
