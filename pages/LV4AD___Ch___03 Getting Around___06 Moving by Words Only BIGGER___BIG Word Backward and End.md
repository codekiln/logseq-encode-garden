- ### 1. What [[Keyshort]] is the shifted form of `b` for BIG words, 2. what is the shifted form of `e`, and 3. what is the shifted form of `ge`? [[card]]
	- 1. `B` — backward by whitespace-delimited words (BIG `b`)
	- 2. `E` — forward to end of a whitespace-delimited word (BIG `e`)
	- 3. `gE` — end of the previous BIG word (BIG `ge`)
