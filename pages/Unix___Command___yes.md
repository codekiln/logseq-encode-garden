- # [yes](https://man.freebsd.org/cgi/man.cgi?query=yes&sektion=1) (Unix command)
	- **Usage:** `yes [string ...]`
	- Repeatedly writes its arguments, or `y` by default, until its output is stopped.
	- The command is distinct from [[Yes]], the English progressive rock band.
	- Source: [FreeBSD yes(1) manual](https://man.freebsd.org/cgi/man.cgi?query=yes&sektion=1)
