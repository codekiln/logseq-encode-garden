- # [18.9 Performance Impact Track Indicators](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/mixing/#performance-impact-track-indicators)
	- Performance Impact indicators show each track's share of the CPU load as six rectangles. Show them from View → Mixer Controls or the mixer view control menu.
		- ![The mixer's Performance Impact indicators](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MixerPerformanceImpactSectionL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Freezing the heaviest track or removing its devices usually lowers the load.
