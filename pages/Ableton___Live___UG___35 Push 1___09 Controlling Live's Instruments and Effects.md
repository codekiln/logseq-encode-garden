- # [35.9 Controlling Live’s Instruments and Effects](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-1/#controlling-lives-instruments-and-effects)
	- **Device** Mode puts device parameters on the encoders. Selection Control buttons choose a device on the track and State Control buttons turn it on or off; an arrow marks the selected device.
	- In and Out reach more parameter banks and the chains inside Racks.
