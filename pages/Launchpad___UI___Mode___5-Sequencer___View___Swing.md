- # Swing view
	- [Hold Shift and press Stop Clip]([[Launchpad/UI/Area/4-Bottom/2-Track-Control/8-Stop-Clip-Button/Shift/Swing]]) to open Swing view, shown in orange and white. A value above 50 delays off-beat notes and a value below 50 moves them earlier; the Up and Down arrows adjust it.
