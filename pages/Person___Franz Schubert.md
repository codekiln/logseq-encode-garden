- # [Franz Schubert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Schubert)
	- ## About
		- Austrian composer of the late Classical and early Romantic eras ([[17/9/7]]–[[18/2/8]]).
		- At Zseliz, the Esterházy family's château where he taught music, he took up Hungarian musical idioms and wrote the *Divertissement à la hongroise* for piano duet.
