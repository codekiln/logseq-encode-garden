- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Fade the selected audio in or out, or crossfade two neighboring clips** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+Option+F` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+F`)
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, a selection that includes an audio clip's start or end sets that fade to the selection's length; a selection across the boundary of two adjacent audio clips makes a crossfade. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/08 Audio Clip Fades and Crossfades]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
