- # Loop brace
	- Marks the section the Arrangement loop repeats. Drag an edge to move the start or end, or drag the bar to move the loop without changing its length.
	- Arrow keys nudge it by the grid or by its own length; Cmd (Mac) / Ctrl (Win) with the arrow keys shortens, lengthens, doubles or halves it.
	- Its start and end are also the punch-in and punch-out points: [[Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Switch/Punch-In]], [[Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Switch/Punch-Out]].
	- Turned on with the [[Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Toggle/Arrangement Loop]].
