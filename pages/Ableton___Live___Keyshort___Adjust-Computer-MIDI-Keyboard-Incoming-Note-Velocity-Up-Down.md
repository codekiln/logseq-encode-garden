- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Raise or lower the velocity of notes played on the Computer MIDI Keyboard** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `V` up, `C` down
		- Description: Works while the Computer MIDI Keyboard is on; with it off, `C` arms the selected tracks. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/24 Key-MIDI Map Mode and the Computer MIDI Keyboard]].
