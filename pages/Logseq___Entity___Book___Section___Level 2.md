- # Book Section Level 2
	- In this garden, **Book Section Level 2** pages model sections directly beneath a book's topmost part.
	- A Level 2 page sits under its Level 1 parent. Its two-digit prefix orders siblings within that parent; its H1 retains the source section number and heading when present.
	- It holds its own introductory material and embeds any Level 3 children at their source positions. [[Microfreak/06 Dig Osc/02 Param Controls]] is an example.
	- The shared section shape lives on [[Logseq/Entity/Book/Section]].
