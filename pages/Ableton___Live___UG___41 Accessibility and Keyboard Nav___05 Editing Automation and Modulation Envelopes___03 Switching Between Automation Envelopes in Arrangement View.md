- # [41.5.3 Switching Between Automation Envelopes in Arrangement View](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/accessibility-and-keyboard-navigation/#switching-between-automation-envelopes-in-arrangement-view)
	- `Option` plus up or down (Mac) / `Alt` plus up or down (Win) cycles through a track's automation envelopes in the Arrangement.
	- Add `Shift` to cycle through every device parameter, automated or not.
