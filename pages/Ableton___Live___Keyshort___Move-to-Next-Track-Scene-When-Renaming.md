	- **Keep renaming by moving on to the next track or scene** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Tab` while renaming
		- Description: Saves the name being typed and opens the next track's or scene's name; for take lanes, `Shift+Tab` goes back. The track list calls it Go to Next Track When Renaming.
