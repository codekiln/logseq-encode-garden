- # [19.10.2 Adding Material to an Existing Live Set](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/recording-new-clips/#adding-material-to-an-existing-live-set)
	- When the transport is running, the Set has clips, or tempo is automated, Capture MIDI keeps the tempo and finds a phrase within it.
	- Playing over a clip on the same track and pressing Capture MIDI adds the new notes on top, for building a pattern in layers.
	- Earlier notes are kept ahead of the start marker here too.
