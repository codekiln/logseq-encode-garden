- # How can we sign in with [[Passkey]]s in [[ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser]]?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- **Website passkey support is undocumented as of November 21, 2026.** OpenAI documents signing in to sites in the cloud browser with a password and, when required, a second-factor code. If a site requires a passkey, use your own browser. [ChatGPT browser guide](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/browser)
		- OpenAI’s [passkey setup guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001039-passkeys-to-secure-your-openai-account) applies to your OpenAI account. [1Password for Agents](https://support.1password.com/1password-for-agents/) currently supports passwords and one-time codes.
