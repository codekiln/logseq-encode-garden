- # What is the state of the art with respect to [[Pay/ment/Manage/ment]] in a [[ChatGPT/Plus]] account?

		- **Plus can help with checkout. Wallet requires Pro.** In [[ChatGPT/Work]], the [[ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser]] can navigate supported sites and asks before making a payment. A merchant may already have your card on file. If you need to enter a card or finish checkout yourself, use the merchant’s payment page.
