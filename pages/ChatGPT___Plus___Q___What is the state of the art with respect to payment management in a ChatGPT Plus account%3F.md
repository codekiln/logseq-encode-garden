- # What is the state of the art with respect to [[Pay/ment/Manage/ment]] in a [[ChatGPT/Plus]] account?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- **There is none. Plus has no payment management.** ChatGPT's only payment management feature is [[ChatGPT/Wallet]], and Wallet is limited to US [[ChatGPT/Pro]] subscribers. A Plus account cannot store a card for an agent to use. [OpenAI Wallet guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet)
		- **What a Plus agent can do is trivial.** In [[ChatGPT/Work]], the [[ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser]] can go to a merchant's site you have signed in to and press Buy on a card that merchant already has on file, after asking you to confirm. That is the merchant's saved card and the merchant's checkout; ChatGPT adds nothing beyond clicking the button. OpenAI's guide says never to paste payment details into the conversation, so a Plus agent has no supported way to pay with a card the merchant doesn't already hold. [OpenAI cloud browser guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001280-using-cloud-browser-in-chatgpt)
		- Shopping results and merchant apps in ChatGPT send you to the retailer's own checkout, where you pay the retailer directly. [OpenAI shopping update](https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/)
