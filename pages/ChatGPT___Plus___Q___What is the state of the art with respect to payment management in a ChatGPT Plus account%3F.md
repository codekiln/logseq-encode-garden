- # What is the state of the art with respect to [[Pay/ment/Manage/ment]] in a [[ChatGPT/Plus]] account?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- **Short answer:** as of [[2026-11-21 Sat]], a Plus account has no stored payment methods for merchant purchases. [[ChatGPT/Wallet]] and dots are Pro-only ([pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing), [release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes), 2026-11-19).
		- **Shopping** — product search, side-by-side comparison, "Try on" for clothes, and saving products to a Library. The purchase happens on the merchant's site or in a merchant app such as Walmart's inside ChatGPT ([Powering product discovery in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/), 2026-03-24).
		- **[[ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser]]** — Plus includes website sign-in in [[ChatGPT/Work]] (release notes, 2026-08-25). ChatGPT can reach a checkout and asks in chat before paying, but the person types card details into the merchant's site during takeover, or relies on a card already saved with the merchant.
		- **Instant Checkout** let Plus users pay merchants with their subscription card from 2025-09-29 until OpenAI scaled it back on 2026-03-24. Whether any merchant still offers it to Plus users is unverified.
