- # [Olivier Messiaen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Messiaen)
	- ## About
		- French composer, organist and ornithologist ([[1908]]–[[1992]]).
		- Set out his modes of limited transposition in *Technique de mon langage musical* ([[1944]]).
		- Taught at the Paris Conservatoire. His pupils included [[Person/Iannis Xenakis]], Karlheinz Stockhausen and Pierre Boulez, and [[Person/Pierre Henry]] studied with him.
