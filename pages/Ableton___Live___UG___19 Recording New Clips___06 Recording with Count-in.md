- # [19.6 Recording with Count-in](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/recording-new-clips/#recording-with-count-in)
	- Set a count-in from the menu next to the Metronome switch. Recording starts once it finishes.
	- The Control Bar's Arrangement Position shows the count-in in blue, counting from negative values (−2.1.1 for two bars) up to 1.1.1.
		- ![The count-in shown in the Control Bar](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/CountIn.png?auto=&w=320)
