- # [31.12.3 Filter/Global Tab](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#filterglobal-tab)
	- A polyphonic filter with envelope, LFO and MIDI modulation, keyboard and portamento options, and a Unison effect.
		- ![Tension's Filter/Global Tab](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/TensionFilterTabL12.png)
	- **Filter** — between String and Body, with its own on switch; 2nd- or 4th-order low-pass, band-pass, notch, high-pass or formant. **Freq** and **Res** (vowels for formant), each modulated by LFO, envelope and pitch; the LFO and Env sliders need those sections on.
	- **Filter Envelope** — [[ADSR]] with **Vel** on Attack (faster at higher velocity) and **Vel** on Sustain (higher at higher velocity).
	- **Filter LFO** — sine, triangle, rectangle, stepped random or smooth random; **Rate** in Hz or synced; **Attack** and **Delay**.
	- **MPE** — Pressure and Slide each reach two destinations with amounts; activity LEDs; global **Pitch Bend** and per-note **Note PB** ranges.
		- ![Tension's MPE Section](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/TensionMPESectionL12.png)
	- **Keyboard** — **Octave**, **Semi**, **Detune** (±50 cents), **Voices**, **Stretch** tuning (sharper highs and flatter lows, or the reverse when negative), **Error** (random tuning per note) and **Priority** (High, Low or Last).
	- **Portamento** — **Time**, **Legato** (only overlapping notes slide) and **Prop.** (time scales with the interval).
	- **Unison** — two or four stacked **Voices**, **Detune** from subtle chorus to wide, and **Delay** between voices.
	- **Volume** — shared with the String tab.
