- # Launchpad sequencer Projects
	- A Project stores the [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer]]'s Patterns, Scenes, scale and root, track types and track MIDI channels. It also stores the Note and Chord mode settings and the MIDI channel of every [[Launchpad/Custom]] mode. The Launchpad Pro has 64 Project slots ([[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/12 Projects]]).
	- ## On the Launchpad
		- The [Projects button]([[Launchpad/UI/Area/0-Top/8-Projects-Button]]) opens Projects view, where Projects are loaded.
		- Hold Shift and press Projects to save, either to the active slot or, from Projects view, to another slot ([[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/12 Projects/01 Save]]).
		- Patterns and steps are written only on the Launchpad's own sequencer.
	- ## In Novation Components
		- [[Novation/Components]] gets Projects from the Launchpad, backs them up to a Novation account or the computer, and sends them back ([[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/12 Projects/02 Backup]]). Per slot it can rename, recolour, download, replace or delete a Project, but it does not edit what is inside one.
