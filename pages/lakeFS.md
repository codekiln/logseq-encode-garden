- # [lakeFS](https://lakefs.io)
	- Company behind the [treeverse](https://github.com/treeverse) GitHub org. Founded in 2020 by Oz Katz and Einat Orr.
	- Ships lakeFS, Git-style version control for data lakes, and since November 2025 stewards [[dvc]] after acquiring it from Iterative. See [DVC Joins lakeFS](https://dvc.org/blog/dvc-joins-lakefs-your-questions-answered/).
