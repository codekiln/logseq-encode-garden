- # [3.2 The Status Bar](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-concepts/#the-status-bar)
	- The Status Bar at the bottom of the window shows error messages and notices of new releases.
	- In the MIDI Note Editor it shows a selected note's position, pitch, velocity and probability. Hovering over an insert marker shows the marker's exact position.
