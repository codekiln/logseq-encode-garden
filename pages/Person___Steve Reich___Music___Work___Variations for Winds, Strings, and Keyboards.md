- # Variations for Winds, Strings, and Keyboards
	- Steve Reich's orchestral work is scored for winds, brass, strings, and keyboards. Reich's catalog also lists an ensemble version under the same work title.
	- The work's long harmonic progression has three statements, described as variations; the catalog does not identify them as separately titled movements.
	- Sources: [Steve Reich work catalog](https://stevereich.com/works/); [IRCAM work record](https://medias.ircam.fr/en/work/variations-for-winds-strings-and-keyboards-1).
