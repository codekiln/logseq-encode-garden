- # 6. Assignment of Rights
	- The Licensee may transfer all software-use rights to another person only if the Licensee transfers this agreement and the software or accompanying hardware, including all copies, upgrades, updates, backup copies, and previous versions that granted upgrade or update rights; retains none of those versions or copies; and the recipient accepts this agreement and the other rules under which the valid license was acquired.
	- After the transfer, the product cannot be returned because the recipient rejects the agreement, including its activation requirements.
