	- Type 1 standard MIDI file at 120 BPM in 4/4, 16:06 long, aligned with the episode recording. The tempo track carries a marker for each chapter. "mf MIDI ALL" holds the notes, controller changes and per-note pressure (as polyphonic aftertouch) that reached the MicroFreak during the full take; the three "mf MIDI 1" tracks belong to the three MicroFreak audio layers; "LPP to MF" and "MF to LPP" hold the Launchpad routing captures. The captures overlap, so enable one at a time. All tracks use MIDI channel 1.
