- # [LazyVim extra: coding.mini-surround](https://www.lazyvim.org/extras/coding/mini-surround)
	- Stock [[LazyVim]] coding extra enabled in my nvim dotfiles. It configures [nvim-mini/mini.surround](https://github.com/nvim-mini/mini.surround) with `gs`-prefixed mappings for adding, deleting, finding, highlighting, and replacing surrounding characters.
	- ## Where to find it
		- Source: `~/.local/share/nvim/lazy/LazyVim/lua/lazyvim/plugins/extras/coding/mini-surround.lua`
		- In nvim: `:LazyExtras` → `coding.mini-surround`
		- Usage: [[LazyVim/Q/How can I surround the visual selection with backticks in a markdown document?]]
