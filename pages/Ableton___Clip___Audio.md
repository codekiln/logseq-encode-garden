- # Audio Clip
	- In [[Ableton/Live]], an **audio clip** is a [clip]([[Ableton/Clip]]) that points to a sample, a sound file such as a WAV or an MP3, and says which part of it to play and how ([[Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/09 Audio Clips and Samples]]).
	- It plays on an [audio track]([[Ableton/Track/Audio]]). Dragging a sample in from the browser, or recording on an audio track, creates one.
	- Its settings in the [Clip View]([[Ableton/Live/UG/08 Clip View]]) change playback without altering the sample file.
	- Warping changes its playback speed independently of its pitch, so it follows the Set's tempo ([[Ableton/Live/UG/09 Audio Clips Tempo and Warping]]).
