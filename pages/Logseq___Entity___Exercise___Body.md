- # Body Exercise
	- In this garden, **Body Exercise** pages model exercises that practice a physical movement or position.
	- Extends [[Logseq/Entity/Exercise]] and inherits its page shape.
	- Setup identifies equipment, stance or starting position, and the body areas involved. Practice describes the movement or hold, controlled range, and breathing cues when relevant.
	- Cues distinguish the intended muscular effort or stretch from loss of control or joint pain. Alternatives include a simpler movement, lighter resistance, smaller range, or additional support where useful.
	- Instances use the most specific body exercise namespace, such as `Exercise/Body/Strength/<Name>` or `Exercise/Body/Flexibility/<Name>`.
