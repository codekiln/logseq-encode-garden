- # [36.3 Playing and Programming Beats](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#playing-and-programming-beats)

		- ![The Note Mode button](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2NoteButton.png?auto=&w=291)
	- On a track with a Drum Rack, the **Layout** button cycles the 8×8 grid between three layouts.
