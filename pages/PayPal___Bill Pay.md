- # [PayPal Bill Pay](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/can-i-pay-my-bills-with-paypal-help906)
	- U.S. PayPal Personal accounts can use the app or website to pay a listed biller. The user links the bill using the account details from their statement, verifies the amount and due date, and submits the payment in PayPal. Eligible bills can also be scheduled. [PayPal Bill Pay help](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/can-i-pay-my-bills-with-paypal-help906)
	- **For a medical lab bill.** Coverage depends on whether the biller appears in PayPal’s directory and accepts the account details on the statement. If the biller is listed, the user can pay through PayPal. PayPal says some payments take several days to appear in the biller’s system. [PayPal Bill Pay help](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/can-i-pay-my-bills-with-paypal-help906)
	- **What an agent can do.** As of November 21, 2026, the documented workflow has the user submit payment in PayPal. An agent may help identify the amount and due date, but a supported integration that lets ChatGPT or Claude submit through PayPal Bill Pay is unverified. [[PayPal/Agent Ready]] documents merchant checkout for shopping, a separate workflow.
