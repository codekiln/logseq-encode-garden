- # Bootloader
	- Hold Setup while plugging in the Launchpad to enter the bootloader. Its settings are saved on the device.
	- Pad functions on the bootloader surface:
		- Top two rows of pads: USB device ID 1-16. The selected ID is lit brightly; the factory default is 1. Give each Launchpad Pro [MK3] on the same USB a different ID.
		- Yellow pad, middle right: Easy Start (Onboarding) on or off. When on, the Launchpad also appears as a Mass Storage Device for first-time setup.
		- Blue row: LED brightness, also adjustable from the Setup menu in normal operation.
		- Green pads, bottom left: show the bootloader version and the application version.
		- Green pad, bottom right: boot the application.
