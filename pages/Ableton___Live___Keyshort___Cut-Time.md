- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Cut the selected span of time out of every Arrangement track and close the gap** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Cmd+Shift+X` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+X`)
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, removes the selected time from every track, time signature markers included, shortens the Arrangement, and keeps the cut time for Paste Time. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/11 Using the Time Commands]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
