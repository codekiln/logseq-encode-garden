- # [Distract-Free Typewriter](https://www.ssp.sh/brain/distract-free-typewriter/)
	- ## Summary
		- A running survey of **writerDecks**: single-purpose writing machines, typically a full mechanical keyboard bolted to an ePaper display and nothing else. No browser, no notifications, no second application to switch into.
		- Späti owns a Micro Journal Rev. 2 and keeps his own build notes at [My Distraction-Free Typewriter (Micro Journal)](https://www.ssp.sh/brain/my-distraction-free-typewriter-micro-journal).
		- The tension the note keeps circling: focus and productivity are not the same purchase. A typewriter delivers the first without promising the second. Späti's suggestion is that a [[Neovim]] setup configured to behave like a typewriter gets past that limit.
	- ## Devices
		- ### DIY and open source
			- [Micro Journal](https://github.com/unkyulee/micro-journal) — Un Kyu Lee. The note traces back to Cassidy Williams' [Building the Micro Journal](https://cassidoo.co/post/micro-journal/).
			- [Foowrite](https://github.com/rberenguel/foowrite) — Ruben Berenguel.
			- [Framedeck](https://github.com/brickbots/framedeck/) — cyberdeck built around a Framework mainboard.
			- [USB Typewriter](https://www.usbtypewriter.com/) — kits for wiring a real typewriter up as a USB keyboard.
		- ### Commercial
			- [Zerowriter Ink](https://www.crowdsupply.com/zerowriter/zerowriter-ink) — hot-swappable mechanical keyboard, low profile. Un Kyu Lee recommends it over the Android-based options, whose screen timeouts and notifications break the flow the device was bought to protect.
			- [Freewrite](https://getfreewrite.com/)
			- [BYOK](https://byok.io/)
			- [AlphaSmart](https://hackeducation.com/2015/07/25/alphasmart) — the 1990s classroom ancestor of the whole category.
			- [MNT Pocket Reform](https://shop.mntre.com/products/mnt-pocket-reform)
		- ### Repurposed ePaper tablets
			- Boox Palma — phone-sized, 6 GB RAM, ships with the Play Store, so [[Obsidian]] installs directly and runs in Vim mode. [Typing experience walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_MWsOrvoKcM).
			- [Boox Note Air2](https://shop.boox.com/products/noteair2) — 10.3" for something closer to a page.
			- [reMarkable 2](https://remarkable.com/store/remarkable-2)
			- [Daylight DC-1](https://daylightcomputer.com/)
