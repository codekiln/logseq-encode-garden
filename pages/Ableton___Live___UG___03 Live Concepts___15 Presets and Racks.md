- # [3.15 Presets and Racks](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-concepts/#presets-and-racks)
	- Every device can save its settings as a preset. Presets live in the User Library, apart from any Set.
	- Instrument, Drum and Effect Racks save several devices and their settings as one preset. See [[Ableton/Instrument/Drum Rack]] and [[Ableton/File/.adg]].
