- # [Talmud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talmud)
	- The [[Judaism/Mishnah]] together with the [[Judaism/Gemara]], the central text of [[Judaism/Rabbinic]].
	- Two Talmuds exist. The Jerusalem Talmud, also called the Palestinian Talmud, dates from roughly the fourth and fifth centuries. The Babylonian Talmud reached its received form in roughly the fifth and sixth centuries and became far more influential.
	- A traditional printed page places the Mishnah and Gemara at the center and surrounds them with [[Person/Rashi]]'s commentary, the Tosafists' discussions, cross-references and later notes, so that one page shows generations of readers talking around one text. [[Judaism/Concept/Layered Interpretation]] describes the structure the page embodies.
