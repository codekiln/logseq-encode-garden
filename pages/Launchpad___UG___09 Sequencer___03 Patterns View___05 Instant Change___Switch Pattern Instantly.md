- # Switch Pattern Instantly
	- [[Launchpad]] [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/03 Patterns View/05 Instant Change]]
		- **In Patterns View during playback, how do you switch to another Pattern or chain right away instead of queueing it, and where does playback land?** [[Card]]
			- Hold **Shift** and press the Pattern (or select the chain).
			- The sequencer jumps in at the point the new selection would have reached had it played since playback began, so tracks stay in time.
