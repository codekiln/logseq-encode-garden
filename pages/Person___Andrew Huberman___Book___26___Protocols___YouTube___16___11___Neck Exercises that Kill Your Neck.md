- # [Neck Exercises that KILL Your Neck (DO THESE INSTEAD!!) - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wjiZaCJ6tCA)
	- Speaker: Jeff Cavaliere
	- Channel: [ATHLEAN-X](https://www.youtube.com/@athleanx) · 7:34
	- Linked from the Day 7 additional resources of [[Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols]] Chapter Two, as "Neck workout with Jeff Cavaliere." In [[Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols/YouTube/26/09/Day 4 - Major Torso Muscles]], Huberman points to it for neck training.
	- ## About
		- Forward and backward neck bridges build neck size but load the neck as a closed-chain exercise, which the neck isn't built for.
		- Compression combined with motion can grind the cervical vertebrae, leading over time to bone spurs that can impinge cervical nerve roots and cause weakness or pain down the arm.
		- Cavaliere shows neck exercises that work the same muscles without that compression.
