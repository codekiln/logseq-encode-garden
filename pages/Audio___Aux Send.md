- # Aux Send
	- A per-channel knob that sends a copy of the channel to a separate bus, apart from the main mix. The bus leaves the mixer through its own output.
	- **Pre-fader** sends ignore the channel's level control. They suit monitor mixes, which shouldn't change when the main mix does.
	- **Post-fader** sends follow the level control. They suit effects, so the reverb on a channel fades with it. A mixer's FX send is a post-fader aux.
	- The effect comes back on a return or a spare channel. In a DAW, return tracks play the same part: [[Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/14 The Mixer]].
