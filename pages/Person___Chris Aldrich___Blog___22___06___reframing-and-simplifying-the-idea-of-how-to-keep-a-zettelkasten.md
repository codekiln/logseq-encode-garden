- # [Reframing and simplifying the idea of how to keep a Zettelkasten](https://boffosocko.com/2022/06/10/reframing-and-simplifying-the-idea-of-how-to-keep-a-zettelkasten/)
	- ## Summary
		- [[Person/Chris Aldrich]] argues that most online [[Zettelkasten]] advice overweights Luhmann's addressing scheme. Start from a commonplace book: collect passages, index them, and only then add slips, bibliographic cards, rewriting in one's own words, and links — each layer optional.
	- ## Notes
		- Forget the slip box at first; a notebook of excerpts and an index (Locke's method is cited) already does most of the work.
		- Index cards make rearrangement and outlining easier; at that point the box meets the literal meaning of *Zettelkasten*.
		- Further refinement — rewriting as "permanent" or "evergreen" notes, then linking related cards — raises combinatorial surprise, but is not required.
		- Two solid notes a day until a few hundred cards is enough to feel the system; dumping unlinked highlights will take longer.
		- Eminem's "stacking ammo" is offered as a minimal artist's box: if locator codes are make-work, skip them.
