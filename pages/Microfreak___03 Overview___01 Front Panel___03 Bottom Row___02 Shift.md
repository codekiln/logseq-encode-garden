- # 03.01.03.02 Shift
	- Hold **Shift** to access functions printed in blue on the panel and other secondary functions. [[Microfreak/20 Appendix C - Cheat Sheet]] lists them.
	- The shift button
		- ![01 The shift button](../assets/Microfreak___03-Overview___01-Front-Panel___03-Bottom-Row___02-Shift___01-The-shift-button.png)
	- Shift functions
		- Toggle between Arpeggiator and Sequencer.
		- Set the swing rate.
		- Control the Attack and Fall shapes of the Cycling Envelope.
		- Transpose or reload sequences, or copy an arpeggio to a sequence.
