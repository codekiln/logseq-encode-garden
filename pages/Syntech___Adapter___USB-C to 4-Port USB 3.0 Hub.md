- # USB-C to USB 3.0 Hub, 4-Port
	- Turns one USB-C or Thunderbolt port into four USB-A ports.
	- ![syntech usb 3.0 hub](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/81CS7pK457L._AC_SL1500_.jpg)
	- ## Input
		- A [[USB/Connector/Ref/USB-C]] plug on a short braided cable. The hub has no power input of its own and runs on the power of the port it plugs into.
	- ## Output
		- Four [[USB/Connector/Ref/USB-A]] USB 3.0 ports, up to 5 Gbps, sharing that power. Syntech advises against using them to charge phones or tablets.
	- ## Included adapter
		- A separate adapter with a USB-C socket and a [[USB/Connector/Ref/USB-A]] plug lets the hub plug into a USB-A port. Through it, data runs at USB 2.0 speed, up to 480 Mbps.
	- ## Works with
		- Connects the [[Launchpad]], the [[Microfreak]] and the [[Zoom H5]] to one USB-C port on [[Macbook/My]].
