- # [Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/design-patterns-elements-of-reusable-object-oriented-software/P200000009480/9780201633610)
	- ## About
		- Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, and John Vlissides published this book with Addison-Wesley in 1994. Pearson describes a catalog of 23 recurring object-oriented design patterns, grouped into creational, structural, and behavioral patterns.
		- The book brought pattern thinking into software design after [[Person/Christopher Alexander]] and his collaborators developed pattern languages for the built environment. For the wider concept, see [[Design/Pattern]].
