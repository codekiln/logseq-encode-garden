- # [30.5 Pitch](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-midi-effect-reference/#pitch)
	- ![The Pitch Effect](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/PitchL12.png?auto=&w=206)
	- Pitch transposes incoming notes by up to ±128 semitones, or ±30 scale degrees with **Use Current Scale** on in the title bar.
	- **Pitch** sets the transposition.
	- **Step Up** and **Step Down** change Pitch by the **Step Width** (1–48 semitones, or 1–30 scale degrees). All of these can be key- or MIDI-mapped for changes during a performance.
	- **Lowest** and **Range** set the band of notes that pass. **Mode** decides what happens to notes outside it:
		- **Block** — they are dropped, and an LED next to Range flashes
		- **Fold** — they are transposed into the range
		- **Limit** — notes below are raised to the lowest note and notes above are lowered to the highest
