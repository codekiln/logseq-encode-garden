- # [ArangoDB](https://www.arangodb.com/)
	- [GitHub](https://github.com/arangodb/arangodb) — [[GitHub/Star]] count: 14275 (checked [[2026-09-20 Sun]])
	- Multi-model database: documents, graphs, and key-value in one engine; AQL is the query language.
	- Written in C++.
	- A prominent alternative to single-model [[Graph/Database]] engines when the same store also needs documents.
