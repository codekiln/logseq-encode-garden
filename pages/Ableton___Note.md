- # [Note](https://www.ableton.com/en/note/)
	- [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ableton-note/id1633243177) — $6.99, published by Ableton AG, version 2.1.0, requires [[iOS]] 15 or later. Checked [[2026-09-24 Thu]].
	- A sketchpad for iPhone and [[iPad]]: start an idea, play with sound, and find a direction away from the desk.
	- Plays through a 16-pad percussion grid, a 25-pad melody grid, or a piano roll. An external [[MIDI]] instrument connects over USB or [[Bluetooth]].
	- Carries instruments and effects from [[Ableton/Live]] — Drum Sampler and Melodic Sampler, plus Chorus-Ensemble, Delay, Phaser-Flanger, Redux, Reverb, Saturator and Channel EQ.
	- Records up to 60 seconds through the device microphone into either sampler, then cuts, filters and repitches the result.
	- Capture MIDI keeps what was just played, detecting its tempo and length and looping it.
	- Note Repeat retriggers a held pad at a chosen subdivision; Automation Capture records a parameter tweak on demand.
	- Session View holds eight tracks, eight clips per track, across eight scenes, and exports the result as an audio file.
	- [Ableton Cloud](https://help.ableton.com/hc/categories/5966520431378) sends five Note Sets to the browser in Live 11.2.5 or later.
	- [Live 12 Lite](https://www.ableton.com/en/products/live-lite/) is free to Note users, through the app.
	- [Ableton Link](https://www.ableton.com/en/link/) keeps playback in time with other devices on the same network.
