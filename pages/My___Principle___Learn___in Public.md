# Learn in Public
	- I tend to learn using deliberate [[Knowledge Gardening]]. The knowledge garden is, for me a [[Memory Palace]], and learning is, for me about calibrating two forces:
		- [[Explore]] - acquiring new knowledge of things in the world, adding new things to the garden, exploring connections, links and adjacencies between the existing garden and the world
		- [[Exploit]] - cultivating and strengthening existing knowledge, taking care of the garden, designing self-quizzes in a way that integrates into the memory palace and helps make the garden more vibrant and real
