- # POWER/HOLD switch
	- Turns the power on and off, and locks the buttons while recording.
	- ## Values
		- ⏻ — slide here to turn the power on; hold here until `Goodbye See You!` appears to turn it off.
		- Center — normal operation.
		- HOLD — the buttons stop responding and the display shows `Hold`. A remote on [[Zoom H5/UI/Area/3-Right/1-Remote-Jack]] still works.
