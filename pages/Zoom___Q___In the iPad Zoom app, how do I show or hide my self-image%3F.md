- # In the iPad [[Zoom]] app, how do I show or hide my self-image (self-view)?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- **Short answer:** During a meeting, tap the screen to bring up the meeting controls, tap **More** (the `•••` icon), then tap **Hide Self View**. To bring it back, tap the screen again and choose **Show Self View**.
		- **Alternative gesture:** Tap and hold (long-press) your own video thumbnail, then choose **Hide Self View** from the menu that appears.
		- **What this does:** Hiding self view only removes *your own* video tile from *your* screen — your camera stays on and other participants still see you normally. This is different from **Stop Video**, which turns your camera off for everyone.
		- **Sources:** [Adjusting your video layout during a virtual meeting](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0063672) (Zoom, self-view vs. stopping video); [How to Hide Self View on Zoom (mobile steps)](https://citizenside.com/technology/how-to-hide-self-view-on-zoom/)
