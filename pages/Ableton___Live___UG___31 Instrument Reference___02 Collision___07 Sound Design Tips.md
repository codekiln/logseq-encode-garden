- # [31.2.7 Sound Design Tips](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#sound-design-tips)
	- For a realistic marimba, follow the physical chain: the Mallet strikes a tuned bar (Resonator 1), and a tube (Resonator 2) amplifies it, in serial 1 > 2.
	- For unrealistic sounds:
		- Use the Noise exciter with long envelopes for washy, near-granular textures, or effects like bowed vibraphone and crystal glasses.
		- Try the parallel 1 + 2 structure.
		- Modulate with the LFOs and MIDI or MPE controllers.
	- The models are idealized and can respond far more strongly than real objects. Some settings jump sharply in volume, so keep levels low while experimenting.
