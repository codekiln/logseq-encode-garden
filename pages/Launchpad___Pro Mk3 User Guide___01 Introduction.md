- # 1. Introduction
	- Launchpad Pro is a grid controller for producing and performing with Ableton Live or external MIDI hardware.
	- Its 64 velocity- and pressure-sensitive RGB pads can launch clips, play notes, and sequence steps. The four-track, 32-step sequencer provides probability and mutation controls for evolving patterns.
	- Eight Custom Modes can send MIDI to software or hardware. The two MIDI output ports extend control to external instruments.
	- The unit can operate without a computer when powered through its USB-C port by the included wall adapter.
