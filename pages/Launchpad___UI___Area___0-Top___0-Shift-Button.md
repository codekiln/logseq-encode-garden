- # Shift button
	- Hold Shift to reach the secondary function printed at the bottom of a button. While Shift is held, available functions glow gold and unavailable ones stay unlit; toggles such as Record Quantise glow red when off and green when on.
	- In Session mode, hold Shift and press a pad to select its clip or empty slot without launching it.
	- In the sequencer's Patterns view, hold Shift and press a Pattern or Scene during playback to switch to it right away; without Shift, the switch waits for the current Pattern to end.
	- In the sequencer's Pattern Settings view, hold Shift to show the Pattern's start point, and press a step to set it.
	- ## Remote control
		- Programmer mode: CC 90
