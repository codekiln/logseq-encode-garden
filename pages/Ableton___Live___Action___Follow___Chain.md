- # Follow Action Chain
	- In [[Ableton/Live]], a **Follow Action Chain** sets several selected [clips]([[Ableton/Clip]]) to play one after another in a loop, using [[Ableton/Live/Action/Follow]]. The clips need not sit in adjacent slots ([[Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions/02 Creating Cycles]]).
	- **Create Follow Action Chain** is in a clip's context menu, or press `Cmd+Shift+Enter`: [[Ableton/Live/Action/Follow/Chain/Keyshort/Create from Selected Clips]].
	- Giving every clip in a group the Next action makes a similar cycle among adjacent clips.
