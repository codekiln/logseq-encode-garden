- # [Johann Sebastian Bach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Sebastian_Bach)
	- ## About
		- German composer and organist of the late Baroque ([[16/8/5]]–[[17/5/0]]).
		- His Toccata and Fugue in D minor, BWV 538, is nicknamed "Dorian" because it is written without a key signature.
