- # Anhemitonic
	- Describes a scale with no semitones between adjacent steps. From the Greek *an-* ("without") and *hēmitonion* ("half tone").
	- The opposite is hemitonic: a scale with one or more semitone steps.
	- The word is used mostly to separate the two families of pentatonic scale.
		- Anhemitonic: [[Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major]] and its modes, including [[Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor]].
		- Hemitonic: [[Music/Scale/Hirajoshi]] and the other Japanese in-scale pentatonics.
	- [[Music/Scale/Whole Tone]] is also anhemitonic. Every seven-note scale in twelve-tone equal temperament is hemitonic.
