- # [28.2.1 Video Clips in the Arrangement View](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/working-with-video/#video-clips-in-the-arrangement-view)
	- A video clip in Arrangement View looks like an audio clip with "sprocket holes" in its title bar.
		- ![A video clip in Arrangement View](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/VideoClipInArranger.png?auto=&w=320)
	- It edits like an audio clip, but Consolidate, Reverse and Crop turn it into a plain audio clip inside the Set. The movie file is never changed.
