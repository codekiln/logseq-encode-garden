- # Ableton Track
	- In this garden, **Ableton Track** pages model one audio or MIDI track in an [[Logseq/Entity/Ableton/Set]].
	- A track page sits at `<performance>/Set/Track/<group name>/<track name>`, or at `<performance>/Set/Track/<track name>` when the track is outside any group. Its parent, a [[Logseq/Entity/Ableton/Track/Group]] or the Set, embeds it.
	- The shared names and page shape live on [[Logseq/Entity/Ableton/Set]].
