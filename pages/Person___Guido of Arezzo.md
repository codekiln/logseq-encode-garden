- # [Guido of Arezzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guido_of_Arezzo)
	- ## About
		- Italian music theorist and teacher, a Benedictine monk (born around [[9/9/1]], died after [[10/3/3]]).
		- Credited with staff notation and with the solmization syllables ut re mi fa sol la, taken from the hymn *Ut queant laxis*.
