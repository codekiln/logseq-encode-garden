- # [31.9.7 Glide and Spread](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#glide-and-spread)
	- **Glide** — polyphonic: new notes start at the last note's pitch and slide over **Glide Time**.
		- ![Operator's Pitch Display and Shell Parameters](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/OperatorPitchL12.png)
	- **Spread** — two detuned voices per note panned left and right for chorus. It is read at note-on, so a sequence can make only some notes stereo. Costs CPU.
	- **Transpose** — global.
