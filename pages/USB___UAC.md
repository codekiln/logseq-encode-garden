- # USB Audio Class (UAC)
	- The USB device-class standard for audio interfaces. A device that follows it is **class compliant**: the operating system's built-in driver runs it, with nothing to install.
	- **UAC 1.0** runs at USB full speed, which limits the channel count and sample rate.
	- **UAC 2.0** runs at USB high speed, adding more channels and higher sample rates. macOS and Linux include a UAC 2.0 driver, and Windows has one built in from Windows 10 version 1703.
	- iPhone and iPad run class-compliant interfaces through a USB-C port or a Lightning camera adapter.
	- Behringer's USB mixers, such as the [[Behringer/Mixer/XENYX 802S]], are UAC 2.0.
