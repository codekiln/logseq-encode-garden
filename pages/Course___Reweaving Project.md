- # [Reweaving Project](https://reweavingproject.org/)
	- ## About
		- **Reweaving Ourselves: Leadership in a World on Fire** is Rebecca Henderson's interdisciplinary course on personal practice, relationships, and systemic change amid ecological, social, and political crises.
		- The course examines whether disconnection from ourselves, one another, and the natural world contributes to these crises, and how inner work might support collective action.
		- Readings, discussion, practitioner visits, and experiential exercises connect organizational governance, grief, power, love, and democratic renewal with participants' choices about their own lives.
	- ## Spring 2027 offering
		- The project website describes a free, in-person cohort of twenty-five participants in Cambridge, Massachusetts, meeting Wednesdays from 4–7 p.m. The intended audience spans professions, backgrounds, and levels of experience with contemplative practice.
		- Classes are to be filmed and edited for later online release. Participants must be comfortable with filming; the website offers an opportunity to request edits after each class.
