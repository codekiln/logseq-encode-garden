- # MIDI Out 1 jack
	- Sends MIDI to external hardware. Note, Chord, Custom, Sequencer and Programmer modes can send MIDI out of it, so the sequencer can play hardware without a computer.
	- ## Remote control
		- Over USB, a computer reaches the MIDI jacks on the `LPProMK3 DIN In/Out` interface.
