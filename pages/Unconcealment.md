- # Unconcealment
	- Unconcealment is the coming into view or hearing of something that had been hidden or unnoticed.
	- In Heidegger's account of truth, unconcealment translates the sense of [[aletheia]]. It describes an event of appearing, rather than only the correctness of a statement.
	- A [[GitP/Light]] is a small, musical instance of unconcealment: a preset's character becomes audible through a particular sound, register, or gesture.
