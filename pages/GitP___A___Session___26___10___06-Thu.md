- # GitP.26.10.06 — Chords, Formants, and Sequencers
	- A MicroFreak improvisation starting from an initialized patch: chord oscillators, cycling-envelope and LFO modulation, and a turn toward formant sounds. Shimmering textures give way to a gruff emergence, a ballpark organ, and a trippy denouement, with the Launchpad sequencer and MicroFreak arpeggiator along the way.

	- ## Session Notes
		- Started on an [[Microfreak/Preset/Initialized]].
		- Flipped to [[Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc/03 Types/10 Chords]].
			- I tried to map the cycling envelope to the wave and other parameters. Later, mapped the LFO to those.
		- Later on, I invoked the [[Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc/03 Types/09 Formant]] for variety and low emphasis.
		- Early on, I used [[Launchpad/UG/05 Session Mode/06 Production Controls/03 Tap Tempo]] to set it to 109. I'm not sure how much it ended up influencing the pulse on the microfreak, which may or may not have been calibrated to receive tempo from [[Ableton/Live]], but I did use the [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer]] just a bit at about `12 min 35 sec`.
			- The effect wasn't especially musical; I just wanted to keep some pattern going while I adjusted the sounds on the Microfreak more.
			- I got a bit confused at one point, since [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/05 Pattern Settings]] and [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View]] look fairly similar, and the bottom two rows are actually playable pads in both. The main difference is that there's four lights missing in lower left half of Pattern Settings so as to demarcate that the third row from the bottom on the right side is a [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/05 Pattern Settings/02 Direction]] selector. I erroneously thought I was on step mode but I was in pattern settings mode.
		- I didn't end up recording any [[Microfreak/Preset]] this time.
		- When looking through the recording later, I made a few notes.
