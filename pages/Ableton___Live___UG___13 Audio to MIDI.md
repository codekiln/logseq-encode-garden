- # [13. Converting Audio to MIDI](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/converting-audio-to-midi/)
	- Commands that turn audio into MIDI: slicing, and converting harmony, melody or drums into a new MIDI track.
	- Besides warping, Live can pull musical information out of an audio clip and write it into a MIDI clip.
	- With an audio clip selected, the Create menu and the clip's context menu offer four commands: Slice to New MIDI Track, and Convert Harmony, Melody or Drums to New MIDI Track.
