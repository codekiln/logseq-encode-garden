- # West-Coast Synth Aesthetic
	- In the 1960s-1990s and beyond, the [[US/Coast/West]] synth community traditionally encouraged a [[Zen]]-like "be-present" mentality, leaning into the 1-time ephemerality and randomness of a given musical session, asking its musicians to start with an empty synth and gradually patch up every time.
