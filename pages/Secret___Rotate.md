- # Secret rotation
	- Replacing a stored secret's value with a new one — on a schedule, on demand, or after suspected exposure — without breaking whatever consumes it.
	- See [[AWS/Secrets Manager/Secret/Rotation]] for what one specific secrets manager supports (and doesn't) for this natively.
