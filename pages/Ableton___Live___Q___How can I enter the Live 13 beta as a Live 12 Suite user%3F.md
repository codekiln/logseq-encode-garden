- # How can I enter the [[Ableton/Live]] 13 beta as a Live 12 Suite user?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- [[Answer/Official]] from [Ableton Beta Program](https://www.ableton.com/en/beta/)
		- **Short answer:** apply on Ableton's Centercode portal. A Live 12 Suite license qualifies, but seats are limited, so applying does not guarantee a place.
		- **Who qualifies:** the beta page says "The latest Live 13 Beta is available to all owners of Live 7 - 12 Standard and Suite." Intro and Lite licenses are not on that list.
		- **Steps:**
			- Open the [Join the Live 13 Public Beta test!](https://ableton.centercode.com/key/l13prpbapplic) page on Centercode, linked from the Ableton beta page as "our Live Beta testing portal on Centercode".
			- Sign in with Google or an email account, or create a Centercode account, and choose **Apply to this test**.
			- Wait to be accepted. The beta page says "Seats are limited."
			- Once accepted, download the beta and authorize it with the Ableton account that holds the Live 12 Suite license. Ableton's [Live Beta FAQ](https://help.ableton.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001663870-Live-Beta-FAQ) says a major-version beta will not authorize without a Standard or Suite license.
		- **Push 3:** the Push beta, version 13, has a separate portal. Claim a Live beta seat first, then take the serial number it gives you to the Push beta portal.
		- Live 13 was announced on 2026-11-10, with release planned for early 2027 ([Beatportal](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/1721100-ableton-announces-live-13-with-step-sequencer-drum-synth-and-ara-2)).
