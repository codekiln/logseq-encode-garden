- # [21.5 Auditioning Take Lanes](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/comping/#auditioning-take-lanes)
	- Click a take lane's speaker button, or press `T`, to audition it.
		- ![The Audition Take Lane button](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/CompingAuditionTakeLaneButtonL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Lanes on different tracks can play at the same time, but only one lane per track. With several selected, the last-selected lane on each track plays.
