- # Consciousness
	- ## Overview
		- Consciousness is subjective experience: there is something it is like to perceive, feel, think, or be aware.
	- ## Questions
		- Research asks how conscious experience relates to brain activity, which organisms or systems experience it, and how it can be studied from both first-person reports and observable evidence.
		- Philosophical accounts disagree about whether experience can be fully explained by physical processes and about what evidence would resolve that question.
	- ## In [[Person/Michael Pollan/Book/26/A World Appears]]
		- Michael Pollan surveys scientific and philosophical approaches to consciousness alongside literary, spiritual, and psychedelic perspectives.
