- # [35.6 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-1/#step-sequencing-melodies-and-harmonies)
	- Press Note again for the **Melodic Sequencer**, where all eight rows place notes. With In Key each row is a scale pitch; with Chromatic, in-key rows are lit. The light blue row, bottom by default, is the root. Each column is a step at the Scene/Grid resolution.
	- Hold Note for the loop length pads in the top row; `Shift` + Note locks them per track.
	- Octave Up and Down or the touch strip shift the pitch range; `Shift` fine-tunes it. Bright touch strip lights show the visible range and dim lights show where the clip has notes.
	- Note again switches to Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes. Velocity and micro-timing are in [[Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/12 Step Sequencing Automation]].
