- # Music as a Gradual Process
	- ## About
		- Reich's 1968 set of short statements about music that is itself a process, which he wants listeners to hear happening. It first appeared in the catalogue for the Whitney Museum exhibition *Anti-Illusion: Procedures/Materials* (1969), and is reprinted on pages 34–36 of [[Person/Steve Reich/Book/02/Writings on Music, 1965-2000]].
