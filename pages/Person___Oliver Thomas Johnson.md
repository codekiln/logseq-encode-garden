- # [Oliver Thomas Johnson](https://www.dorianconcept.com/)
	- ## About
		- Austrian electronic producer and keyboardist who releases music as Dorian Concept. Based in Vienna; his music draws on jazz, ambient and club music.
		- [Ninja Tune biography](https://ninjatune.net/artist/dorian-concept) describes his keyboard improvisations and recording process; [The Nature of Imitation release notes](https://downloads.ninjatune.net/release/dorian-concept/the-nature-of-imitation) give his full name.
