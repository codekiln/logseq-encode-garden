- # USB functions
	- In MULTI TRACK mode the H5 is a four-in, two-out USB audio interface; direct monitoring lets performers hear inputs without delay. PHONE and LINE OUT both play the computer's one stereo mix, so a click only the performer hears needs another audio output, such as the computer headphone port.
	- [Read in the official manual](https://zoomcorp.com/media/documents/E_H5_v2.pdf#page=70)
