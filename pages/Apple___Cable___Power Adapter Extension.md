- # Power Adapter Extension Cable
	- An AC extension cord that takes the place of the removable plug on an Apple power adapter, so the adapter can sit away from the outlet.
	- ## Ends
		- Grounded three-prong North American plug, into the outlet
		- Apple's AC connector, slid onto the power adapter where its plug was
	- ## Length
		- 1.8 m
	- ## Carries
		- Mains power
	- ## Works with
		- [[Apple/Adapter/140W USB-C Power]], and Apple's other USB-C and MagSafe power adapters with a removable plug
	- ## Source
		- [Power Adapter Extension Cable - Apple](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MW2N3LL/A/power-adapter-extension-cable)
