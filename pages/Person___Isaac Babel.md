- # Isaac Babel
	- **Role:** Russian-Soviet journalist, playwright, and short-story writer.
	- **Bio:** Best known for *Red Cavalry* and the *Odessa Tales*. His 1932 short story "Guy de Maupassant" contains a widely quoted line about the power of the period; [[Person/Raymond Carver]] cites it in his essay "On Writing," and [[Person/Gary Saul Morson]] has noted that English translations of the line vary considerably from the Russian.
