- # [Douglas Lenat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Lenat)
	- ## About
		- American computer scientist and artificial intelligence researcher who founded and led Cycorp.
		- Known for the AM, Eurisko, and Cyc projects, and for pioneering work in knowledge representation and ontological engineering.
		- Born September 13, 1950; died August 31, 2023.
	- ## Knowledge principles
		- In [On the Thresholds of Knowledge](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/000437029190055O), Douglas Lenat and Edward Feigenbaum argued that intelligent systems need substantial, explicit knowledge about the world.
		- **Knowledge Principle:** competent performance depends primarily on the concepts, facts, representations, methods, models, metaphors, and heuristics a system can bring to bear—not on search and reasoning alone.
		- **Explicit Knowledge Principle:** knowledge may be compiled into opaque code for efficiency, but a declarative form should remain available for inspection and meta-reasoning.
		- **Breadth Hypothesis:** intelligent behavior in unexpected situations requires fallback to increasingly general knowledge and analogy to specific knowledge from distant domains.
		- **Empirical Inquiry Hypothesis:** investigate AI by building programs and testing them on large, reality-bearing problems; premature mathematization and toy problems can hide important details.
