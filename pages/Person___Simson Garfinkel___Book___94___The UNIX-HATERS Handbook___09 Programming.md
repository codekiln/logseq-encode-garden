- # 9 Programming — Hold Still, This Won't Hurt a Bit
	- Summary:: Examines the Unix programming environment, its abstractions, makefiles, debugging assumptions, and the habit of restarting when systems fail.
	- Why it is included:: Argues that Unix's reputation as a programmer's system hides brittle tools and workflows that make software development harder to reason about.
