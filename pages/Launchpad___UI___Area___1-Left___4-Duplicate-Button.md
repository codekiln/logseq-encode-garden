- # Duplicate button
	- In Session mode, hold Duplicate and press a clip to copy it into the slot below, replacing any clip there.
	- In Note mode, press Duplicate to copy the selected clip and select the copy.
	- In Sequencer mode, hold Duplicate and press a step, Pattern, Scene or Micro Step, then press one or more others of the same kind to copy it there.

		- [[Launchpad/UI/Area/1-Left/4-Duplicate-Button/Shift/Double]]
