- # [Interim Computer Museum](https://icm.museum/?visit)
	- The Interim Computer Museum (ICM) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that preserves and shares the history of computing through hands-on exhibits using vintage hardware with modern enhancements.
	- ICM operates in partnership with SDF Public Access UNIX System, Inc., a 501(c)(7) membership organization. Membership supports community events, remote access, and artifact preservation.
	- ## Visit
		- The museum is open by appointment to supporters Saturday through Monday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.
		- The museum is at SDF.ORG, 952 Industry Dr., [[Tukwila]], WA 98188, south of Seattle and about 10 minutes from [[Seattle-Tacoma International Airport]] (SEATAC).
