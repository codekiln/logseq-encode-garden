- # [26.2 Recording Automation in Arrangement View](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/automation/#recording-automation-in-arrangement-view)
	- In Arrangement View, automation records on all tracks during Arrangement recording when [[Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Automation Arm]] is on. Individual track arming is unnecessary for automation.
	- [[Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Arrangement Record]] starts transport and recording; then changes to tempo, mixer and devices are recorded.
	- Press Arrangement Record again to stop recording but keep playing, or [[Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Stop]] to stop both.
	- The result appears in the tracks' automation lanes.
		- ![A track's automation envelope in an automation lane](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/AutomationLaneL12.png?auto=&w=320)
