- # [ChatGPT Free](https://chatgpt.com/pricing)
	- The no-cost tier of [[ChatGPT]], open to anyone with an account.
	- As of [[2026-11-21 Sat]], per the [pricing page](https://chatgpt.com/pricing): [[ChatGPT/Work]] and [[Codex]] are limited, and uploads, images, voice, deep research and memory have low caps.
	- Free accounts in the US may see ads, a test that began on 2026-02-09 per the [release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes).
	- Shopping search and product comparison are available; purchases happen on the merchant's own site.
