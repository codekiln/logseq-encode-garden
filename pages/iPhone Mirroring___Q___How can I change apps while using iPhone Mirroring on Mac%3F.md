	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- [[Answer/Official]] from [Use iPhone Mirroring on your Mac – Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchl6a91d321/mac)
		- Short answer: switch apps the same way you would on the Mac itself — the iPhone Mirroring window behaves like a regular app window, not a fixed single-app view.
		- **From within the mirrored window:**
			- Swipe up from the bottom of the mirrored window (like the iPhone Home gesture) to return to the iPhone Home Screen, then tap a different app's icon.
			- Swipe left/right along the bottom edge of the mirrored window to switch between recently used apps (mirrors the iPhone's app-switcher swipe gesture).
		- **From macOS, outside the mirrored window itself:**
			- Recently used iPhone apps also appear as separate icons in the Mac Dock while mirroring is active — click one directly to bring that app to the front in the mirrored window.
			- `Cmd+Tab` on the Mac switches between Mac apps as usual, including the iPhone Mirroring window as one entry; it does not switch between iPhone apps inside the mirrored session.
		- Note: iPhone Mirroring requires the iPhone to stay locked with a passcode/Face ID/Touch ID and on the same Wi-Fi network (or paired via Bluetooth) as the Mac; it is unavailable while the iPhone is actively being used or unlocked nearby.
