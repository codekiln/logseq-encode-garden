- # Right column
	- The eight buttons down the right edge of the grid, one beside each row. They launch scenes in Session mode. In Sequencer mode they open the sequencer's views, and the bottom one prints a Pattern to an Ableton Live clip.
	- With [[Launchpad/UI/Area/4-Bottom/0-Setup-Button]] held, the green button in this column chooses Live mode and the orange button at the bottom chooses Programmer mode.
