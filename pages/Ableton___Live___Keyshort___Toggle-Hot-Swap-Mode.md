- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Swap the selected device's preset by browsing in place** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Q`
		- Description: Links the selected device, or a Drum Rack pad's sample, to the browser, so that loading an item replaces it; press `Q` or `Esc` again to leave. The device list calls it Hot-Swap Selected Device. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects/02 Using Devices/04 Hot-Swapping Presets]], [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/01 Showing and Hiding Views]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/04 Working with Devices and Plug-Ins]].
