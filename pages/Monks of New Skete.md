- # [Monks of New Skete](https://www.newskete.org/)
	- Orthodox Christian monastic community in Cambridge, New York, founded in 1966. It is one of the two communities at New Skete, alongside the Nuns of New Skete, founded in 1969.
	- The monks breed and train German shepherds, and train dogs of other breeds, which is the work their books come out of.
	- Wrote [[Book/The Art of Raising a Puppy]] and the earlier *How to Be Your Dog's Best Friend*.
