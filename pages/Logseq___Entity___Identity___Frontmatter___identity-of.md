- # `identity-of::` — people behind a public identity
	- On a [[Logseq/Entity/Identity]] instance, links the known real people behind that public identity.
	- ## Values
		- One or more comma-separated links to person hubs, including [[Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker]] instances. Omit the property when the people are unknown.
		- A solo identity normally links one person; a collective identity may link several. The property is neither an exhaustive membership history nor a claim of current membership.
		- Specific membership periods and roles belong in the identity's body. A work's individual credits require evidence about that work.
	- ## Example
		- On [[Bibio]], `identity-of::` links [[Person/Stephen Wilkinson]].
