- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Time-stretch a warped Arrangement clip to a new length** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Shift`-drag in the clip title bar
		- Description: In the Arrangement View, dragging a warped clip's edge in its title bar with `Shift` stretches the audio to fill the new length; a plain drag trims or extends it instead. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/16 Arrangement View]].
