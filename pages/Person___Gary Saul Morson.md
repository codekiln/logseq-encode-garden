- # Gary Saul Morson
	- **Role:** Literary scholar; professor of Slavic languages and literatures, Northwestern University.
	- **Bio:** Scholar of Russian literature who has commented that English translations of [[Person/Isaac Babel]] vary considerably, rendering Babel's line from "Guy de Maupassant" more literally as "No iron can enter the heart as icily as a period placed in time."
