- # [23. Working with Instruments and Effects](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/working-with-instruments-and-effects/)
	- The Device View and how MIDI effects, instruments and audio effects sit in a track's device chain, including delay compensation.
	- Every track can hold devices of three kinds:
		- **MIDI effects** — work on MIDI; MIDI tracks only.
		- **Audio effects** — work on audio; audio, return and Main tracks, or MIDI tracks after an instrument.
		- **Instruments** — take MIDI in and put audio out; MIDI tracks only.
	- Individual devices are described in [[Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference]], [[Ableton/Live/UG/30 MIDI Effect Reference]] and [[Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference]]; grouping them is covered in [[Ableton/Live/UG/25 Racks]]; sharing one device across tracks through returns is in [[Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO]].
