- # Gerd Gigerenzer
	- German psychologist, born 3 September 1947 in Wallersdorf, emeritus director at the Max Planck Institute for Human Development and director of the Harding Center for Risk Literacy at the University of Potsdam.[^1]
	- Studies bounded rationality and heuristics: how people infer and decide with limited time and knowledge.[^1]
	- Co-author, with Peter M. Todd and the ABC Research Group, of *Simple Heuristics That Make Us Smart* (Oxford University Press, 1999), on fast and frugal heuristics as a model of bounded rationality: [[Satis/fice]].[^2]
