- # Ghost in the Patch Participant
	- A person who listens to a [[Ghost in the Patch]] episode, on the website or in a [[Podcast/Catcher]]. **Partis** is the short plural.
	- [[Person/codekiln]] calls listeners participants because each episode is a ceremony they take part in. Over time the ceremony is meant to become two-way, with participants answering in sounds of their own.
	- Pages that participants read, such as [[Logseq/Entity/Podcast/Episode/GitP/A]] episodes, are written for them first. [[GitP/Vibe]] describes how those pages speak.
