- # [Drukqs (Expanded Edition)](https://warp.net/releases/86219-drukqs)
	- Digital expanded edition of [[Aphex Twin]]'s double album Drukqs, first released by [[Label/Warp Records]] on 22 October 2001. Released on 13 November 2026, after Warp's 25th anniversary reissue, it adds five tracks (31–35) to the original thirty. 35 tracks, about 1 hr 59 min; [Deezer listing](https://www.deezer.com/album/1106021492).
	- Many original titles are in Cornish, such as Jynweythek ("machine"). Three of the new tracks are versions of Avril 14th; two were re-recorded in 2009 on a Nagra tape recorder.
