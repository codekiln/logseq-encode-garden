- # [Rebecca Henderson](https://pll.harvard.edu/instructor/rebecca-m-henderson)
	- ## About
		- Scholar of organizational change, innovation, and the role of business in building a sustainable economy. Her work examines how established firms respond to major technological shifts and how organizational purpose can support systemic change.
		- Research fellow at the National Bureau of Economic Research and fellow of the British Academy and the American Academy of Arts and Sciences. [Harvard Salata Institute biography](https://salatainstitute.harvard.edu/faculty/rebecca-henderson/)
	- ## Academic Appointment
		- John and Natty McArthur University Professor at [[Uni/Harvard]], with a joint appointment in the General Management and Strategy units at Harvard Business School.
	- ## Selected Publications
		- [[Person/Rebecca Henderson/Book/20/Reimagining Capitalism]] (2020) explores business responses to environmental and social crisis; it was shortlisted for the FT/McKinsey Business Book of the Year award.
		- [Innovation in the 21st Century: Architectural Change, Purpose, and the Challenges of Our Time](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3746), *Management Science* 67, no. 9 (2021): 5479–5488 — organizational purpose and systemic innovation.
		- [Research and cases](https://rebeccahenderson.com/about/research-and-cases/) — selected scholarship on organizational change, purpose, and capitalism.
	- ## Teaching
		- Developed [[Course/Reweaving Project]], connecting personal and collective inner work with transformative systemic change. The spring 2027 syllabus brings readings, case discussions, experiential practice, and learning circles together. [Reweaving Project](https://reweavingproject.org/)
		- Teaches Reimagining Capitalism in the Harvard Business School MBA program.
