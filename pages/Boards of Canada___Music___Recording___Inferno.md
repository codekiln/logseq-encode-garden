- # [Inferno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inferno_(Boards_of_Canada_album))
	- Fifth studio album by [[Boards of Canada]], released by [[Label/Warp Records]] on 29 May 2026. It is their first studio album since Tomorrow's Harvest (2013). 18 tracks, 69:51.
	- Recorded at their Hexagon Sun studio in the Pentland Hills, Scotland. The physical release credits all writing to Mike Sandison; ASCAP lists Marcus Eoin as co-writer on four tracks.
