- # MIDI In jack
	- Receives MIDI from external hardware. While the sequencer plays with [[Launchpad/UI/Area/1-Left/8-Record-Button]] on, notes arriving here are recorded into the sequencer.
	- ## Remote control
		- Over USB, a computer reaches the MIDI jacks on the `LPProMK3 DIN In/Out` interface.
