- # [Paper](https://paper.design/)
	- AI-native design platform (browser and desktop) whose canvas is real HTML and CSS, so designs share web standards with production code and coding agents.
	- Connects designers, engineers, and agents on one canvas: MCP integrations, design-to-code sync, and real content/data pulled into layers.
	- Raised a $34M Series A (Accel and ICONIQ, Jul 2026) after a $4.2M seed; used at companies including Ramp, Lovable, Vercel, and PostHog.
	- Open-source side projects live under [paper-design](https://github.com/paper-design) (e.g. shaders); the design app itself is proprietary.
