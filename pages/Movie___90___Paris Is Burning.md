- # [Paris Is Burning](https://www.criterion.com/films/29647)
	- ## About
		- Jennie Livingston's documentary about New York City's 1980s ballroom scene and the Black and Latino queer people who built its houses, competitions, and performance styles.
		- The film was released in 1990 and selected for the U.S. National Film Registry in 2016 for preservation.
	- ## Context
		- Its interviews and footage document ballroom participants' accounts of family, gender, race, class, and aspiration. The film's authorship and representation have also prompted debate about a white filmmaker documenting a community of color and about who receives recognition and economic benefit.
		- The film is an important source for understanding ballroom culture, but it is one documentary made from a particular perspective, not a complete account of the community.
