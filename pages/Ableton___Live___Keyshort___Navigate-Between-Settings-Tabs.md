- [[Keyshort]] [[Ableton/Live]] [[Ableton/Live/Keyshort]]
	- **Switch to the next or previous tab of the Settings window** [[Card]]
		- Shortcut: `Option+Tab` / `Option+Shift+Tab` (Win `Ctrl+Tab` / `Ctrl+Shift+Tab`)
		- Description: In the Settings, moves between tabs such as Display & Input and Audio; the arrows on the focused Settings Page Chooser do the same. See [[Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/26 General Keyboard Navigation and Workflow/02 Navigating Between Controls in the Settings Menu]].
