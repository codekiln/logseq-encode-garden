- # [Dummy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dummy_(album))
	- Debut studio album by [[Portishead]], released on 22 August 1994. Recorded at State of Art and Coach House Studios in Bristol and produced by the band. It won the 1995 Mercury Music Prize.
	- ## Production
		- Built with hip hop techniques: sampling, scratching and loops. The band recorded their own parts to vinyl, then sampled and manipulated those records on turntables. To age the sound they walked on the records and played them through a broken amplifier.
		- Samples include Lalo Schifrin's "The Danube Incident" on Sour Times, Weather Report's "Elegant People" on Strangers, War's "Magic Mountain" on Wandering Star, Johnnie Ray's "I'll Never Fall in Love Again" on Biscuit and Isaac Hayes's "Ike's Rap II" on Glory Box.
	- ## Personnel
		- [[Person/Beth Gibbons]] — vocals
		- [[Person/Geoff Barrow]] — Rhodes piano, drums, programming, string arrangements
		- [[Person/Adrian Utley]] — guitar, bass guitar, theremin, Hammond organ, string arrangements
		- Additional players include Clive Deamer on drums and Andy Hague on trumpet; Dave McDonald engineered.
