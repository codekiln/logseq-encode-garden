- # 12 Security — Oh, I'm Sorry, Sir, Go Ahead, I Didn't Realize You Were Root
	- Summary:: Surveys Unix's security model, holes in its defenses, privilege mistakes, and the worms and compromises that exploit them.
	- Why it is included:: Shows that Unix's assumptions about trusted users and discretionary privilege become dangerous when systems are networked and widely shared.
