- # Ghost in the Patch - Front of House vs Back of House
	- In [[Restaurant]]s, the back of house staff prepare the food and run the kitchen, and the front of house staff take orders and serve the public. To be both great and sustainable, a restaurant requires these two types of experts to work in careful symbiosis.
	- ## Front of House Staff Qualities - [[GitP/Front of House]]
		- Oriented at serving the public and ensuring the highest experience of the customer.
		- Salespeople; responsible for beauty, clarity, suitability and elegance in all things that the public can see.
		- Friendly, sociable, responsive, great communicators.
		- Akin to the UX and product teams in software.
	- ## Back of House Staff Qualities - [[GitP/Back of House]]
		- Oriented at producing a high quality product on time at a great value.
		- Responsible for the "how" of production and the pipelines that feed it.
		- Great collaborators and systems thinkers. Always finding ways to make the product better.
		- Akin to the engineering and builder teams in software.
