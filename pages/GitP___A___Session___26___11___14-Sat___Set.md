	- ## The performance and this copy
		- Live saved the Set during the performance as `26.11.14-Fallveter-04-perf-pre.als`. It kept recording for about an hour; the performance is the first 4:40, between the locators "1" and "end". At 60 BPM a beat lasts one second, so beat numbers in the Arrangement read as seconds of the episode. [[Ableton/Live/UG/07 Session View/05 Recording Sessions into the Arrangement]]
		- This page describes the copy saved afterwards, `26.11.14-Fallveter-04-perf-post.als`, which rendered the episode. In it, MF AUDIO 1 and MF AUDIO 2 are switched off and the rec archive tracks play to Master, so the MicroFreak comes from the archive recordings. The drums were raised by 4 dB, reverb sends were added to the archive tracks, and the Arrangement ends at 4:40.
	- ## Signal path
		- The [[Microfreak]] arrives on external inputs 1/2 at both MF AUDIO 1 (orange) and MF AUDIO 2 (green). Both are armed with monitoring on Auto, so each records takes from the MicroFreak and loops them in Session View. [[Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/01 Monitoring]]
		- Rec MF AUDIO 1 and rec MF AUDIO 2 record the two MicroFreak tracks after their faders. [[Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/05 Internal Routings]]
		- POOL PIANO DRUMS (purple) listens to channel 2 of the Launchpad Pro's MIDI port, where Launchpad sequencer track 2 plays the Piano Pool drum rack. Cueball Drums (coral) listens to channel 4. [[Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/13 Settings/05 MIDI Channel]]
		- LPP to MF passes the Launchpad's channel 1 to the MicroFreak but carried no notes during the performance. MF to LPP is not armed, so the MicroFreak's keys left no MIDI.
		- No track is assigned to either side of the crossfader. [[Ableton/Live/UG/18 Mixing/05 Using Live's Crossfader]]
		- A-Reverb holds a Reverb and B-Delay a Delay.
	- ## Session View
		- Sixteen unnamed scenes. "silence" clips sit in scenes 1, 8 and 9 on the four main tracks, in scene 16 on MF AUDIO 1 and MF AUDIO 2, and in scenes 1 to 9 on the END tracks.
		- The performance takes sit in scenes 2 to 4: "MF AUDIO 1 22", "MF AUDIO 1 24-1" and "MF AUDIO 1 27-1" on MF AUDIO 1, and "MF AUDIO 2 8" and "MF AUDIO 2 11-1" on MF AUDIO 2.
