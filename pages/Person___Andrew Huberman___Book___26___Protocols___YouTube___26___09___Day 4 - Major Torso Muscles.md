- # [Day 4: Major Torso Muscles - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UEU6NYLRi6o)
	- Speaker: [[Person/Andrew Huberman]]
	- Channel: [Andrew Huberman](https://www.youtube.com/@hubermanlab) · 14:04 · unlisted
	- Book: [[Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols]], Chapter Two: Exercise
	- ## About
		- Torso day of the Chapter Two resistance training program: chest, back, core, and neck.
	- ## [[Video]]
		- {{video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UEU6NYLRi6o}}
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 7}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Barbell Bench Press]]
			  id:: cef17f00-8c35-4496-a037-a97e00f17a21
				- Compound movement for the chest that also works the front and side deltoids and the triceps. Grip just outside shoulder width, lower the bar slowly to the chest, press up.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 68}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Dumbbell Bench Press]]
			  id:: e79ae95f-5009-47e5-921d-e100fb2df8a2
				- Dumbbells can be dropped to the side if a rep fails; getting into position with a knee kick-up takes care.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 125}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Incline Bench Press]]
			  id:: 484942d7-73a6-4455-a03c-fb4fd473bfe9
				- Barbell or dumbbell; shifts work toward the upper chest and front deltoids. Dumbbells allow a deeper stretch at the bottom.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 195}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Dip]]
			  id:: 1792ee0a-d1c7-4331-921b-49055187aeec
				- Lean forward somewhat to work the chest, front and side shoulders, and triceps. Add weight, such as a dumbbell held between the feet, once 10 to 15 bodyweight reps come easily. Huberman calls weighted dips an upper body squat.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 260}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Dumbbell Fly]]
			  id:: 1a4639cf-76e4-4611-9270-29042252ad87
				- Slight-incline dumbbell flies with a fixed bend in the elbows. Resistance fades at the top of the movement.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 291}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Cable Fly]]
			  id:: 524713da-ce84-4511-a449-aaf09bcc80fc
				- Cables or a fly machine keep resistance through the whole movement. Two to three work sets of 6 to 15 reps. Stand up before re-racking a weight rather than straining to set it down.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 335}} Back overview
			  id:: a26c99ea-3c9a-4f8e-a2ae-ae7862484dc5
				- Lats, traps, and lower back. Two or three exercises, each with two or three work sets taken to failure.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 373}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Pull-Up]]
			  id:: d6c65c35-9c7d-4df5-8254-c4d608228b73
				- Palms facing away. Think of driving the elbows back, as if elbowing someone behind you, and bring the chest toward the bar. Add weight once 10 to 12 good-form reps are possible.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 416}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Lat Pulldown]]
			  id:: d4f77d4c-c58c-42e6-8f15-6469d462770e
				- For anyone not yet strong enough for pull-ups. Huberman prefers an underhand, close grip, pulling to just below the chest line.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 463}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Dumbbell Row]]
			  id:: b15df1e9-1bed-4f6b-94af-7f19b2a84e28
				- Brace the core, lift the weight slightly to find the target muscle, row, and set the weight down rather than dropping it. Barbell and machine rows also work. Huberman shows no back isolation movements; compound pulls and rows with progressive overload are enough.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 568}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Shrug]]
			  id:: 17a80f05-1d17-4b76-a914-6ba7317adb0d
				- Deadlifts and rows work the traps. Shrugs are optional; people with rounded shoulders may want to skip them.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 593}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Back Extension]]
			  id:: bdb8450c-91d6-4681-a533-0dffd109e207
				- Back hyperextensions for the lower back, 8 to 25 controlled reps. Huberman does 100 reps a week.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 709}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Hanging Leg Raise]]
			  id:: db99f82c-b2d9-470a-b1e0-948a994cad28
				- A hanging progression that trains abs and grip together: knee raises, then L-sits, then full pikes, working toward three sets of five pikes.
			- ### {{youtube-timestamp 821}} [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Plate Neck Raise]] and [[Exercise/Body/Strength/Chin Tuck]]
			  id:: 330a11fd-6b04-4b90-921d-9619310f7c93
				- Huberman points to [[Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols/YouTube/16/11/Neck Exercises that Kill Your Neck]] for neck training.
