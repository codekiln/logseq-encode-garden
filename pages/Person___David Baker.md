- # [David Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Baker_(composer))
	- ## About
		- American jazz composer and educator ([[1931]]–[[2016]]).
		- Wrote 70 books, including *Jazz Improvisation* ([[1988]]).
		- Named the bebop scales after the bebop-era players who used them, among them [[Person/Charlie Parker]] and [[Person/Dizzy Gillespie]].
