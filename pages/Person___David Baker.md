- # [David Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Baker_(composer))
	- ## About
		- American jazz composer and educator ([[1931]]–[[2016]]).
		- Wrote dozens of books on jazz, including *Jazz Improvisation* ([[1988]]).
		- Gave the bebop scales their name, because players of the bebop era such as [[Person/Charlie Parker]] and [[Person/Dizzy Gillespie]] used them often.
