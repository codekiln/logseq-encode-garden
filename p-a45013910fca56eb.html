  • My thoughts

    • I just discovered that I can't really easily populate 1Password/Environments, because the secrets are stored in a different part of the app. I would need to click back and forth many, many times to copy the secrets over.
      • So essentially I have two problems. I have the problem of needing to create the environment with the correct values. And then I have the problem of setting up the repos to use the environment.
      • So I'm kind of wondering if maybe the right answer is to use a tool like fnox, which can integrate with 1Password secrets (not sure if it can integrate into 1Password/Environments, though). The nice thing about fnox is that it supports local overrides. So in theory, I could provide a local 1Password reference to a secret, and I could use that to provision the 1Password/Environment. But at that point, I might as well just use fnox.
      • I suppose that there's a chance that fnox might ask me individually for each secret rather than all of them as a bundle. So this is kind of a weird design challenge. The other thing that kind of stinks about 1Password environments is that I can't put a comment next to these. I can't say when they expire. I also can't link them to an existing secret. My agents tell me that in the implementation, it looks like this wouldn't be the case; it bundles them into a single ask.
      • Why use fnox for provisioning the 1Password/Environments, when it could just as easily access 1Password directly? Well, that's because of the concern in My/Pref/Dev/Tool/Secrets/fnox. op asks for access to your 1Password vaults as a whole, where an Environment's approval names one Environment.
