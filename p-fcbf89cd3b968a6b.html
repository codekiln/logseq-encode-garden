IK Multimedia/iLoud Micro Monitors

  • iLoud Micro Monitor

    • A 2-way active, bi-amped studio monitor, sold as a left and a right cabinet sharing one power supply.

    • Drivers and Amplification

      • Tweeter: 3/4" silk dome, neodymium magnet
      • Woofer: 3" high-rigidity custom composite
      • Crossover: 3 kHz
      • Amplifiers: 4, high-efficiency Class-D — 18W RMS low and 7W RMS high per speaker
      • Total output: 50W RMS, 70W peak across both speakers
      • Maximum SPL at 50 cm: 107 dB, both speakers playing, averaged sinewave from 100 Hz to 10 kHz
      • Bass-reflex port, with the whole system under DSP control

    • Frequency Response

      • 45 Hz – 22 kHz at -10 dB
      • Down to 55 Hz at -3 dB

    • Acoustic Settings

      • High shelf: 0 dB or +2 dB from 4 kHz up
      • Low shelf: 0 dB or -3 dB from 250 Hz down
      • Desktop: +3.5 dB between 1 kHz and 10 kHz, -1 dB below 400 Hz

    • Connectors

      • Audio/Connector/RCA/Stereo — 2 × RCA input, cable included
      • Audio/Connector/Phone/TRS/3.5mm — stereo input
      • 4-pin speaker connector linking the two cabinets, cable included

    • Wireless

      • Bluetooth, A2DP

    • Latency

      • 1.02 ms from the analog input
      • 155 ms over Bluetooth, varying with the host

    • Dimensions

      • Size: 180mm/7.09" x 135mm/5.31" x 90mm/3.54"
      • Weight: 920g/32.45oz (left speaker), 800g/28.22oz (right speaker), 1,720g/60.67oz (left + right speaker)

    • In the Box

      • 2 × iLoud Micro Monitor speaker
      • Power supply unit and cable
      • 4-pin speaker connection cable, 2m/6.56'
      • TRS 1/8" stereo to 2 × RCA cable, 1.5m/4.92'
