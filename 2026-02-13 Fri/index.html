  • AI/Public/Sentiment
    • AI has become the 2nd most divisive Rorschach test in public opinion, just behind the political elephant in the room.
    • Stanford puts out an annual study of public sentiment about AI (AI Index 2025: State of AI in 10 Charts | Stanford HAI) and the US ranks higher in nervousness and lower in excitement about AI than other countries.  Other surveys show similar results (Google Ipsos poll says Americans wary of AI, Views of AI Around the World | Pew Research Center).
    • Whenever I go to talks that prominent individuals give, it seems like the vast majority of the Q&A is basically "holding people of power to account for what they're doing to us."
    • AI has become the trendiest scapegoat in the room, and tech absolutely, 100% needs to address this head on. I recently heard this take:

    • 2020: blue and tech against red
      2024: red and tech against blue
      2028: blue and red against tech
