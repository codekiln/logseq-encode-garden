        • PayPal does offer merchants some tools they can add to their own websites to accept payments from agents, but I haven't seen that yet
      • PayPal/Bill Pay
        • Payment details saved once in PayPal can pay many listed billers, and eligible bills can be scheduled ahead. In theory an agent could use that to help with bills through AI/Browser Control, though no supported agent integration is documented yet.
      • ChatGPT/Q/Does ChatGPT have any payment management?
        • Wallet saves cards for approved purchases.
      • ChatGPT/Pro/Q/I heard that payment management is being introduced into ChatGPT Pro accounts now, is that right?
        • Wallet is rolling out to eligible US Pro users.
      • ChatGPT/Plus/Q/What is the state of the art with respect to payment management in a ChatGPT Plus account?
        • SOTA stinks right now
          • Plus has no payment management. Wallet requires Pro.
          • basically, SOTA is either roll your own or pre-configure saved cards.
    • chatgpt cloud browser
      • ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser
      • ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser/Q/Does it have any password management or credential management?
        • Private sign-in. The browser can stay signed in.
      • ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser/Q/Can we install a Chrome extension like 1Password?
        • The cloud browser has no documented extension support.
      • ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser/Q/How can we sign in with passkeys?
        • Doesn't appear to be easy right now; website passkey support is undocumented. 1Password does support storing passkeys, though, and functioning as a passkey provider.
      • ChatGPT/Cloud/Browser/Q/How can I use the cloud browser to pay a bill from an iPhone in a way that's both more secure and more convenient for a human than having the human pay it in a browser?
        • Pay the biller directly. ChatGPT payment is unverified.
