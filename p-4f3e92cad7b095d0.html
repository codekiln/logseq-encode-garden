Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/01 Max for Live Instruments

  • 33.1 Max for Live Instruments

    • Eight drum synthesizers built in Max for Live, one per drum sound. Each has Decay and Volume controls, and clicking the upper half of its display plays a preview with the current settings.
    • Ableton/Max for Live/Instrument
    • Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/01 Max for Live Instruments/01 DS Clang

      • 33.1.1 DS Clang

        • Cowbell, clave and noise percussion from two tones, white noise and a filter.
        • Tone A and Tone B — level of each cowbell tone
        • Filter — high-pass and band-pass cutoff; higher is brighter
        • Noise — amount of white noise
        • Clave — switches to clave; Repeat then adds repeats
        • Pitch, Decay (length) and Volume
    • Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/01 Max for Live Instruments/02 DS Clap

      • 33.1.2 DS Clap

        • Claps from filtered noise and an impulse sent through two panned delay lines, from tight electronic claps to looser hand claps.
        • Sloppy — time between the two delay lines, so the claps line up tightly or loosely
        • Tail — adds filtered noise after the impulse
        • Spread — stereo width, from mono at 0% to wide at 100%
        • Tone — brightness
        • Tune, Decay (length) and Volume
    • Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/01 Max for Live Instruments/03 DS Cymbal

      • 33.1.3 DS Cymbal

        • Cymbals from sine and pulse waves plus high-passed noise, from a thin ride to a heavy crash.
        • Tone — high-pass cutoff; higher is brighter
        • Pitch, Decay (length) and Volume
    • Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/01 Max for Live Instruments/04 DS FM

      • 33.1.4 DS FM

        • FM percussion modeled on a classic Japanese FM synthesizer, from noise bursts to metallic zaps.
        • Tone — low-pass cutoff; higher is brighter
        • Feedb. — FM feedback; more is noisier
        • Amnt — FM amount; Mod blends between modulation types
        • Pitch, Decay (length) and Volume
    • Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/01 Max for Live Instruments/05 DS HH

      • 33.1.5 DS HH

        • Hi-hats from noise and sine waves, from closed to open.
        • Noise — white or pink
        • Tone — high-pass cutoff on the noise; higher is brighter
        • The pitched part runs through a resonant high-pass filter switchable between 12 and 24 dB slopes; Attack sets its attack.
        • Pitch, Decay (length) and Volume
    • Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/01 Max for Live Instruments/06 DS Kick

      • 33.1.6 DS Kick

        • Kick drum from a modulated sine wave.
        • Pitch — tuning in Hz
        • Drive — distortion; OT — added harmonics
        • Attack — smooths the start of the sine
        • Click — adds a click for a sharper attack
        • Env — pitch modulation amount
        • Decay (length) and Volume
    • Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/01 Max for Live Instruments/07 DS Snare

      • 33.1.7 DS Snare

        • Snare from a pitched oscillator and noise, from acoustic-style snares to gated noise snares.
        • Color — tone of the pitched part
        • Tone — amount of noise
        • Noise filter: low-pass, high-pass or band-pass
        • Tune, Decay (length) and Volume
    • Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/01 Max for Live Instruments/08 DS Tom

      • 33.1.8 DS Tom

        • Toms from an impulse and pitched oscillator waves, from deep to sharp.
        • Pitch — tuning in Hz
        • Color — filter gain and cutoff
        • Tone — level of resonant band-pass filters that imitate a drum head's tuning
        • Bend — pitch envelope
        • Decay (length) and Volume
