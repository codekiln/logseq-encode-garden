  • 7. Chord Mode

    • Chord Mode groups scale notes, ready-made triads, saved chords, and sustain on the grid. Its scale, root, and MIDI channel come from Launchpad/UG/06 Note Mode/04 Note Mode Settings.
    • Launchpad/UG/07 Chord Mode/01 Overview

      • 7.1 Overview

        • The Chord layout divides the grid into a Note Area, an orange triad column, and a white Chord Bank. Sustain and Chord Bank Lock sit along the lower edge.
        • 7.1.A — Chord Mode layout
    • Launchpad/UG/07 Chord Mode/02 Triads

      • 7.2 Triads

        • The orange column plays three-note chords, such as C–E–G for C major. Moving upward raises the root while keeping the chord tones within the viewed scale octave for compact voicing.
    • Launchpad/UG/07 Chord Mode/03 Note Area

      • 7.3 The Note Area

        • The left column steps through the selected scale from bottom to top. Shorter scales reach their octave before the top pad.
        • Each horizontal row offers chord tones. The three leftmost pads make a triad with its third raised an octave; the fourth adds a seventh, and the fifth adds a fifth raised an octave. Playing adjacent pads along one row makes it easy to hear related chords.
    • Launchpad/UG/07 Chord Mode/04 Save and Play Chords

      • 7.4 Saving Chords & Playing Them Back

        • The fourteen white pads at the right form the Chord Bank. Hold an empty bank pad and play notes or triads to store a chord. You can also hold the notes first, then press the destination bank pad.
        • Press an assigned bank pad to play its chord. Stored slots glow bright white; empty slots glow dim white.
        • Press Chord Bank Lock in the lower-right corner to play stored chords and notes in the Note Area at the same time without changing assignments.
    • Launchpad/UG/07 Chord Mode/05 Sustain Control

      • 7.5 Sustain Control

        • Press Sustain to hold played notes like a sustain pedal and build a chord gradually. Pressing a sustained note again retriggers it at the new velocity.
