    • About

      • The shoulders and arms day of the Chapter Two resistance training program. The video covers shoulders, biceps, and triceps; the resources page links forearm, face pull, and neck videos for the rest of the day.
        • Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols/YouTube/18/12/The Perfect Forearm Workout
        • Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols/YouTube/26/05/Jeff Cavaliere Shows Andrew Huberman the Face Pull
        • Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols/YouTube/16/11/Neck Exercises that Kill Your Neck

    • Video

        • 00:11 Exercise/Body/Strength/Overhead Press

          • Barbell version. Huberman uses a full range of motion rather than stopping with the elbows at 90 degrees.

        • 00:52 Exercise/Body/Strength/Dumbbell Overhead Press

          • Lift the dumbbells off the floor with bent knees, as in a deadlift. Seated on a flat bench or with back support.

        • 01:45 Exercise/Body/Strength/Machine Shoulder Press

          • Machine variations of the overhead press, still compound movements. Huberman favors barbells and dumbbells because balancing the weight takes extra coordination.

        • 02:22 Exercise/Body/Strength/Lateral Raise

          • Light dumbbells, sometimes as little as 5 pounds, raised under control with thumbs level with or higher than the pinkies, slightly in front of the body.

        • 03:05 Exercise/Body/Strength/Front Raise

          • With dumbbells or cables, if the bench and shoulder presses don't develop the front deltoids enough.

        • 03:20 Exercise/Body/Strength/Rear Delt Fly

          • Bent-over dumbbell fly, or a cable or machine version. Back training works the rear deltoids only indirectly.

        • 04:44 Exercise/Body/Strength/Biceps Curl

          • The biceps flex the elbow, turn the palm up (supination), and lift the shoulder slightly. Dumbbells allow all three; curl both arms at once or alternate. Barbell and EZ-bar curls are other options.

        • 06:16 Exercise/Body/Strength/Preacher Curl

          • Machine version; only the elbow bends. At failure, stand up and set the weight down instead of letting the arm drop straight.

        • 07:06 Triceps overview

          • Dips on torso day already work the triceps, so shoulders and arms day adds only a little direct work.

        • 07:40 Exercise/Body/Strength/Overhead Cable Triceps Extension

          • The compound triceps movement, with the cable set above and behind. Usually three sets to or near failure.

        • 08:43 Exercise/Body/Strength/Triceps Pressdown

          • The isolation triceps movement: same cable setup with only the elbows moving, pressing the handles out behind and to the sides. Huberman then names it a pushdown. Two or three sets to failure.
