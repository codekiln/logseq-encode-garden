# [[LazyVim]] cheat sheet [thesujit lazyvim neovim Cheatography](https://cheatography.com/thesujit/cheat-sheets/lazyvim-neovim/)
	- The sheet documents `:Neotree` and the fzf-lua git pickers; neither plugin is in my setup.
	- The picker I currently use is [[nvim/Plugin/snacks.nvim]], so `<leader>ss` from the LazyVim cheat sheet is not bound in mine.
	- Two entries were wrong in that cheat sheet.
		- `<leader>sd` and `<leader>sD` are **reversed** from the sheet: lowercase is every diagnostic, uppercase is the buffer's.
		- In [[Lazygit]] `<ctrl+z>` is not redo — it suspends the app. Redo is `Z`. Confirmed against the v0.64 keybindings doc.
	- `config/autocmds.lua` disables diagnostics on `VeryLazy`, so its `]d`, `[d`, `<leader>sd` and `<leader>sD` all do nothing until `<leader>ud` turns them on.
	- Also: with `wrap` on, LazyVim remaps `j`/`k` to `gj`/`gk` **only when no count is given**. A bare `j` moves by display line, `10j` moves ten real lines — the gutter and the keyboard measuring different things on the same paragraph.
