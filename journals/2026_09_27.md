	- ## [[Security]] and [[Secrets Management]]
		- ### [[1Password/Environment]]s
			- [[2026-09-26 Sat]] I learned about and prototyped with using [[1Password/Environment]]s in [[ChatGPT/App/Desktop]] and [[Codex/Desktop]].
			- This morning I finished watching a video about it from the 1Password team.
			- #### The good
				- Unlike [[1Password/Dev/op]], the [[Biometric]] request from [[MacOS]] actually discloses a narrow scope to what the fingerprint unlocks, so that's a big improvement in security. The dialog names a particular environment and a particular set of tool calls in its request for fingerprint approval.
				  id:: 6ab8d755-548f-47dd-9087-ee8c59e4475c
				- They went out of their way multiple times to explain that [[1Password/Environment/MCP]] doesn't give the agent access to the credentials. That's very necessary at this point in time. I appreciate their insistence on clarity over concision in this; it seems in line with [[My/Principle/Dispel Ambiguity]].
			- #### The bad
				- The request for permission does not yet appear to explain to the human approver what and why an AI agent is requesting for those permissions. It also doesn't clearly point to where the request was made, or how the current request relates to ones that were given in the last five minutes. Basically, it's just a dumb first implementation.
				- It wasn't very clear to me how environments syncing with [[AWS/Secrets Manager]] works, or how it doesn't violate IAM boundaries. Nice idea, hard to build confidence on team with until this is crystal clear.
