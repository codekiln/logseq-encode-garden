Zoom H5/UG/03 Parts

  • Names of parts

    • Left and front

      • Left side and front panel
      • The left side carries the LINE OUT jack, the PHONE jack, the VOLUME +/− button for headphone and speaker level, the USB port, and the POWER/HOLD switch.
      • The XY mic sits on top, with its own Input volume L/R knob. Below the display are the Input volume 1 and Input volume 2 knobs, one per bottom input.
      • The Track buttons L, R, 1 and 2 select which inputs record; each has an indicator above it.
      • The transport buttons are STOP, PLAY/PAUSE, REC with its indicator, Back, and Forward.

    • Right, back and bottom

      • Right side, back and bottom
      • The right side carries the REMOTE jack, the scroll button, the MENU button, and the SD card slot.
        • Move the scroll button up or down to select menu items. Press it to choose the selected item.
        • Press MENU to open the menu or return to the previous screen.
      • The XY mic has a MIC/LINE input jack that supports Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/04 Plug-in Power.
      • The speaker and the battery cover are on the back.
      • Input 1 and Input 2 are XLR/TRS combo jacks on the bottom, with a strap attachment hole between them.
      • XLR wiring: pin 1 GND, pin 2 HOT, pin 3 COLD.
      • TRS wiring: tip HOT, ring COLD, sleeve GND.
