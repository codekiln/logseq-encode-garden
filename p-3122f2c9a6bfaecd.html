Zoom H5/UG/05 Inputs

  • Connecting mics/other devices to Inputs 1/2

    • Besides the L/R inputs on the XY mic, the H5 has Input 1 and Input 2 on the bottom of the main unit. Use all of them together to record up to 4 tracks at once.
    • Inputs 1/2 accept mics, instruments and other devices.
    • L/R inputs and Inputs 1/2

    • Connecting mics

      • Connect dynamic and condenser mics to the XLR jacks of Inputs 1/2.
      • Inputs 1/2 can supply phantom power at +12V, +24V or +48V to condenser mics. See Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/03 Phantom Power.

    • Connecting instruments and other devices

      • Connect keyboards and mixers directly to the TRS jacks of Inputs 1/2.
      • Passive guitars and basses cannot be plugged in directly. Connect them through a mixer or an effects device.
      • When connecting a mixer or other device with a standard output level of +4 dB, turn the PAD function on. See Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/05 PAD.

    • Connection examples

      • While filming

        • L/R input mic: the main subject.
        • Shotgun or lapel mics on Inputs 1/2: the performer.
        • Shotgun mic on Input 1 and lapel mic on Input 2

      • Concert recording

        • L/R input mic: the performance on stage.
        • Inputs 1/2: the line outputs of the PA mixer.
        • PA mixer outputs on Inputs 1/2
