6.1 LayoutUpper area:
  • Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Scrub Area/Marker/Locators mark launchable sections. Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Button/Set Locator adds them during playback or recording and becomes Delete Locator when one is selected; Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Button/Previous Locator and Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Button/Next Locator jump between them, quantized.
Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/03 Transport and Playback
6.3 Transport and Playback
  • With Permanent Scrub Areas on (the default, in Display & Input Settings), click anywhere in the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Scrub Area to play from there; jumps follow Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Menu/Quantization, and holding the button repeats that portion. With it off, Shift-click the scrub area or beat-time ruler.
Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/04 Launching the Arrangement with Locators
6.4 Launching the Arrangement with Locators
  • Locators in the scrub area (Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Scrub Area/Marker/Locator) are launch points in the Arrangement.
