    • 35.3.6 Real-time Recording

      • Turn on Metronome for a click if wanted, then press Record and play the pads. Record again stops recording but keeps playing; a third press starts overdub, and further presses toggle playback and overdub.
      • New stops the selected clip and readies a new one on the track, so you can practice first. By default it also copies the clips playing on other tracks to a new scene and keeps them playing; the Workflow preference changes this.
