Logseq/Entity/Person
PersonFrontmatter on person hub pages
  • Mark the hub with logseq-entity:: [[Logseq/Entity/Person]], or with its maker entities alone when Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker applies.
PersonRelated types
  • Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker — a person who makes music, music theory, music technology or art, such as a Composer or a Sound Artist. A maker's hub lists only its maker entities in logseq-entity::, so this page's linked references leave makers out. The hierarchy on Person lists every Person/<Name> hub, makers included.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art
Artist
  • In this garden, Artist pages model a real person who makes works of art: painting, drawing, printmaking, sculpture, installation, video, performance art or sound art. An Artist is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker, and the rest follows that page.
ArtistWhat counts as an instance
  • A page carries Artist itself when the art form is not sound or is unknown. An artist whose material is sound carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art/Sound in place of Artist.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art/Sound
Sound Artist
  • In this garden, Sound Artist pages model a real person who makes art whose material is sound, presented as art: sound installations, sound sculptures, soundwalks, and site-specific or gallery works with sound. A Sound Artist is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art, so a page marked Sound Artist leaves Artist out.
Sound ArtistBoundary cases
  • A sound artist who also writes concert pieces, or fixed-media works presented as compositions, adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition: Person/Wei Yang. One who releases tracks as an electronic producer adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production.
Sound ArtistBoundary cases
  • A sound artist who performs their work live carries Sound Artist and Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance: Person/Huichun Yang.
Sound ArtistBoundary cases
  • A sound artist who also makes music in an unknown role carries Sound Artist and Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music: Person/Ranger Liu, Person/Tom Whiston.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Discussion
Maker Discussion
  • Why each segment under Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker names what the person makes, why Songwriter and Conductor nest under Composer and Performer, why the shared rules sit on Maker, and what the family replaced.
Maker DiscussionEach entity name under Maker says what the person makes
  • Maker/Music/Theory names a maker of theory whose field is music. Heinrich Glarean is known for his theory, including his naming of the Ionian and Aeolian modes, so his hub carries Music Theorist alone, without Musician. Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Tech works the same way for a person who builds a synthesis method or a music programming language.
Maker DiscussionSongwriter and Conductor nest under Composer and Performer
  • Writing a song is composing, and conducting is performing through an ensemble. Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song is a kind of Composer, and Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting is a kind of Performer.
Maker DiscussionShared rules live on Maker
  • Marking, created-by:: on works, the lean hub with one line saying what the person makes, and the marking for an unknown role are the same for every maker. They are written once, on Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker, following My/Principle/Simplify/Don't Repeat Yourself DRY. Each child page holds only what differs: what counts as its instance, the cases near its boundary, and any frontmatter of its own.

Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music
Musician
  • In this garden, Musician pages model a real person who makes music: who composes it, performs it or produces it. Marking, works and hub shape follow Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker.
MusicianWhat counts as an instance
  • A music teacher, critic, historian, broadcaster, curator or label owner who makes no music leaves Musician out. One who published theory, a theory textbook or a teaching method carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Theory, as Guido of Arezzo does for his solmization syllables. Everyone else in this list stays a Logseq/Entity/Person.
MusicianChild entitiesKinds of Musician; a page marked with one of them leaves Musician out:

MusicianChild entitiesKinds of Musician; a page marked with one of them leaves Musician out:

MusicianChild entitiesKinds of Musician; a page marked with one of them leaves Musician out:

Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition
Composer
  • In this garden, Composer pages model a real person who writes music as works: pieces, scores, soundtracks, and fixed-media or electroacoustic works. A Composer is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music, and the rest follows Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker.
ComposerWhat counts as an instance
  • A person known mainly for songs in the popular, folk, blues or rock traditions carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song and leaves Composer out, since a Songwriter is a kind of Composer. Franz Schubert, who wrote art songs, and Tito Puente, who wrote tunes for his band, carry Composer.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • An improviser whose music exists only in performance carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance. An improviser who also writes pieces carries Composer as well.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • An electronic producer who releases tracks carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production, and adds Composer for works presented as compositions, such as concert pieces or film scores: Person/Daniel Lopatin.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • A person who makes sound installations or sound sculptures carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art/Sound, and adds Composer when they also write pieces for concert or release: Person/Wei Yang.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • A composer who also writes music theory adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Theory; one who also leads ensembles from the podium adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting, as Person/John Adams does.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song
Songwriter
  • In this garden, Songwriter pages model a real person who writes songs: the music, the words, or both. A Songwriter is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition, so a page marked Songwriter leaves Composer out.
SongwriterWhat counts as an instance
  • A composer of art songs, such as Franz Schubert and his Lieder, carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition. So does a jazz or Latin bandleader who writes tunes for the band, such as Tito Puente and "Oye Como Va".
SongwriterBoundary cases
  • A singer-songwriter carries Songwriter and Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance: Person/Madonna, Person/Adrianne Lenker.
SongwriterBoundary cases
  • A producer who shapes a song in the studio without writing it carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance
Performer
  • In this garden, Performer pages model a real person who performs music for listeners: who plays an instrument, sings, improvises, DJs or live-codes. A Performer is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music, and the rest follows Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker.
PerformerBoundary cases
  • A person who leads an ensemble from the podium carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting in place of Performer.
PerformerBoundary cases
  • A live coder performs by writing code in front of an audience, and carries Performer: Person/Atsushi Tadokoro. A live coder who also builds the language or software that live coders perform with adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Tech.
PerformerBoundary cases
  • A performer who writes the pieces they play adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition. One known mainly for songs in the popular, folk, blues or rock traditions adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song, as Person/Adrianne Lenker does.
PerformerBoundary cases
  • A sound artist whose work is performed live, such as a breath or object performance in a gallery, carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art/Sound and Performer.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting
Conductor
  • In this garden, Conductor pages model a real person who leads an orchestra, choir or other ensemble as its conductor. A Conductor is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance, so a page marked Conductor leaves Performer out.
ConductorBoundary cases
  • A bandleader who plays in the band carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance.
ConductorBoundary cases
  • A composer who conducts carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition and Conductor: Person/John Adams, Person/Guy Reibel.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production
Music Producer
  • In this garden, Music Producer pages model a real person who makes recorded music: who produces, records, mixes or masters recordings, or makes tracks as an electronic producer. A Music Producer is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music, and the rest follows Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker.
Music ProducerBoundary cases
  • An electronic producer who also writes works presented as compositions, such as concert pieces or film scores, adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition: Person/Daniel Lopatin.
Music ProducerBoundary cases
  • A DJ who plays sets carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance, and adds Music Producer when they release tracks of their own.
Music ProducerBoundary cases
  • A person who builds the plugins, devices or software that producers use carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Tech.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Tech
Music Technologist
  • In this garden, Music Technologist pages model a real person who makes technology for music: instruments, synthesis methods, music software and programming languages, plugins and devices, and the hardware or firmware music runs on. A Music Technologist is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker whose field is music, and the rest follows that page.
Music TechnologistWhat counts as an instance
  • A Music Technologist is a maker of technology. A person who also makes music carries the kind of Musician that names their role as well, or Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music when the role is unknown.
Music TechnologistBoundary cases
  • A live coder who also builds the language or software live coders perform with carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance and Music Technologist.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Theory
Music Theorist
  • In this garden, Music Theorist pages model a real person who makes music theory: treatises, systems of scales, modes or harmony, methods of analysis, and theory textbooks. A Music Theorist is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker whose field is music, and the rest follows that page.
Music TheoristWhat counts as an instance
  • A Music Theorist is a maker of theory. A person who also made music carries the kind of Musician that names their role as well, as a composer-theorist carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition and Music Theorist.
Music TheoristBoundary cases
  • A researcher who invents a synthesis method or builds music software carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Tech.
