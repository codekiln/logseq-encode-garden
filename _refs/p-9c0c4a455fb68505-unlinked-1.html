      • Can I automatically sync on startup/every n minutes?

        • Yes, in your eilmeldung/config/.toml  add sync to your startup_commands (startup_commands=["sync"]). To automatically sync every 10 minutes, set sync_every_minutes=10.
    • eilmeldung/FAQ/Can I sync or refresh without starting eilmeldung?

      • Can I sync/refresh without starting eilmeldung?

        • Yes, use --sync. See CLI Arguments. This even outputs sync statistics for further shell shenanigans.
