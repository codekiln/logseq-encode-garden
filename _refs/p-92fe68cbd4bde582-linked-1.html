Logseq/Entity/Discussion
DiscussionWhat counts as an instance
  • The subject can be any page: an entity definition such as Logseq/Entity/Music/Scale, a principle such as My/Principle/Simplify/Prefer Standards and Defaults, or a preference such as My/Pref/Dev/mise/Tasks. The subject page may exist only as a logical page, as Logseq/Entity/Hierarchy does.
DiscussionPage shape
  • H1 — the subject page's H1 followed by Discussion: - # Music Scale Discussion for Logseq/Entity/Music/Scale, whose H1 is Music Scale. When the subject has no page file, the H1 names the subject in Title Case without the Logseq/ prefix, as - # Entity Hierarchy Discussion does for Logseq/Entity/Hierarchy. Logseq/Entity/Definition/Discussion keeps the H1 codekiln wrote for it.
DiscussionExamples in this garden
  • Logseq/Entity/Definition/Discussion, Logseq/Entity/Music/Scale/Discussion — combined with Explanation.
Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music
Music IdentityAttribution and placement
  • Place releases under the first credited identity as <Identity>/Music/Recording/<Title>, following Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording. A joint release has one page and lists every credited identity.
Music IdentityAttribution and placement
  • Place a separately represented composition under its primary credited identity as <Identity>/Music/Work/<Title>, following Logseq/Entity/Music/Work. A recording's artist credit does not establish the composition's authorship.

Page reference

Musical FormRelationships
  • A Logseq/Entity/Music/Work page links each of its forms with music-form::, defined at Logseq/Entity/Music/Work/Frontmatter/music-form. Those links are the form's list of works, so a form page lists works only to add notes about them.

Page reference

Musical Form DiscussionForms get their own pages, linked from works
  • Each work page links its form with music-form::, defined at Logseq/Entity/Music/Work/Frontmatter/music-form. The filter works because music-form:: and created-by:: sit in the same frontmatter block, so no query is needed.
Musical Form DiscussionForms get their own pages, linked from works
  • The pattern follows Logseq/Entity/Music/Scale: concept pages under Music/<Concept>/<Name> with an index page above them.

Logseq/Entity/Music/Label
Record LabelWhat counts
  • A record label is the business or imprint named on a release. A label page collects the Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording pages it released.
Record LabelRelationships
  • A recording page links each of its labels with record-label::, defined at Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording/Frontmatter/record-label. Those links are the label's release list, so a label page lists recordings only to add notes about them.
Logseq/Entity/Music/Performance
Music PerformanceWhat counts
  • An audio or video capture of the event is a separate Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording when the recording itself needs a page.
Music PerformanceRelationships
  • Use work:: for the performed work, performer:: for performers as defined at Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording/Frontmatter/performer, and recording:: for a recording page that documents the event.
Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording
Music RecordingRelationships

Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording/Frontmatter/performer
performer:: — who plays on a recording
  • The known people or groups who perform on a Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording. Published artist identities are recorded separately through Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities; when present, its first identity supplies the recording namespace.
performer:: — who plays on a recordingOther entities that use it
  • Logseq/Entity/Music/Performance uses performer:: with the same values for the people and groups who played at the event.
Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording/Frontmatter/record-label
record-label:: — who released a recording

Logseq/Entity/Music/Work
Musical WorkWhat counts
  • An album is a release or collection of recordings, not a musical work. Model an album under Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording and connect its tracks to their works.
Musical WorkRelationships
  • Use music-form:: to link the Logseq/Entity/Music/Form pages a work belongs to, as music-form:: [[Music/Form/Prelude]].
Musical WorkFrontmatter
  • Logseq/Entity/Music/Work/Frontmatter/music-form
Logseq/Entity/Music/Work/Frontmatter/music-form
music-form:: — the form a work is written in
  • Links a Logseq/Entity/Music/Work to the Logseq/Entity/Music/Form pages it belongs to.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition
ComposerWorks
  • A composed work follows Logseq/Entity/Music/Work for placement under its credited identity or the composer's hub, with created-by:: linking the known composer and Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities preserving a separate published identity when applicable.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song
SongwriterWorks
  • A song is a Logseq/Entity/Music/Work, filed under the songwriter's hub as Composer works are.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance
PerformerWorks
  • A performance page links its performers with performer::, as Logseq/Entity/Music/Performance and Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording describe, and those links list the performances in the performer's linked references.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production
Music ProducerWorks
  • A release follows Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording: it lives under the credited Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music when represented separately, or under the producer's hub otherwise.
Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities
created-by-identities:: — credited public identitiesPlacement
  • The first credited identity supplies the namespace when the creation's entity definition uses identity-based placement. Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording and Logseq/Entity/Music/Work define musical placement.
