29. Live Audio Effect Reference
  • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/10 Corpus

    • 29.10 Corpus

      • Corpus simulates seven kinds of resonant objects, built with Applied Acoustics Systems using physical modeling.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/10 Corpus/01 Resonator Parameters

        • 29.10.1 Resonator Parameters

          • Resonance Type:
            • Beam — beams of various materials and sizes
            • Marimba — a beam tuned like arch-cut marimba bars
            • String — strings of various materials and sizes
            • Membrane — a rectangular membrane such as a drum head
            • Plate — a flat rectangular plate
            • Pipe — a tube open at one end, with a variable opening at the other
            • Tube — a tube open at both ends
          • Resonator Quality — fewer overtones (Eco) for less CPU, more (High) for richer resonance; not for Pipe or Tube
          • Decay — internal damping and so decay time
          • Material — how damping varies with frequency: low values let lows ring longer (wood, nylon, rubber), high values let highs ring longer (glass, metal). Pipe and Tube show Radius instead: a wider tube rings longer and brighter, and at extremes changes pitch.
          • The X-Y controller also sets Decay and Material/Radius.
          • Bright — level of higher frequencies; not for Pipe or Tube
          • Inharm — squeezes (negative) or stretches (positive) the harmonics; not for Pipe or Tube
          • Opening (Pipe only) — 0% closed at one end, 100% open at both
          • Ratio (Membrane and Plate) — proportions of the x and y axes
          • Hit — strike point, from center (0%) toward the edge; not for Pipe or Tube
          • Width — stereo spread of the left and right resonators, mono at 0%
          • Pos. L / Pos. R — where each resonator is "listened to", from center toward edge; Pipe and Tube are always heard at the open end
          • Tune — resonator frequency in Hz, or coarse offset when MIDI Frequency is on in the sidechain
          • Fine — fine offset in cents for MIDI Frequency
          • Spread — detunes the two resonators in opposite directions
      • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/10 Corpus/02 LFO Section

        • 29.10.2 LFO Section

          • An LFO modulates the resonant frequency; Amount sets how much.
          • Rate — in Hz or synced to tempo
          • Waves — sine, square, triangle, saw up, saw down, and stepped or smooth noise
          • There are two LFOs, one per channel:
            • Phase — same rate, offset in degrees; 180 is fully opposite, 0 or 360 in step
            • Spin (Hz mode only) — runs the two at different rates
            • Neither affects the noise waves.
            • The LFO Stereo mode icons choose Phase or Spin in Hz mode.
          • Offset (synced mode) — moves the LFO's start point
      • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/10 Corpus/03 Filter Section

        • 29.10.3 Filter Section

          • Filter switches a band-pass filter on the output; Freq sets its center and Bdwidth its width.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/10 Corpus/04 Global Parameters

        • 29.10.4 Global Parameters

          • Bleed — mixes in some of the unprocessed signal, which restores highs lost at low tunings or low quality; off for Pipe and Tube
          • Gain — output level. A built-in limiter engages when the level is too high, shown by the LED next to Gain.
          • Dry/Wet — balance of dry input and processed signal. Turning it down stops new input from being processed but lets current resonances ring.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/10 Corpus/05 Sidechain Parameters

        • 29.10.5 Sidechain Parameters

          • The title bar button opens the sidechain, which lights while it is active. Its MIDI From choosers pick a MIDI track and tapping point.
          • Frequency — incoming notes tune the resonance. Last/Low picks the last or the lowest held note; Transpose and Fine offset it; PB Range sets pitch bend range in semitones. With Frequency off, Tune sets a fixed frequency in Hz, shown with its MIDI note and cents below.
          • Off Decay — Note Off damps the resonance; its slider sets how much. At 0% only Decay matters, like a marimba or glockenspiel; at 100% Note Off cuts the sound at once.
