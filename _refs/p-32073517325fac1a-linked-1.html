    • 03.01.01.02 Paraphonic

      • When the Paraphonic button is lit, the MicroFreak can trigger four voices independently. The voices share one analog filter but can have different amplitudes. Internal VCA envelopes shape each voice's volume along with the Main Envelope.
      • The Main Envelope, Pressure, Keyboard, and Arpeggiator can produce polyphonic modulation. Routing them to polyphonic destinations such as Oscillator Type, Wave, Timbre, or Shape gives each voice its own modulation.
