    • 29.32.5 Global Settings

      • Predelay — ms before the first early reflection; affects perceived room size. Natural values are about 1–25 ms.
      • Smooth — how Size changes take effect: None jumps (may cause artifacts), Slow or Fast glide.
      • Size — room volume; very large gives a shifting, diffuse delay, very small a colored, metallic sound
      • Decay — time for the tail to fall by 60 dB
      • Freeze — holds the diffuse sound almost forever.
        • Flat — bypasses the shelf filters while frozen, so the frozen sound does not lose energy in those bands
        • Cut — stops new input adding to the frozen sound
      • Stereo — width, from mono up to 120°, where each ear gets an independent tail
      • Density — quality versus CPU, from Sparse to High
