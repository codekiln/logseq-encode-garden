Repository
A repository is a storage location for a project's files along with the complete history of changes made to those files.
  • Repositories are the root abstraction of most Version/Control systems, such as git, mercurial, svn, and cvs.
source/control/management
Source Control Management (SCM)
  • The most popular CLI/Tool for this is git, but other tools exist, such as mercurial, svn, cvs.
svn
SVN (Apache Subversion) is a centralized Version/Control system and CLI/Tool.
  • It was created as a replacement for cvs and was widely used before the rise of distributed version control.
Version/Control
Version control is the practice of tracking and managing changes to files over time.Tools
