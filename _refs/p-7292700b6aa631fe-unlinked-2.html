Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art

  • Artist

    • In this garden, Artist pages model a real person who makes works of art: painting, drawing, printmaking, sculpture, installation, video, performance art or sound art. An Artist is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker, and the rest follows that page.

    • What counts as an instance

      • A person whose artworks are a significant part of their public identity.
      • A page carries Artist itself when the art form is not sound or is unknown. An artist whose material is sound carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art/Sound in place of Artist.
      • Architecture, design and typeface design are fields the family does not cover yet.

    • Boundary cases

      • A creative coder who makes visual art carries Artist. A creative coder who also makes music adds Musician or the kind of Musician that names their role.
      • Album covers and stage visuals made as part of a music career count when the garden records them as artworks.

    • Works

      • An artwork page has created-by:: linking the artist's hub, as Art/Work/The Sudarium of Saint Veronica links the engraver Claude Mellan.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art/Sound

  • Sound Artist

    • In this garden, Sound Artist pages model a real person who makes art whose material is sound, presented as art: sound installations, sound sculptures, soundwalks, and site-specific or gallery works with sound. A Sound Artist is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art, so a page marked Sound Artist leaves Artist out.

    • What counts as an instance

      • A person whose sound art is a significant part of their public identity: Person/George Rhoads and his audiokinetic sculptures, Person/Courtney Brown.

    • Boundary cases

      • A sound artist who also writes concert pieces, or fixed-media works presented as compositions, adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition: Person/Wei Yang. One who releases tracks as an electronic producer adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production.
      • A sound artist who performs their work live carries Sound Artist and Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance: Person/Huichun Yang.
      • A sound artist who also makes music in an unknown role carries Sound Artist and Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music: Person/Ranger Liu, Person/Tom Whiston.

    • Examples in this garden

      • Person/George Rhoads, Person/Courtney Brown
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Discussion
  • That shape has no common parent for music and art. A sound artist and a composer share the marking, created-by:: and hub rules, which would either sit on Logseq/Entity/Person, where they would apply to every person, or be copied into Person/Music and a separate Person/Art.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance
  • A sound artist whose work is performed live, such as a breath or object performance in a gallery, carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art/Sound and Performer.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Tech
  • A person who programs patches or builds systems only for their own pieces leaves Music Technologist out: Person/Huichun Yang carries Sound Artist and Performer, and Person/Wei Yang carries Sound Artist and Composer. Music Technologist is for technology other musicians use: a language, a synthesis method, or a plugin, device or instrument other people play, such as Person/Tobias Hunke's Max for Live devices.

Person/Huichun Yang
  • Huichun Yang is a Providence-based Taiwanese sound artist, improviser, and computer music programmer. Inspired by the physicality of balloons, her performance explores breath as sonic material, speaking with her history and surroundings.
