  • 2025-09-05 Fri
    • works for me: Take an empty Drum-Rack and drop a Simpler on C1 pad , set to One-Shot with whatever adjusted parameters you need. Drag the entire Drum-Rack into User Library>Defaults>Slicing. Select your sample and choose Slice to New MIDI Track. In the slicing pop-up window you have the ability to select the slicing preset, you should be able to select the Drum-Rack that you have just saved.
