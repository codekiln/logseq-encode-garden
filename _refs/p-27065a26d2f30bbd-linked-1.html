      • Deflation

        • In this trade-off, deflation means a broad and sustained fall in prices, not simply slower inflation or one item becoming cheaper.
        • Falling prices can benefit buyers in the short run, but expected deflation can lead households and firms to delay spending. It also raises the real burden of debts fixed in money terms and can leave central banks with less room to reduce interest rates.
