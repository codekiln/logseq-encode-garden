CyberneticsHistory of the termThe Macy Conferences, 1946 to 1953
  • The Josiah Macy, Jr. Foundation funded a run of meetings chaired by Warren McCulloch. Ross Ashby, Gregory Bateson, Heinz von Foerster, Margaret Mead, Person/John von Neumann and Wiener were among the participants, and mathematicians, neurophysiologists, anthropologists and engineers worked out a shared vocabulary of Feedback, information and circular causality (Macy conferences, Wikipedia). In Britain the Ratio Club did similar work over dinner from 1949 to 1958.
