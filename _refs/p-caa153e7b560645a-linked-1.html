Does LazyVim integrate with rg?AI Answer
  • The keys this shows up on (see LazyVim/Keyshort/Search): <leader>/ and <leader>sg are live grep over the root, <leader>sw greps the word under the cursor — all ripgrep processes streaming into the picker.
LazyVim/Q/What are Telescope, fzf-lua, and aerial.nvim for symbol search, and how do they compare with Trouble.nvim?
What are nvim/Plugin/telescope.nvim, nvim/Plugin/fzf-lua, and nvim/Plugin/aerial.nvim for symbol search, and how do they compare with nvim/Plugin/trouble.nvim?AI Answer
  • In this garden's actual LazyVim config: none of the telescope.nvim, fzf-lua, or aerial.nvim extras are enabled (see the My Note on LazyVim/Keyshort/Search and LazyVim/Keyshort/Code Action). General fuzzy pickers (files, grep, etc.) run on nvim/Plugin/snacks.nvim instead, and document symbol search is bound to <leader>cs (Document Symbols), which opens nvim/Plugin/trouble.nvim rather than a picker or an aerial sidebar — so cheat sheets written against the telescope.nvim/fzf-lua/aerial.nvim extras (e.g. describing a <leader>ss symbol picker) don't match the keys actually bound here.

vim/Keyshort/Mark
Keyshort vim vim/KeyshortMy Note
  • Marks are only worth setting if they can be found again; <leader>sm in LazyVim opens a picker over every set mark with its file and line, which removes the need to remember letters (see LazyVim/Keyshort/Search).
vim/Q/What exactly are vim marks, and what are they good for?
What exactly are vim/Marks, and what are they good for?AI Answer
  • Set with m{letter}; jump with `{letter} for the exact position or '{letter} for the first non-blank of that line; clear with :delmarks. See vim/Keyshort/Mark for the shortcut reference, and LazyVim/Keyshort/Search for <leader>sm, the picker that lists every set mark with its file and line.
