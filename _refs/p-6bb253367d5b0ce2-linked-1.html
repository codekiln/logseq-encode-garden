    • AI/Concept/Explore/vs/Exploit/Explore
      • Choosing to explore means spending a turn on an option whose payoff is unknown, to learn what it is worth.
      • Gains — information: a truer map of which options pay, and the chance of finding something better than the current best. The value is highest early, when little is known and a long horizon remains to use what is learned.
      • Costs — the reward forgone on that turn, since the known best was passed over, plus the chance that the unknown option is worse. Returns arrive late and uncertain.
      • Too far — the perpetual survey: an agent, team or gardener that keeps sampling and never settles, so nothing learned is ever cashed in. March described the result as too many undeveloped ideas and too little distinctive competence.
