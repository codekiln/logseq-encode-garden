Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/21 Glue Compressor
  • Glue Compressor is an analog-modeled bus compressor made with Cytomic, based on the bus compressor of a famous 1980s mixing console. It works on single tracks but is aimed at the Main track or Group Tracks, to "glue" several sources into one mix.

Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/24 Limiter
  • Limiter is a mastering-grade limiter that keeps the output below a set level — a compressor with an infinite ratio. It belongs on the Main track to prevent clipping in the converters. Compression basics are in Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/09 Compressor.
  • Anything after Limiter can raise the level again. To be sure the output never clips, put Limiter last on the Main track and keep the Main volume below 0 dB.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/25 Looper
  • Drag me! — drag the buffer to the browser or a track as a new clip (Warp Mode Re-Pitch), or drag an audio file in to replace the buffer.
  • Always — input always heard; usual on a single track
  • Never — input never heard; usual on a return track fed by sends
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/25 Looper/01 Feedback Routing
  • Looper can send to and receive from other tracks, so overdubs keep passing through another track's effects:
    • 1. Put Looper on a track and record at least one pass.
    • 2. Create another audio track.
    • 3. Set its top Audio From and Audio To to the Looper track.
    • 4. Set its bottom Audio From and Audio To to "Insert-Looper".
    • 5. Set its Monitoring to In.
    • 6. Add effects to the new track.
    • 7. Put Looper in Overdub.
  • Each pass goes through the other track's effects and back into Looper, getting more processed every time.

Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/40 Utility
  • Gain — −∞ to +35 dB. Handy for automating fades while leaving the track's Volume for mixing. Arrow keys step 1 dB from −18 to +35 dB and speed up below −18 dB.
  • Mute — silences the input at Utility's position. Since a track's own mute is at the end of the chain, Utility before a delay or reverb can cut its input and let the tail continue.
  • Related question: Ableton/Track/Q/How can I turn a mono signal into stereo in a channel?
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/42 Vocoder
  • Put Vocoder on the modulator's track. Carrier options:
    • Noise — internal noise; its X-Y display sets downsampling (horizontal, left lowers the sample rate) and density (vertical, down thins it)
    • External — any internal routing point; the usual choice for robot voices
    • Modulator — the modulator carries itself, a resynthesized version shaped by Vocoder's controls
    • Pitch Tracking — a monophonic oscillator that follows the modulator's pitch, limited by High and Low. Saw or three pulse waves, with Pitch for coarse tuning. It holds each detected pitch until it finds a new one, so changing its settings or resetting it (for instance by grouping the track) can change the sound unexpectedly. Best on single melodic lines or voice; unpredictable but interesting on chords and drums.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/42 Vocoder/01 Vocoder Tips
  • 1. Put Vocoder on the vocal track — a recorded clip, or a live mic chosen as the track input.
  • 2. Put a synth such as Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/01 Analog on another track, played from a clip or live.
  • 3. Set Carrier to External and pick the synth track in Audio From (Post FX in the lower chooser works best).
  • 5. Play the synth while speaking: the speech rhythm comes through with the synth's tone. Solo the voice track to hear only the vocoded sound.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/06 External Instrument
  • MIDI To — two choosers. The first picks a MIDI port or a track holding a multitimbral plug-in; the second picks the MIDI channel.
  • Audio From — for hardware, the audio interface inputs wired to the synth's outputs, as set in the Audio Settings; for a plug-in, its auxiliary outputs. Main outputs play on the plug-in's own track.

Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/02 Max for Live Audio Effects/02 Envelope Follower
  • Sidechain: by default the curve comes from the track's own input. The triangle at the top left opens the Audio Routing panel.
    • turn on Sidechain, choose the source track in Audio From, and choose Pre FX, Post FX or Post Mixer (and a Rack channel if needed)

Ableton/Live/UG/34 MIDI and Key Remote/01 MIDI Remote Control
  • Remote control is separate from playing a MIDI track. A key or controller assigned to a Live control stops reaching the MIDI track: if C-1 launches a clip, it no longer plays C-1 on the instrument. The Control Bar's MIDI indicators show which way incoming MIDI went.

Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/02 Browsing and Loading Sounds
  • What Browse shows depends on the last selected device: replacement instruments for an instrument, effects for an effect, and everything loadable for an empty MIDI track. Folders appear only when they hold something loadable at that moment; samples show only when browsing a single Drum Rack pad.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Turn on Note Mode and load a Drum Rack preset from Browse. On a Drum Rack track, pressing Note cycles the 8x8 grid through three layouts.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/04 Loading Individual Drums
  • Browse can load or replace a single pad. Press Device to show the track's devices, tap the pad, then press the selection button under its name. Browse now targets that pad, and tapping other pads while browsing retargets.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/06 Real-time Recording
  • New stops the selected clip and readies a new one on the track, so you can practice first. By default it also copies the clips playing on other tracks to a new scene and keeps them playing; the Workflow preference changes this.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/04 Additional Recording Options/01 Recording with Repeat
  • With Repeat on, holding a pad plays an even stream of notes, handy for steady hi-hats; finger pressure sets their volume. The Scene/Grid buttons set the rate, and Push remembers Repeat per track.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/05 Playing Melodies and Harmonies
  • Add Track adds a MIDI track and opens Browse to load an instrument; hold it to choose Audio, MIDI or Return. Inside a Group Track, new tracks go into the group.
  • On an instrument track the grid plays notes, in C major by default. The bottom-left pad is C1; each row up is a fourth higher and each pad to the right is the next scale note. A scale is three pads in a row, then three in the next row, and so on.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/06 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies
  • Hold Note for the loop length pads in the top row; Shift + Note locks them per track.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/06 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Adjusting the Loop Length
  • Shift + Note locks the pads per track. Hold Duplicate and press two page pads to copy a page on top of the destination; hold Delete and tap a page pad to clear it first.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/07 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes/01 32 Notes
  • The bottom half plays scale notes and selects them for sequencing; selected notes show in a lighter track color. Hold Select to select without playing.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/08 Navigating in Note Mode
  • Left and right arrows move between tracks. Selecting a MIDI track arms it, shown in Live by a pink Arm button.
  • Up and down move one scene and start playback right away, taking over the play position like Legato Mode. In Scene Workflow the whole scene launches; in Clip Workflow only the selected track's clip does.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/09 Controlling Live's Instruments and Effects
  • Device Mode puts device parameters on the encoders. Selection Control buttons choose a device on the track and State Control buttons turn it on or off; an arrow marks the selected device.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/10 Mixing with Push 1
  • Volume puts eight track volumes on the encoders. Pan & Send cycles through pans and each send. Shift gives fine control.
  • Track Mode puts one track's volume, pan and first six sends on the encoders; Selection Control buttons pick the track, and Master picks the Main track. Hold a Group Track's Selection Control button to fold or unfold it.
  • With Split Stereo Pan Mode, Pan & Send shows the pan value but disables the dial; Track Mode shows the pan control or the stereo pan sliders.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/13 Controlling Live's Session View
  • Session switches the grid to clip launching and the Scene/Grid buttons to scene launching; holding Session or Note switches temporarily. Pressing an empty slot on the selected track records a new clip.
  • Turn on Stop and press a track's State Control button to stop it; Shift + Stop stops all clips.
  • Arrows move one scene or track; with Shift they move eight. Octave Up and Down also move eight scenes.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/14 Setting User Preferences
  • Scene Workflow (default) — Duplicate captures the playing clips into a new scene and keeps playing, like Create → Capture and Insert Scene; New does the same but leaves an empty slot on the current track; arrows move and launch whole scenes.
  • Clip Workflow — Duplicate copies the selected clip to the next slot (Shift + Duplicate captures a scene); New readies an empty slot on the current track only; arrows launch only the selected track's clip.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/15 Push 1 Control Reference
  • Note — on an instrument track the pads play notes, and further presses switch between real-time playing and the melodic sequencer. On a Drum Rack track the lower-left 16 pads play, the lower-right 16 set loop length, and the top four rows step-sequence; press again for 64-pad mode.
  • Add Track — adds a MIDI track to the right; hold to choose Audio, MIDI or Return. Inside a Group Track, the new track joins the group.
  • Scales — on an instrument track in Note Mode, picks the key and scale, with Fixed Y/N, In Key/Chromatic and scale type. No effect on Drum Rack tracks or in Session Mode.
  • Six buttons beside the display set the encoder mode; hold one to switch temporarily. The ninth encoder is Main track volume, or Pre-Cue volume with Shift. Shift with any of the first eight encoders gives fine control.
  • Volume — eight track volumes.
  • Track — one track's volume, pan and first six sends; Master selects the Main track.
  • Delete — in Note Mode deletes the selected clip; in Session Mode hold and press a clip. Hold with a Selection Control button to delete a device or track, or touch an encoder to clear its automation or reset it.
  • New — stops the selected clip and readies new recording. On an armed MIDI track, Record + New triggers Capture MIDI.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2
  • Much of its behaviour depends on the current mode and the selected track type. The chapter walks through the main workflows, then lists every control. Ableton's videos are at https://www.ableton.com/learn-push/
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/11 Track Control And Mixing

    • 36.11 Track Control And Mixing

      • Mix toggles between Track Mix and Global Mix modes.
      • Track Mix: lower buttons pick the track; the first two upper buttons switch between Mix (volume, pan, sends) and Input & Output (routing and monitoring). With more than six returns, arrows scroll the sends.
      • Global Mix: the encoders control volume, pan or sends for the eight visible tracks, chosen with the upper buttons. Arrows scroll extra sends or tracks.
      • Shift gives fine adjustment. Master selects the Main track; press again to go back.
      • With Split Stereo Pan, Global Mix shows the pan value but disables the dial; Track Mix shows the split sliders.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/11 Track Control And Mixing/01 Rack and Group Track Mixing

        • 36.11.1 Rack and Group Track Mixing

          • Group Tracks and tracks holding Instrument or Drum Racks have their own icons and can be unfolded with a second press of their lower button, so their tracks or chains can be mixed. Arrows reach any that are pushed off the display.
          • In an unfolded Drum Rack, hold Select and tap a pad to jump to its chain.
