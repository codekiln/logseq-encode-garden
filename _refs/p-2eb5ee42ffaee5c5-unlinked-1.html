    • note/my/side clarifying heuristic on when to make things more private

      • this week I've been thinking of a Heuristic (see also Epistemics/Concept/Heuristic vs Axiom) that I should, by default, make information as public as I can, and make information more private when they are tied to specifically articulated perceived harms or Threat/Models. One could make a claim that Openness is a right, an ideal or a quality, but those aren't really connotations that drip off of that term, that the concept just naturally comes with or wicks off.
