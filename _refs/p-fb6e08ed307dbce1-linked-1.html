What Is a Shim in mise? Shims | mise-en-placeContext
  • mise/activate rewrites Shell/PATH on every prompt; this is great for interactive shells but unavailable to non-interactive environments like CI, IDEs, or scripts. Shims offer a static directory of stand-ins that works even when the shell cannot evaluate mise activate. See Shims vs PATH activation.
