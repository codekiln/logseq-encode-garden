Ableton/Live/UG/02 First Steps/03 Live's Settings/03 Audio
  • On macOS, Live can use any CoreAudio device, and the Use System Device option follows whatever output macOS Sound settings select. On Windows, an ASIO driver is recommended, and devices are managed in the driver's own program.
Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts
  • Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/04 Sound Similarity

    • 3.4 Sound Similarity

      • Live analyzes sounds to support two features: Similarity Search, which lists Core Library and User Library items that sound like a reference, and Similar Sample Swapping, which replaces samples in Drum Rack, Simpler or Drum Sampler.
      • Similarity Search works on samples up to 60 seconds long, instrument presets and drum presets. Open it with the Show Similar Files icon next to a selected file, the context menu, or Cmd+Shift+F (Mac) / Ctrl+Shift+F (Win). Results run from most to least similar.
      • Analysis runs in the background on new user audio files and can be paused from the Status Bar. Core Library content comes pre-analyzed.

Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/08 Audio and MIDI
  • An instrument turns MIDI into audio. Ableton/Instrument/Simpler plays one sound chromatically; Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/07 Impulse assigns a different drum sound to each key.
Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/09 Audio Clips and Samples
  • An audio clip points to a sample (sound file), or a compressed one such as an MP3, and says which part to play and how.
  • Warping changes playback speed independently of pitch to match the song tempo. Auto-Warp lines up most material with the tempo, and extreme settings can change a sound's character.
Ableton/Live/UG/04 Browser/04 Filters and Tags/02 Tags
  • Background sound analysis auto-tags user samples up to 60 seconds long with the best-matching tag. Turn this off with Enable Auto Tags in the Filter View menu, or change or remove auto tags in the Tag Editor.
Ableton/Live/UG/04 Browser/07 Places/03 Splice
  • Splice is a royalty-free sample library reachable from the Splice label. Its Home page has navigation arrows, a search bar, a Search with Sound drop area and category buttons.
  • Browsing and previewing work without an account, but adding samples, saving to your Splice library and Search with Sound require logging in. An internet connection is required.
  • Once logged in, the Library icon becomes active and the Home page rearranges its browsing buttons and Search with Sound area.
  • Search with Sound: drag a clip, or capture a time selection, onto the drop area to get 50 compatible samples.
  • Drag a sample onto a track or into Sampler, Simpler, Drum Rack or Impulse; in Session View, double-click or press Enter. Splice clips are set to Complex warp mode so they sound like the preview.
Ableton/Live/UG/05 Managing Files and Sets/04 Live Sets/03 Merging Sets
  • Unfold a track further to browse, preview and import its Session clips as Live Clips, so any Set can serve as a sound pool for another.
Ableton/Live/UG/08 Clip View/03 Extended Clip Properties/02 MIDI Clip Bank and Program Change Controls
  • A MIDI clip can send a bank and program change when it launches, so each clip can call up a different sound on an external synth or plug-in. Live offers 128 banks, each with 128 sub-banks of 128 programs; check how many your synth supports.
Ableton/Live/UG/09 Audio Clips Tempo and Warping/02 Warping
  • Warping plays any sample in sync with the chosen tempo, for beat matching, rhythmic experiments and sound design. Its controls are in the Clip View's Audio Utilities panel.
Ableton/Live/UG/09 Audio Clips Tempo and Warping/03 Warp Modes/03 Texture Mode
  • For sounds without a clear pitch: polyphonic orchestral music, pads, noise, drones, and sound design.
Ableton/Live/UG/09 Audio Clips Tempo and Warping/03 Warp Modes/05 Complex and Complex Pro Mode
  • For mixed material such as whole songs. Complex Pro is a variant that can sound better on polyphonic textures and full mixes.
Ableton/Live/UG/13 Audio to MIDI/01 Slice to New MIDI Track
  • Slicing cuts the audio into pieces of time, one per MIDI note, without analysing pitch or rhythm. The pieces keep playing the original sound.
  • Preserve warped timing keeps timing changes from warping; with it off, slices sound like the raw audio.
Ableton/Live/UG/13 Audio to MIDI/02 Convert Harmony to New MIDI Track
  • Detects the pitches in polyphonic audio and writes them to a clip on a new MIDI track, which comes with an Instrument Rack playing a piano sound.
Ableton/Live/UG/13 Audio to MIDI/03 Convert Melody to New MIDI Track
  • Detects the pitches in monophonic audio and writes them to a new MIDI track with a synth Instrument Rack. Its "Synth to Piano" Macro moves the sound from an analog-style synth to an electric piano.
Ableton/Live/UG/15 Tuning Systems/02 The Tuning Section
  • Only Ref. Pitch/Freq changes the sound. Changing Octave or Note updates the displayed frequency without an audible jump until the frequency itself is changed.
Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions/06 Creating Nonrepetitive Structures
  • Clips with odd Follow Action Times interact so they seldom play in the same order or position, which suits sound installations meant to run for weeks without repeating themselves.
Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/03 External MIDI In-Out/01 MIDI Port Inputs and Outputs
  • A controller's output port needs Track only if the controller makes its own sound.
Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/03 External MIDI In-Out/03 Connecting External Synthesizers
  • When one keyboard synth is both your controller and a sound source, turn on its Local Off so Live becomes the hub that passes keyboard and clip MIDI to it.
Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/05 Internal Routings/02 Making Use of Internal Routing
  • Point a second MIDI track's Output Type at the instrument track, and choose the instrument itself (not Track In) as Output Channel. Both tracks then play one instrument, so sound changes affect both.
Ableton/Live/UG/19 Recording New Clips/04 Recording in Sync/01 Metronome Settings
  • Sets the recording count-in length and the tick sound.
Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio
  • Bouncing turns tracks, clips or time selections into audio on new audio tracks — to experiment with the result, to lock in sound design before mixing, or to lower CPU load.
Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects/03 Device Delay Compensation
  • Tempo-synced effects placed after delay-causing devices can sound out of time.
Ableton/Live/UG/25 Racks/07 Using the Macro Controls/03 Macro Control Variations
  • Variations store snapshots of a Rack's Macro settings — for capturing sound design states, comparing mix settings, or jumping between settings for builds and drops while performing or recording.
Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/02 Audio Clip Envelopes
  • With audio effects, clip envelopes make Live a sound design tool: many variations of one clip, from small corrections to unrecognisable new sounds, all in real time.
Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/02 Audio Clip Envelopes/01 Clip Envelopes are Non-Destructive
  • Envelopes are computed live and never change the sample on disk, so hundreds of clips can share one sample and all sound different.

Ableton/Live/UG/28 Video
  • Ableton/Live/UG/28 Video/03 Matching Sound to Video

    • 28.3 Matching Sound to Video

      • To fit music to edits or hit points:
        • Show Arrangement View (Tab switches views on a single monitor).
        • Drag a QuickTime movie from the browser onto an audio track; the Video Window opens.
        • Drag an audio clip into the drop area to create its own track, and unfold both tracks.
        • Double-click the video clip, make sure Warp is on in its Audio tab, and set Leader so the rest of the Set follows the video's normal speed.
        • Add and move Warp Markers on the video clip to set sync points; the waveform updates as you go.
        • Optionally loop a section with the Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Toggle/Arrangement Loop.
        • Finish with File → Export Audio/Video, which mixes the audio to one file and can also export the video.

Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/01 Amp/01 Amp Tips
  • An amp's circuits share a fixed amount of power, so raising one control can take energy from another. Turning up Treble can reduce bass and mids, so getting a sound may mean adjusting controls that seem unrelated.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/02 Auto Filter/01 Filter Types
  • Vowel — emphasizes formants so the sound resembles a voice
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/02 Auto Filter/05 Filter Drive and Circuits
  • Drive adds input gain before the filter for saturation; 0% leaves the sound unchanged.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/04 Auto Shift/05 Pitch Section
  • Formant Shift — −100% to 100%. Formants are the resonances that give a sound its character; shifting them makes a voice sound higher or lower without changing its pitch.
