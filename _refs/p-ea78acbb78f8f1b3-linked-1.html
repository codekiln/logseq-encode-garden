Making/Music/Log/26/09/23 Wed - iPad Microfreak Session 3 - Fallinter 01

  • Public Improv Session with Ableton/Note and Microfreak

    • Summary

      • 2026-09-23 Wed over lunch, I practiced performing in public with a new configuration: Microfreak, iPad, Ableton/Note, Zoom H5/Mode/Audio Interface/iPad, and IK Multimedia/iLoud Micro Monitorss.
      • Recording: Fallinter 01.m4q

    • Session - My Notes

      • Preparation

        • I did need to borrow a power adapter with three jacks to get power to the iPad, the Microfreak and the IK Multimedia/iLoud Micro Monitors. Next time, I should bring a small adapter along.
        • Everything fit into a reusable cloth grocery tote. I used a 1-gallon ziplock bag for all the cables.

    • Recording - My Notes

      • I don't have a good way to record sessions with this setup. When the Zoom H5 is attached in iPad audio interface mode, it can't also write to the internal Zoom H5/SD Card, at least in my version. In addition, Ableton/Note doesn't yet support traditional kinds of multitrack recording.
      • I tried using Zoom/Screen/Share on the iPad so I could record, but that caused Ableton/Note to no longer take audio input from the Zoom H5, so I stopped the Zoom meeting. I did configure Zoom/Audio/For Musicians and that did not help with the issue.
      • Nevertheless, I was using Ableton/Note's reduction of Ableton/Live/Mode/Session to record some samples, and there's a sample export of audio from the Ableton/Note Fallinter 01 set that gives a sense of some of the sounds and musical gestures, though it isn't technically a recording of the session here:
