Music/Scale/Blues
Blues Scale
  • Also called the minor blues or hexatonic blues scale. On C, in its standard textbook form: C E♭ F G♭ G B♭. Steps in semitones: 3 2 1 1 3 2. Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor with an added ♭5.
Blues ScaleUse
  • A "major blues" scale adds ♭3 to Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major: C D E♭ E G A on C.
Music/Scale/Dorian
Dorian Mode
  • Also called the protus authentic mode or Mode 1. On C: C D E♭ F G A B♭. Steps in semitones: 2 1 2 2 2 1 2. The second mode of Music/Scale/Major.
Dorian ModeQuirks
  • Music/Scale/Dorian/Ukrainian and Music/Scale/Dorian/Bebop carry Dorian in their names. Ukrainian Dorian derives from harmonic minor, and Bebop Dorian adds a chromatic passing tone.
Music/Scale/Dorian/Bebop
Bebop Dorian Scale
  • Also called the bebop minor scale. On C, in the form most reference lists give: C D E♭ E F G A B♭. Steps in semitones: 2 1 1 1 2 2 1 2. Music/Scale/Dorian with an added chromatic passing tone.
Music/Scale/Dorian/Ukrainian
Ukrainian Dorian Scale
  • Also called Romanian minor, Dorian ♯4 and, in klezmer, Mi sheberakh or Av harachamim. On C: C D E♭ F♯ G A B♭. Steps in semitones: 2 1 3 1 2 1 2. The fourth mode of Music/Scale/Minor/Harmonic.
Ukrainian Dorian ScaleQuirks
  • The name says Dorian, and the only change from Music/Scale/Dorian is the raised fourth. Its pitches still come from harmonic minor a fifth below: C Ukrainian Dorian uses the notes of G harmonic minor.
Music/Scale/Hirajoshi
Hirajoshi ScaleQuirks
  • It is a hemitonic pentatonic, the opposite of Music/Anhemitonic: two of its five steps are semitones, each followed by a major third. That pairing gives it a sound unlike the semitone-free Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major.
Music/Scale/Major
Major ScaleQuirks
  • Inverting the step pattern about the tonic produces Music/Scale/Phrygian. Inverting Mixolydian produces Music/Scale/Minor/Natural, and Music/Scale/Dorian inverts to itself.
Major ScaleQuirks
  • The pitch-class complement of the major scale is Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major: on a piano, white keys against black keys.
Music/Scale/Minor/Harmonic
Harmonic Minor Scale
  • On C: C D E♭ F G A♭ B. Steps in semitones: 2 1 2 2 1 3 1. Music/Scale/Minor/Natural with a raised seventh.
Harmonic Minor ScaleUse
  • Its fourth mode is Music/Scale/Dorian/Ukrainian.
Music/Scale/Minor/Hungarian
Hungarian Minor Scale
  • Also called Gypsy minor and double harmonic minor. On C: C D E♭ F♯ G A♭ B. Steps in semitones: 2 1 3 1 1 3 1. Music/Scale/Minor/Harmonic with a raised fourth.
Hungarian Minor ScaleHistory
  • Person/Béla Bartók and Person/Zoltán Kodály's fieldwork after 1905 found that older Hungarian peasant song was largely pentatonic. The "Hungarian" in the scale's name therefore names an urban, Romani-performed popular style rather than Hungarian village song. See Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor.
Music/Scale/Minor/Melodic
Melodic Minor Scale
  • Also called jazz minor and, in its ascending form, the acoustic collection. On C, ascending: C D E♭ F G A B. Steps in semitones: 2 1 2 2 2 2 1. The classical descending form is Music/Scale/Minor/Natural.
Melodic Minor ScaleHistory
  • The classical form records voice leading. Raising both sixth and seventh on the way up avoids the augmented second of Music/Scale/Minor/Harmonic while keeping the leading tone. Lowering both on the way down lets the line fall toward the dominant.
Music/Scale/Minor/Natural
Natural Minor Scale
  • Also called Aeolian and pure minor. On C: C D E♭ F G A♭ B♭. Steps in semitones: 2 1 2 2 1 2 2. The sixth mode of Music/Scale/Major.
Natural Minor ScaleHistory
  • Common-practice minor was never this collection alone. Cadences raised the seventh, first through musica ficta and later in notation, and melodic lines raised or lowered the sixth as voice leading required. Natural, Music/Scale/Minor/Harmonic and Music/Scale/Minor/Melodic are later pedagogical slices of one flexible minor mode.
Natural Minor ScaleQuirks
  • The scale shares its key signature with its relative major. Baroque composers sometimes wrote minor pieces with one flat fewer than the modern signature, a holdover from Dorian notation; see Music/Scale/Dorian.
Natural Minor ScaleQuirks
  • The MicroFreak's "Minor" setting is listed in its manual as C D E♭ F G A B♭ B, which lacks A♭, the note that separates natural minor from Dorian, and adds B natural. See Microfreak/UG/15 Using Scales/01 Scale Settings and Music/Scale/Dorian/Bebop. The Launchpad's "Natural Minor" setting carries this scale's name; its manual lists no pitches.
Music/Scale/Mixolydian
Mixolydian Mode
  • Also called the tetrardus authentic mode or Mode 7. On C: C D E F G A B♭. Steps in semitones: 2 2 1 2 2 1 2. The fifth mode of Music/Scale/Major.
Mixolydian ModeHistory
  • The ancient Greek Mixolydian octave species ran from B to B, the collection now called Locrian. The medieval G mode inherited the name in the Carolingian renaming; see Music/Scale/Dorian.
Mixolydian ModeQuirks
  • Inverting its step pattern gives Music/Scale/Minor/Natural.
Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major
Major Pentatonic Scale
  • Also called the Music/Anhemitonic pentatonic, gong mode in Chinese theory, and the MicroFreak's "Pentatonic" setting. On C: C D E G A. Steps in semitones: 2 2 3 2 3.
Major Pentatonic ScaleUse
  • Country, gospel and rock guitar use the major pentatonic for major-key soloing. Its five modes include Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor.
Major Pentatonic ScaleQuirks
  • The pitch-class complement of Music/Scale/Major is a pentatonic collection: the black keys of a piano form G♭ major pentatonic.
Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor
Minor Pentatonic Scale
  • Also called la-pentatonic in Kodály solmization and yu mode in Chinese theory. On C: C E♭ F G B♭. Steps in semitones: 3 2 2 3 2. The fifth mode of Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major.
Minor Pentatonic ScaleHistory
  • Person/Béla Bartók and Kodály's fieldwork after 1905 found that the oldest layer of Hungarian peasant song is in a minor pentatonic, often with a melody that repeats a fifth lower in its second half. Bartók set out that finding in The Hungarian Folk Song (1924). It overturned the nineteenth-century assumption that Hungarian music meant the style hongrois of Music/Scale/Minor/Hungarian.
Minor Pentatonic ScaleHistory
  • The scale is also the foundation of blues and rock melody, where Music/Scale/Blues adds a ♭5 to it.
Music/Scale/Phrygian
Phrygian Mode
  • Also called the deuterus authentic mode or Mode 3. On C: C D♭ E♭ F G A♭ B♭. Steps in semitones: 1 2 2 2 1 2 2. The third mode of Music/Scale/Major.
Phrygian ModeHistory
  • The ancient Greek Phrygian octave species ran from D to D, the collection now called Dorian. The medieval name shifted with the rest of the Greek names; see Music/Scale/Dorian.
Phrygian ModeUse
  • Flamenco's characteristic sound is usually Phrygian with a major tonic, close to the fifth mode of Music/Scale/Minor/Harmonic, over the Andalusian cadence i–♭VII–♭VI–V.
Phrygian ModeUse
  • Carnatic music's Hanumatodi, melakarta 8, is this collection. See Music/Scale/Todi for the different Hindustani Todi.
Music/Scale/Todi
Todi ThaatHistory
  • One of Person/Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande's ten Hindustani thaats, named for raga Todi. See Music/Scale/Marwa for how a thaat relates to the ragas inside it.
Todi ThaatQuirks
  • The name points to two different scales. Carnatic Todi, Hanumatodi or melakarta 8, has the notes of Music/Scale/Phrygian, while Hindustani Todi has a raised fourth and a raised seventh.
Todi ThaatQuirks
  • Its step pattern, 1 2 3 1 1 3 1, contains two augmented seconds, like Music/Scale/Minor/Hungarian, from which it differs only in the second degree.
