ICMC/25/d1/1400 Concert n02/03 Sluicer
  • Sluicer is the most recent performance system I have developed that focuses on HDLAs as new interfaces for musical expression. A core strategy in Sluicer is the use of spatially positioned, multichannel audio effects that alter specific regions of a sound field rather than the audio signal of a specific source (e.g. instrument, voice, track). This approach makes it possible to lock an effect at spatial coordinates such that an audio source moving in 3d space is transformed when its path crosses into a specific zone.
ICMC/25/d4/1120 Concert n13/08 Camouflage
  • Camouflage is a work for piano and live electronics. All of the electronics are created from sounds sampled and controlled in real-time by the piano and act as an extension of the instrument, creating a hybrid electro-acoustic entity. In the first section of the work, the computer responds to the piano attacks with rhythmic patterns built from processed versions of the chord that triggered the pattern. This gives the music a regular pulsing rhythm without resorting to a fixed track and allowing the performer control over the musical expression and flow. As the piece progresses, an underlying harmonic progression hinted at from the beginning, gradually becomes more prominent, enhanced by sustaining electronics, creating blurry impressionistic washes of color. As the piano moves increasingly towards an ecstatic outburst of Romanticism the electronics respond by becoming increasingly noisy and aggressive, eventually departing from the performer’s direct control, and attempting to suppress and hide the emotions displayed by the human performer.

Launchpad/UG/05 Session Mode/01 Ableton Live Session View
  • Session View arranges clips in tracks (columns) and scenes (rows). A clip usually contains looping MIDI or audio. A track hosts an instrument or audio, and a scene launches the clips across its row.
  • Press a clip pad to launch it. Its color matches Live. A queued clip flashes green; a playing clip pulses green. Each track plays one clip at a time, and launching an empty slot stops its playing clip. Use the scene launch buttons at the right to launch a row.
  • For an armed track, Session Record overdubs the playing clip. Shift + Session Record calls Capture MIDI, which retrieves recently played notes from armed or input-monitored tracks into a new clip or overdubs the playing clip.

Launchpad/UG/05 Session Mode/05 Record Arm and Recording

Page reference

  • On an armed track, empty slots glow dim red. Press one to queue recording: the pad and record button flash red. During recording, the pad pulses red and the record button stays bright red.
  • Press Record to queue the end of recording; the clip flashes red until it stops. Disarming the track stops recording immediately.
Launchpad/UG/05 Session Mode/07 Momentary View Switching
  • Hold a Track Control button to use its view temporarily, then release it to return to the earlier view. For example, hold Volume while viewing Mute, adjust a fader, and release Volume to return to Mute.
Launchpad/UG/06 Note Mode
  • Use Up/Down to shift octaves and Left/Right to transpose by semitones. In Live, arm the MIDI track and set monitoring to Auto to hear the instrument.
Launchpad/UG/06 Note Mode/01 Overview
  • Load an instrument or drum kit onto a MIDI track in Live, arm that track, and set its monitor to Auto. Use the arrow buttons to move the playable range.
Launchpad/UG/06 Note Mode/04 Note Mode Settings
  • Choose a Note Mode MIDI channel from 1–16 to address a specific armed track.
Launchpad/UG/06 Note Mode/06 Drum Mode
  • Arming a Live track containing Drum Rack switches Note Mode to a four-quadrant Drum layout. Filled Drum Rack slots light on the grid; the lower-left quadrant matches the slots currently visible in Live.
  • Arming a track with another instrument restores the chosen Scale or Chromatic layout. To hear a Drum Rack, arm its track and set Live monitoring to Auto.
Launchpad/UG/08 Custom Modes/02 Default Custom Modes
  • Press Custom, then use the white Track Select buttons to choose among eight stored modes. The active mode lights pale green.
Launchpad/UG/08 Custom Modes/03 Master MIDI Channel
  • Select a mode with Track Select, then press its desired channel pad. The active mode and its channel glow green; channels stored for inactive modes show dim red.
Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/01 Overview
  • For software, connect by USB and set each instrument to receive the MIDI channel of the sequencer track that controls it. Track MIDI channels can be changed in Sequencer Settings.
Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/08 Tracks
  • The four sequencer tracks play simultaneously. Select a track with its lit track-select button; each track’s button and Play Area use the same color.
Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/09 Ableton Live
  • Ableton Live MIDI tracks accept input from any channel by default. If several sequencer tracks play while multiple Live tracks are armed, the notes can reach every armed track. Assign a separate input channel to each Live track.
  • Set each Live track to the channel of its Launchpad sequencer track. By default, Launchpad tracks 1–4 send on MIDI channels 1–4. Sequencer Settings can change those channels.

Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/03 Patterns View
  • A Pattern is a 32-step sequence. Each track has eight Patterns per project. Open Patterns View to see them; the playing Pattern pulses and is the one displayed in Steps View.
Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/02 Chain
  • Press two Scenes together to set the start and end of a chain. When you press Play, the Scenes play in order. The sequencer advances after every track has completed its Pattern chain at least once.
Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/03 Queue
  • During playback, select a Scene or Scene chain to queue it. The queued pads flash; after Track 1’s current Pattern ends, the new selection starts from the beginning without losing sync.
Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/04 Clear
  • Hold Clear and press a Scene to return it to the default assignment: Pattern 1 on every track.
Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/11 Print to Clip
  • Print to Clip transfers the selected sequencer track’s current Pattern or Pattern chain directly into an Ableton Live clip slot.
  • In Patterns View, hold Print to Clip and press a pulsing track-select button to print that track’s selected Pattern or chain.

  • Note/Info Print to Clip works only while connected to Ableton Live. In Ableton Live Lite, the button goes dark when all eight clip slots in a track are full.
