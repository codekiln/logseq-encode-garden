NotesPerformance and session context
  • Hold several notes in paraphonic mode to hear the noise-and-pitched blend across the keyboard. The looping Cycling Envelope sweeps noise character while key position changes Timbre and cutoff. The LFO modulates its own rate and the Cycling Envelope’s amount and fall, so the saved modulation wiring can produce changing motion without manual knob turns. NotesPerformance and session context
  • Play single notes low on the keyboard. Each note starts a short main-envelope pluck that opens the low-pass filter and, through the negative ENV → Wave route, pulls the waveform and timbre on the attack. The looping Cycling Envelope at high amount adds a repeating pulse, and the small glide joins legato notes. Pressure brightens the timbre.
