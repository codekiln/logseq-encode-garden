    • 36.2 Browsing and Loading Sounds

      • Browse mode loads sounds from Push 2 without Live's browser.
      • The display shows columns: the leftmost is the device category being browsed or Collections; each column to the right is the next subfolder or the folder's contents. Scroll with the eight encoders above the display or step with the arrow buttons; the two rightmost upper display buttons move up and down the hierarchy. Live's default devices load from the top level.
      • Official Pack and Core Library items preview when selected; the Preview button toggles this, and Shift + the Master encoder sets preview volume.
      • Load loads the selection.
      • What Browse shows follows the last selected device: instruments replace instruments, effects replace effects. An empty MIDI track shows everything — sounds, instruments, drum kits, effects, Max for Live devices and plug-ins.
