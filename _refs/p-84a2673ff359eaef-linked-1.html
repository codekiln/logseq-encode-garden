The Elements of StyleWhy it is here
  • Book/Clear and Simple as the Truth is written against books of this kind. Thomas and Turner argue that rules of grammar, sense and sentence structure do not by themselves produce effective prose, and that step-by-step programmes make the problem worse by ignoring that there are several styles with different standards. What they offer instead is a stance the writer takes toward truth, reader and occasion.
