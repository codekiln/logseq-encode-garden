Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/01 Monitoring
17.1 MonitoringEach audio and MIDI track has three monitor modes:
  • Auto (Ableton/Track/IO/Monitor/Auto) — monitors while the track is armed, but not while it plays clips. Suits most recording.
17.1 MonitoringEach audio and MIDI track has three monitor modes:
  • Off (Ableton/Track/IO/Monitor/Off) — never monitors. For acoustic instruments heard in the room, an external mixer, or an interface's direct monitoring. Set Overall Latency in the Audio Settings when recording this way.
Ableton/Live/UG/40 MIDI Fact Sheet/05 Tips for Achieving Optimal MIDI Performance
40.5 Tips for Achieving Optimal MIDI PerformancePractices for the tightest MIDI timing:
  • Turn track monitoring off when you listen to a hardware synth directly rather than through Live's External Instrument, and when recording MIDI generated by another device such as a drum machine. Monitoring adds latency to cover playthrough jitter, so use it only when actually playing through Live. See Ableton/Track/IO/Monitor/Off.
