  • There's something special about radio. I remember making short-wave radio as a kid from an electronics kit. I climbed out the window and drove a screwdriver in the earth to ground the kit. I had a single earpiece that pulled in signals from far across the country. I felt like I was talking to distant planets. I wanted to say back, "I'm here, too." I always wanted to get into ham radio, and even started studying the test for it as a kid, thinking that might be in the cards, but it failed to materialize.
