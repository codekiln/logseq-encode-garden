9.2 Steps View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/01 Play Area

    • 9.2.1 Using the Play Area

      • On Chromatic and Scale tracks, Up and Down shift the Play Area by an octave.
      • On Drum tracks, Up and Down shift by 16 notes (one 4 × 4 grid). Left and Right move the Drum layout by one row of four notes.
9.2 Steps View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/02 Assign Notes

    • 9.2.2 Assigning to steps

      • Hold a note in the Play Area and press a step, or hold a step and press notes in the Play Area. Each step can hold up to eight notes.
      • Hold a step to preview it. Assigned notes light red in the Play Area.
      • Hold a Play Area note briefly to see its assigned steps light red. If you hold a chord, only steps containing every held note light red.
9.2 Steps View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/03 Clear Steps

    • 9.2.3 Clearing steps

      • Hold Clear and press a step to remove all its notes.
      • To remove one note, hold its step and press the assigned red note in the Play Area.
9.2 Steps View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/04 Duplicate Steps

    • 9.2.4 Duplicating steps

      • Hold Duplicate and press the source step, then press a destination step while still holding Duplicate. Keep holding Duplicate to paste to more steps.
9.2 Steps View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/05 Playback

    • 9.2.5 Playback

      • Press Play to begin at the start of the patterns.
      • To resume a stopped sequence at its stopped position, hold Shift and press Play. Play lights gold while Shift is held.
9.2 Steps View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/06 Record

    • 9.2.6 Recording into the sequencer

      • Press Record [O] to enable live recording; the button lights red. While the sequencer plays, notes from the Play Area are recorded.
      • Incoming MIDI from USB or the TRS MIDI In port is recorded too.
9.2 Steps View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/07 Gate Length

    • 9.2.7 Setting gate length

      • Press and briefly hold a step until it pulses green. Keep holding it and press a later step to set how many steps the notes last. The intervening steps pulse green.
      • To reset the gate to one step, press the pad that would set a two-step gate twice. The second press returns the gate to one step.
      • Ten-step gate length
9.2 Steps View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/08 Tracks

    • 9.2.8 Using multiple sequencer tracks

      • The four sequencer tracks play simultaneously. Select a track with its lit track-select button; each track’s button and Play Area use the same color.
9.2 Steps View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/02 Steps View/09 Ableton Live

    • 9.2.9 Using the Sequencer with Ableton Live

      • Ableton Live MIDI tracks accept input from any channel by default. If several sequencer tracks play while multiple Live tracks are armed, the notes can reach every armed track. Assign a separate input channel to each Live track.
      • In Live, choose “Launchpad Pro MK3 (LPProMK3 MIDI)” as MIDI From on Mac, or “LPProMK3 MIDI (MIDI 1)” on Windows.
      • Ableton Live MIDI input port
      • Set each Live track to the channel of its Launchpad sequencer track. By default, Launchpad tracks 1–4 send on MIDI channels 1–4. Sequencer Settings can change those channels.
      • Ableton Live track MIDI channels
      • Set Monitor to Auto and arm the Live tracks. To arm several at once, hold Command on Mac or Control on Windows while clicking each record-arm button. You can instead disable “Arm Exclusive” from a record-arm button’s context menu.
      • Ableton Live Arm Exclusive setting

9.3 Patterns View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/03 Patterns View/01 Chain

    • 9.3.1 Chaining Patterns

      • Press two Pattern pads at once to set the start and end of a chain. During playback, the Patterns play in order. A chain can span all eight Patterns, for up to 256 steps.
9.3 Patterns View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/03 Patterns View/02 Queue

    • 9.3.2 Queuing Patterns

      • During playback, press a Pattern or Pattern chain to queue it. The selection flashes and begins after the current Pattern ends.
9.3 Patterns View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/03 Patterns View/03 Clear

    • 9.3.3 Clearing a Pattern

      • Hold Clear and press the Pattern to erase it.
9.3 Patterns View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/03 Patterns View/04 Duplicate

    • 9.3.4 Duplicating a Pattern

      • Hold Duplicate and press the source Pattern, then press a destination Pattern slot. Keep Duplicate held to paste the copy into additional slots.
9.3 Patterns View
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/03 Patterns View/05 Instant Change

    • 9.3.5 Instant Pattern Change

      • During playback, hold Shift and choose a Pattern or chain to switch immediately. The sequencer enters the new selection at the point it would have reached if that selection had played since playback began.

      • Note/Info An instant switch can bring tracks back into time after queued switches between Patterns of different lengths.

9.4 Scenes
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/01 Assign

    • 9.4.1 Assigning to Scenes

      • Hold a Scene to preview its assigned Patterns or Pattern chains; after a short delay they light red.
      • While holding a Scene, press a Pattern or Pattern chain to assign it. You can also hold a Pattern chain and press a Scene.
      • Editing the Scene does not change current playback. The new assignment takes effect when you reselect the Scene or its Scene chain cycles back to it.
      • A Scene changes from green to white when the currently playing Pattern chains differ from its saved assignments.
9.4 Scenes
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/02 Chain

    • 9.4.2 Chaining Scenes

      • Press two Scenes together to set the start and end of a chain. When you press Play, the Scenes play in order. The sequencer advances after every track has completed its Pattern chain at least once.
9.4 Scenes
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/03 Queue

    • 9.4.3 Queuing Scenes

      • During playback, select a Scene or Scene chain to queue it. The queued pads flash; after Track 1’s current Pattern ends, the new selection starts from the beginning without losing sync.
      • For an immediate switch, hold Shift and select a Scene or chain. The sequencer enters it at the point it would have reached if it had played since playback began.

      • Note/Info An instant switch can bring tracks back into time after queued switches between Scenes of different lengths.

9.4 Scenes
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/04 Clear

    • 9.4.4 Clearing a Scene

      • Hold Clear and press a Scene to return it to the default assignment: Pattern 1 on every track.
9.4 Scenes
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/04 Scenes/05 Duplicate

    • 9.4.5 Duplicating a Scene

      • Hold Duplicate and press the source Scene, then press a destination Scene slot. Keep Duplicate held to paste it into additional slots.

9.5 Pattern Settings
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/05 Pattern Settings/01 Sync Rate

    • 9.5.1 Pattern Sync Rate

      • Eight peach pads set how long each step lasts relative to the current tempo: 1/4, 1/4T, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16, 1/16T, 1/32, or 1/32T. T denotes a triplet.
      • The default is 1/16, one sixteenth note per step. Faster rates give finer step resolution but shorten the Pattern’s total duration. Slower rates make longer Patterns with less timing detail.
9.5 Pattern Settings
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/05 Pattern Settings/02 Direction

    • 9.5.2 Pattern Playback Direction

      • Four pink pads select playback order from left to right:
        • Forward: Play from the start step to the end step.
        • Backward: Play from the end step to the start step.
        • Ping-Pong: Alternate forward and backward. Both endpoint steps play twice, so the Pattern cycle doubles in length.
        • Random: Choose each step anew during playback. A step may repeat any number of times in one cycle, but the cycle keeps its length when chained to other Patterns.
      • Changes made during playback take effect at the end of the current Pattern cycle.
9.5 Pattern Settings
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/05 Pattern Settings/03 Start End

    • 9.5.3 Pattern start and end points

      • In Pattern Settings, press a step to set the Pattern’s end point; the selected end step lights peach.
      • Hold Shift to display the start point in peach, then press a step while Shift is held to move the start point.
      • Steps containing notes outside the selected range are dim red. Empty steps outside the range stay unlit.

9.6 Velocity
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/06 Velocity/01 Step Velocity

    • 9.6.1 Step Velocity edit

      • Press Velocity to see and edit the velocity of each step. Select a step in the top half of the grid; its velocity appears on the two-row slider at the top of the Play Area.
      • Press a slider pad to set the selected step’s velocity from 1 (minimum) to 16 (maximum). Manual edits set every note on a multi-note step to the same velocity.

      • Live recording can put notes with different velocities on one step. Bright pads show the lowest velocity; dim pads show the range up to the highest recorded velocity.

9.7 Probability
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/07 Probability/01 Edit

    • 9.7.1 Editing Step Probability

      • Press Probability, then select a step in the top half of the grid. The top row of the Play Area shows the selected step’s probability on an eight-pad slider.
      • The pads, from left to right, set the chance of a note playing to 13%, 25%, 38%, 50%, 63%, 75%, 88%, or 100%.
      • Every note on a step shares one probability setting, but each note is evaluated independently. At 50%, a two-note step may play both notes, one note, or neither.
      • Assigned or recorded notes default to 100%. Clearing a step, Pattern, or Project also resets its probability to 100%.
      • Probability view
9.7 Probability
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/07 Probability/02 Print

    • 9.7.2 Printing Probability

      • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/11 Print to Clip resolves the probability of each step once when it transfers the Pattern to Ableton Live. Print the same Pattern repeatedly to capture repeatable variations in separate clips.

9.8 Mutation
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/08 Mutation/01 Edit

    • 9.8.1 Editing Step Mutation

      • Press Mutation and select a step in the top half of the grid. The top row of the Play Area shows an eight-pad Mutation slider for that step.
      • The leftmost value means no Mutation; the rightmost is the maximum. Newly recorded or assigned steps begin with no Mutation (one pad lit).
      • Mutation view
9.8 Mutation
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/08 Mutation/02 Print

    • 9.8.2 Printing Mutation

      • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/11 Print to Clip resolves each step’s Mutation once as it transfers the Pattern to Ableton Live. Print repeatedly to capture several repeatable variations.
      • Combine Mutation with Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/07 Probability to make evolving Patterns, then print the results as clips.

  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/09 Micro Steps/01 Edit

      • Hold Duplicate and press a source Micro Step, then press a destination Micro Step while still holding Duplicate.
      • The paste does not occur if it would raise the total notes on the whole step above eight.
