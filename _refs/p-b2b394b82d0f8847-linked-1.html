  • Purchase should have a Logseq/Entity/Time/Year and a Logseq/Entity/Time/Date. you'll need to define the later. Look at Logseq/Frontmatter attributes like date-created on pages to get a sense. Keep in mind that the date should be a journal reference, and it doesn't matter if the journal reference exists on disk. Also, in my logseq configuration, references that include the day of the week at the end resolve to a journal in either YYYY-MM-DD or the format that includes the weekday abbreviation suffix, e.g. 2026-09-24 or 2026-09-24 Thu resolve to the same file in Logseq.
