31.9 Operator
  • Operator combines Synthesis/Frequency Modulation with subtractive and additive synthesis. Four multi-waveform oscillators can modulate each other's frequencies, and it adds a filter, an LFO, global controls, and envelopes for each oscillator, the filter, the LFO and pitch.

The Wind Blowing Sounds of Nature#Description
  • A tribute to Person/John Chowning’s Synthesis/Frequency Modulation: four independent Csound voices, each mapped to wind parameters, are sculpted in real time with a custom MIDI controller; swirling glissandi evoke mountain gusts, while filtered noise suggests distant storms.
