35.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/01 Loop Selector

    • 35.3.1 Loop Selector

      • The grid splits into three parts so you can play, step-sequence and set the clip length at once.
      • The 16 drum pads use Drum Rack's 4x4 layout. Pad colors: bright yellow has a sound, dull yellow is empty, green is playing, light blue is selected, dark blue is soloed, orange is muted.
      • The touch strip or Octave Up and Down move through larger racks 16 pads at a time; add Shift to move by single rows.
      • Holding Note briefly shows the 16 Velocities layout; Shift + Note locks it, and Note again unlocks it.
35.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/02 16 Velocities Mode

    • 35.3.2 16 Velocities Mode

      • Here the bottom-right 16 pads are 16 velocities for the selected drum pad; tap one to enter steps at that velocity.
      • Holding Note briefly shows the loop length controls; Shift + Note locks them.
35.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/03 64-Pad Mode

    • 35.3.3 64-Pad Mode

      • Uses the whole 8x8 grid for playing drums, which suits large kits such as sliced samples. Press Note again to toggle to it, and again to return to Loop Selector. Pad colors match the three-part layout.
      • The 16 pads shown for step sequencing don't follow layout changes, so you may need the touch strip or Octave keys to bring the right ones into view.
35.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/04 Loading Individual Drums

    • 35.3.4 Loading Individual Drums

      • Browse can load or replace a single pad. Press Device to show the track's devices, tap the pad, then press the selection button under its name. Browse now targets that pad, and tapping other pads while browsing retargets.

      • 35.3.4.1 Additional Pad Options for Push 1

        • Hold Duplicate, press a pad, then press the destination pad to copy it. The destination's devices and sound are replaced, but its recorded notes stay.
35.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/05 Step Sequencing Beats

    • 35.3.5 Step Sequencing Beats

      • Tapping a pad also picks it for step sequencing; hold Select while tapping to pick it silently. Tap step pads to place notes; the clip starts as soon as you tap a step. Each step is a 16th note by default, changed with the Scene/Grid buttons.
      • A moving green pad marks the playing step, red while recording. Tapping a filled step deletes the note; hold Mute to deactivate a step instead, or Solo to solo a sound. Hold Delete and tap a pad to clear its notes within the loop.
      • Step colors: unlit is empty, blue has a note (darker means higher velocity), dull yellow is a muted note. With triplet steps the two right columns turn red and are unused.
      • Velocity and micro-timing per note are covered in Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/12 Step Sequencing Automation; loop length pads in Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/06 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Adjusting the Loop Length.
35.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/06 Real-time Recording

    • 35.3.6 Real-time Recording

      • Turn on Metronome for a click if wanted, then press Record and play the pads. Record again stops recording but keeps playing; a third press starts overdub, and further presses toggle playback and overdub.
      • New stops the selected clip and readies a new one on the track, so you can practice first. By default it also copies the clips playing on other tracks to a new scene and keeps them playing; the Workflow preference changes this.
35.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/07 Fixed Length Recording

    • 35.3.7 Fixed Length Recording

      • Fixed Length makes new clips a set length; hold it to choose the length.
      • With it off, clips record until you press Record, New or Play/Stop. Turning it on mid-recording stops recording and loops the last few bars, per the Fixed Length setting.

35.4 Additional Recording Options
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/04 Additional Recording Options/01 Recording with Repeat

    • 35.4.1 Recording with Repeat

      • With Repeat on, holding a pad plays an even stream of notes, handy for steady hi-hats; finger pressure sets their volume. The Scene/Grid buttons set the rate, and Push remembers Repeat per track.
      • A quick press latches Repeat on; holding it makes it momentary. The Swing knob adds swing, shown on the display while touched.
35.4 Additional Recording Options
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/04 Additional Recording Options/02 Quantizing

    • 35.4.2 Quantizing

      • Quantize snaps notes in the selected clip to the grid. Hold it for options:
        • Swing Amount — also on Encoder 1 or the Swing knob.
        • Quantize To — the nearest note value to snap to.
        • Quantize Amount — how far notes may move.
        • Record Quantize — quantize while recording, value on Encoder 8; mirrors the Record Quantization chooser in Live's Edit menu.
      • Hold Quantize and press a drum pad to quantize only that drum's notes.

35.5 Playing Melodies and Harmonies
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/05 Playing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Playing in Other Keys

    • 35.5.1 Playing in Other Keys

      • Scales changes key and scale with the display and the Selection and State Control buttons. The first encoder or the two far-left buttons pick the scale type.
      • Fixed Y/N — with Fixed on, the grid stays put when the key changes and the bottom-left pad stays C, or the nearest key note. With it off, the bottom-left pad is always the root.
      • In Key/Chromatic — In Key folds the grid to scale notes only; Chromatic shows every note with in-key notes lit.
      • Shift in Scales offers more layouts: "4th" and "3rd" set the interval between rows, ^ and > set whether it runs up or right, and "Sequent" lays notes out in order with no duplicates for a wide range.
      • Key, scale, In Key/Chromatic and Fixed are saved with the Set.
      • Drum recording options (fixed length, Repeat, quantize) work here too. Hold Delete and tap a pad to remove every note of that pitch in the loop.

35.6 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/06 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Adjusting the Loop Length

    • 35.6.1 Adjusting the Loop Length

      • Each loop length pad is a page of steps; a page's length depends on the step size. Drums at 16ths show two pages, or two bars; the Melodic Sequencer shows one page of eight steps, or two beats.
      • Hold one pad and tap another to set the loop; double-tap a pad for a one-page loop.
      • Pages follow the play position by default. Single-tap a page to lock the view to it while the loop keeps its length; reselect the loop to follow again. Tapping a page outside the loop sets the loop to it.
      • Colors: unlit is outside the loop, white is in the loop but not shown, dull yellow is shown but not playing, green is playing.
      • Shift + Note locks the pads per track. Hold Duplicate and press two page pads to copy a page on top of the destination; hold Delete and tap a page pad to clear it first.

35.7 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/07 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes/01 32 Notes

    • 35.7.1 32 Notes

      • The bottom half plays scale notes and selects them for sequencing; selected notes show in a lighter track color. Hold Select to select without playing.
      • Colors: blue is the root, blue-green is selected, green is playing, white is in scale.
      • Octave Up and Down shift the range; Shift with the touch strip moves by octaves and Shift with Octave moves by one scale note. The Scale menu applies here too.
35.7 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/07 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes/02 Sequencer

    • 35.7.2 Sequencer

      • Tapping a step in the top half adds all selected notes to it. Holding a step shows its notes in blue-green below; tap one to remove it. Holding several steps adds the notes to all of them.
      • Hold Duplicate, press a step, then another to copy notes on top of the destination.
      • Colors: blue has notes, green is playing, white is selected, dull yellow is muted, gray is empty; with triplets the two right columns go unlit and unused.
      • Two pages, or two bars, show at 16ths. Hold Note for loop length pads in the fifth row; Shift + Note locks them. Page copy and clear work as in Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/06 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Adjusting the Loop Length.

35.12 Step Sequencing Automation
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/12 Step Sequencing Automation/01 Note-Specific Parameters

    • 35.12.1 Note-Specific Parameters

      • In Clip Mode, hold a step to put its note settings on the encoders:
        • Nudge — offset from the previous grid line as a percentage; negative means early.
        • Length — coarse and fine.
        • Velocity
      • Hold several steps to edit them together; the display shows each value's range. Holding an empty step and turning an encoder creates a note with those values.
      • For drums, hold Select and press a pad to edit every note that pad plays. Hold Duplicate, tap a step, tap a destination, and release to copy a step with its settings.
35.12 Step Sequencing Automation
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/12 Step Sequencing Automation/02 Per-Step Automation

    • 35.12.2 Per-Step Automation

      • In Device or Volume Mode, hold steps and turn an encoder to write automation for just those steps' time, even on steps without notes.

35.13 Controlling Live’s Session View
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/13 Controlling Live's Session View/01 Session Overview

    • 35.13.1 Session Overview

      • Hold Shift in Session Mode to zoom out: each pad becomes an 8x8 block of clips, covering 64 scenes by 64 tracks, and each scene button a block of 64 scenes. Press a pad to jump there; row three, column one is scenes 17–24 and tracks 1–8.
      • Colors: amber is the current block, green has playing clips, red has none playing, unlit has no tracks or scenes.
