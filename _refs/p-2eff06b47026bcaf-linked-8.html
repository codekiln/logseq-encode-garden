Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc/01 as Soundgen
06.01 The oscillator as a sound generator
  • The ear can perceive sound around 50 Hz as bass (think huge organ pipes), but around 30 Hz it's difficult for the ear to hear a sound as a pitch; it's perceived as a low rumble. In electronic music, low-frequency sounds are often used as voltage or control signals to modulate another module. An LFO is an oscillator specifically designed to generate frequencies in this range. The LFO in the MicroFreak can generate signals in the range from 0.1Hz to 100Hz. Please refer to Microfreak/UG/08 LFO for details.

Microfreak/UG/07 Filter/02 Animating Sound
07.02 Animating sound
  • Changing the cutoff and resonance dynamically makes the filter a musical tool. The MicroFreak can use an LFO, an Arpeggiator, or an Envelope to control these parameters. See Microfreak/UG/08 LFO for the LFO; the Arpeggiator and Envelope chapters cover the other modulators.

08.2 LFO Rate
  • Microfreak/UG/08 LFO/02 LFO Rate/01 LFO Retriggering

    • 08.2.1 LFO Retriggering

      • LFO retriggering restarts the LFO each time you press a keyboard key or the arpeggiator or sequencer generates a gate signal. This can make the LFO add a small pitch rise at the start of each note.
      • To enable retriggering, open Utility and set Preset > LFO Retrig to ON.
