4. Device Settings
  • Arturia/MCC/UG/04 Device Settings/01 Device Settings Tab

    • 4.1. The Device Settings tab

      • MCC reads connected device settings automatically. Open the tab and scroll to see all parameters.
      • Global parameters can include clock and CV/Gate settings, tempo, MIDI Thru, user channel, pedal behavior, and velocity or aftertouch curves. Available parameters depend on the device.
      • Changing a Device Setting in MCC also changes the connected device immediately.
