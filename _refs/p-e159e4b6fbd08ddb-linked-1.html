36. Using Push 2
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/04 Additional Recording Options

    • 36.4 Additional Recording Options

      • Repeat, quantizing and Arrangement recording from Push 2.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/04 Additional Recording Options/01 Recording with Repeat

        • 36.4.1 Recording with Repeat

          • With Repeat on, holding a pad plays an even stream of notes, such as steady hi-hats; pressure changes their volume. Scene/Grid sets the rate.
          • Push 2 remembers Repeat per track. Tap to latch; hold for momentary use.
          • The Swing knob adds swing to repeated notes and shows the amount when touched.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/04 Additional Recording Options/02 Quantizing

        • 36.4.2 Quantizing

          • Quantize snaps the selected clip's notes to the grid; hold it for options:
            • Swing Amount — swing applied while quantizing (also the Swing knob);
            • Quantize To — the note value;
            • Quantize Amount — how far notes move;
            • Record Quantize — quantize while recording, at the value set by encoder 5. Recorded notes don't get swing this way.
          • Hold Quantize and tap a drum pad to quantize only that drum's notes.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/04 Additional Recording Options/03 Arrangement Recording

        • 36.4.3 Arrangement Recording

          • With Arrangement View in focus, Record toggles Arrangement recording, capturing everything you do on Push 2.
          • With Session View in focus, Shift+Record starts Arrangement recording; with the Arrangement in focus, Shift+Record toggles Session recording instead.
