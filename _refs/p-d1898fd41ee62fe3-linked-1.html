36. Using Push 2
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/07 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes

    • 36.7 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes

      • This layout combines step sequencing (top half) with real-time playing (bottom half), covering several octaves and steps on one page — good for working out chords to sequence, and for longer phrases.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/07 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes/01 32 Notes

        • 36.7.1 32 Notes

          • The bottom half plays scale notes in real time; pressing a pad plays it and selects it for sequencing. Hold Select to choose without playing.
          • Pad colours: track colour for the root, lighter track colour for selected, green for playing, white for other scale notes.
          • Octave buttons shift the range; Shift+touch strip by octaves, Shift+Octave by one scale step. The Scale menu sets which notes appear.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/07 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes/02 Sequencer

        • 36.7.2 Sequencer

          • Tap a step in the top half to add all selected notes there. Hold a step to see its notes highlighted below; tap one to remove it. Holding several steps adds notes to all of them; hold Duplicate to copy one step to another.
          • Step colours: clip colour has notes; green playing; white selected; light grey muted note; grey empty; unlit unavailable with triplet steps.
          • Hold Layout for the loop length pads in the fifth row; Shift+Layout locks them (per track); Page Left/Right move between pages. Duplicate and Delete with a loop length pad copy or clear a page.
