Launchpad/Scale/Q/What scales are available?
Where are the Music Scales in the Launchpad documented? card
  • Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/13 Settings/04 Select Scale

    • 9.13.4 Selecting the Scale

      • Press one of the 16 scale pads. The selected scale lights bright white; the others are dim blue. The choice applies in Launchpad/UG/06 Note Mode, Launchpad/UG/07 Chord Mode, and Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer modes.

      • Available scales

        • Top Row (1)

          • Left

            • (1, 1) - Music/Scale/Minor/Natural
            • (1, 2) - Music/Scale/Major
            • (1, 3) - Music/Scale/Dorian
            • (1, 4) - Music/Scale/Phrygian

          • Right

            • (1, 5) - Music/Scale/Mixolydian
            • (1, 6) - Music/Scale/Minor/Melodic
            • (1, 7) - Music/Scale/Minor/Harmonic
            • (1, 8) - Music/Scale/Dorian/Bebop

        • Bottom Row (2)

          • Left

            • (2, 1) - Music/Scale/Blues
            • (2, 2) - Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor
            • (2, 3) - Music/Scale/Minor/Hungarian
            • (2, 4) - Music/Scale/Dorian/Ukrainian

          • Right

            • (2, 5) - Music/Scale/Marwa
            • (2, 6) - Music/Scale/Todi
            • (2, 7) - Music/Scale/Whole Tone
            • (2, 8) - Music/Scale/Hirajoshi
