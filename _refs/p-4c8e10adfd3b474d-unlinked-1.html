Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Discussion
  • Writing a song is composing, and conducting is performing through an ensemble. Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song is a kind of Composer, and Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting is a kind of Performer.
  • The other shape put the music entities under Logseq/Entity/Person/Music, with Person/Music/Composition, Person/Music/Theory and the rest beneath it.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music
  • Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition — Composer, with Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song beneath it
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition
  • A person known mainly for songs in the popular, folk, blues or rock traditions carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song and leaves Composer out, since a Songwriter is a kind of Composer. Franz Schubert, who wrote art songs, and Tito Puente, who wrote tunes for his band, carry Composer.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song
  • In this garden, Songwriter pages model a real person who writes songs: the music, the words, or both. A Songwriter is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition, so a page marked Songwriter leaves Composer out.
  • A composer of art songs, such as Franz Schubert and his Lieder, carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition. So does a jazz or Latin bandleader who writes tunes for the band, such as Tito Puente and "Oye Como Va".
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance
  • A performer who writes the pieces they play adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition. One known mainly for songs in the popular, folk, blues or rock traditions adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song, as Person/Adrianne Lenker does.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting
  • A composer who conducts carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition and Conductor: Person/John Adams, Person/Guy Reibel.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production
  • An electronic producer who also writes works presented as compositions, such as concert pieces or film scores, adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition: Person/Daniel Lopatin.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Theory
  • A Music Theorist is a maker of theory. A person who also made music carries the kind of Musician that names their role as well, as a composer-theorist carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition and Music Theorist.
