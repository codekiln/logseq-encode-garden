    • 4.6 Library

      • Library labels group content by type, searchable and filterable:
        • All: every item, devices first, then files.
        • Sounds: Instrument Racks and instrument presets, plus anything tagged in the Sounds group.
        • Drums: drum devices, Drum Racks, single hits, loops and one-shots, and anything tagged Drums.
        • Instruments, Audio Effects and MIDI Effects: built-in devices, their presets and your Racks, organized by device.
        • Modulators: modulator devices and anything tagged Modulator.
        • Max for Live: Max for Live devices, presets and Racks built from them.
        • Plug-Ins: VST and Audio Units plug-ins.
        • Clips, Samples, Grooves and Templates: Live Clips, raw samples, grooves and template Sets.
        • Tunings: Core Library tunings and saved .ascl or .scl files.
      • The Edit button beside the Library header chooses which labels show.
