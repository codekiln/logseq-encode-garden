⋯Goal: Decrease Friction to Making And Then Sharing MusicI've been trying to get a synth podcast up for quite a while. I made a bit of progress yesterday on GitP/A, but I got bogged down with the Distractors of Secrets Management and Knowledge Gardening.When I first started Ghost in the Patch, in each "episode" I narrated my creation of Microfreak/UG/04 Presets step by step, using Jargon and key vocabulary. Usually, by the end of the episode, I had longer periods without narration as I just played. It was a bit like falling asleep or into a trance. That was nice, in a way, but it was more than just a bit tangled in approach. I knew that I might be able to use AI/Voice/to/Text turn my narration into a transcription, then relate that to keywords in the Microfreak/Docs and use that to examine the MIDI captures to provide more informed commentary or linkages between various sounds of different episodes.
  • I kind of planned to eventually use some kind of Embedding model to find similar parts from similar episodes, then use that to drive some kind of a knowledge garden and composition system.
