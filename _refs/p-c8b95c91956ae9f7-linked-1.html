35. Using Push 1
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/12 Step Sequencing Automation

    • 35.12 Step Sequencing Automation

      • Both drum and melodic step sequencers can automate parameters for selected steps. What can be changed depends on the display mode.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/12 Step Sequencing Automation/01 Note-Specific Parameters

        • 35.12.1 Note-Specific Parameters

          • In Clip Mode, hold a step to put its note settings on the encoders:
            • Nudge — offset from the previous grid line as a percentage; negative means early.
            • Length — coarse and fine.
            • Velocity
          • Hold several steps to edit them together; the display shows each value's range. Holding an empty step and turning an encoder creates a note with those values.
          • For drums, hold Select and press a pad to edit every note that pad plays. Hold Duplicate, tap a step, tap a destination, and release to copy a step with its settings.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/12 Step Sequencing Automation/02 Per-Step Automation

        • 35.12.2 Per-Step Automation

          • In Device or Volume Mode, hold steps and turn an encoder to write automation for just those steps' time, even on steps without notes.
