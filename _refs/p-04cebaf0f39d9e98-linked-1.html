Major ScaleHistory
  • Over the seventeenth century Ionian and Aeolian absorbed the other modes into major-minor tonality. Person/Jean-Philippe Rameau's Traité de l'harmonie (17/2/2) grounded the major triad in the resonance of a vibrating body and gave major its theoretical priority.

Jean-Philippe RameauAbout
  • His Traité de l'harmonie réduite à ses principes naturels (17/2/2), his most important work of music theory, analyzed chord progressions by a "fundamental bass" of chord roots.
