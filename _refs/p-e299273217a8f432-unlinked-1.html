AI/Agent/Agentic/Engineer/ing/Q/What is fait accompli and amor fati, how are these related if at all, and why are more and more podcasts related to agentic engineering talking about these terms?
  • Short answer: Fait accompli is a French phrase for an irreversible done deal — something already decided or executed, so later objection is mostly theater. Amor fati is Latin for "love of fate" — a Stoic / Nietzschean stance of actively affirming what has happened (and what you cannot control), not merely enduring it. They are related as situation vs stance: a fait accompli is the irreversible fact; amor fati is one response to living with irreversible facts. Agentic-engineering discourse leans on both because agents keep producing irreversible outcomes faster than humans can deliberate, and the cultural shock of that speed needs a vocabulary for both the done deal and how to live with it.
  • Practical dual use. Governance writing uses fait accompli as a warning (don't let the agent decide first). Culture writing uses amor fati as a prescription (don't waste life resenting the curve). Same discourse community; complementary jobs.
