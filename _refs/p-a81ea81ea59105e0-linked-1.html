Poesy
  • Archaic word for Poetry or the art of writing it (e.g. Sir Philip Sidney's The Defence of Poesy). A period synonym, not a distinct concept.
Prose
Prose
  • Ordinary written or spoken language with no fixed metrical structure or deliberate line breaks. The true counterpart of Verse — not of Poetry.
Prosody
Prosody
  • The study of rhythm, meter, stress, and intonation in Verse or speech. Not a genre like Poetry — a descriptive framework for the sound system underneath it.
Verse
Verse
  • Language deliberately arranged into metrical lines — a formal container, contrasted with Prose. Poetry is usually written in verse, but the two aren't identical: verse names the form, poetry names the effect.

    • Verse describes the form: metrical, lineated language. Poetry describes the effect: heightened, musical, imagistic language used to convey feeling or meaning.
