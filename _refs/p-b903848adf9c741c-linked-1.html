Poetry
Poetry
  • The art form that uses rhythm, sound, and compressed imagery to evoke feeling or meaning. Usually written in Verse, but not always — prose poetry keeps poetry's devices while running as unbroken Prose. Prosody studies the sound patterns poetry (and speech) relies on.
Verse
Verse
  • Language deliberately arranged into metrical lines — a formal container, contrasted with Prose. Poetry is usually written in verse, but the two aren't identical: verse names the form, poetry names the effect.
