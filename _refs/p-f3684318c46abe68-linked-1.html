    • Risk/vs/Reward/Reward
      • Reaching for reward means choosing the option with the largest expected gain.
      • Gains — the upside itself: return, capability, reach or learning that the cautious option leaves on the table.
      • Costs — the risk that comes attached, since the options with the most reward carry the widest spread, and the habit of counting the upside while discounting the downside.
      • Too far — chasing: taking on any exposure that promises a payoff, until the accumulated spread swamps the expected gain. Its mirror image is leaving reward on the table out of habit, taking only what is certain and small.
