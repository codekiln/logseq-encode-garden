    • 4.1 Content Pane

      • The content pane lists the items in the selected label, or the results of a search or filter.
      • Name is always shown. Add more columns from the Content Options menu, also opened by right-clicking a column name, and drag column names to reorder them.
      • Click a column name to sort by it, ascending or descending. The Content Options menu also shows or hides file extensions.
