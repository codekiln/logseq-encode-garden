Audio/Connector/Q/What is the difference between a line out port and a headphone port, and how do they differ on the Zoom H5?

  • On the Zoom H5

    • The H5 carries both, as two separate unbalanced 1/8 in (3.5 mm) stereo jacks:
    • JackMaximum output levelOutput impedanceLevel set by
      Zoom H5/Port/Line Out+1 dBu110 Ω or lessthe LINE OUT LEVEL menu setting
      Zoom H5/Port/Headphone20 mW + 20 mW into 32 Ω10 Ω or lessthe physical volume buttons
    • Answer/Official from the H5 Operation Manual: LINE OUT LEVEL sits under the IN/OUT menu and runs from −30 to 0 dB. Its stated purpose is feeding "the external mic input jack on an SLR camera or other jack with a high input gain" without an attenuator cable in the line — the H5 pads its own line out rather than asking for a resistive cable.
    • The manual is explicit that the two outputs are independent: the LINE OUT LEVEL setting "does not affect the level output from the PHONE jack." That independence is the practical payoff, and it is what makes a simultaneous send-and-monitor rig possible on this recorder.
    • Both jacks are live at once, so a single H5 can feed a camera or interface from LINE OUT while the performer or engineer monitors on PHONE.

Zoom H5/Q/How can I record the line out on a MacBook or iPhone while monitoring on the headphone jack?

Page reference

  • Zoom H5/Port/Line Out and Zoom H5/Port/Headphone are separate jacks with separate level controls, and the LINE OUT LEVEL setting does not touch the headphone jack. The send and the monitoring never fight each other — see Audio/Connector/Q/What is the difference between a line out port and a headphone port, and how do they differ on the Zoom H5?.
