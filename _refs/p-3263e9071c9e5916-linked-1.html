    • 16.7.4 Adding Variations in Sync

      • Randomly triggering clips that differ only in clip envelopes or warp settings gives small variations, such as changing pitch bend, effects or transposition.
      • Identical melody clips in Legato Mode can hand over to each other without the melody changing; editing each clip's settings, envelopes or notes a little at a time then makes the melody change gradually.
