Arturia does have their own piece of software for interacting with presets on the Microfreak, the Arturia MIDI Control Center, but it's a GUI application and as such it suck my life away like a vampire with all its clicking and dragging. The original idea here came from Arturia/MicroFreak/Idea/Rust CLI Preset Manager, which aimed to create a CLI called freakio based on Elektroid and Arturia/Forum/24/05/MIDI Control Center alternative for MicroFreak on Linux. Ok, so here's what I'm aiming for: either mise/Task/File (see also My/Pref/Dev/mise/Tasks) or a dedicated CLI which can enable AI agents to keep my Microfreak presets in sync with my knowledge garden's Microfreak/Preset area of the garden without me needing to get too involved. It's okay to download elektroid and use it if that works, or you can make a new repo with a fresh CLI that make a streamlined version of this possible in a reusable way. BTW Elektroid source code is available to you locally.
