Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio/03 Pasting Bounced Audio
  • Copy clips in one Session track, or clips or a time selection in the Arrangement (a group's main lane works too). Then paste into an audio track, an empty MIDI track or a take lane from the main lane's context menu, or with Cmd+Option+V (Mac) / Ctrl+Alt+V (Windows). An empty MIDI track becomes an audio track.
Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping/01 Take Lanes
  • Every audio or MIDI track in Arrangement View has a main lane, heard by default, and any number of take lanes, heard only when auditioned.
  • Show or hide them with Show Take Lanes in the track header's context menu, the toggle on the main lane, or Cmd+Option+U (Mac) / Ctrl+Alt+U (Windows).
Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping/02 Inserting and Managing Take Lanes
  • Delete: Delete/Backspace or Edit → Delete. Delete All Take Lanes clears a track; Delete All Unused Take Lanes removes lanes that contribute nothing to the main lane's comp.
Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping/03 Recording Takes
  • Takes share the track colour unless Clip Color is set to Random in Theme & Colors Settings.
Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping/04 Inserting Samples
  • Drag samples or MIDI files onto take lanes from the browser or Finder/File Explorer. Hold Cmd (Mac) / Ctrl (Windows) while dragging several to place each on its own track.
Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping/05 Auditioning Take Lanes
  • Lanes on different tracks can play at the same time, but only one lane per track. With several selected, the last-selected lane on each track plays.
Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping/07 Source Highlights
  • Take material used in the main lane's comp is highlighted in the track colour; unused material is desaturated. Highlights only show when position and clip properties match.
Ableton/Live/UG/22 Stem Separation/02 Separating Audio Files and Clips
  • Merge to Single Track combines two or three chosen stems into one;
  • Each stem gets its own track inside a new Group Track, coloured to match the dialog's toggles; the group takes the source track's colour. A single stem, or a merged one, goes on one track instead.
  • Source track effects move to the Group Track; track automation is copied to the group, and clip envelopes and modulation to each stem clip.
Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects
  • The Device View and how MIDI effects, instruments and audio effects sit in a track's device chain, including delay compensation.
  • Every track can hold devices of three kinds:
    • MIDI effects — work on MIDI; MIDI tracks only.
    • Audio effects — work on audio; audio, return and Main tracks, or MIDI tracks after an instrument.
    • Instruments — take MIDI in and put audio out; MIDI tracks only.
Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects/01 Device View
  • The Device View at the bottom of the window shows the selected track's devices; double-click a track's title bar to open it.
Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects/02 Using Devices
  • Double-click a device to add it to the selected track (or a new track), select it and press Enter, or drag it to a track, a drop area or the Device View. Dropping a sample into a MIDI track's Device View creates a Ableton/Instrument/Simpler with it.
  • With Auto monitoring, a track must be armed to hear an external input through its devices; adding an instrument to a MIDI track usually arms it. See Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/01 Monitoring for other setups.
  • Signals run left to right, so order matters. In a MIDI track, everything before the instrument is MIDI and everything after is audio, so a chain can go MIDI effects → instrument → audio effects.
  • Select a device's title bar and press Delete to remove it; drag title bars to reorder, or into another track to move. Cut, copy, paste and duplicate work too; pasted devices go before the selected one, or at the end if you click after the last device. Changes don't interrupt the audio.
Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects/02 Using Devices/03 Live Device Presets
  • Browse with the arrow keys: up and down to move, left and right to close and open folders, Enter to load onto the selected track. Double-click or drag also work.
Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects/02 Using Devices/04 Hot-Swapping Presets
  • Q swaps the selected device, or else the first audio effect on an audio track or the instrument on a MIDI track.
Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects/02 Using Devices/06 Defaults

  • 23.2.6.3 Track Defaults

    • Set up a track's devices and mixer settings (routing, volume, pan, sends) and choose Save as Default Audio Track or Save as Default MIDI Track from its title bar's context menu. It goes to Creating Tracks → Audio Track or MIDI Track and applies to every new track of that kind.
    • Dragging a track there also works if it is named Default Audio Track or Default MIDI Track.
  • To choose the instrument made when a sample is dropped into an empty MIDI track's Device View or onto a Drum Rack pad, set up an empty Sampler or Simpler and drag it to Dropping Samples → On Device View or On Drum Rack. A pad's Save as Default Pad adds to On Drum Rack too.
  • Build an empty Drum Rack with one chain holding an empty Sampler or Simpler, plus any effects and Macro mappings, and drag it into the Slicing folder. It then appears in the Slicing Preset chooser of Slice to New MIDI Track; see Ableton/Live/UG/13 Audio to MIDI.
Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects/03 Device Delay Compensation
  • Very large track delays or plug-in latencies can make Live sluggish. Turning compensation off is not usually advised and also disables Track Delay; adjusting track delays may help instead. Compensation can add CPU load.
Ableton/Live/UG/24 Plug-Ins
  • Plug-ins appear under the browser's Plug-Ins label, instruments with a keyboard icon. Only Audio Units presets show in the browser, and some AU factory presets appear only once the device is on a track with Hot-Swap on.
Ableton/Live/UG/24 Plug-Ins/01 Plug-Ins in the Device View/01 Showing Plug-In Panels in Separate Windows
  • Auto-Hide Plug-In Windows — show only the selected track's windows.
  • View → Show/Hide Plug-In Windows, or Cmd+Option+P (Mac) / Ctrl+Alt+P (Windows), hides and shows windows already opened. Each window's title names its track.
Ableton/Live/UG/24 Plug-Ins/01 Plug-Ins in the Device View/02 Plug-In Configure Mode
  • After that, a plug-in works like a Live device: MIDI mapping, moving and copying, clip envelopes, multiple I/O in track routing (Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO) and custom info text.
Ableton/Live/UG/25 Racks
  • A Rack holds effects, plug-ins and instruments in a track's device chain. Racks can build complex processors, playable performance instruments and stacked synths, while gathering the most important controls in one place. Even a single device gains from a Rack, through new control relationships between its parameters.
Ableton/Live/UG/25 Racks/01 An Overview of Racks/01 Signal Flow and Parallel Device Chains
  • A track's devices normally run in series, left to right, in one chain. A Rack adds parallel chains.
Ableton/Live/UG/25 Racks/03 Looking at Racks
  • Right-click the Device View selector for a list of every device in the track, nested Racks included; choose one to jump to it.
Ableton/Live/UG/25 Racks/04 Chain List
  • The Chain List is where incoming signals branch: each parallel chain is one entry. Drop presets, devices or whole chains into the area below to add chains. Racks, chains and devices drag freely between Racks and tracks; hovering over another track while dragging opens its Device View.
Ableton/Live/UG/25 Racks/08 Mixing With Racks
  • Instrument and Drum Racks with more than one chain can be shown in the Session mixer: a button in the track's title bar, and on nested chains, folds the Rack's chains in or out.
Ableton/Live/UG/25 Racks/08 Mixing With Racks/01 Extracting Chains
  • A drum chain dragged from the mixer to a new track takes its MIDI notes along — for example, moving the snare from a drum loop to its own track with its devices and notes. Drag from the chain list to move only the devices.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/01 Recording Automation in Session View
  • Copying Session clips into the Arrangement turns their automation into track automation. Recording the Session into the Arrangement captures existing clip automation along with live changes.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/02 Recording Automation in Arrangement View
  • In Arrangement View, automation records on all tracks during Arrangement recording when Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Automation Arm is on. Individual track arming is unnecessary for automation.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/05 Viewing Automation
  • An automation envelope is a line of breakpoints drawn over the clip or track: value on the vertical axis, time on the horizontal. Drawing it over the content helps line breakpoints up with audio or notes.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/05 Viewing Automation/01 Viewing Automation in Session View
  • The Device chooser lists the track's devices, the mixer and MIDI Ctrl (MIDI controller clip envelopes). LEDs mark automated controls and the devices holding them. A control without automation shows as a dashed line at its current value.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/05 Viewing Automation/02 Viewing Automation in Arrangement View
  • Turn on Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Toggle/Automation Mode above the track headers, or press A, to open each track's main automation lane.
  • Lane header context menus offer Add Automation Lane (main lane), Add Lane for Each Automated Envelope, Remove Automation Lane (extra lanes), and commands to clear all envelopes for the track or one of its devices.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/09 Additional Tips for Working with Automation/01 Automating the Tempo
  • To see or draw it, turn on Automation Mode and choose Mixer → Song Tempo in the Main track's lane.
Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/01 The Clip Envelope Editor
  • audio clips — "Clip" (sample controls), each effect in the track, and the mixer;
  • MIDI clips — "MIDI Ctrl" (controller data), each device in the track, and the mixer.
  • Session clips show Automation and Modulation toggles below the choosers. Arrangement clips only take modulation here; their automation lives in the track's automation lanes.
Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/03 Mixer and Device Clip Envelopes/01 Modulating Mixer Volumes and Sends
  • Two modulation envelopes affect volume: Clip Gain, and Track Volume, which acts on the mixer after the effects. A small dot under the volume slider shows the modulated value and follows the slider.
Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/03 Mixer and Device Clip Envelopes/03 Modulating Device Controls
  • Every device on the clip's track is in the Device chooser. As with the mixer, a clip envelope can only move a device control relative to its current value, unlike a preset, which sets values outright.
Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/05 Unlinking Clip Envelopes From Clips/01 Programming a Fade-Out for a Live Set
  • Choose Clip Gain or Mixer Track Volume and unlink it from the sample. The envelope's loop braces turn coloured and its own loop and region controls become active; the sample keeps looping while the envelope plays once.
Ableton/Live/UG/28 Video/03 Matching Sound to Video
  • Drag a QuickTime movie from the browser onto an audio track; the Video Window opens.
  • Drag an audio clip into the drop area to create its own track, and unfold both tracks.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/02 Auto Filter
  • Auto Filter applies a choice of filter types and analog-modeled filter circuits, moved by an LFO and an envelope follower. The envelope follower can track the filtered signal or an external sidechain.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/04 Auto Shift
  • Auto Shift tracks and corrects pitch in real time, with vibrato, pitch shifting and formant shifting. Built mainly for vocals, it works on any monophonic audio. It corrects to a scale or to MIDI notes from another track, and has its own LFO.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/04 Auto Shift/01 Input Section
  • MIDI input corrects pitch to notes from another MIDI track instead of to a scale.
  • External Source picks the track; Tapping Point takes the notes before (Pre FX) or after (Post FX, Post Mixer) that track's MIDI effects.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/04 Auto Shift/03 MIDI Tab
  • The MIDI tab replaces the Quantizer tab when MIDI input is on. A keyboard shows the notes arriving from the source track.
  • Scale awareness is off with MIDI input. To keep notes in a scale, put a Ableton/Live/UG/30 MIDI Effect Reference/07 Scale effect on the source track and set Tapping Point to Post FX or Post Mixer.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/05 Beat Repeat
  • Gate — repeats only; suits a return track
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/08 Chorus-Ensemble
  • Dry/Wet — set to 100% on a return track; not available in Vibrato
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/09 Compressor
  • The orange Gain Reduction meter shows the current reduction. The manual warns that more than about 6 dB changes the sound a lot and can flatten dynamics in a way that cannot be undone later — especially on the Main track.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/09 Compressor/01 Sidechain Parameters
  • Right side, sidechain EQ — triggers from one frequency band, of this track or of the external source.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/09 Compressor/02 Compressor Tips
  • To make background music duck under a narrator, put Compressor on the music track and pick the narration track as the external sidechain.
  • Put Compressor on the bass (or Main) track with the kick as the sidechain, so the bass makes room for each kick.
  • With only a mixed drum track: use it as the sidechain, enable the sidechain EQ with the low-pass filter, and tune Frequency and Q until only the kick triggers it. Sidechain listen helps.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/10 Corpus/05 Sidechain Parameters
  • The title bar button opens the sidechain, which lights while it is active. Its MIDI From choosers pick a MIDI track and tapping point.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/11 Delay
  • Dry/Wet — set to 100% on a return track
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/14 Echo/04 Global Controls
  • Dry/Wet — set to 100% on a return track. Its context menu has Equal-Loudness, which makes a 50/50 mix as loud as either end.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/18 External Audio Effect
  • External Audio Effect brings a hardware effects unit into a track's device chain.
  • Dry/Wet — set to 100% on a return track
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/19 Filter Delay

Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/20 Gate
