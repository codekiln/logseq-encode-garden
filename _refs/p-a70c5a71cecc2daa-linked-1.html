  • Microfreak/UG/16 Paraphonic Chord Mode/03 Spread Modulation

    • 16.3. Unison Spread as a modulation target

      • Assign modulation to Unison spread in the Matrix by holding an Assign button and pressing the Paraphonic button.
      • To modulate oscillator spread with the Cycling Envelope:
        • Hold Shift and press Paraphonic to enter Unison mode.
        • Select the Cycling Env/Assign 2 Matrix crossing and press the Matrix encoder to set its modulation amount to 90.
        • Set the Cycling Envelope to Env mode, with Rise at 2.5 seconds, Fall at 150 milliseconds, and Hold/Sus at 0 milliseconds.
        • Turn the amount knob to explore the modulation intensity.
      • The Cycling Envelope changes Unison spread when you press a key or when the arpeggiator or sequencer triggers its next step.

      • Note/Info Modulating Unison spread can work especially well with Vocoder presets.

      • Unison is available with every oscillator model.
