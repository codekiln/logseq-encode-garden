Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/07 Tracks
3.7 Tracks

Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/03 Transport and Playback
6.3 Transport and Playback
  • Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Play and Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Stop, or the space bar, start and stop playback. Keys and MIDI can be mapped to the transport.
Ableton/Live/UG/07 Session View/05 Recording Sessions into the Arrangement
7.5 Recording Sessions into the Arrangement
  • With Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Arrangement Record on, Live logs Session playing into the Arrangement: launched clips, changes to their properties, mixer and device automation, and tempo and meter changes from launched scenes. Press it again or stop to finish.
Ableton/Live/UG/19 Recording New Clips/03 Recording/01 Recording Into the Arrangement
19.3.1 Recording Into the Arrangement
  • Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Arrangement Record starts recording. With Start Playback with Record on (Record, Warp & Launch Settings) it starts at once; with it off, recording waits for Play or a clip launch. Shift-click does the opposite of the setting.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/01 Recording Automation in Session View
26.1 Recording Automation in Session View
  • To record into Session clips, turn on Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Automation Arm in the Control Bar and arm the tracks.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/02 Recording Automation in Arrangement View
26.2 Recording Automation in Arrangement View
  • In Arrangement View, automation records on all tracks during Arrangement recording when Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Automation Arm is on. Individual track arming is unnecessary for automation.
26.2 Recording Automation in Arrangement View
  • Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Arrangement Record starts transport and recording; then changes to tempo, mixer and devices are recorded.
26.2 Recording Automation in Arrangement View
  • Press Arrangement Record again to stop recording but keep playing, or Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Stop to stop both.
Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Insert Marker
Insert marker
  • The flashing blue marker on a track where playback starts. Click in a track to move it; double-click the Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Stop or press Home (Win) / Fn+Left (Mac) to return it to the start.
