Does LazyVim support indenting with tabs the way Logseq expects? If so, how do I indent and de-indent with tabs in markdown?AI AnswerIndenting and de-indenting
  • Normal mode: >> indents the line one level, << outdents. Take a count or a motion for more: 3>> for three lines, >ip for the paragraph. See vim/Keyshort/Indent.
