1Password/Account/Service
  • According to 1Password/Dev/Doc/service-accounts/Manage/creating service accounts, only 1Password/Business admins can enable a group to create and manage service accounts. So you can use service accounts if you are a 1Password/Teams admin or if you in a 1Password Business setting in a Group that can create service accounts. 1Password/Family doesn't have any ability to use service accounts.
      • 1Password/Dev/Doc/service-accounts/Manage/creating service accounts
        • By default, only owners and administrators can create and manage service accounts in 1Password/Teams and 1Password/Business.
        • To allow other groups to create service accounts, an owner or administrator can allow certain 1Password/Business/Groups to be able to manage service accounts.
    • to fully embrace Security/Service Accounts with fine-grained permissions, a team would need to create many service users, preferably with short-lived access credentials.

Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/16 Routing
  • Tracks can send to audio and MIDI interfaces, to other programs, or to a group track that acts as a submixer. They can take input from outside Live or from another track or device, with monitoring controls deciding when the input is heard.
Ableton/Live/UG/04 Browser/04 Filters and Tags/01 Filter Groups
  • Click tags in a group to narrow the content pane; Cmd-click (Mac) / Ctrl-click (Win) selects several in one group.
  • Each label keeps its own set of visible groups and selected filters. Hide a group by right-clicking its name; "Reset Filter Groups to Default" in the Filter View menu brings the defaults back.
Ableton/Live/UG/04 Browser/04 Filters and Tags/02 Tags
  • Tags help find, organize and discover content. All factory content from the Core Library and bundled Packs carries descriptive tags; third-party Packs from the Ableton shop are tagged in the Sounds group.
Ableton/Live/UG/04 Browser/04 Filters and Tags/03 Tag Editor
  • Add Tag… adds a custom tag to any group; Add Group… at the bottom creates a new group, which appears in the Filter View once one of its tags is used, for example a group for field recordings.
  • Right-click a tag and choose Add Subtag in… to nest tags under a parent tag. The Creator group doesn't allow subtags.
Ableton/Live/UG/04 Browser/04 Filters and Tags/04 Quick Tags
  • Quick Tags shows the selected item's tags. Add… assigns another, with autocomplete, or creates a new tag after a "Create new tag…" prompt, asking for its parent tag or group.
  • Selecting a tag shows its filter group in the Status Bar. Hide the section with Open Quick Tags in the Filter View menu.
Ableton/Live/UG/04 Browser/06 Library
  • Library labels group content by type, searchable and filterable:
    • All: every item, devices first, then files.
    • Sounds: Instrument Racks and instrument presets, plus anything tagged in the Sounds group.
    • Drums: drum devices, Drum Racks, single hits, loops and one-shots, and anything tagged Drums.
    • Instruments, Audio Effects and MIDI Effects: built-in devices, their presets and your Racks, organized by device.
    • Modulators: modulator devices and anything tagged Modulator.
    • Max for Live: Max for Live devices, presets and Racks built from them.
    • Plug-Ins: VST and Audio Units plug-ins.
    • Clips, Samples, Grooves and Templates: Live Clips, raw samples, grooves and template Sets.
    • Tunings: Core Library tunings and saved .ascl or .scl files.
Ableton/Live/UG/05 Managing Files and Sets/04 Live Sets/03 Merging Sets
  • Unfold a Set in the browser like a folder to drag individual tracks, Group Tracks, its grooves, or just a track's device chain via the Devices icon.
Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/14 Linked-Track Editing/01 Linking and Unlinking Tracks
  • Link selected tracks with Link Tracks in a track header's context menu, or link a Group Track's tracks from the group header. Linked tracks show an indicator button in their headers.
  • A Set can have several linked groups, but a track belongs to only one. Hover the indicator to highlight a group's tracks; click it to select them.
  • To add tracks to a group, select them and Cmd-click (Mac) / Ctrl-click (Win) Link Tracks in a linked track's header. Any mix of linked and unlinked tracks can be linked into a new group.
  • Unlink Track(s) in the header's context menu removes tracks from their group.
Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/14 Linked-Track Editing/02 Editing Linked Tracks
  • These apply to every track in the group at once:
    • Moving and resizing clips; selecting clips and time; the "… Time" commands; splitting and consolidating.
    • Clip fades, for fades that start at the same position.
    • Arming and disarming.
    • Renaming, inserting and deleting take lanes, and Audition Mode, even where take lanes are hidden.
Ableton/Live/UG/07 Session View/01 Session View Clips
  • Group Track slots are shaded, in the color of the leftmost clip, when any track in the group has a clip in that scene. Their launch buttons launch those clips together, empty ones hold stop buttons, and clicking one selects its clips.
Ableton/Live/UG/07 Session View/03 The Track Status Fields
  • A pie chart: a looping Session clip, with the loop length in beats on the right and the number of repetitions on the left. On a Group Track, without numbers, it means a track inside is playing.
Ableton/Live/UG/08 Clip View/01 Clip View Layout/01 Clip Title Bar
  • Assign Track Color to Clips, in a track header's context menu, resets them; on a Group Track, Assign Track Color to Grouped Tracks and Clips colors everything inside. Each affects only the view you run it in, Session or Arrangement.
Ableton/Live/UG/08 Clip View/03 Extended Clip Properties/01 Follow Action and Launch Controls
  • Empty slots split a track's clips into groups; Follow Actions set how clips in a group trigger one another.
Ableton/Live/UG/10 Editing MIDI/05 Editing MIDI Notes/12 Editing Velocities
  • Shift-click to select several markers. Drag a group to the top or bottom to make them equal, then set the value.
Ableton/Live/UG/10 Editing MIDI/05 Editing MIDI Notes/13 Editing Probabilities
  • A group of notes shares one probability: Play All plays them all or none; Play One plays one of them at random.
  • Select notes and press Play All or Play One in the Clip Content Toolbar, or use the Group Notes commands in the context or Edit menu. Cmd+G (Mac) / Ctrl+G (Win) makes a group of the last type used.
  • A group has one marker: a diamond for Play All, a triangle for Play One. Right-click it, or use the buttons, to change the type.
  • Hovering a grouped note highlights the group, and the Status Bar shows the type. Grouped notes always show the triangle, even at 100%.
  • Ungroup, Cmd+Shift+G (Mac) / Ctrl+Shift+G (Win), or Ungroup Notes removes notes from a group; adding a note to another group moves it.
Ableton/Live/UG/11 MIDI Tools/01 Using MIDI Tools
  • Native tools are built in and fixed; Max for Live tools can be edited, built, or installed from other makers, and show under the browser's MIDI Tools filter group or the MIDI Tool tag.
Ableton/Live/UG/13 Audio to MIDI/01 Slice to New MIDI Track/02 Using Effects on Slices
  • Each slice has its own Drum Rack chain, so it can take its own audio effects. To share effects across several slices, select their chains and press Cmd+G (Mac) / Ctrl+G (Windows) to group them into a nested Rack, then add effects after it.
Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions
  • A Follow Action decides what happens next in a clip's group after it plays. A group is the run of clips in adjacent slots of one track; empty slots separate groups. Scenes have Follow Actions too, in the Scene View.
  • First and Last — play the top or bottom clip of the group.
  • Any — plays any clip in the group.
Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions/01 Looping Parts of a Clip
  • Drag both pieces into adjacent slots of one Session track, forming a group.
Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions/02 Creating Cycles
  • Give every clip or scene in a group the Next action and they play in a cycle until stopped. Adding Any at a low Chance occasionally reshuffles it.
Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/05 Internal Routings/02 Making Use of Internal Routing
  • A Group Track sets up the same routing automatically and can fold its tracks away.
Ableton/Live/UG/18 Mixing
  • The mixer: audio, MIDI, group and return tracks, the Main track, the crossfader, solo and cue, and track delays.
  • Ableton/Live/UG/18 Mixing/03 Group Tracks

    • 18.3 Group Tracks

      • Edit → Group Tracks puts the selected tracks, or Group Tracks, into a Group Track. Group Tracks can't hold clips but have mixer controls and audio effects.
      • Grouped tracks are routed to the group automatically unless they already went somewhere other than Main, which makes groups quick submixes. Rerouting their outputs makes the group a plain folder.
      • The Unfold button in the title bar shows or hides the grouped tracks.
      • Drag tracks in or out. Deleting a group deletes its tracks; Edit → Ungroup Tracks dissolves it instead.
      • In Arrangement View a folded group shows an overview of its tracks' clips. In Session View a group slot launches, stops or selects all the clips in that scene.
      • Assign Track Color to Grouped Tracks and Clips in the group's context menu recolours its contents, for the clips of the current view only.
      • A group's Solo button shows half coloured when something inside it is soloed.

Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio
  • Bouncing tracks, group tracks and time selections to new audio tracks.
  • Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio/02 Bouncing Group Tracks

    • 20.2 Bouncing Group Tracks

      • Bounce Group in Place replaces a whole Group Track with one audio track. Find it in the group's title bar, a group slot, or the group's main lane context menu.
      • Bounce Group to New Track renders selected clips or a time selection in the group to a new track and mutes the originals; also Cmd+B / Ctrl+B.
      • Groups bounce after the mixer, from what reaches the Main output — including effects and sends inside the group, excluding the Main track's own effects.
      • Tracks routed outside the group are left out and may leave silence; return tracks count only if their output reaches Main.

Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio/03 Pasting Bounced Audio

Page reference

  • Copy clips in one Session track, or clips or a time selection in the Arrangement (a group's main lane works too). Then paste into an audio track, an empty MIDI track or a take lane from the main lane's context menu, or with Cmd+Option+V (Mac) / Ctrl+Alt+V (Windows). An empty MIDI track becomes an audio track.
Ableton/Live/UG/22 Stem Separation/02 Separating Audio Files and Clips
  • Each stem gets its own track inside a new Group Track, coloured to match the dialog's toggles; the group takes the source track's colour. A single stem, or a merged one, goes on one track instead.
  • Source track effects move to the Group Track; track automation is copied to the group, and clip envelopes and modulation to each stem clip.
Ableton/Live/UG/25 Racks/02 Creating Racks
  • To wrap existing devices, select their title bars, right-click and choose Group (Cmd+G / Ctrl+G) or Group to Drum Rack. Grouping again, or grouping chains, nests a Rack inside a Rack; the Device View shows each level as a pair of brackets.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/12 Drum Buss
  • Drum Buss is an analog-style processor for adding body and character to a group of drums and holding them together in a mix.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/21 Glue Compressor
  • Glue Compressor is an analog-modeled bus compressor made with Cytomic, based on the bus compressor of a famous 1980s mixing console. It works on single tracks but is aimed at the Main track or Group Tracks, to "glue" several sources into one mix.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/28 Pedal/01 Pedal Tips

  • 29.28.1.3 Drum Group Fizzle

    • Fuzz, Gain 50%, Sub off; Bass and Mid at −100%, Mid Frequency to taste, Treble 100%; Output −20 dB. Then raise Dry/Wet from 0% until the drums fizz enough.

Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/05 Playing Melodies and Harmonies

Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/10 Mixing with Push 1
  • Track Mode puts one track's volume, pan and first six sends on the encoders; Selection Control buttons pick the track, and Master picks the Main track. Hold a Group Track's Selection Control button to fold or unfold it.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/15 Push 1 Control Reference

Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/04 Loading Individual Drums

Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/05 Playing Melodies and Harmonies
