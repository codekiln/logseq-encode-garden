Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/21 Saving and Exporting
3.21 Saving and Exporting
  • Dragging a Session clip to the User Library saves it as a Live Clip (Ableton/File/.alc), which keeps the clip settings and the track's instruments and effects. Useful for drum patterns with their kit, loop variations, and ideas set aside for later. See Ableton/Clip/How To/Save to Library.
