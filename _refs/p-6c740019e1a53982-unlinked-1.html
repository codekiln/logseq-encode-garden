Ableton/Live/UG/09 Audio Clips Tempo and Warping/01 Tempo/02 Tapping the Tempo
  • Click Tap Tempo on every beat to set the tempo; the more taps, the more accurate.
  • To tap with a key: turn on the Key switch, select Tap Tempo, press the key, and turn the Key switch off. MIDI Map Mode assigns a note or controller, such as a footswitch.
  • With Start Playback with Tap Tempo on in Record, Warp & Launch Settings, tapping starts playback: four taps in 4/4. Link-connected apps start in phase.
Ableton/Live/UG/09 Audio Clips Tempo and Warping/02 Warping/05 Auto-Warping Long Samples
  • Warp From Here (Start At …): uses the Set's tempo as the starting guess. Unwarp the clip, tap the tempo to match it, rewarp, then run this.
Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/05 Internal Routings
  • Track B takes from A through B's Input Type. Suits one-to-many setups such as layering, where several tracks tap one source.
Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/05 Internal Routings/01 Internal Routing Points
  • Every chain of an Instrument or Effect Rack, and every return chain of a Drum Rack, offers the same three tap points, listed as Rack | Chain | Pre FX, Post FX or Post Mixer. Post Mixer is the chain's output just before the chains are summed.
Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/05 Internal Routings/02 Making Use of Internal Routing
  • A multi-timbral instrument plays a different part on each MIDI channel. Extra MIDI tracks feed its parts by pointing Output Type at the instrument's track and Output Channel at each part's channel; audio tracks tap its individual outputs.

Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/04 Loading Individual Drums
  • Browse can load or replace a single pad. Press Device to show the track's devices, tap the pad, then press the selection button under its name. Browse now targets that pad, and tapping other pads while browsing retargets.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/03 Playing and Programming Beats/05 Step Sequencing Beats
  • Tapping a pad also picks it for step sequencing; hold Select while tapping to pick it silently. Tap step pads to place notes; the clip starts as soon as you tap a step. Each step is a 16th note by default, changed with the Scene/Grid buttons.
  • A moving green pad marks the playing step, red while recording. Tapping a filled step deletes the note; hold Mute to deactivate a step instead, or Solo to solo a sound. Hold Delete and tap a pad to clear its notes within the loop.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/05 Playing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Playing in Other Keys
  • Drum recording options (fixed length, Repeat, quantize) work here too. Hold Delete and tap a pad to remove every note of that pitch in the loop.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/06 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Adjusting the Loop Length
  • Hold one pad and tap another to set the loop; double-tap a pad for a one-page loop.
  • Pages follow the play position by default. Single-tap a page to lock the view to it while the loop keeps its length; reselect the loop to follow again. Tapping a page outside the loop sets the loop to it.
  • Shift + Note locks the pads per track. Hold Duplicate and press two page pads to copy a page on top of the destination; hold Delete and tap a page pad to clear it first.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/07 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes/02 Sequencer
  • Tapping a step in the top half adds all selected notes to it. Holding a step shows its notes in blue-green below; tap one to remove it. Holding several steps adds the notes to all of them.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/12 Step Sequencing Automation/01 Note-Specific Parameters
  • For drums, hold Select and press a pad to edit every note that pad plays. Hold Duplicate, tap a step, tap a destination, and release to copy a step with its settings.
Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/15 Push 1 Control Reference
  • Tap Tempo — the Set's tempo follows your taps. With "Start Playback with Tap Tempo" on in the Record, Warp & Launch Settings, four taps in 4/4 also start playback.
  • On instrument tracks it is pitch bend (default) or mod wheel while playing live, and the note range while step sequencing; Select + tap the strip toggles bend and mod. On Drum Rack tracks it picks the pad bank.
  • Two jacks take momentary footswitches. Footswitch 1 is sustain. Footswitch 2 toggles Record on a single tap and acts as New on a double tap.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/02 16 Velocities Mode
  • The bottom-right 16 pads play the selected drum at 16 velocities; tap one to enter steps at that velocity.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/04 Loading Individual Drums
  • To load or replace a single pad, press Device, tap the pad, then press the second upper display button (the square icon marks a pad). Browse now loads into that pad, which flashes; tap other pads to browse for them in turn.
  • Shift+tap a pad, then tap a pad in the outer ring, to colour it. Pad colours are saved with the Set but appear only on Push 2.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/05 Step Sequencing Beats
  • Tapping a drum pad selects it for step sequencing. Tap steps to place notes; the clip starts playing at the first tap. Steps are 16th notes by default; the Scene/Grid buttons change the step size.
  • A moving green step shows playback (red while recording). Tap a filled step to delete it; hold Mute and tap to deactivate it; hold Solo and tap a pad to solo that sound.
  • Delete clears the pattern; hold Delete and tap a pad to clear its notes, or its devices if it has no notes in the pattern.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/06 Real-time Recording
  • Accent makes every played or step-sequenced note full velocity (127), overriding velocity sensitivity and the 16 Velocities pads. Tap to latch it; hold for momentary use.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/04 Additional Recording Options/01 Recording with Repeat
  • Push 2 remembers Repeat per track. Tap to latch; hold for momentary use.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/04 Additional Recording Options/02 Quantizing
  • Hold Quantize and tap a drum pad to quantize only that drum's notes.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/05 Playing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Playing in Other Keys
  • Accent, Fixed Length, Repeat and Quantize all work for melodies too. Hold Delete and tap a pad to remove every note of that pitch in the loop.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/06 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Adjusting the Loop Length
  • Hold one pad and tap another to set the loop; double-tap a pad for a one-page loop.
  • Pages follow the playhead by default. Single-tap a page to lock the view there without changing the loop; tapping a page outside the loop sets the loop to it. Page Left/Right step through pages; reselect the loop or hold Page Left/Right to follow again.

Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/07 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes/02 Sequencer
  • Tap a step in the top half to add all selected notes there. Hold a step to see its notes highlighted below; tap one to remove it. Holding several steps adds notes to all of them; hold Duplicate to copy one step to another.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/08 Working with Samples/03 Slicing Mode
  • Manual — none automatically. Turn on Pad Slicing, play from a slice, tap an empty pad where a new slice should start (later pads shift up), and turn Pad Slicing off when done.
  • Nudge moves the selected slice marker (Shift for tiny steps; Zoom to look closely); Split Slice halves the selected slice. Hold Delete and tap a pad to remove its slice.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/11 Track Control And Mixing/01 Rack and Group Track Mixing
  • In an unfolded Drum Rack, hold Select and tap a pad to jump to its chain.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/15 Controlling Live's Session View
  • Hold Duplicate, press a clip, then tap another slot to copy it there.
  • With Clip mode in focus the display folds to show every note of the clip, handy for recognising it or watching incoming MIDI. Hold Select and tap a pad to select a clip without launching it.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/15 Controlling Live's Session View/01 Session Overview
  • Session Overview zooms the grid out so each pad is a block of eight scenes by eight tracks, covering 64 by 64; each scene button stands for 64 scenes. Tap a pad to jump there — row three, column one is scenes 17–24, tracks 1–8.
Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/17 Push 2 Control Reference
  • Tap Tempo — tap on each beat to set the tempo; with Start Playback with Tap Tempo on, four taps in 4/4 also start playback. The encoder above changes tempo by 1 BPM, or 0.1 with Shift.

Claude/Code/Q/Is there a way to rewind to a previous point in a session and fork the discussion?
  • Short answer: Yes. /rewind (or double-tap Esc when the prompt is empty) rolls back to a prior checkpoint; it mutates the session in place. To preserve the original and branch, follow with /branch. There is no single command that rewinds and forks atomically as of 2026-06.
  • Type /rewind in the prompt (or double-tap Esc with an empty input) to open a list of every prior user turn as a checkpoint.
