    • For a long time, I've wished that I could offer a form of SSO to my friends and family, so that different people could transparently be granted authorization to view different sides of my family and me, all without needing to tell them in advance about it. So, for instance, maybe I would have some kind of sign-in/sign-up form, and the sign-up would ask questions one after another, in a way that triangulated who they were and how we knew each other without giving things away, until they established their proximity to me some how. Basically, I'm thinking that I could grant people an SSO account ahead of when they created it, and they could choose to take it or not. It would be like telling the concierge of one's building, "if you ever see this person, let them in ..."
