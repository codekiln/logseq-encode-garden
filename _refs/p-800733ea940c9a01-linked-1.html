35. Using Push 1
  • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/04 Additional Recording Options

    • 35.4 Additional Recording Options

      • Repeat and quantize options for recording on Push 1.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/04 Additional Recording Options/01 Recording with Repeat

        • 35.4.1 Recording with Repeat

          • With Repeat on, holding a pad plays an even stream of notes, handy for steady hi-hats; finger pressure sets their volume. The Scene/Grid buttons set the rate, and Push remembers Repeat per track.
          • A quick press latches Repeat on; holding it makes it momentary. The Swing knob adds swing, shown on the display while touched.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/35 Push 1/04 Additional Recording Options/02 Quantizing

        • 35.4.2 Quantizing

          • Quantize snaps notes in the selected clip to the grid. Hold it for options:
            • Swing Amount — also on Encoder 1 or the Swing knob.
            • Quantize To — the nearest note value to snap to.
            • Quantize Amount — how far notes may move.
            • Record Quantize — quantize while recording, value on Encoder 8; mirrors the Record Quantization chooser in Live's Edit menu.
          • Hold Quantize and press a drum pad to quantize only that drum's notes.
