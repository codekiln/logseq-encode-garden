Unlinked references: Graphviz

diagrams
  • Renders via Graphviz (Graphviz/dot under the hood), so output is compiled from code rather than hand-drawn; supports custom icons when built-ins miss a vendor (e.g. AI or niche APIs).
  • Tradeoffs: layout and visual language stay Graphviz-like (“competent but stiff” without spacing, labels, clustering, and icon choices); elaborate diagrams can hit cluster/edge limitations (GitHub issue #17).
