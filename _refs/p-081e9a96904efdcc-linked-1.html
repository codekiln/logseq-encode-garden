11.3 Generative Tools
  • Ableton/Live/UG/11 MIDI Tools/03 Generative Tools/02 Seed

    • 11.3.2 Seed

      • Generates random notes within ranges. Set the pitch range with its sliders or handles, or Option-drag (Mac) / Alt-drag (Win) on the piano ruler; overlapping the handles gives a single pitch. The slider is purple when a scale is active.
      • Duration (1/128 to a whole note) and Velocity (1 to 127) ranges work the same way.
      • Voices caps simultaneous notes; Density sets how many notes, as a share of the pitch range.
