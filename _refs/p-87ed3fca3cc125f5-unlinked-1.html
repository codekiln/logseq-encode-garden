iPhone Mirroring/Q/How can I change apps while using iPhone Mirroring on Mac?
  • Answer/Official from Use iPhone Mirroring on your Mac – Apple Support
  • Short answer: switch apps the same way you would on the Mac itself — the iPhone Mirroring window behaves like a regular app window, not a fixed single-app view.
  • Cmd+Tab on the Mac switches between Mac apps as usual, including the iPhone Mirroring window as one entry; it does not switch between iPhone apps inside the mirrored session.
  • Note: iPhone Mirroring requires the iPhone to stay locked with a passcode/Face ID/Touch ID and on the same Wi-Fi network (or paired via Bluetooth) as the Mac; it is unavailable while the iPhone is actively being used or unlocked nearby.
