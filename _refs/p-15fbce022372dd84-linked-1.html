  • Ableton/Live/UG/33 Max for Live Devices/03 Max for Live MIDI Effects/03 MIDI Monitor

    • 33.3.3 MIDI Monitor

      • A utility that shows incoming MIDI and MPE data.
      • Note display — incoming notes on a keyboard, with root, chord name and velocity
      • Flow diagram — a running list of notes and other messages such as pitch bend and aftertouch; Freeze pauses it and Clear empties it
      • MPE display — a running view of note, velocity, slide, pressure and per-note pitch
