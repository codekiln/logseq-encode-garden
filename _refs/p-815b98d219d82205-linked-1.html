31.8 Meld
  • Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/08 Meld/05 LFOs Tab

    • 31.8.5 LFOs Tab

      • Two LFOs per engine, each in Hz or synced, with Phase Offset and Retrigger.
      • LFO 1 types: Basic Shapes, Ramp, Wander, Alternate, Euclid, Pulsate, each with Rate and two macros. The LFO 1 FX panel chains two of 18 effects (FX1, FX2) onto it, each with a macro. LFO 1 and LFO 1 FX are separate sources in the matrix.
      • LFO 2 — Sine, Tri, Saw Up, Saw Down, Rectangle, Random S&H; a third matrix source.
