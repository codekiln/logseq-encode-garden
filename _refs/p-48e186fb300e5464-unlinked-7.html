Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/05 Viewing Automation
  • An automation envelope is a line of breakpoints drawn over the clip or track: value on the vertical axis, time on the horizontal. Drawing it over the content helps line breakpoints up with audio or notes.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/06 Creating Automation Envelopes/03 Inserting Automation Shapes
  • Shapes from the Envelope Editor's or lane's context menu fill the time selection, or one grid step at the insert marker, scaled to the parameter's range. Useful for rhythmic patterns, swells and builds.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/07 Editing Automation/01 Editing Breakpoints and Segments
  • Drag near a segment to move it, or Shift-click it, then release Shift and drag. Moving a segment in or out of a time selection adds breakpoints at the selection's edges.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/07 Editing Automation/02 Stretching and Skewing Envelopes
  • Hovering over a time selection shows handles around it.
  • Top and bottom centre handles stretch vertically (Shift for fine control); left and right centre handles stretch in time; corner handles skew. An outline previews the result and snaps to the grid unless you hold Cmd/Alt.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/07 Editing Automation/03 Simplifying Envelopes
  • Simplify Envelope in the context menu, on a time selection, replaces dense breakpoints with the fewest straight or curved segments that keep the shape.
Ableton/Live/UG/26 Automation/09 Additional Tips for Working with Automation/03 Using Edit Menu Commands
  • With a time selection in an automation lane, Cut, Copy, Duplicate and Delete act only on the envelope inside it, leaving clips alone. A selection across several lanes affects all of them.
Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/02 Audio Clip Envelopes
  • With audio effects, clip envelopes make Live a sound design tool: many variations of one clip, from small corrections to unrecognisable new sounds, all in real time.
Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/02 Audio Clip Envelopes/04 Scrambling Beats
  • A descending staircase repeats the first step; a smooth downward ramp slows time, with a slurred sound when it is slightly off 45° (try a 1/32 Granulation Resolution).
Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/05 Unlinking Clip Envelopes From Clips/01 Programming a Fade-Out for a Live Set
  • Drag up on the envelope's time ruler to zoom out fully.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference
  • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/37 Spectral Time

    • 29.37 Spectral Time

      • Spectral Time combines spectral freezing and spectral delay. Filters, delays and frequency shifting resynthesize the input: sustain a sound forever, add timed fades, smear frequencies over time, or add metallic echoes.
      • Two sections, Freezer and Delay, used alone or in series (freezer into delay), switched with Freezer On and Delay On.
      • The spectrogram shows dry (yellow) and wet (blue) signals and can be hidden.
      • Zero Dry Signal Latency in the context menu passes the dry signal with no delay instead of aligning it with the effect — useful when playing an instrument through it live.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/37 Spectral Time/01 Freezer Section

        • 29.37.1 Freezer Section

          • The Freeze button must be on in both modes.
          • Manual — click Freeze to freeze; Fade In and Fade Out in ms
          • Retrigger — freezes automatically:
            • Onsets — at each detected transient; Sensitivity 0–100%
            • Sync — at regular intervals set by Interval, in ms or beat values (Freezer Time Unit)
            • Fade shape for both:
              • Crossfade — new freeze fades in as the old one (or dry signal) fades out; X-Fade sets the length as a percentage of the interval
              • Envelope — each freeze fades in and out by Fade In and Fade Out; up to eight freezes can overlap
      • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/37 Spectral Time/02 Delay Section

        • 29.37.2 Delay Section

          • Makes delayed copies of the spectrum; fed by the Freezer when it is on.
          • Time — delay time; Mode sets the unit: Time (ms), Notes (beat divisions), 16th, 16th Triplet, 16th Dotted
          • Feedback — more repeats
          • Shift — each repeat moves up or down in frequency by this amount
          • Stereo — width of Tilt and Spray
          • Dry/Wet — affects the Delay section only
          • Tilt — delays high frequencies more (positive) or low frequencies more (negative)
          • Spray — scatters delay times across frequencies at random
          • Mask — limits Tilt and Spray to highs (positive) or lows (negative)
      • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/37 Spectral Time/03 Resolution Section

        • 29.37.3 Resolution Section

          • Resolution, at the top right of the spectrogram, trades accuracy for latency. Lower it while recording to reduce latency.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/37 Spectral Time/04 Global Controls

        • 29.37.4 Global Controls

          • Input Send — input gain
          • Frz > Dly / Dly > Frz — order of the two sections
          • Dry/Wet — overall mix; 100% on a return track
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/02 Auto Filter/03 LFO Controls
  • LFO Phase Offset moves both LFOs' start point; it only works in synced or sixteenth time modes.
  • Rate — in Hz, seconds (shown as Time), synced divisions or sixteenths, set by Mode
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/03 Auto Pan-Tremolo
  • Time modes — Rate (Hz), Time (100 ms to 200 s, for long cycles), Synced, Dotted, Triplet, 16th
  • Phase Offset, in the beat-based time modes, moves each LFO's start against the beat grid. With Random, Wander or S&H it widens the stereo spread instead.
  • the rate control, labeled Freq, Time or Rate depending on time mode. Amount and rate are shared by both modes.
  • Attack Time — how long after a transient the modulation reaches full depth, so attacks stay centered or keep their punch
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/04 Auto Shift
  • Auto Shift tracks and corrects pitch in real time, with vibrato, pitch shifting and formant shifting. Built mainly for vocals, it works on any monophonic audio. It corrects to a scale or to MIDI notes from another track, and has its own LFO.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/04 Auto Shift/01 Input Section
  • Mono — one note at a time; Glide sets how long overlapping notes take to slide
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/04 Auto Shift/02 Quantizer Tab
  • Smooth and Smoothing Time (0–200 ms) — smooth transitions between notes and keep natural vibrato. Together with Correction Strength they range from subtle to hard, stepped correction.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/04 Auto Shift/03 MIDI Tab
  • Attack Time (0–1000 ms) and Release Time (0–5 s) shape each note's envelope.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/04 Auto Shift/06 Vibrato Section
  • Vibrato Fade In — fade-in time in ms
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/05 Beat Repeat
  • Variation — randomizes the grid size around Grid. Its mode sets when: Trigger (each time repeats start), 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 (at regular intervals) or Auto (after every repeat, the most varied).
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/08 Chorus-Ensemble
  • Delay Time — Auto scales the delay with the modulation amount; a fixed value keeps it steady, avoiding phasing and pitch wobble on bass or guitar
  • Amount — depth of delay-time modulation
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/09 Compressor
  • Activity — input level in light gray, with GR or Output showing reduction in orange or output in dark gray over time
  • Attack — time to reach full compression once over the threshold; Release — time to recover after the signal drops. Auto Release sets release from the material.
    • 10–50 ms of attack lets the start of each hit through and keeps it lively; very short attack can sound lifeless or buzzy.
    • Short release can cause pumping, sometimes used on drums on purpose.
    • The manual suggests practicing with a drum loop, adjusting Attack, Release, Threshold and Gain.
  • Lookahead — 0, 1 or 10 ms of delay so compression can start on time
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/10 Corpus/01 Resonator Parameters
  • Decay — internal damping and so decay time
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/11 Delay
  • Delay Time Mode — Sync (beat divisions) or Time (1 ms to 5 s)
    • In Sync, numbered buttons set the delay in sixteenths; 4 is one beat. Offset sliders move each delay slightly early (negative) or late (positive), for swing.
  • Stereo Link — one delay time for both channels; Offset stays separate
  • An LFO can modulate delay time and filter frequency, from chorus to chopped glitches. The LFO Controls toggle expands the full LFO section; its LED flashes at the LFO rate.
    • Delay — LFO amount on delay time; Filter — on filter frequency
    • Rate mode — Rate, Time, Synced, Triplet, Dotted, Sixteenth
    • Wave — Sine, Triangle, Ramp Up, Ramp Down, Square, S&H, Wander; Morph reshapes it
  • Smoothing, for when the delay time changes during playback:
    • Repitch (default) — pitch bends like an old tape delay
    • Fade — crossfades old and new times; gradual changes can sound granular
    • Jump — switches at once, possibly clicking
    • LFO modulation sounds different in each mode.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/11 Delay/01 Context Menu Options for Delay
  • Hi-Quality — better interpolation in Repitch and Fade, and tape-style smoothing of time changes in Repitch
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/11 Delay/02 Delay Tips
  • Stereo Link on, Sync time 6, Feedback 50%
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/14 Echo
  • Stereo Link — changes to time, Sync and Sync Mode apply to both sides
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/14 Echo/01 Echo Tab
  • The Echo Tunnel draws each repeat as a ring moving toward the center; spacing shows time between repeats, and white dots mark a fixed 1/8 grid. Drag in it to change delay times.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/14 Echo/02 Modulation Tab
  • An LFO modulates filter frequency and delay time, and can be blended with an envelope follower.
  • Mod Delay — modulation of delay time; Modulation x4 multiplies it by four, giving deep flanging at short times
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/14 Echo/03 Character Tab
  • Wobble — irregular delay-time modulation like a tape delay; Amount and Morph between wobble types
  • Repitch — pitch bends when delay time changes; off, the change crossfades
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/19 Filter Delay
  • Sync on — delay in sixteenths from numbered buttons (4 is one beat); the Delay Time percentage nudges it for swing. Sync off — delay in ms, dragged or typed.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/20 Gate
  • Lookahead — 0, 1 or 10 ms of delay so the gate can open on time
  • Attack — time to open; very short can click, long softens the attack
  • Hold — time the gate stays open after the signal falls below the threshold; then Release sets how long it takes to close
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/22 Grain Delay
  • Any parameter can be put on the X-Y controller: pick the X parameter from the row below it and the Y parameter from the row at its left. Delay time can go on X and dry/wet on Y.
  • Sync on — delay in sixteenths from numbered buttons (4 is one beat); the Delay Time percentage nudges it for swing. Sync off — delay in ms.
  • Spray — random delay-time changes; low values smear and add noise, high values break the source into rhythmic chaos
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/23 Hybrid Reverb/02 Input Section
  • Predelay — delay before the first early reflection. It affects how big a room sounds; natural values are about 1–25 ms. Predelay Sync chooses time or beat-synced values.
  • Feedback — predelay output fed back into its input; time and synced modes each keep their own setting
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/23 Hybrid Reverb/04 Algorithmic Reverb Engine
  • Decay — time for the tail to fall by 60 dB
  • Bass X — crossover for the low end of the tail; Bass Mult scales its decay time
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/25 Looper/01 Feedback Routing
  • Each pass goes through the other track's effects and back into Looper, getting more processed every time.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/26 Multiband Dynamics/02 Interface and Controls
  • The column to the right of the display also shows the values. Buttons T, B and A switch it between Time (attack and release), Below (threshold and ratio) and Above (threshold and ratio).
  • Above thresholds — Attack is the time to full effect once the signal rises past the threshold; Release the time to recover after it falls back
  • Below thresholds — Attack is the time to full effect once the signal drops below; Release the time to recover after it rises again
  • Time — scales all attack and release times together
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/29 Phaser-Flanger
  • Flanger — a modulated, fed-back delay gives a moving comb filter. Time sets the delay; in the display, moving right shortens the delay and left lengthens it.
  • Doubler — modulated delays imitate stacked takes. Time sets the delay; modulation is bipolar, so right lengthens and left shortens.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/32 Reverb/05 Global Settings
  • Decay — time for the tail to fall by 60 dB
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/33 Roar/04 Feedback Section
  • Feedback Mode — Time, Synced, Triplet and Dotted use the feedback as a delay; Note makes it ring at a chosen pitch.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/35 Shifter
  • Shifter shifts pitch or frequency in real time, or ring-modulates the input.

Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/38 Spectrum

Page reference

  • Spectrum analyzes frequencies in real time and draws level (dB, vertical) against frequency or pitch (horizontal). Peaks stay on the graph until the song restarts. It measures only and never changes the sound.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/39 Tuner/01 View Switches
  • Two buttons at the lower left switch between Classic View (like an analog tuner) and Histogram View (pitch over time). Green means in tune, red out of tune.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/39 Tuner/03 Histogram View
  • Pitch is drawn over time. Note names run down the right side; gray bars mark the exact center of each note, with sharp notes above and flat notes below.
Ableton/Live/UG/30 MIDI Effect Reference/01 Arpeggiator
  • The Velocity toggle shows the velocity controls. Decay is the time to reach the Target velocity; a long Decay with Target 0 fades the pattern out. With Retrigger on, the velocity ramp restarts with the pattern.
