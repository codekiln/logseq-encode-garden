  • Snapshot testing for command-line tools. A test is a transcript file — a $ command and the expected output beneath it — that trycmd runs and compares; TRYCMD=overwrite rewrites the expected output in place. Built on the snapbox toolkit, in the spirit of trybuild and cram.
