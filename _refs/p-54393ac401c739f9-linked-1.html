Layered Interpretation in Rabbinic JudaismOverview
  • Judaism/Rabbinic is organized around a layered tradition in which later texts interpret earlier texts, preserve the arguments about those interpretations, and then become objects of interpretation themselves. The stack runs from the Judaism/Torah through the Judaism/Mishnah, the Judaism/Gemara and the Judaism/Talmud to medieval commentary and commentary on commentary, with no terminal layer.
Layered Interpretation in Rabbinic JudaismThe stack

  • Judaism/Torah

    • The five books of Moses command without specifying. "Remember the Sabbath day and keep it holy" says nothing about what counts as work, when the day begins, or what may be carried. Interpretation is unavoidable from the first layer.
Layered Interpretation in Rabbinic JudaismWritten Torah and Oral Torah
  • Rabbinic Judaism understands revelation as both the Judaism/Torah/Written and the Judaism/Torah/Oral, the interpretive tradition needed to understand and practice it. Interpretation is built into the transmission of revelation, so the chain of interpretation carries religious significance, and disagreement within it counts as part of the tradition rather than as a failure to recover it.
Judaism/Mishnah
Mishnah
  • The early systematic written formulation of the Judaism/Torah/Oral, compiled around 200 CE under Person/Judah ha-Nasi.
Judaism/Rabbinic
Rabbinic Judaism
  • The mainstream form of Judaism since late antiquity, built on the teaching of the rabbis and on the Judaism/Talmud as the authoritative interpretation of the Judaism/Torah.
Rabbinic Judaism
  • It understands revelation as both the Judaism/Torah/Written and the Judaism/Torah/Oral, and it develops as a layered tradition in which each generation's interpretation becomes the next generation's text. Judaism/Concept/Layered Interpretation explains that structure.
Judaism/Torah/Oral
Oral Torah
  • The interpretive tradition that Judaism/Rabbinic holds was revealed alongside the Judaism/Torah/Written and is needed to understand and practice it.
Judaism/Torah/Written
Written Torah
  • The written Pentateuch, the Judaism/Torah in its narrow sense, as Judaism/Rabbinic distinguishes it from the Judaism/Torah/Oral that is held to have been revealed with it.
