Zoom/Q/In the iPad Zoom app, how do I show or hide my self-image?
  • Short answer: During a meeting, tap the screen to bring up the meeting controls, tap More (the ••• icon), then tap Hide Self View. To bring it back, tap the screen again and choose Show Self View.
