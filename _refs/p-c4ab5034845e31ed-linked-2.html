Person/codekiln/GitHub/logseq-gardener/Analysis/Fable/26/09/12/0933 ET Expand embeds before evaluating queries in the publisher

Page reference

Expand embeds before evaluating queries in the publisherWhat the encode garden uses
  • codekiln asked on the pull request how the Frontmatter page is assembled from subpages. The body of Logseq/Frontmatter is a list of {{embed [[Logseq/Frontmatter/tags]]}} lines and their siblings, so a publisher that copies the file's text renders a page of macro syntax, and a publisher that expands embeds renders the conventions. That page is the first fixture for the embed expander.
