2.1.3. Firmware Revision
  • MCC displays available firmware updates when the computer has internet access. See Arturia/MCC/UG/06 Updates/01 Device Firmware Upgrades.
Arturia/MCC/UG/06 Updates
6. Updates
  • Arturia/MCC/UG/06 Updates/01 Device Firmware Upgrades

    • 6.1. Device Firmware Upgrades

      • With internet access, MCC can notify you of a firmware update for the connected device. Use Install and follow its prompts to update.

      • Note/Warning A notice repeated in the center of MCC indicates an important firmware update and keeps appearing until the update is installed.
