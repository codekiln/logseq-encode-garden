TAM

  • The Uber debate, 2014

    • In June 2014 Aswath Damodaran valued Uber at about $6 billion, against a funding round that had just priced it at $17 billion. He took the worldwide taxi and limousine market, about $100 billion, as Uber's TAM and gave Uber a tenth of it (A Disruptive Cab Ride to Riches: The Uber Payoff).
    • Bill Gurley, an Uber board member, answered that the taxi market measured demand under the old product's constraints. A cheaper, faster and more reliable ride expands the market: people take rides they would never have hailed a cab for, and some give up owning a car. He argued the potential market could be 25 times Damodaran's figure (How to Miss By a Mile: An Alternative Look at Uber's Potential Market Size).
    • The exchange is the standard example of a product changing its own market. Whether a TAM is a fact about the world or a claim about what the product will do to the world depends on how new the product is.
