Explore vs ExploitThrough cybernetics
  • Seen through cybernetics, a learner is a regulator holding a course through an environment it can only partly see. Exploitation is the corrective action along the known heading. Exploration is the sampling that keeps the map of the environment current, so the corrections stay pointed at the real world. Ashby's requisite variety says a regulator must carry at least as many responses as the world has disturbances, and exploration is how it acquires the variety it lacks. Second-order cybernetics adds the observer to the loop: a gardener steering their own learning is regulating the system that does the steering, which is why the balance is never set once and left alone.
