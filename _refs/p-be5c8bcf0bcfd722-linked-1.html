Layered Interpretation in Rabbinic JudaismWritten Torah and Oral Torah
  • Rabbinic Judaism understands revelation as both the Judaism/Torah/Written and the Judaism/Torah/Oral, the interpretive tradition needed to understand and practice it. Judaism/Rabbinic
Rabbinic Judaism
  • It understands revelation as both the Judaism/Torah/Written and the Judaism/Torah/Oral, and it develops as a layered tradition in which each generation's interpretation becomes the next generation's text. Judaism/Concept/Layered Interpretation explains that structure.
Judaism/Torah
Torah
  • The text commands without specifying. "Remember the Sabbath day and keep it holy" leaves open what counts as work, when the day begins and what may be carried, so interpretation starts at the first layer of the tradition. Judaism/Rabbinic distinguishes the Judaism/Torah/Written from the Judaism/Torah/Oral that accompanies it.
Judaism/Torah/Written
Written Torah
  • The written Pentateuch, the Judaism/Torah in its narrow sense, as Judaism/Rabbinic distinguishes it from the Judaism/Torah/Oral that is held to have been revealed with it.
