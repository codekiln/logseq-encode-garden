  • Keyshort nvim nvim/Keyshort nvim/Keyshort/Outline
    • Show the Buffer Outline Card
      • Shortcut: gO
      • Description: Opens a filetype-specific navigable outline of the current buffer. In help and :Man buffers it shows a table of contents; with an active LSP client it shows document symbols. Select an entry and press Enter to jump to it. See LazyVim/Tutorial/Navigate Man Pages by Heading.
