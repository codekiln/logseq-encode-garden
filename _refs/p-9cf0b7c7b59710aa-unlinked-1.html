Person/Sergei Rachmaninoff/Music/Work/Morceaux de fantaisie Op. 3/02 Prelude in C-sharp Minor

Page reference

  • A 62-bar prelude in ternary form, also called The Bells of Moscow. Rachmaninoff gave the first performance on 26 September 1892 at the Moscow Electrical Exhibition, and it became his most famous piece.
