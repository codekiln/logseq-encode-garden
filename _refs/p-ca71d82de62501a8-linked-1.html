36.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/01 Loop Selector

    • 36.3.1 Loop Selector

      • Loop Selector splits the grid into three areas for playing, step sequencing and setting clip length at the same time.
      • The 16 drum pads sit in a 4×4 block like Live's Drum Rack. Pad colours:
        • track colour — has a sound;
        • lighter track colour — empty;
        • green — playing;
        • white — selected;
        • dark blue — soloed;
        • grey — muted.
      • The touch strip or Octave Up/Down move through larger kits 16 pads at a time; with Shift, one row at a time.
      • Hold Layout for momentary access to the 16 Velocities layout; Shift+Layout locks it, and Layout again unlocks.
36.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/02 16 Velocities Mode

    • 36.3.2 16 Velocities Mode

      • The bottom-right 16 pads play the selected drum at 16 velocities; tap one to enter steps at that velocity.
      • Hold Layout for the loop length controls; Shift+Layout locks them; Layout unlocks.
36.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/03 64-Pad Mode

    • 36.3.3 64-Pad Mode

      • The whole 8×8 grid plays drums in real time — useful for large kits, such as sliced ones.
      • Switching back doesn't move the 16 step-sequencer pads; use the touch strip or Octave buttons to find the ones you want.
      • Hold Layout for the loop length controls; Shift+Layout locks them; Layout unlocks.
36.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/04 Loading Individual Drums

    • 36.3.4 Loading Individual Drums

      • To load or replace a single pad, press Device, tap the pad, then press the second upper display button (the square icon marks a pad). Browse now loads into that pad, which flashes; tap other pads to browse for them in turn.
      • After loading, Load becomes Load Next, with Load Previous beside it, for auditioning neighbours in context.
      • To select a pad without playing it, hold Select while tapping, or press the Drum Rack track's lower display button to expand its pads onto the lower buttons and move with the arrow keys.

      • 36.3.4.1 Additional Pad Options for Push 2

        • Hold Duplicate, press a pad, then press another to copy it there. This replaces the destination's devices and sound but keeps its recorded notes.
        • With one pad selected, encoder 1 sets its choke group and encoder 2 transposes it.
        • Shift+tap a pad, then tap a pad in the outer ring, to colour it. Pad colours are saved with the Set but appear only on Push 2.
36.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/05 Step Sequencing Beats

    • 36.3.5 Step Sequencing Beats

      • Tapping a drum pad selects it for step sequencing. Tap steps to place notes; the clip starts playing at the first tap. Steps are 16th notes by default; the Scene/Grid buttons change the step size.
      • The Tempo encoder changes tempo by 1 BPM per click, or 0.1 BPM with Shift.
      • A moving green step shows playback (red while recording). Tap a filled step to delete it; hold Mute and tap to deactivate it; hold Solo and tap a pad to solo that sound.
      • Delete clears the pattern; hold Delete and tap a pad to clear its notes, or its devices if it has no notes in the pattern.
      • Step colours:
        • grey — empty;
        • clip colour — note, brighter for higher velocity;
        • lighter clip colour — muted note;
        • unlit — unavailable; with triplet steps only the first six pads of each row are used.
      • Velocity and micro-timing are covered under step sequencing automation; loop length under Adjusting the Loop Length.
36.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/06 Real-time Recording

    • 36.3.6 Real-time Recording

      • Optionally turn on Metronome (Shift + Master encoder sets its volume; lit buttons pulse when on), then press Record.
      • A count-in, if set in Live, shows as a bar flashing across the display.
      • Pads you play are recorded. Record again stops recording but keeps playing; a third press overdubs; further presses toggle between playback and overdub. Progress bars show each playing clip's position.
      • Accent makes every played or step-sequenced note full velocity (127), overriding velocity sensitivity and the 16 Velocities pads. Tap to latch it; hold for momentary use.
      • New stops the selected clip and prepares a new one on the track for practising. By default it also copies clips playing on other tracks to a new scene and keeps them playing; the Setup menu's Workflow setting changes this.
36.3 Playing and Programming Beats
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/03 Playing and Programming Beats/07 Fixed Length Recording

    • 36.3.7 Fixed Length Recording

      • Fixed Length makes new clips a set length; hold it to choose the length. With it off, recording continues until Record, New or Play/Stop.
      • Recording creates an empty clip of that length and starts at its beginning, following global launch quantization. With Phrase Sync, it starts at the matching point in the phrase: with 4 bars starting at bar 7, recording begins at bar 3 of the new clip.
      • Turning Fixed Length on mid-recording stops recording and loops the last bars of the clip.

36.4 Additional Recording Options
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/04 Additional Recording Options/01 Recording with Repeat

    • 36.4.1 Recording with Repeat

      • With Repeat on, holding a pad plays an even stream of notes, such as steady hi-hats; pressure changes their volume. Scene/Grid sets the rate.
      • Push 2 remembers Repeat per track. Tap to latch; hold for momentary use.
      • The Swing knob adds swing to repeated notes and shows the amount when touched.
36.4 Additional Recording Options
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/04 Additional Recording Options/02 Quantizing

    • 36.4.2 Quantizing

      • Quantize snaps the selected clip's notes to the grid; hold it for options:
        • Swing Amount — swing applied while quantizing (also the Swing knob);
        • Quantize To — the note value;
        • Quantize Amount — how far notes move;
        • Record Quantize — quantize while recording, at the value set by encoder 5. Recorded notes don't get swing this way.
      • Hold Quantize and tap a drum pad to quantize only that drum's notes.
36.4 Additional Recording Options
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/04 Additional Recording Options/03 Arrangement Recording

    • 36.4.3 Arrangement Recording

      • With Arrangement View in focus, Record toggles Arrangement recording, capturing everything you do on Push 2.
      • With Session View in focus, Shift+Record starts Arrangement recording; with the Arrangement in focus, Shift+Record toggles Session recording instead.

36.5 Playing Melodies and Harmonies
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/05 Playing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Playing in Other Keys

    • 36.5.1 Playing in Other Keys

      • Scale opens key and scale settings.
      • Upper and lower display buttons pick the key (selected in white); encoders 2–7 pick the scale type.
      • Layout (encoder 1) and Direction (encoder 8) set the grid: 4ths (default) puts each row a fourth above the one below; 3rds a third; Sequent lays notes out in order with no repeats, for the widest range. Horiz rotates the grid 90°.
      • Fixed on keeps note positions when changing key (bottom left stays C, or the nearest scale note); off moves the root to the bottom left.
      • In Key folds the grid to scale notes only; Chromatic shows every note with out-of-key notes unlit.
      • Scale settings save with the Set; put favourites in your Default Set.
      • Accent, Fixed Length, Repeat and Quantize all work for melodies too. Hold Delete and tap a pad to remove every note of that pitch in the loop.

36.6 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/06 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies/01 Adjusting the Loop Length

    • 36.6.1 Adjusting the Loop Length

      • Each loop length pad is a page of steps; page length depends on step size. Drums at 16ths show two pages (two bars); the Melodic Sequencer shows one page of eight steps (two beats).
      • Hold one pad and tap another to set the loop; double-tap a pad for a one-page loop.
      • Pages follow the playhead by default. Single-tap a page to lock the view there without changing the loop; tapping a page outside the loop sets the loop to it. Page Left/Right step through pages; reselect the loop or hold Page Left/Right to follow again.
      • Loop length pad colours: unlit outside the loop; grey in the loop but not shown; white shown but not playing; green playing; red recording.
      • Hold Duplicate, press a page's pad, then a destination's, to copy notes onto it (adding to what's there). Hold Delete and tap a page to clear it first.

36.7 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/07 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes/01 32 Notes

    • 36.7.1 32 Notes

      • The bottom half plays scale notes in real time; pressing a pad plays it and selects it for sequencing. Hold Select to choose without playing.
      • Pad colours: track colour for the root, lighter track colour for selected, green for playing, white for other scale notes.
      • Octave buttons shift the range; Shift+touch strip by octaves, Shift+Octave by one scale step. The Scale menu sets which notes appear.
36.7 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/07 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes/02 Sequencer

    • 36.7.2 Sequencer

      • Tap a step in the top half to add all selected notes there. Hold a step to see its notes highlighted below; tap one to remove it. Holding several steps adds notes to all of them; hold Duplicate to copy one step to another.
      • Step colours: clip colour has notes; green playing; white selected; light grey muted note; grey empty; unlit unavailable with triplet steps.
      • Hold Layout for the loop length pads in the fifth row; Shift+Layout locks them (per track); Page Left/Right move between pages. Duplicate and Delete with a loop length pad copy or clear a page.

36.8 Working with Samples
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/08 Working with Samples/01 Classic Playback Mode

    • 36.8.1 Classic Playback Mode

      • Start and End set the usable region of the sample, for example skipping silence at the start. S Start and S Length are percentages of that region: 50% and 25% play its third quarter. S Loop Length sets how much loops, when Loop is on.
      • The Zoom encoder zooms the waveform around the last position control touched.
      • Press Simpler's upper display button for Edit mode: lower buttons choose more parameter pages, upper buttons toggle settings; press again to leave.
        • Loop On/Off — loop while a pad is held.
        • Warp as… — fit Start to End into a number of bars; ÷2 and ×2 fix a wrong guess.
        • Crop removes audio outside Start and End; Reverse plays the sample backwards. Both work on a copy, leaving the original file alone.
36.8 Working with Samples
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/08 Working with Samples/02 One-Shot Mode

    • 36.8.2 One-Shot Mode

      • Zoom, Start, End, Warp as…, ÷2, ×2, Crop and Reverse work as in Classic mode.
      • Trigger plays the sample to the end whatever the pad length; Fade In and Fade Out shape its volume, with Fade Out starting that long before the region ends. Shift+Play/Stop cuts it off.
      • Gate starts fading out on release, over the Fade Out time.
      • Transpose shifts up to ±48 semitones; the result depends on the Warp Mode. Gain sets Simpler's volume.

      • 36.8.2.1 Legato Playback

        • Classic and One-Shot can repitch a sample without restarting it, like Legato Mode for clips: in Edit mode, open the Global bank (second lower display button), set Glide Mode to Glide, and in Classic set Voices to 1 (One-Shot is always mono).
        • Playing pads legato then transposes without changing playback position. Keep Warp on; Complex Pro usually sounds best.
36.8 Working with Samples
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/08 Working with Samples/03 Slicing Mode

    • 36.8.3 Slicing Mode

      • Zoom, Start, End, Warp as…, ÷2, ×2 and Reverse work as in the other modes.
      • Slice By chooses how slices are made:
        • Transient — at transients; Sensitivity sets how many, up to 64.
        • Beat — at the beat Division chosen.
        • Region — a chosen number of equal slices.
        • Manual — none automatically. Turn on Pad Slicing, play from a slice, tap an empty pad where a new slice should start (later pads shift up), and turn Pad Slicing off when done.
      • Slices fill the left half of the grid from the bottom left in rows of four going up, then the right half the same way.
      • The grid uses the 64-pad drum layout; Layout cycles to Loop Selector and 16 Velocities.
      • Playback: Mono plays one slice at a time; Poly several; Through is mono but plays on through the rest of the region. Trigger/Gate work as in One-Shot, according to the Playback setting.
      • Nudge moves the selected slice marker (Shift for tiny steps; Zoom to look closely); Split Slice halves the selected slice. Hold Delete and tap a pad to remove its slice.

36.10 Working With Instruments and Effects
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/10 Working With Instruments and Effects/01 Adding Deleting and Reordering Devices

    • 36.10.1 Adding, Deleting, and Reordering Devices

      • Add Device opens the browser with every device type the track can take. Loading an instrument this way replaces the current one, as in Browse.
      • New devices go after the selected one; MIDI effects always go before the instrument and audio effects after.
      • Hold Delete and press a device's upper button to delete it. Hold Mute and press it to switch the device off or back on (shown grey). Holding Mute for a moment locks it on until pressed again.
      • Hold an effect's upper button and turn any encoder to move it in the chain.
36.10 Working With Instruments and Effects
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/10 Working With Instruments and Effects/02 Working with Racks

    • 36.10.2 Working with Racks

      • Racks have their own icons on the display.
      • Press a selected Rack's upper button to unfold or fold it; an underline marks what it contains. Drum Racks fold only from within Live.
      • With the Rack selected the encoders control its Macros; select a device inside to control that device instead.
      • Hold the Rack's upper button to show its chains along the bottom, selectable with the lower buttons.

  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/11 Track Control And Mixing/01 Rack and Group Track Mixing

36.14 Clip Mode
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/14 Clip Mode/01 Using MIDI Tracks in Clip Mode

    • 36.14.1 Using MIDI Tracks in Clip Mode

      • Clip mode shows played or sequenced MIDI for review and editing; the display changes with the pad layout.
      • Every layout adds Crop, which deletes material outside the loop.
36.14 Clip Mode
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/14 Clip Mode/02 Real-Time Playing Layouts

    • 36.14.2 Real-Time Playing Layouts

      • In the 64 Notes, 64 Pads and 64 Slices layouts, the display shows incoming notes, folded so every note fits. Session mode shows the same view.
36.14 Clip Mode
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/14 Clip Mode/03 Sequencing Layouts

    • 36.14.3 Sequencing Layouts

      • In the step sequencer layouts, a translucent white box marks the sequenceable area — one page of steps. A box at the far left shows its pitch range.
      • Lines at the far left show where the clip has notes, thicker where there are more, so notes can be found without the computer screen.

      • 36.14.3.1 Melodic Sequencer

        • Eight notes per page. Move the area sideways with the loop length pads or Page Left/Right, and up or down with the Octave buttons or touch strip.

      • 36.14.3.2 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes

        • The display fits every note in the clip and highlights where selected notes sit. Move sideways with Page Left/Right or the loop length pads (fifth row while holding Layout).
        • In the drum and slicing Loop Selector and 16 Velocities layouts, only the selected pad shows; move sideways with the loop length pads or Page Left/Right, and pick a different pad to change rows.
36.14 Clip Mode
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/14 Clip Mode/04 Note-Specific Parameters

    • 36.14.4 Note-Specific Parameters

      • Hold a step in any sequencing layout: the display zooms to its page, highlights its notes, and the encoders set:
        • Nudge — offset from the previous grid line as a percentage; negative is early;
        • Length — coarse, and Fine length;
        • Velocity.
      • Hold several steps to edit them together (the display shows the range); hold an empty step and turn an encoder to create a note with those settings.
      • For drums, hold Select, press a pad, and adjust every note that pad plays.

36.15 Controlling Live’s Session View
  • Ableton/Live/UG/36 Push 2/15 Controlling Live's Session View/01 Session Overview

    • 36.15.1 Session Overview

      • Session Overview zooms the grid out so each pad is a block of eight scenes by eight tracks, covering 64 by 64; each scene button stands for 64 scenes. Tap a pad to jump there — row three, column one is scenes 17–24, tracks 1–8.
      • Hold Layout for momentary access; Shift+Layout locks it; Layout unlocks.
      • Colours: white for the current block (bordered in Live), green where clips are playing, unlit where there are no tracks or scenes.
