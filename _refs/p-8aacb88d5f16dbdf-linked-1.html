    • 8.4 Setting Up a Custom Mode

      • Connect Launchpad Pro and open Novation Components. Assign each grid pad a MIDI note, control change, or Program Change.
      • A pad can toggle, trigger, or act momentarily. Momentary notes turn off on release; triggers send a defined CC value or Program Change. Entire rows or columns can act as horizontal or vertical unipolar or bipolar CC faders.
      • Give each pad its own off color. One shared on color applies across a Custom Mode when pads are pressed or toggled. Combine notes, CCs, Program Changes, and faders on the same grid.
