  • Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/10 Restore Defaults

    • Restoring the default settings

      • A factory reset returns the H5 to its default settings.
      • 1. Press MENU, then use the scroll button to select SYSTEM > FACTORY RESET.
      • 2. At Are you sure?, select YES and press the scroll button.
        • Factory reset screen
        • The default settings are restored and the H5 turns off.

      • Note/Info Input level settings are not reset.
