    • 42.26.1 Using Tab for Navigation

      • With Use Tab to Move Focus on, Tab / Shift+Tab move to the next or previous control, and Option+Tab / Option+Shift+Tab (Mac), Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+Shift+Tab (Win) move along a row in the Session or Arrangement mixer.
      • With it off, Tab switches between Session and Arrangement View.
      • Wrap Tab Navigation — Tab past the last control returns to the first, and Shift+Tab on the first goes to the last.
      • Move Clips with Arrow Keys — on by default; left and right arrows move selected clips or the time selection in the Arrangement. With it off, left and right collapse the time selection to its start or end.
