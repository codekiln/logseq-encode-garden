    • 26.8 Deleting Automation

      • Delete Automation in a parameter's context menu, or Cmd+Delete (Mac) / Ctrl+Backspace (Windows) with it selected, removes all its automation; the LED goes away and the value returns to default.
      • Context menus in the Envelope Editor or lane headers also offer Clear Envelope, Clear All Envelopes of… (one device; lane headers only) and Clear All Envelopes (the whole Set).
      • To remove only part of an envelope, edit its breakpoints.
