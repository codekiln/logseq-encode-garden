    • 31.8.7 MIDI and MPE Tabs

      • MIDI and MPE data become modulation sources:
        • Velocity, Pitch — the note's value, held for its duration.
        • Random — a new value per note.
        • Pitch Bend, Press, Modulation Wheel — standard controller sources.
        • Note Pitch Bend, Slide, Press — MPE sources. Without a controller, clip envelopes can drive them.
