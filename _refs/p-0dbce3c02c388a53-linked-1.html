Music/Scale/Dorian
Dorian ModeHistory
  • Baroque composers often wrote D minor with no key signature, a habit inherited from Dorian notation. Person/Johann Sebastian Bach's Toccata and Fugue in D minor BWV 538 is called "Dorian" for that reason alone: its notation has no flat, and its music is in D minor.

Melodic Minor ScaleHistory
  • Baroque lines follow the harmony more than the direction. Over a dominant chord, Person/Johann Sebastian Bach's descending lines keep the raised sixth and seventh.

Phrygian ModeHistory
  • That cadence outlived the mode. The Baroque "Phrygian half cadence", iv6–V in minor, uses the same half-step bass descent to the dominant. The two-chord Adagio of Person/Johann Sebastian Bach's Brandenburg Concerto No. 3 is one.
