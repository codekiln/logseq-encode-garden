Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition
ComposerBoundary cases
  • An improviser whose music exists only in performance carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance. An improviser who also writes pieces carries Composer as well.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • A composer who also writes music theory adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Theory; one who also leads ensembles from the podium adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting, as Person/John Adams does.

Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting
Conductor
  • In this garden, Conductor pages model a real person who leads an orchestra, choir or other ensemble as its conductor. A Conductor is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance, so a page marked Conductor leaves Performer out.
ConductorBoundary cases
  • A bandleader who plays in the band carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production
Music ProducerBoundary cases
