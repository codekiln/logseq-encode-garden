    • 4.5 Collections

      • Collections are color labels for grouping favorite or frequently used items of any kind, folders included. See Ableton/Collection.
      • Assign one from an item's context menu or with keys 1 to 7; 0 clears colors. Several items can be colored at once, and an item can carry several colors, though the content pane shows at most three squares.
      • Clicking a Collection in the sidebar lists everything with that color.
      • Rename a Collection from its context menu or with Cmd+R (Mac) / Ctrl+R (Win). The Edit button beside the Collections header chooses which labels show; Done exits. A hidden color reappears once it's assigned, but an empty visible color doesn't hide itself.
