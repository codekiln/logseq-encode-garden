    • 31.13.7 MIDI Tab

      • MIDI sources in their own matrix:
        • Velocity and Note — held for the note. Note is centered on C3, so Note at 100% on Filter Frequency makes the cutoff follow the played note.
        • Pitch Bend, Aftertouch, Modulation Wheel — controller sources, also reachable with clip envelopes.
        • Random — a new value per note.
      • Rows are shared with the Matrix tab.
