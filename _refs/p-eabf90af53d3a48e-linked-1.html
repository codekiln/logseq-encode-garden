  • Claude/Code permission prompts: when Claude asks for permission, option 2 is often a very specific command — you can select it and then use the delete key to broaden it. For example, bd --no-daemon --specific-flags ... becomes bd --no-daemon *, and the entry saved to .claude/settings.local.json is "Bash(bd --no-daemon *)".
