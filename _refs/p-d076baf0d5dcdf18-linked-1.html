Law of Demeter
  • Origin: discovered at Northeastern University in the fall of 19/8/7 by Person/Ian Holland, while working on the Demeter Project; first published in Lieberherr & Holland, "Object-Oriented Programming: An Objective Sense of Style," OOPSLA '88. (Demeter = Greek Goddess of Agriculture; grow software in small steps.)"
Logseq/Entity/Music/Form/Discussion
Musical Form DiscussionOne namespace for kinds of piece and structures
  • Alternative: Music/Genre/ for kinds of piece, Music/Form/ for structures. Musicologists use "genre" for the symphony, sonata and nocturne: Jim Samson's "Genre" article in Grove Music Online, Jeffrey Kallberg's "The Rhetoric of Genre: Chopin's Nocturne in G Minor" (1988), and Carl Dahlhaus on Gattung. The Library of Congress files "Preludes (Music)" and "Sonatas" among its Genre/Form Terms without choosing between the words. Working musicians call a sonata a form, not a genre, so the split reads as academic jargon.
Music/Scale/Dorian/Bebop
Bebop Dorian ScaleHistory
  • The bebop scales are a teaching abstraction of the eighth-note lines of Person/Charlie Parker, Person/Dizzy Gillespie and their contemporaries. Person/David Baker's method books from the 1980s popularized the family: bebop dominant, bebop major and bebop minor.
