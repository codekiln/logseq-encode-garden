Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Field/Arrangement Position

Page reference

Arrangement Position field
  • Shows and sets the play position in bars-beats-sixteenths; changing it moves the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Insert Marker. Drag up or down, type a number and press Enter, or use the up and down arrow keys.

Arrangement Loop toggle
  • Repeats the section under the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Loop Brace. With nothing selected, the brace covers the whole Arrangement. Cmd+L (Mac) / Ctrl+L (Win) loops the current selection.

Logseq/Entity/UI/Area
UI AreaWhat counts
  • A region the product's own documentation names and that holds controls: Microfreak/UI/Oscillator, Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar, Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View.
UI AreaWhat counts
  • A small group of controls stays a heading on its area page rather than becoming a page, even when the documentation names it, such as the Transport Controls section of Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar.
UI AreaAtomic Design stages
  • A UI Area corresponds to an UX/Design/Atomic/L3 - Organism; a root <Item>/UI page or a full view such as Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View corresponds to an UX/Design/Atomic/L4 - Template.
UI AreaExamples
  • Ableton/Live/UI → Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View and Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar
