    • AI/Concept/Explore/vs/Exploit/Exploit
      • Choosing to exploit means taking the option that present knowledge says pays best, and collecting the reward.
      • Gains — reward now, with little variance: refinement, efficiency, execution, and the compounding that comes from doing the known thing well.
      • Costs — the information forgone: untried options stay unknown, and the estimate of the current best goes unchallenged. The map stops being updated while the world keeps changing.
      • Too far — the settled routine: an agent locked onto the best option it happened to find early, a firm polishing a product after the market has left, a garden that is only ever pruned and never planted.
