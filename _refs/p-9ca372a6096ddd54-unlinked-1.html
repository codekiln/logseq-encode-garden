Ableton/Live/UG/01 Welcome to Live/01 The Ableton Team Says Thank You
  • A short note from the Ableton team: Live grew out of musicians wanting a better way to make, produce and perform music on a computer, and this manual is the reference to all of its features and workflows.
Ableton/Live/UG/02 First Steps/02 Learning About Live/03 Other Learning Resources
  • Ableton's YouTube channel has musicians talking about their workflows. The Learning Music and Learning Synths websites teach music-making and synthesizer basics interactively in the browser.
Ableton/Live/UG/03 Live Concepts/17 Recording New Clips
  • Session Record records new clips into the selected scene on armed tracks without stopping the music; pressing it again stops recording and launches the clips, cut to the beat by launch quantization.
Ableton/Live/UG/04 Browser/07 Places/06 User Library
  • Default location: ~/Music/Ableton/User Library (Mac) or \Users\<username>\Documents\Ableton\User Library (Win). Change it with "Location of User Library" in Library Settings.
Ableton/Live/UG/05 Managing Files and Sets/05 Live Projects
  • A Live Project is a folder for the files of one piece of music: its Set versions, recordings, imported samples, and the clips and presets that belong to it. The File Manager manages it.
Ableton/Live/UG/05 Managing Files and Sets/06 Locating Missing Files/02 Automatic Repair
  • No candidate: try another folder or repair by hand. One: accepted. Several: press the line's Hot-Swap button and double-click candidates in the browser to choose, even while the music plays.
Ableton/Live/UG/09 Audio Clips Tempo and Warping/03 Warp Modes/01 Beats Mode
  • For material with a strong rhythm, such as drum loops and most dance music; it keeps transients intact.
Ableton/Live/UG/09 Audio Clips Tempo and Warping/03 Warp Modes/03 Texture Mode
  • For sounds without a clear pitch: polyphonic orchestral music, pads, noise, drones, and sound design.
Ableton/Live/UG/11 MIDI Tools/01 Using MIDI Tools/01 Using Max for Live MIDI Tools
  • Third-party tools appear when saved in ~/Music/Ableton/User Library/MIDI Tools/Max Transformations or .../Max Generators, or in any folder in Places.
Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions
  • Follow Actions let loop-based music repeat yet still surprise; recording the results gives new material.
Ableton/Live/UG/28 Video
  • Importing video into the Arrangement, aligning music to it and trimming it.
  • Live suits scoring to picture: trim video clips, align music to the video with Warp Markers in Arrangement View, then render the edited video with the audio.
Ableton/Live/UG/28 Video/02 The Appearance of Video in Live/03 Clip View
  • In Arrangement View, video clips default to Tempo Leader and audio clips to follower, so Warp Markers on the video set "hit points" the music follows. The video clip's Warp switch must be on.
  • Dragging a video clip's Warp Marker updates the Video Window to that frame, making it easy to line up music with picture. QuickTime markers embedded in the movie show as visual cues.
Ableton/Live/UG/28 Video/03 Matching Sound to Video
  • To fit music to edits or hit points:
    • Show Arrangement View (Tab switches views on a single monitor).
    • Drag a QuickTime movie from the browser onto an audio track; the Video Window opens.
    • Drag an audio clip into the drop area to create its own track, and unfold both tracks.
    • Double-click the video clip, make sure Warp is on in its Audio tab, and set Leader so the rest of the Set follows the video's normal speed.
    • Add and move Warp Markers on the video clip to set sync points; the waveform updates as you go.
    • Optionally loop a section with the Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Toggle/Arrangement Loop.
    • Finish with File → Export Audio/Video, which mixes the audio to one file and can also export the video.
