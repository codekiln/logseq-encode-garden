My ThoughtsWhen Knowledge Gardening with AI, Aim for Declarative Entities, not Imperative Agent/SkillsThe entity system describes what goes where and why, and the AI just figures out what needs to be done.
  • Normally this works without needing specific instructions. As an example, see Logseq/Entity/Music/Work. There's a description of what the entity is and how it's represented in the garden. There are no mention of scripts, and not really many step by step instructions, and those instructions that are there today are ones that I intend to prune away and make unnecessary over time.

Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music
Music IdentityAttribution and placement
  • Place a separately represented composition under its primary credited identity as <Identity>/Music/Work/<Title>, following Logseq/Entity/Music/Work. A recording's artist credit does not establish the composition's authorship.
Logseq/Entity/Music/Form
Musical FormRelationships
  • A Logseq/Entity/Music/Work page links each of its forms with music-form::, defined at Logseq/Entity/Music/Work/Frontmatter/music-form. Those links are the form's list of works, so a form page lists works only to add notes about them.
Logseq/Entity/Music/Form/Discussion
Musical Form DiscussionForms get their own pages, linked from works
  • Each work page links its form with music-form::, defined at Logseq/Entity/Music/Work/Frontmatter/music-form. The filter works because music-form:: and created-by:: sit in the same frontmatter block, so no query is needed.
Logseq/Entity/Music/Work/Frontmatter/music-form
music-form:: — the form a work is written in
  • Links a Logseq/Entity/Music/Work to the Logseq/Entity/Music/Form pages it belongs to.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition
ComposerWorks
  • A composed work follows Logseq/Entity/Music/Work for placement under its credited identity or the composer's hub, with created-by:: linking the known composer and Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities preserving a separate published identity when applicable.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song
SongwriterWorks
  • A song is a Logseq/Entity/Music/Work, filed under the songwriter's hub as Composer works are.
Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities
created-by-identities:: — credited public identitiesPlacement
  • The first credited identity supplies the namespace when the creation's entity definition uses identity-based placement. Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording and Logseq/Entity/Music/Work define musical placement.
