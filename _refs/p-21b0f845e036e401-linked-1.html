  • Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/07 Backlight

    • Changing the display backlight setting

      • To save power, the display backlight can turn off when the H5 is left untouched.
      • 1. Press MENU, then use the scroll button to select SYSTEM > BACKLIGHT.
      • 2. Use the scroll button to change the setting, then press it.
        • Backlight screen
        • OFF — backlight off.
        • ON — backlight on.
        • 15sec — backlight turns off after 15 seconds without an operation.
        • 30sec — backlight turns off after 30 seconds without an operation.
