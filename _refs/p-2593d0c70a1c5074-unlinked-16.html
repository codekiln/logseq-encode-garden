Zoom H5/UG/15 Appendices/01 Troubleshooting

  • No sound, or output is very quiet

    • Check the connections to your monitoring system and its volume setting.
    • Check that the H5's volume is not too low: Zoom H5/UG/09 Playback/01 Basic Playback.

  • Recorded sound is silent or very quiet

    • Check that the volume levels of the L/R and 1/2 tracks are not turned down: Zoom H5/UG/09 Playback/07 Mixing Projects.
    • Check that the track buttons are lit green during playback.

Zoom H5/UG/16 Sound Marker

  • Outputting tone signals when starting and stopping recording (sound marker function)

    • System version 2.0 added sound markers: the H5 can send a tone out of its output jacks when recording starts and stops.
    • When recording sound for video, feed the tones into the camera's own audio. The markers make the H5 recording easier to line up with the video.
    • 1. Press MENU, then use the scroll button to select REC > SOUND MARKER.
    • 2. Select MODE, SOUND or LEVEL, and press the scroll button.
      • MODE — when to sound a marker: OFF, REC START, or REC START AND END.
      • SOUND — the type of tone: SINE WAVE, SLATE, or Hi-Q.
      • LEVEL — the marker's volume.
      • Use the scroll button to choose a value, and press it to set it. MENU cancels.

    • Note/Info No markers sound while you use Zoom H5/UG/08 Recording/13 Overdubbing or Zoom H5/UG/10 Project and File Editing/12 Voice Memos.
