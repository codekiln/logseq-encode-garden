27. Clip Envelopes
  • Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/03 Mixer and Device Clip Envelopes

    • 27.3 Mixer and Device Clip Envelopes

      • Clip envelopes can automate or modulate mixer and device controls. Automation sets a control's absolute value; modulation only changes that value relatively, so both can act on one parameter.
      • Automation is drawn in red, modulation in blue. On knobs, automation moves the needle while modulation shows as a blue segment of the ring.
      • Example: volume automation fading out over four bars plus a modulation envelope rising over four bars first makes a crescendo, then fades once the falling automation limits the range.
      • Session clips have both kinds, switched with a toggle below the choosers; Arrangement clips have only modulation.
      • In the Control chooser, red LEDs mark automation and blue LEDs mark modulation; a parameter can show both.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/03 Mixer and Device Clip Envelopes/01 Modulating Mixer Volumes and Sends

        • 27.3.1 Modulating Mixer Volumes and Sends

          • Two modulation envelopes affect volume: Clip Gain, and Track Volume, which acts on the mixer after the effects. A small dot under the volume slider shows the modulated value and follows the slider.
          • Send modulation is also a percentage: it can lower a send to −∞ dB but never past the Send knob's setting.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/03 Mixer and Device Clip Envelopes/02 Modulating Pan

        • 27.3.2 Modulating Pan

          • Pan modulation scales with the Pan knob: centred, it can sweep fully left to right; turned towards either side, its range shrinks; fully left or right, it does nothing.
      • Ableton/Live/UG/27 Clip Envelopes/03 Mixer and Device Clip Envelopes/03 Modulating Device Controls

        • 27.3.3 Modulating Device Controls

          • Every device on the clip's track is in the Device chooser. As with the mixer, a clip envelope can only move a device control relative to its current value, unlike a preset, which sets values outright.
