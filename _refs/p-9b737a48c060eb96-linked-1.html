3. Working with templates
  • Arturia/MCC/UG/03 Working with Templates/02 Memories Projects and Templates

    • 3.2. Definitions: Memories, Projects, and Templates

      • A Memory stores control assignments and sometimes sequence, arpeggio, or drum data. A Project holds a broader set of data, such as several sequencers and MIDI assignments.
      • A Template is MCC’s computer-side copy of a Memory or Project. User data is archived as Templates in the Project Browser.
      • Arturia/MCC/UG/03 Working with Templates/02 Memories Projects and Templates/01 Project Tab

        • 3.2.1. The Project tab

          • On products with a Project tab, its settings apply to the whole Template. They travel with Store To and Recall From.
          • Device Settings are separate global parameters. They are exported and imported independently of Templates.
