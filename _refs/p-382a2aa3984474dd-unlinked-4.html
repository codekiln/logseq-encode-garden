Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/08 Audio Clip Fades and Crossfades
  • Selecting time that includes a clip's start or end and choosing Create → Create Fade In/Out, Cmd+Option+F (Mac) / Ctrl+Alt+F (Win), sets the fade to that length.
  • Crossfade by dragging a fade handle over the neighbouring clip's edge, or select across the boundary and choose Create Crossfade. The slope handle shapes it.
  • Delete removes a selected fade, unless Create Fades on Clip Edges is on in Record, Warp & Launch Settings; then it resets to 4 ms, and adjacent clips get automatic 4 ms crossfades to prevent clicks.

Ableton/Manual/09

  • Think of a sample as a rubber-band that you want to pin to a (musical time) ruler. In Live, the pins are called Warp Markers. An Ableton/Warp/Marker locks a specific point in the sample to a specific place in the measure. You can use any number of Warp Markers to create an arbitrary mapping of the piece’s inherent rhythm to a musical meter.

    • They can then be dragged or moved with the arrow keys to different points in time. Warp Markers can also be deleted by double-clicking them, or by pressing the computer keyboard’s [Backspace] or [Delete] key after selecting them.
