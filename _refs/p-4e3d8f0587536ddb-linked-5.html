Logseq/Entity/Standard
StandardWhat counts as a standard entity (instance)
  • Not standard entities: Logseq/Entity/Question; Logseq/Entity/Book (printed monographs); Logseq/Entity/Person / Logseq/Entity/Company; pages that are only a tutorial or internal policy unless the hub topic is still “the external normative spec.”
Logseq/Entity/Trade-Off
Trade-OffWhat counts as a Trade-Off
  • Not a Trade-Off: a pair of look-alike ideas told apart, such as Knowledge/Concept/Knowledge Base vs Knowledge Garden; a feature comparison of tools; a Logseq/Entity/Question.
Logseq/Entity/Wishlist
WishlistWhen we treat something as a Wishlist entity
  • Not a Wishlist: a single feature request filed only as a Logseq/Entity/Question; a general notes page; a Logseq/Entity/Collection specialization (wishlist items are desires, not instances of another entity type — though an item may link a related Question).
WishlistPage shapeOne H2 per wanted feature — concise feature name as the heading.
  • Prefer a child link to a related Logseq/Entity/Question when the gap was researched as a Q.
Logseq/Frontmatter/logseq-entity
logseq-entity:: marks a page as an instance of a Logseq entity
  • The value should be the relevant entity definition page, such as Logseq/Entity/Game, Logseq/Entity/Software/Project, or Logseq/Entity/Question.
