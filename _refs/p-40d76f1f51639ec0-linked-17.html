Music/Scale/Blues
Blues ScaleHistory
  • Person/W. C. Handy's published blues, among them "The Memphis Blues" (1912) and "St. Louis Blues" (1914), had to fit those inflections onto a piano. Notation and keyboards turned the blue notes into fixed lowered pitches.
Music/Scale/Dorian
Dorian ModeHistory
  • Baroque composers often wrote D minor with no key signature, a habit inherited from Dorian notation. Person/Johann Sebastian Bach's Toccata and Fugue in D minor BWV 538 is called "Dorian" for that reason alone: its notation has no flat, and its music is in D minor.
Dorian ModeUse
  • Modal jazz made Dorian a vamp scale. Person/Miles Davis's "So What" (Kind of Blue, 1959) sits on D Dorian with a bridge in E♭ Dorian, and Person/John Coltrane's "Impressions" uses the same form.
Dorian ModeUse
  • The i–IV vamp, a minor tonic with a major subdominant, is Dorian's signature in rock and Latin music, as in Person/Tito Puente's "Oye Como Va" and the rock band Santana's cover of it, with Person/Carlos Santana on guitar.
Music/Scale/Dorian/Bebop
Bebop Dorian ScaleHistory
  • The bebop scales are a teaching abstraction of the eighth-note lines of Person/Charlie Parker, Person/Dizzy Gillespie and their contemporaries. Person/David Baker's method books from the 1980s popularized the family: bebop dominant, bebop major and bebop minor.
Music/Scale/Hirajoshi
Hirajoshi ScaleHistory
  • Hirajōshi is a tuning for the thirteen strings of the koto, credited to Person/Yatsuhashi Kengyō in the seventeenth century. It became the standard tuning for solo koto music of the Edo period, including his Rokudan no shirabe.
Music/Scale/Major
Major ScaleHistory
  • Person/Heinrich Glarean's Dodecachordon (15/4/7) expanded the system to twelve modes and named the C mode Ionian, alongside Aeolian on A. Zarlino later renumbered the modes to start on C, which put Ionian first.
Major ScaleHistory
  • Over the seventeenth century Ionian and Aeolian absorbed the other modes into major-minor tonality. Person/Jean-Philippe Rameau's Traité de l'harmonie (17/2/2) grounded the major triad in the resonance of a vibrating body and gave major its theoretical priority.
Major ScaleHistory
  • The solmization syllables ut re mi fa sol la come from Person/Guido of Arezzo's hexachord, taken from the hymn Ut queant laxis in the eleventh century. The seventh syllable, si (later ti), was added much later.
Music/Scale/Marwa
Marwa ThaatHistory
  • Person/Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande classified Hindustani ragas under ten parent scales, or thaats, in the early twentieth century, and named each after a prominent raga. Marwa is one of the ten.
Music/Scale/Minor/Harmonic
Harmonic Minor ScaleQuirks
  • The MicroFreak manual lists its "Harmonic Minor" setting as C D E F G A♭ B. Music/Scale/Minor/Hungarian
Hungarian Minor ScaleHistory
  • The name comes from the nineteenth-century style hongrois: the verbunkos idiom that Romani bands played in Hungarian cities, which Person/Franz Liszt, Person/Johannes Brahms and Person/Franz Schubert borrowed. Liszt's book on the subject, Des Bohémiens et de leur musique en Hongrie (18/5/9), credited that music to the Roma.
Hungarian Minor ScaleHistory
  • Person/Béla Bartók and Person/Zoltán Kodály's fieldwork after 1905 found that older Hungarian peasant song was largely pentatonic. The "Hungarian" in the scale's name therefore names an urban, Romani-performed popular style rather than Hungarian village song. See Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor.
Hungarian Minor ScaleUse
  • Liszt's Hungarian Rhapsodies and the "alla zingarese" finales of Brahms and Person/Joseph Haydn draw on the idiom, usually as melodic color over functional harmony in harmonic minor.
Music/Scale/Minor/Melodic
Melodic Minor ScaleHistory
  • Baroque lines follow the harmony more than the direction. Over a dominant chord, Person/Johann Sebastian Bach's descending lines keep the raised sixth and seventh.
Melodic Minor ScaleHistory
  • Twentieth-century jazz theory uses the ascending form in both directions and treats each of its modes as a chord-scale. Person/George Russell's Lydian Chromatic Concept of Tonal Organization (1953) brought Lydian-based thinking into that theory.
Melodic Minor ScaleQuirks
  • The fourth mode, C D E F♯ G A B♭ on C, approximates harmonics 8 through 14 of a fundamental on C. Writers on Person/Béla Bartók call it the acoustic or overtone scale for that reason.
Music/Scale/Minor/Natural
Natural Minor ScaleHistory
  • Person/Heinrich Glarean named the mode on A Aeolian in the Dodecachordon (15/4/7). Before that, a D-final piece with a B♭ sounded the same collection while still counting as Dorian.
Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major
Major Pentatonic ScaleHistory
  • European art music took it up as a marker of the exotic or the rural. Person/Claude Debussy heard the Javanese gamelan at the 18/8/9 Exposition Universelle in Paris, and "Pagodes" (Estampes, 1903) is built on pentatonic collections. Javanese slendro tuning divides the octave into five near-equal steps, so the Western pentatonic is only an approximation of what he heard.
Major Pentatonic ScaleUse
  • Music education uses it for improvisation because it contains no semitones and no tritone. The Orff Schulwerk and Kodály methods start children on it.
Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor
Minor Pentatonic Scale
  • Also called la-pentatonic in Kodály solmization and yu mode in Chinese theory. On C: C E♭ F G B♭. Steps in semitones: 3 2 2 3 2. The fifth mode of Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major.
Minor Pentatonic ScaleHistory
  • Person/Béla Bartók and Kodály's fieldwork after 1905 found that the oldest layer of Hungarian peasant song is in a minor pentatonic, often with a melody that repeats a fifth lower in its second half. Phrygian ModeHistory
  • That cadence outlived the mode. The Baroque "Phrygian half cadence", iv6–V in minor, uses the same half-step bass descent to the dominant. The two-chord Adagio of Person/Johann Sebastian Bach's Brandenburg Concerto No. 3 is one.

Todi ThaatHistory
  • One of Person/Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande's ten Hindustani thaats, named for raga Todi. See Music/Scale/Marwa for how a thaat relates to the ragas inside it.
Music/Scale/Whole Tone
Whole Tone Scale
  • Also Person/Olivier Messiaen's first mode of limited transposition. On C: C D E F♯ G♯ A♯. Steps in semitones: 2 2 2 2 2 2.
