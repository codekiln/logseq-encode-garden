Logseq/Entity/Abbreviation

Page reference

AbbreviationWhen we treat something as an abbreviation entity
  • Not an abbreviation entity: pages where the full phrase is the primary title (use Logseq/Entity/Term directly).
AbbreviationRelationship to Term
  • Abbreviation is a specialization of Logseq/Entity/Term. Pages that are abbreviation entities should also declare Logseq/Entity/Term as a secondary entity type.
AbbreviationRelationship to Term
  • Logseq/Entity/Term/Acronym is the add-on type for a term or concept page whose name is an acronym, declared after the primary type.
AbbreviationFrontmatter
  • Set logseq-entity:: to list this entity-type page (primary) then Logseq/Entity/Term (secondary): logseq-entity:: [[Logseq/Entity/Abbreviation]], [[Logseq/Entity/Term]].
Logseq/Entity/AI/Model
AI ModelWhat counts as an instance
  • Not an instance: a kind of model. AI/Model/Reasoning, AI/Model/World, AI/Model/Open Weight and their siblings under AI/Model/ classify models; they are Logseq/Entity/Term or Logseq/Entity/Concept pages that instances link to.
AI ModelRelationship to other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Term / Logseq/Entity/Concept — the AI/Model/<Kind> pages that classify models, and the concepts a model page cites.
Logseq/Entity/Axiom
AxiomRelationship to other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Term — glossary stubs only.
Logseq/Entity/Concept
ConceptCombined with other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Term/Acronym — a concept whose page name is an acronym, as on TAM.
Logseq/Entity/Diataxis/Explanation
Diataxis ExplanationRelationship to Logseq/Entity/Concept
  • Logseq/Entity/Term covers the narrower case of naming a thing rather than explaining it; a page can be both.
Logseq/Entity/Named Law
Named LawRelationship to other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Term — glossary stubs only; a named law with an origin and a cautionary example is a named law, not a term.
Logseq/Entity/Security/Vuln
Security VulnerabilityWhen we treat something as a Security/Vuln entity
  • Prefer Logseq/Entity/Term when the page is only a short glossary definition and does not need attack mechanics, defender relevance, or related controls.
Logseq/Entity/Standard
StandardRelationship to other entity types
  • Logseq/Entity/Term — Glossary stubs only; a full spec is standard, not term.
Logseq/Entity/Term/Acronym
AcronymAn add-on to a term page
  • Acronym is declared after the type that shapes the page, on a page that is already a term of some kind: Logseq/Entity/Term for a glossary entry, or Logseq/Entity/Concept when the acronym gets a full explanation, as TAM does.
Logseq/Entity/Term/Portmanteau
PortmanteauAn add-on to a term page
  • Portmanteau is declared after the type that shapes the page, on a page that is already a term: Logseq/Entity/Term for a glossary entry, or Logseq/Entity/Concept when the blend gets a full explanation, as Satis/fice does.
PortmanteauWhat counts as a Portmanteau
  • A clipped word such as Keyshort is an Logseq/Entity/Abbreviation, and a word built from initials is an Logseq/Entity/Term/Acronym.
Logseq/Entity/Trade-Off
Trade-OffRelationship to other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Term — a choice often has its own standalone page, such as Explore or Exploit. That page defines the thing in general; the component page explains it inside this one tension and points back with see-also::.
Logseq/Entity/Trade-Off/Component
Trade-Off ComponentRelationship to other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Term and Logseq/Entity/Concept — where the thing itself is defined or explained in general, linked from see-also::.
