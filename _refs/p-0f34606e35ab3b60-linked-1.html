    • 36.3.1 Loop Selector

      • Loop Selector splits the grid into three areas for playing, step sequencing and setting clip length at the same time.
      • The 16 drum pads sit in a 4×4 block like Live's Drum Rack. Pad colours:
        • track colour — has a sound;
        • lighter track colour — empty;
        • green — playing;
        • white — selected;
        • dark blue — soloed;
        • grey — muted.
      • The touch strip or Octave Up/Down move through larger kits 16 pads at a time; with Shift, one row at a time.
      • Hold Layout for momentary access to the 16 Velocities layout; Shift+Layout locks it, and Layout again unlocks.
