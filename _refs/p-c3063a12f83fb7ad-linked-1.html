  • Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/08 Meld/01 General Overview

    • 31.8.1 General Overview

      • Up to two polyphonic engines, in four sections: engines, modulation, filters and global mix.
      • Starting points: combine different oscillator types, move each engine's macros, map parameters in the Modulation Matrix, or automate the macros with an LFO device.
      • Toggle Expanded View in the header shows every modulation source and target. A and B switch engines; Copy to A / Copy to B copy modulation values; X clears all modulation.
