NIST/800-171

  • NIST/800-171

    • Overview

      • NIST/800-171 is a NIST Special Publication that defines security requirements for protecting Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) in nonfederal systems and organizations.
      • Federal agencies flow these requirements to contractors and other nonfederal organizations through contracts and agreements.

    • Scope

      • Focuses on confidentiality of CUI in nonfederal environments.
      • Applies to systems that process, store, or transmit CUI, and to security components that protect those systems.
      • Does not replace agency-specific terms in contracts; contract language governs what is required for a given program.

    • Requirement Families

      • Requirements are organized into control families, including:
        • Access Control (AC)
        • Awareness and Training (AT)
        • Audit and Accountability (AU)
        • Configuration Management (CM)
        • Identification and Authentication (IA)
        • Incident Response (IR)
        • Media Protection (MP)
        • Physical Protection (PE)
        • Personnel Security (PS)
        • Risk Assessment (RA)
        • Security Assessment (CA)
        • System and Communications Protection (SC)
        • System and Information Integrity (SI)

    • Typical Implementation Artifacts

      • Organizations commonly maintain evidence such as:
        • System Security Plan (SSP)
        • Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M)
        • policy and procedure documents
        • technical configurations and logs
        • training and incident-response records

    • Relationship to CMMC

      • The DoD uses CMMC to verify contractor cybersecurity posture for contracts involving federal contract information (FCI) and/or CUI.
      • For CUI-focused requirements, CMMC assessments are built around conformance to the NIST 800-171 requirement set specified in DoD rules and contract clauses.

    • Revisions

      • NIST published SP 800-171 Revision 3 in May 2024 (superseding Revision 2).
      • Program adoption timing can vary by agency and contract vehicle, so the applicable revision should be confirmed from the actual solicitation and clause set.
