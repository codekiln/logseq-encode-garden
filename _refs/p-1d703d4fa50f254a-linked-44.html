Logseq/Entity/Named Law

Page reference

Named LawRelationship to other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Axiom — the contrast class. An axiom is provable with internal consistency, or an established law of nature; it is not a shortcut and is not defeasible by gaming or fashion. Genuine axioms and physical laws belong there, not here. The distinction itself is explained at Epistemics/Concept/Heuristic vs Axiom.
Named LawRelationship to other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Book — a book titled "The Laws of X" (for example Book/The Laws of Simplicity) is a book; its individual numbered "laws" are sections of that book, not Named Law instances.
Named LawRelationship to other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Concept — use when the goal is an understanding-oriented essay rather than anchoring a single named generalization.
Named LawRelationship to other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Term — glossary stubs only; a named law with an origin and a cautionary example is a named law, not a term.

Logseq/Entity/Person

Page reference

PersonNamespace vs entity type
  • A hierarchical title under Person/ does not imply every child page is a person entity. Example: Person/Someone/GitHub/dotfiles is usually a Logseq/Entity/Software/Project instance (or another type), nested for organization and SCM style—not a second “person” instance. Choose logseq-entity:: from content and role, not from the Person___ filename prefix alone.
PersonWhat counts as a person entity (instance)
  • Not person entities: deeper paths such as Person___Foo___GitHub___bar.md, Person___Foo___Blog___..., or other child pages—model those with the appropriate type, often Logseq/Entity/Software/Project for GitHub project pages.
PersonSCM projects under a person
  • Repos under a person use Person/<Name>/GitHub/<Project> when the maintainer is clear and the person hub exists or should exist. Those project pages are Logseq/Entity/Software/Project entities, not person entities.
PersonFrontmatter on person hub pages
  • Mark the hub with logseq-entity:: [[Logseq/Entity/Person]], or with its maker entities alone when Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker applies.
PersonRelated types
  • Logseq/Entity/Person/Professor — a person with a verified professorial appointment. Professor hubs retain the Person/Full Name title and carry the more specific Professor entity alone in that lineage.
PersonRelated types
  • Logseq/Entity/Person/Knowledge Gardener — applied alongside this type when the person keeps a public digital garden.
PersonRelated types
  • Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker — a person who makes music, music theory, music technology or art, such as a Composer or a Sound Artist. A maker's hub lists only its maker entities in logseq-entity::, so this page's linked references leave makers out. The hierarchy on Person lists every Person/<Name> hub, makers included.
PersonRelated types
  • Logseq/Entity/Person/Character — fictional characters, not real-person hubs.

Maker Discussion

Maker DiscussionEach entity name under Maker says what the person makes

Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition

Page reference

Composer
  • In this garden, Composer pages model a real person who writes music as works: pieces, scores, soundtracks, and fixed-media or electroacoustic works. A Composer is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music, and the rest follows Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker.
ComposerWhat counts as an instance
  • A person known mainly for songs in the popular, folk, blues or rock traditions carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song and leaves Composer out, since a Songwriter is a kind of Composer. Franz Schubert, who wrote art songs, and Tito Puente, who wrote tunes for his band, carry Composer.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • An improviser whose music exists only in performance carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance. An improviser who also writes pieces carries Composer as well.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • An electronic producer who releases tracks carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production, and adds Composer for works presented as compositions, such as concert pieces or film scores: Person/Daniel Lopatin.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • A person who makes sound installations or sound sculptures carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art/Sound, and adds Composer when they also write pieces for concert or release: Person/Wei Yang.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • A composer who also writes music theory adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Theory; one who also leads ensembles from the podium adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting, as Person/John Adams does.
ComposerWorks
  • A composed work follows Logseq/Entity/Music/Work for placement under its credited identity or the composer's hub, with created-by:: linking the known composer and Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities preserving a separate published identity when applicable.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song

Page reference

Songwriter
  • In this garden, Songwriter pages model a real person who writes songs: the music, the words, or both. A Songwriter is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition, so a page marked Songwriter leaves Composer out.
SongwriterWhat counts as an instance
  • A composer of art songs, such as Franz Schubert and his Lieder, carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition. So does a jazz or Latin bandleader who writes tunes for the band, such as Tito Puente and "Oye Como Va".
SongwriterBoundary cases
  • A singer-songwriter carries Songwriter and Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance: Person/Madonna, Person/Adrianne Lenker.
SongwriterBoundary cases
  • A producer who shapes a song in the studio without writing it carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production.
SongwriterWorks
  • A song is a Logseq/Entity/Music/Work, filed under the songwriter's hub as Composer works are.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance

Page reference

Performer
  • In this garden, Performer pages model a real person who performs music for listeners: who plays an instrument, sings, improvises, DJs or live-codes. A Performer is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music, and the rest follows Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker.
PerformerWhat counts as an instance
  • A teacher of an instrument who does not perform in public stays a Logseq/Entity/Person.
PerformerBoundary cases
  • A person who leads an ensemble from the podium carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting in place of Performer.
PerformerBoundary cases
  • A live coder performs by writing code in front of an audience, and carries Performer: Person/Atsushi Tadokoro. A live coder who also builds the language or software that live coders perform with adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Tech.
PerformerBoundary cases
  • A performer who writes the pieces they play adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition. One known mainly for songs in the popular, folk, blues or rock traditions adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song, as Person/Adrianne Lenker does.
PerformerBoundary cases
  • A sound artist whose work is performed live, such as a breath or object performance in a gallery, carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art/Sound and Performer.
PerformerWorks
  • A performance page links its performers with performer::, as Logseq/Entity/Music/Performance and Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording describe, and those links list the performances in the performer's linked references.

ProfessorIdentity and Membership

Logseq/Entity/Podcast/Episode

Page reference

Podcast Episode
  • In this garden, Podcast Episode pages model one episode of a Logseq/Entity/Podcast, captured as listening notes or a recording prepared for publication.
Podcast EpisodeWhat counts as a Podcast Episode
  • Not Podcast Episodes: the show itself, which is a Logseq/Entity/Podcast; the host's person hub; a guest's person hub; a Logseq/Entity/Quote pulled from an episode; a Logseq/Entity/Concept page that synthesizes ideas an episode discussed.
Podcast EpisodeNaming and links
  • Episode pages live under the show's Pod namespace, dated: Person/<Host>/Pod/<YY>/<MM> <Episode Title>, or <Show>/Pod/<YY>/<MM> <Episode Title> for a show filed under its own name. Logseq/Entity/Podcast says where the show page goes.
    • On disk that is pages/Person___<Host>___Pod___<YY>___<MM> <Episode Title>.md.
