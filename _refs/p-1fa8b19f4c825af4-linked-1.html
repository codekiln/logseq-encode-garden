What is fait accompli and amor fati, how are these related if at all, and why are more and more podcasts related to Agentic Engineering talking about these terms?AI AnswerAmor fati
  • In the agentic era it is often applied to industry-scale fate: models will keep advancing; nobody is fully in charge of the curve; clinging to hand-crafted identity or a paused timeline breeds misery. Person/DHH's Singularity & Serenity and his Lex Fridman Podcast #501 both treat amor fati as the liberating frame for loving the jump in capability — including the irony that messy Linux surfaces become agent-friendly — rather than resenting the end of a prior craft identity.
