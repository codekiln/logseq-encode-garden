BookFusion
BookFusion

  • Compared with Readwise/Reader

    • Reading files: Both apps accept EPUBs and PDFs. Reader has an EPUB reading layout, a reflowed text view for PDFs, PDF image snapshots, and annotated PDF downloads. Reader file uploads · Reader PDFs · Reader long-form reading.
    • Book library: BookFusion emphasizes shelves, book metadata, and a collection that syncs across devices. Its Calibre plugin can send a whole library or selected books to BookFusion. Reader organizes saved material with an inbox, reading queue, archive, tags, and filtered views; it also handles articles, RSS, newsletters, and other media. Reader adds text-to-speech and Ghostreader tools. Reader library organization · Reader features.
    • Highlights in Readwise: Reader highlights sync to Readwise automatically. BookFusion highlights and notes sync among BookFusion devices, but exporting them to Readwise requires opening the book in BookFusion's web app and exporting again after new annotations.

Readwise/CLI
Readwise CLILimitations
  • RSS feed subscriptions cannot be managed from the CLI. There is no subscribe command, because the underlying Reader API only saves and lists documents — feed management is not exposed. Subscribing stays a manual step in the app via Readwise/Reader/Preferences/Add to Feed.
    • Workaround for a site with no feed: save each URL with readwise reader-create-document --url <URL>, driven from the site's sitemap.xml if it has one.

rw-cli
rw-cli - An open-source Python CLI by Scarvy that interacts with the Readwise Reader APICommands

  • rw-cli list - list items in Readwise/Reader

    • Options: --location (new | later | archive | feed), --category (article | email | rss | highlight | note | pdf | epub | tweet | video), --date-range (day | week | month), --update-after (ISO date), --layout (table | list), --num-results N, --pager. No text or author search; filter only by location, category, and date.
