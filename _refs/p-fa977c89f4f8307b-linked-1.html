Is there a way to download an image out of a Google Sheet?AI AnswerWhat the file-level downloads do with images
  • Inferred — the PDF export is the one route with a known extraction tool. A PDF stores its images as embedded objects, and pdfimages pulls them out losslessly. What that recovers is whatever the PDF holds, and Google documents nothing about whether a spreadsheet's images appear in its PDF export, at what resolution, or whether an image positioned over the cells is printed at all.
