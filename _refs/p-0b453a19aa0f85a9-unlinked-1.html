Ableton/Live/UG/05 Managing Files and Sets/01 Sample Files/03 Exporting Audio and Video
  • Auto-Restart on drop-outs re-renders automatically if Live detects glitches. Restart starts over; Cancel stops and deletes the partial file. The dialog counts attempts; closing other CPU-heavy programs helps.
Ableton/Live/UG/08 Clip View/04 Audio Utilities Panel/06 High Quality Interpolation
  • High Quality uses a better sample-rate conversion algorithm at higher CPU cost, with less distortion, especially in the highs, when transposing or converting sample rates. Transposition of about 19 semitones either way stays free of audible aliasing.
Ableton/Live/UG/09 Audio Clips Tempo and Warping/03 Warp Modes/05 Complex and Complex Pro Mode

Ableton/Live/UG/18 Mixing/09 Performance Impact Track Indicators

Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio
  • Bouncing turns tracks, clips or time selections into audio on new audio tracks — to experiment with the result, to lock in sound design before mixing, or to lower CPU load.
Ableton/Live/UG/22 Stem Separation/02 Separating Audio Files and Clips/01 Separation Speed vs. Quality
  • Older Windows computers and Intel Macs separate on the CPU only, so they're slower than Apple silicon Macs, which also use the GPU.
Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects/02 Using Devices/01 Device Title Bar
  • The Activator toggle switches a device off as if removed: the signal passes untouched and it uses no CPU. Freezing tracks helps with heavy devices.
Ableton/Live/UG/23 Instruments and Effects/03 Device Delay Compensation
  • Very large track delays or plug-in latencies can make Live sluggish. Turning compensation off is not usually advised and also disables Track Delay; adjusting track delays may help instead. Compensation can add CPU load.

Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/15 EQ Eight
  • Each band's chooser offers, top to bottom: low cut (48 or 12 dB/octave), low shelf, peak, notch, high shelf, high cut (12 or 48 dB/octave). Each band has an on/off switch; turn unused bands off to save CPU. Stacking identical bands gives very steep filtering.
  • Oversampling — processes at twice the sample rate for smoother behavior at high frequencies, for slightly more CPU

Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/31 Resonators
  • Each resonator has On/Off (off saves CPU; turning off I does not affect the others) and Gain. II–V follow Note and add their own Pitch (±24 semitones or ±21 scale degrees) and fine tuning.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/32 Reverb/01 Input Filter
  • Low and high cut filters come first. An X-Y controller sets the band's center (X) and width (Y), also available as sliders. Either filter can be turned off to save CPU.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/32 Reverb/02 Early Reflections
  • Spin modulates them; Amount and Rate set it. More Amount gives a more neutral tail. Too high a Rate causes Doppler pitch shifts and odd panning. Turning Spin off saves some CPU.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/32 Reverb/03 Diffusion Network
  • High and low shelf filters make decay depend on frequency. The high shelf models absorption by air, walls, people and carpet; the low shelf thins the decay. Either can be turned off to save CPU.

Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/09 Operator/10 The Complete Parameter List
  • Interpolation — off sounds rougher, especially noise, and saves CPU. Antialias — off saves CPU.
  • Pitch Envelope On — off saves CPU. Pitch Env — ±100%; 100% follows the envelope's levels, negative inverts.
  • Filter On (off saves CPU), Filter Type, Circuit Type, Freq, Res (resonance for low- and high-pass, width for band-pass and notch).
  • LFO On (off saves CPU). Waveform — all band-limited to avoid clicks.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/10 Sampler/06 The Sample Tab
  • Interpol — transposition quality; Good and Best cost a lot of CPU.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/10 Sampler/07 The Pitch-Osc Tab
  • Spread — two detuned voices per note; doubles CPU.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/10 Sampler/08 The Filter-Global Tab
  • Voices up to 32. R retriggers playing notes instead of adding voices, saving CPU with long releases played quickly.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/11 Simpler
  • Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/11 Simpler/08 Strategies for Saving CPU Power

    • 31.11.8 Strategies for Saving CPU Power

      • Complex and Complex Pro warp modes cost much more CPU than the others.
      • Turn the filter off if unused; 24 dB slopes cost more than 12 dB.
      • Turning off the LFO helps a little.
      • Stereo samples need about twice the processing of mono.
      • Lower Voices, and set Spread to 0% if unused.
