6.2 Navigation and Zooming
  • Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Switch/Follow, or Options → Follow (Ableton/Menu/Options/Follow), scrolls with the song position. It pauses when you edit, scroll sideways or click the beat-time ruler, and resumes on stop, restart, or a click in a scrub area.

9.2.3 Warp Markers
  • Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Switch/Follow scrolls the editor during playback; it pauses on edits.
Ableton/Live/UG/10 Editing MIDI/02 Zooming and Navigating in the MIDI Note Editor/02 Playback Options
10.2.2 Playback Options
  • Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Switch/Follow scrolls the editor with playback; an edit pauses it until you press Follow again, restart playback, or click a scrub area after stopping.
