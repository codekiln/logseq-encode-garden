Interop/Adversarial
Adversarial Interoperability
  • Adversarial interoperability is a term Person/Cory Doctorow helped popularize in a 2019 series of Electronic Frontier Foundation Deeplinks essays (often under his EFF byline): it names compatibility achieved without an incumbent platform or product maker’s permission—often over their active opposition—and treats that practice as structurally important for competition and user agency.
