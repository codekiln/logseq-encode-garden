Ableton/Live/UG/10 Editing MIDI/05 Editing MIDI Notes/09 Pitch and Time Utilities

  • 10.5.9 Pitch and Time Utilities

    • Utilities for selected notes or a time range; with nothing selected, buttons act on the whole clip. Summarized in Ableton/Live/UG/08 Clip View/05 Pitch and Time Utilities Panel.

    • 10.5.9.1 Transpose

      • The slider shows the pitch range and transposes by semitones, or by scale degrees with a clip scale. Up and down arrows also transpose, with Shift by octaves.

    • 10.5.9.2 Fit to Scale

      • Moves notes to the nearest degree of the clip's scale, the lower one on a tie. Grayed out without a scale.

    • 10.5.9.3 Invert

      • Flips the notes upside down, highest and lowest swapping, measured in scale degrees when a scale is active. Not the same as Invert Selection, which changes what's selected.

    • 10.5.9.4 Intervals

      • Interval Size and Add Interval add notes a set number of semitones or scale degrees from the originals, for quick chords. With notes selected, moving the slider adds them at once; without a selection, Add Interval applies to every note in the clip.

    • 10.5.9.5 Stretch

      • The Stretch factor changes note lengths; ×2 and /2 double or halve notes, the time selection or the loop. Stretch doesn't change the loop length.

    • 10.5.9.6 Note Duration

      • Choose Fit to Time Range, the grid, or a fixed length, then press Set Length; with nothing selected it applies to every note.

    • 10.5.9.7 Humanize

      • Humanize Amount sets random variation of note starts, up to a quarter of a grid step either way; press Humanize to apply.

    • 10.5.9.8 Reverse

      • Flips the order of the notes in time, or the whole clip when nothing is selected.

    • 10.5.9.9 Legato

      • Lengthens or shortens each note to reach the next; the last note extends to the loop end. The Span tool also does this.

    • 10.7.2 The …Time Commands in the MIDI Note Editor

      • Cut, Copy and Paste act on notes; the "… Time" commands insert or remove time in the whole clip, without moving its start, end or loop brace.

        • Delete Time removes it and closes the gap.
        • Insert Time adds empty time the length of the selection, before it.

Ableton/Live/UG/14 Grooves/01 Groove Pool/01 Adjusting Groove Parameters
  • Each groove in the pool has buttons to save and hot-swap it, and these parameters, which change playback in real time:
    • Base — the note grid the groove is measured against, such as 1/4 or 1/8. Groove notes that fall on that grid don't move your notes.
    • Quantize — straight quantization applied before the groove: 100% snaps to the Base grid, 0% leaves notes where they were.
    • Timing — how strongly the groove's timing moves the clip.
    • Random — random timing offsets, different for each voice; small amounts loosen tightly quantized loops.
    • Velocity — how much the groove's velocities affect the clip, from −100 to +100. Negative values invert loud and quiet.
    • Global Amount — scales Timing, Random and Velocity for every groove, up to 130%. It also appears in the Control Bar once any clip has a groove.
Ableton/Live/UG/14 Grooves/03 Groove Tips/02 Non-Destructive Quantization
  • Set a groove's Timing, Random and Velocity to 0% and use only Quantize and Base for quantization that can be changed or undone at any time. Which groove file you use then doesn't matter.
Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions
  • Linked/Unlinked (clips only) — Linked fires at the end of the clip, or after the number of loops in the Multiplier field. Unlinked fires after the Follow Action Time.
  • Follow Action Time — bars-beats-sixteenths from where play starts; one bar by default. A draggable marker shows it in the clip editor.
  • Stop — stops the clip after the Follow Action Time, overriding its loop settings.
Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions/01 Looping Parts of a Clip
  • On the first clip set Follow Action Time to its length, Action A to Next at 100%, and Action B to No Action.
Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions/03 Temporarily Looping Clips
  • A single clip with Play Again at 80% and No Action at 20%, and a one-bar Follow Action Time, repeats its first bar a few times before playing on through the rest of the sample.
Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions/04 Adding Variations in Sync
  • Identical melody clips in Legato Mode can hand over to each other without the melody changing; editing each clip's settings, envelopes or notes a little at a time then makes the melody change gradually.
Ableton/Live/UG/16 Launching Clips/07 Follow Actions/05 Mixing up Melodies and Beats
  • Make several copies of a beat or melody with different start and end points and envelopes, set each Follow Action Time to the length you want played, give each two actions with different Chances, and launch one for an unpredictable remix.

Ableton/Live/UG/18 Mixing/01 The Live Mixer
  • Solo (S) — mutes all other tracks, or cues when set up for it. Only one track solos at a time unless you hold Cmd (Mac) / Ctrl (Windows) or turn off Exclusive Solo.
  • Arm — record-enables the track, again one at a time unless you hold the modifier or turn off Exclusive Arm. With Exclusive Arm on, adding an instrument to an empty MIDI track arms it.

Ableton/Live/UG/19 Recording New Clips/04 Recording in Sync
  • Live keeps recordings in sync even when the tempo changes later, so a hard part can be recorded slowly and sped up afterwards. That only works if the recording was made in time.
Ableton/Live/UG/19 Recording New Clips/04 Recording in Sync/01 Metronome Settings
  • Rhythm sets the tick's beat division. Auto follows the time signature's denominator; divisions that don't fit a bar are disabled. If a time signature change makes the chosen division not fit, the tick falls back to Auto, and returns once it fits again.
Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio
  • Bouncing tracks, group tracks and time selections to new audio tracks.
  • Bouncing turns tracks, clips or time selections into audio on new audio tracks — to experiment with the result, to lock in sound design before mixing, or to lower CPU load.
Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio/01 Bouncing Individual Tracks
  • Bounce to New Track renders selected clips or a time selection to a new audio track and mutes the originals. Context menu, or Cmd+B (Mac) / Ctrl+B (Windows).
  • A time selection across several Arrangement tracks makes one new audio track per track with a selected clip.
Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio/02 Bouncing Group Tracks
  • Bounce Group to New Track renders selected clips or a time selection in the group to a new track and mutes the originals; also Cmd+B / Ctrl+B.
Ableton/Live/UG/20 Bounce to Audio/03 Pasting Bounced Audio
  • Copy clips in one Session track, or clips or a time selection in the Arrangement (a group's main lane works too). Then paste into an audio track, an empty MIDI track or a take lane from the main lane's context menu, or with Cmd+Option+V (Mac) / Ctrl+Alt+V (Windows). An empty MIDI track becomes an audio track.
Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping/05 Auditioning Take Lanes
  • Lanes on different tracks can play at the same time, but only one lane per track. With several selected, the last-selected lane on each track plays.
Ableton/Live/UG/21 Comping/06 Creating a Comp
  • Select clips or a time range and press Cmd/Ctrl+up/down arrow to swap in the same span from the next or previous non-empty take. On a take lane the selection moves with it.
  • In Draw Mode, click and drag across a take to copy it to the main lane in one gesture, or click a take to fill a main-lane time selection from it.
