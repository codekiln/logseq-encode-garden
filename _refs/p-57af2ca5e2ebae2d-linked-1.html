Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer/13 Settings/04 Select Scale
9.13.4 Selecting the Scale
  • Press one of the 16 scale pads. The selected scale lights bright white; the others are dim blue. The choice applies in Launchpad/UG/06 Note Mode, Launchpad/UG/07 Chord Mode, and Launchpad/UG/09 Sequencer modes.
