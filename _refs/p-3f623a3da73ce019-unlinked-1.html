Ableton/Live/UG/08 Clip View/03 Extended Clip Properties/02 MIDI Clip Bank and Program Change Controls
  • A MIDI clip can send a bank and program change when it launches, so each clip can call up a different sound on an external synth or plug-in. Live offers 128 banks, each with 128 sub-banks of 128 programs; check how many your synth supports.
Ableton/Live/UG/12 Editing MPE/06 MPE-Multi-channel Settings/02 The MPE-Multi-Channel Settings Dialog
  • Two tracks routed to the same MIDI output, one per zone, can drive two MPE synths chained through MIDI thru, or two sounds in one synth.
Ableton/Live/UG/13 Audio to MIDI/03 Convert Melody to New MIDI Track
  • Detects the pitches in monophonic audio and writes them to a new MIDI track with a synth Instrument Rack. Its "Synth to Piano" Macro moves the sound from an analog-style synth to an electric piano.
Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/03 External MIDI In-Out/01 MIDI Port Inputs and Outputs
  • Turn it on for an input to play or record notes, or to record CC from a hardware synth into clips.
  • Input: to follow an external sequencer, drum machine, groovebox or another DAW. Output: to drive those from Live, or to sync an external synth's LFO and arpeggiator.
Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/03 External MIDI In-Out/03 Connecting External Synthesizers
  • To play an external synth, set the track's Output Type to the synth's MIDI port and Output Channel to its channel.
  • The External Instrument device does the same from inside the device chain, and also brings the synth's audio back into the same track. See Ableton/How To/Record MIDI and Audio from external device.
  • When one keyboard synth is both your controller and a sound source, turn on its Local Off so Live becomes the hub that passes keyboard and clip MIDI to it.
Ableton/Live/UG/17 Routing and IO/05 Internal Routings/02 Making Use of Internal Routing
  • A vocoder on a strings track needs a speech signal at its sidechain input: create a "Speech" track whose Output Type is the strings track and whose Output Channel is the vocoder's sidechain. Vocoder plug-ins with their own carrier synth go on a MIDI track and are fed the same way.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/34 Saturator
  • Bass Shaper — like Analog Clip with smoother harmonics on heavily driven lows; made for 808 kicks and synth bass. Bass Shaper Threshold (0 to −50 dB) goes from soft clipping (low values, best for bass with high Drive) to hard clipping (high values).
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/42 Vocoder
  • A vocoder imposes the level contour of one signal, the modulator (usually speech or drums), onto the frequencies of another, the carrier (usually a rich synth such as strings or a pad). The classic result is a talking synth or robot voice.
Ableton/Live/UG/29 Audio Effect Reference/42 Vocoder/01 Vocoder Tips
  • 2. Put a synth such as Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/01 Analog on another track, played from a clip or live.
  • 3. Set Carrier to External and pick the synth track in Audio From (Post FX in the lower chooser works best).
  • 5. Play the synth while speaking: the speech rhythm comes through with the synth's tone. Solo the voice track to hear only the vocoded sound.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/01 Analog
  • Analog is a virtual analog synth made with Applied Acoustics Systems. It doesn't copy one vintage synth; it combines features of several.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/03 Drift
  • Drift is a light-on-CPU subtractive synth with a simple interface and full MPE support.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/06 External Instrument
  • A routing utility, not a sound source: it sends MIDI to a hardware synth or a multitimbral plug-in and brings its audio back.
  • Audio From — for hardware, the audio interface inputs wired to the synth's outputs, as set in the Audio Settings; for a plug-in, its auxiliary outputs. Main outputs play on the plug-in's own track.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/08 Meld/09 Filters
  • MS2 — low- and high-pass from a Sallen-Key design in a well-known Japanese semi-modular mono synth, with soft clipping on resonance.
  • OSR — band-pass from a rare British mono synth, resonance limited by a hard-clipping diode.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/09 Operator/05 Filter Section
  • The filter shapes the oscillators' output; with classic waveforms Operator can work as a subtractive synth.
  • OSR — state variable with a hard-clipping diode, from a rare British mono synth; all types.
  • PRD — a ladder with no resonance limiting, from an American dual-oscillator mono synth; low- and high-pass.
Ableton/Live/UG/31 Instrument Reference/11 Simpler
  • Simpler combines a basic sampler with classic synth controls: each voice plays a region of a sample through envelope, filter, LFO, volume and pitch.
  • The Sample tab shows the waveform and the Controls tab the synth controls. A title bar button moves the sample view into Live's main window, leaving the Controls tab in the Device View.
Ableton/Live/UG/40 MIDI Fact Sheet/01 Ideal MIDI Behavior
  • Playback — stored MIDI goes out to a hardware synth, or is turned into audio by an instrument such as Operator, ideally as an exact reproduction.
  • Playthrough — MIDI from hardware passes through the DAW in real time to a synth or plug-in, ideally feeling as immediate as a piano.
Ableton/Live/UG/40 MIDI Fact Sheet/05 Tips for Achieving Optimal MIDI Performance
  • Turn track monitoring off when you listen to a hardware synth directly rather than through Live's External Instrument, and when recording MIDI generated by another device such as a drum machine. Monitoring adds latency to cover playthrough jitter, so use it only when actually playing through Live. See Ableton/Track/IO/Monitor/Off.
Ableton/Track/Q/How can I turn a mono signal into stereo in a channel?
  • A mono sample, a mono input and a mono synth all reach the device chain as two identical channels. Stereo is whatever difference you put between them.
