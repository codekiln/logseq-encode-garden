Logseq/Entity/Identity/Frontmatter/identity-of
identity-of:: — people behind a public identity
  • On a Logseq/Entity/Identity instance, links the known real people behind that public identity.
Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music
Music IdentityInherited rules
  • Naming, person relationships, deduplication and marking follow Logseq/Entity/Identity. A Music Identity is an identity, not a biographical musician.
Music IdentityChild entities
  • Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music/Band
Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music/Band
BandInherited rules
  • Attribution and release placement follow Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music, which inherits Logseq/Entity/Identity. A Band instance carries only the Band entity from this lineage.
BandMembership
  • Known people can be linked through Logseq/Entity/Identity/Frontmatter/identity-of. Membership periods, instruments and roles belong in dated body blocks when known; an undated person link makes no claim about current membership.
Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording
Music RecordingNaming and placement
  • Place a recording under its first credited Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music as <Identity>/Music/Recording/<Title> when Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities is present. For a joint release, keep one page under the first credited identity and list all credited identities. Without a separate identity, use Person/<Artist>/Music/Recording/<Title>. Existing group namespaces remain valid until deliberately renamed.
Logseq/Entity/Music/Work
Musical WorkNaming and placement
  • Place a work under its primary credited Logseq/Entity/Identity as <Identity>/Music/Work/<Title> when Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities is present. Without a separate credited identity, use Person/<Creator>/Music/Work/<Title>. A recording artist credit alone does not establish authorship of the underlying composition.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker
MakerWorks
  • A page for something a maker made points back to the maker's hub with created-by::, as software project and book pages in the garden already do. A published stage or project identity is represented separately by Logseq/Entity/Identity; Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities records that identity's credit on the creation. The maker's linked references then list their works, so the hub needs no hand-kept list of them.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music
MusicianNaming and identity
  • A stage or project name with its own release identity has a separate Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music page linked to the person through Logseq/Entity/Identity/Frontmatter/identity-of. The published name is the identity page title, so it appears directly in autocomplete.
MusicianNaming and identity
  • A band with its own identity follows Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music/Band and has a page separate from its members' hubs. Existing solo aliases and group pages remain valid until deliberately separated or renamed.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production
Music ProducerWorks
  • A release follows Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording: it lives under the credited Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music when represented separately, or under the producer's hub otherwise.
Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities
created-by-identities:: — credited public identities
  • On a work, recording or other creation, lists the Logseq/Entity/Identity pages credited as its creators or artists, in published order.
