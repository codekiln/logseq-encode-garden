Risk vs RewardThe Trade-Off

  • Risk/vs/Reward/Risk

    • Risk/vs/Reward/Risk
      • Accepting risk means choosing an option whose outcomes spread wide, including outcomes that lose.
      • Gains — access to payoffs that safe options never reach. The spread is what makes the large upside available at all, and bearing it is what the reward pays for.
      • Costs — the chance of loss, the attention it takes to hold the exposure in mind, and the compounding damage when a loss cannot be recovered from.
      • Too far — ruin: a single bad draw that ends the game, so no later reward is ever collected. A portfolio, a project or a reputation staked on one outcome.
Risk vs RewardThe Trade-Off

  • Risk/vs/Reward/Reward

    • Risk/vs/Reward/Reward
      • Reaching for reward means choosing the option with the largest expected gain.
      • Gains — the upside itself: return, capability, reach or learning that the cautious option leaves on the table.
      • Costs — the risk that comes attached, since the options with the most reward carry the widest spread, and the habit of counting the upside while discounting the downside.
      • Too far — chasing: taking on any exposure that promises a payoff, until the accumulated spread swamps the expected gain. Its mirror image is leaving reward on the table out of habit, taking only what is certain and small.
