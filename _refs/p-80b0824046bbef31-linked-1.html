    • 35.2 Browsing and Loading Sounds

      • Browse Mode loads sounds and effects from Push 1 without Live's browser. The display shows devices, presets and the browser's Places in columns, one column per folder level; In and Out shift the columns to reach deeper folders or show more presets.
      • The Selection Control buttons (first row under the display) move up a level and the State Control buttons (second row) move down one. Core Library and Pack items preview when selected.
      • The green button on the right loads the selected preset; the one on the left loads the device's default preset. A loaded entry's button turns amber, and pressing it again loads the next entry, which makes auditioning quick. Encoders scroll quickly; Shift with up or down moves a page.
      • What Browse shows depends on the last selected device: replacement instruments for an instrument, effects for an effect, and everything loadable for an empty MIDI track. Folders appear only when they hold something loadable at that moment; samples show only when browsing a single Drum Rack pad.
