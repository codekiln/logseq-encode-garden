Unlinked references: Wabi

Wabi/Sabi

  • Wabi-sabi

    • celebrating objects and lives that show the marks of use, change, and time rather than demanding pristine perfection.

    • Wabi

      • Refers to rustic simplicity, freshness, or quietness, and finding joy in a modest, unpretentious life removed from material excess

    • Sabi

      • Refers to the beauty and value that comes with age, wear, and the natural passage of time (such as the weathered look of an old wooden table).
