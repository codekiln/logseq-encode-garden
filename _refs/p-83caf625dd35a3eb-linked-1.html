Hidden Folders Access
  • Obsidian community plugin by Person/Sebastien Dubois that opts specific root-level dot-folders (e.g. .claude, .github) into the file explorer, search, graph, metadata cache, and Bases — without renaming, symlinking, or duplicating anything on disk.
