Logseq/Entity/Hierarchy/Discussion/Analysis/Opus/26/10/01/0611 ET Mark each page with the most specific entity in each lineage
Mark each page with the most specific entity in each lineage
  • Each page should list only its most specific entities. A musician who is also a knowledge gardener lists Logseq/Entity/Person/Musician and Logseq/Entity/Person/Knowledge Gardener and leaves out Logseq/Entity/Person, because each of those is already a kind of person. Musician, then Person, is one lineage: an entity followed by each entity it is a kind of. This narrows the hypothesis codekiln wrote in the 2026-10-01 Thu journal: the rule covers only a child entity that is a kind of its parent, and it lets one page carry two sibling entities. The Musician, Knowledge Gardener and User Guide definition pages currently say to mark each instance with the parent entity as well.
Mark each page with the most specific entity in each lineageA child entity is either a kind of its parent or a part of it
  • Is-a. Every instance of the child is also an instance of the parent: Logseq/Entity/Article/Blog, Logseq/Entity/Repo/Awesome, the Logseq/Entity/Book/Section levels, Logseq/Entity/Person/Musician, Logseq/Entity/Person/Knowledge Gardener, Logseq/Entity/Book/User Guide.
Mark each page with the most specific entity in each lineageSibling entities may share a page; an entity and its own ancestor may not
  • Taken literally, "more than one entity with the same entity ancestry" also forbids a person who is both a musician and a knowledge gardener, since Logseq/Entity/Person/Musician and Logseq/Entity/Person/Knowledge Gardener both descend from Person. Neither entity implies the other, so marking both carries information.
Mark each page with the most specific entity in each lineageMusician, Knowledge Gardener and User Guide instances list the parent entity too
  • Logseq/Entity/Person/Knowledge Gardener and Logseq/Entity/Person/Musician each say "Frontmatter lists both types, Person first". Logseq/Entity/Book/User Guide marks a guide with both User Guide and Book, child first.
Mark each page with the most specific entity in each lineageMusician, Knowledge Gardener and User Guide instances list the parent entity too
  • The likely reason for marking both is backlinks: the backlinks of Logseq/Entity/Person list a musician only when the musician's page links Person directly. Moving to child-only markers would mean:
    • rewriting the Musician, Knowledge Gardener and User Guide definition pages to mark the child alone;
    • changing logseq-entity:: on the eight instance pages;
    • adding the lineage rule to Logseq/Entity, together with the statement that an instance of a child entity is an instance of every ancestor;
    • listing all people with a query over Person and every entity that descends from it, because backlinks alone would miss the musicians.

Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition
Composer
  • In this garden, Composer pages model a real person who writes music as works: pieces, scores, soundtracks, and fixed-media or electroacoustic works. A Composer is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music, and the rest follows Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker.
ComposerWhat counts as an instance
  • A person known mainly for songs in the popular, folk, blues or rock traditions carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition/Song and leaves Composer out, since a Songwriter is a kind of Composer. Franz Schubert, who wrote art songs, and Tito Puente, who wrote tunes for his band, carry Composer.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • An improviser whose music exists only in performance carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance. An improviser who also writes pieces carries Composer as well.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • An electronic producer who releases tracks carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Production, and adds Composer for works presented as compositions, such as concert pieces or film scores: Person/Daniel Lopatin.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • A person who makes sound installations or sound sculptures carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Art/Sound, and adds Composer when they also write pieces for concert or release: Person/Wei Yang.
ComposerBoundary cases
  • A composer who also writes music theory adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Theory; one who also leads ensembles from the podium adds Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Performance/Conducting, as Person/John Adams does.

Songwriter
  • In this garden, Songwriter pages model a real person who writes songs: the music, the words, or both. A Songwriter is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition, so a page marked Songwriter leaves Composer out.
SongwriterWhat counts as an instance
  • A composer of art songs, such as Franz Schubert and his Lieder, carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition. So does a jazz or Latin bandleader who writes tunes for the band, such as Tito Puente and "Oye Como Va".
SongwriterBoundary cases

SongwriterBoundary cases

PerformerBoundary cases

  • A researcher who publishes about music technology without building it carries only the entities for what else they make, or stays a Logseq/Entity/Person.
Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Theory
Music Theorist
  • In this garden, Music Theorist pages model a real person who makes music theory: treatises, systems of scales, modes or harmony, methods of analysis, and theory textbooks. A Music Theorist is a kind of Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker whose field is music, and the rest follows that page.
Music TheoristWhat counts as an instance
  • A Music Theorist is a maker of theory. A person who also made music carries the kind of Musician that names their role as well, as a composer-theorist carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Composition and Music Theorist.
Music TheoristBoundary cases
  • A teacher whose published textbook or teaching method sets out how music is organized counts. A teacher who has published no theory, method or textbook carries only the entities for what else they make, or stays a Logseq/Entity/Person.
Music TheoristBoundary cases
  • A researcher who invents a synthesis method or builds music software carries Logseq/Entity/Person/Maker/Music/Tech.
