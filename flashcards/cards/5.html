Ableton/Live/Keyshort/Momentary-Latching-ShortcutsKeyshort Ableton/Live Ableton/Live/Keyshort
  • Make Automation Mode, Draw Mode, solo, Arrangement zoom, a track activator or the view switch last only while its key is held Card
  • Shortcut: hold A, B, S, Z, F1…F8 or Tab for about 500 ms, then release
  • Description: The held key toggles its action, and releasing it restores the previous state. The Options.txt entry -DisableHotKeyLatching turns latching off. See Ableton/Live/UG/42 Keyboard Shortcuts/25 Momentary Latching Shortcuts.
