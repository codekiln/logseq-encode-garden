Ableton/Live/Keyshort/Solo-Selected-TracksKeyshort Ableton/Live Ableton/Live/Keyshort
  • Solo the selected tracks, or the selected chain in a Rack Card
  • Shortcut: S
  • Description: Solos the selected tracks, or the selected chain when a Rack's chain list has focus, which the Session View list calls Solo Selected Chain.
