LV4AD/Ch/03 Getting Around/04 Find Mode/To Mode for Operations

  • 1. What does t do differently from f in Find/To mode, and 2. what does d2ts delete? card

  • 1. t lands before the target character — easier combined with change/delete operators than f on the char itself
  • 2. d2ts — deletes from cursor up to (but not including) the second s ahead on the line
LV4AD/Ch/03 Getting Around/05 Moving by Words/Web Words Backward

  • 1. What Keyshort moves backward to word beginnings, and 2. what Keyshort pair moves to the end of the previous word? card

  • 1. b — beginning of the current word, or the previous word if already at the beginning (countable)
  • 2. ge — go to end of the previous word (g then e; not g4e)
LV4AD/Ch/03 Getting Around/05 Moving by Words/Web Words Forward

  • 1. What Keyshort moves to the beginning of the next word, and 2. what moves to the end of the current (or next) word? card

  • 1. w — start of the next word (count repeats for farther jumps)
  • 2. e — end of the current word, or end of the next word if already at the current word's end
LV4AD/Ch/03 Getting Around/06 Moving by Words Only BIGGER/BIG Word Backward and End

  • 1. What Keyshort is the shifted form of b for BIG words, 2. what is the shifted form of e, and 3. what is the shifted form of ge? card

  • 1. B — backward by whitespace-delimited words (BIG b)
  • 2. E — forward to end of a whitespace-delimited word (BIG e)
  • 3. gE — end of the previous BIG word (BIG ge)
LV4AD/Ch/03 Getting Around/06 Moving by Words Only BIGGER/W vs w Word Boundaries

  • 1. What Keyshort stops at punctuation-delimited words on myObj.methodName('foo'), and 2. what Keyshort jumps by whitespace-delimited words instead? card

  • 1. w — treats punctuation as word boundaries (many stops across methodName('foo', …))
  • 2. W — moves to just after the next whitespace character (fewer, bigger stops on the same line)
LV4AD/Ch/03 Getting Around/07 Line Targets/Line End Keyshorts

  • 1. What Keyshort moves to the end of the line, and 2. what moves to the last non-blank character? card

  • 1. $ (Shift+4) — last character before the newline, regardless of trailing spaces
  • 2. g_ — last non-blank on the line (g then _; alternative combos include $ge or $be)
LV4AD/Ch/03 Getting Around/07 Line Targets/Line Start Keyshorts

  • 1. What Keyshort moves to the first non-whitespace character on the line, and 2. what moves to column zero? card

  • 1. ^ (Shift+6) — first text on the line; skips leading indentation
  • 2. 0 — the very beginning of the line (first column), including whitespace
LV4AD/Ch/03 Getting Around/08 Jump History/Jump List Back and Forward

  • 1. What Keyshort jumps back to where you came from in the jump list, and 2. what jumps forward again? card

  • 1. <C-o> — older position ("go to the place I jumped from")
  • 2. <C-i> — opposite of <C-o>; steps forward through jump history (same as <Tab> in many contexts)
LV4AD/Ch/04 Opening Files/01 Introducing File Pickers/File Picker Smart Case

  • 1. What's the default case sensitivity for the Fuzzy/Matching LazyVim/Picker/File, 2. what makes it case sensitive, and 3. what is that UX called? card

  • 1. the default is case insensitive

  • 3. "smart case"
LV4AD/Ch/04 Opening Files/02 Root vs Cwd/Find Files Cwd Mode Keyshort

LV4AD/Ch/04 Opening Files/02 Root vs Cwd/Local Change Directory Command

  • :lcd — "local change directory"
  • lets different windows have different working directories (e.g. full-stack frontend + backend)
LV4AD/Ch/04 Opening Files/02 Root vs Cwd/Print Working Directory Command

  • What command prints the current working directory? card

  • :pwd — "print working directory"
  • same as Bash/zsh pwd; shows the cwd Neovim uses for :e and pickers scoped to cwd

LV4AD/Ch/04 Opening Files/04 Mini.files Alternative/Mini.files Text Editor Keyshorts

  • 1. What Keyshort creates a file or folder in nvim/Plugin/mini.files, 2. what deletes, 3. what copies, and 4. what pastes? card

LV4AD/Ch/04 Opening Files/04 Mini.files Alternative/Open Mini.files Keyshorts
