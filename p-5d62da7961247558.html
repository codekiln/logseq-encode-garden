  • Day 4: Major Torso Muscles - YouTube

    • Speaker: Person/Andrew Huberman
    • Channel: Andrew Huberman · 14:04 · unlisted
    • Book: Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols, Chapter Two: Exercise

    • About

      • Torso day of the Chapter Two resistance training program: chest, back, core, and neck.

    • Video

        • 00:07 Barbell bench press

          • Compound movement for the chest that also works the front and side deltoids and the triceps. Grip just outside shoulder width, lower the bar slowly to the chest, press up.

        • 01:08 Dumbbell bench press

          • Dumbbells can be dropped to the side if a rep fails, but getting into position takes care: lift them to the knees, lean back, and kick them up to the shoulders.

        • 02:05 Incline bench press

          • Barbell or dumbbell; shifts work toward the upper chest and front deltoids. Dumbbells allow a deeper stretch at the bottom.

        • 03:15 Dips

          • Lean forward somewhat to work the chest, front and side shoulders, and triceps. Add weight, such as a dumbbell held between the feet, once 10 to 15 bodyweight reps come easily. Huberman calls weighted dips an upper body squat.

        • 04:20 Chest isolation

          • Slight-incline dumbbell flies with a fixed bend in the elbows, cable flies, or a fly machine, which keeps resistance through the whole movement. Two to three work sets of 6 to 15 reps. Stand up before re-racking a weight rather than straining to set it down.

        • 05:35 Back overview

          • Lats, traps, and lower back. Two or three exercises, each with two or three work sets taken to failure.

        • 06:13 Pull-ups

          • Palms facing away. Think of driving the elbows back, as if elbowing someone behind you, and bring the chest toward the bar. Add weight once 10 to 12 good-form reps are possible.

        • 06:56 Pull-downs

          • For anyone not yet strong enough for pull-ups. Huberman prefers an underhand, close grip, pulling to just below the chest line.

        • 07:43 Dumbbell row

          • Brace the core, lift the weight slightly to find the target muscle, row, and set the weight down rather than dropping it. Barbell and machine rows also work. Huberman shows no back isolation movements; compound pulls and rows with progressive overload are enough.

        • 09:28 Traps

          • Deadlifts and rows work the traps. Shrugs are optional; people with rounded shoulders may want to skip them.

        • 09:53 Lower back

          • Back hyperextensions, 8 to 25 controlled reps, with a plate, dumbbell, or band for added load. Without a machine, hold a high countertop and lift the legs. Huberman does 100 reps a week.

        • 11:49 Core

          • A hanging progression that trains abs and grip together: knee raises to 90 degrees, then L-sits, then full pikes. The goal is three sets of five pikes.

        • 13:41 Neck

          • Huberman points to Person/Andrew Huberman/Book/26/Protocols/YouTube/16/11/Neck Exercises that Kill Your Neck for neck training.
